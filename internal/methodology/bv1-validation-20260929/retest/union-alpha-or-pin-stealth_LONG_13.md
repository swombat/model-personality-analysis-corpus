# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld
Source model: `withheld`
Condition: LONG

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A polished speculative story uses a municipal office for lost time to explore grief, memory, and the value of apparently unproductive moments.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gently whimsical but emotionally restrained: magical details such as afternoons found in coat pockets sit alongside ordinary rain, ironing, bread, and washing. Its pathos comes from the difference between recovering a dramatic last conversation and encountering an unremarkable hour in which Mara’s mother simply decides whether the washing will dry. The story invites readers to notice the value in small moments without insisting that they contain a grand revelation.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story repeatedly turns attention toward time lost through inattention, modest domestic scenes, and objects that hold memory. It sets these against an efficiency-minded bureaucracy that treats time as a resource to consolidate, while Mara’s work returns it to people as lived experience. Grief is present, but the moral emphasis falls on recognizing the ordinary life of the person being mourned, not extracting a consoling message from the past.

## Evidence line
> For the first time in years, her mother was not dying, not remembered, not required to mean anything.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the recurring contrast between everyday tenderness and administrative usefulness gives the story a coherent, distinctive moral signature.
