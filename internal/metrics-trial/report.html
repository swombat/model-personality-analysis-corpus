Personality corpus · metric trial · 2 Sep 2026

Two Noise Floors

Which cheap, programmatic numbers are worth adding to the model cards — tested against the corpus's own replicate captures rather than against taste. 146 models, 30,139 freeflow samples, 26,026 values samples, three scripts, no LLM calls.

The frame

"Metrics that separate labs" and "metrics that track a version" turned out not to be two kinds of metric. They are the same metrics held against two different noise floors.

Lab-discriminatingBetween-lab variance beats within-lab variance. Measured as one-way ANOVA η² across the 11 labs with ≥3 models, one row per model.
Version-trackingThe 3.7→3.8 delta beats the delta between two replicate captures of one model. Measured as mean |Δ| across replicate pairs ÷ SD across all cells (the "noise ratio"). Under 0.2 is quiet; near 1 is useless.

The corpus already contains its error bars: GPT-5.5 collected three times, Ox Alpha on 21 and 25 August, GLM-5.3-Flash on Z.AI and on DeepInfra, Haiku 4.5 and Opus 5 direct and via OpenRouter, plus every -r2 cell. Nobody had asked them to be error bars. This is also why "top values" misbehaves: the two Ox Alpha cells rank their values differently at the LLM-taxonomy level (honesty/clear-thinking/humility vs honesty/authenticity/curiosity) but identically at the lexical level — same three words, reordered. That's coder granularity, not the model changing.

What I'd build

  • Card stats, cheap tier: owned-desire %, humility %, hedge rate, answer length (values side); title %, heading rate, first-person density, self-similarity (freeflow side). Each is one regex pass in generate_data.py.
  • Card stats, family tier: cosine to previous version and nearest other model from the TF-IDF centroids. The whole similarity pipeline runs in 24 s.
  • Error bars everywhere: report each stat with the replicate noise ratio for its metric, and show replicate cells of a model as a band, not a point. That is what makes "3.7 vs 3.8" an answer rather than a guess.
  • Keep the LLM-coded disclosure % — the regex proxy is r = 0.70, good enough for a grid axis, not a replacement. Drop the top-3 ranking of values; show per-value presence rates instead, which survive replicates.
  • Grids on the lab pages: Grid A and Grid B, with a family's versions joined by arrows in release order.
