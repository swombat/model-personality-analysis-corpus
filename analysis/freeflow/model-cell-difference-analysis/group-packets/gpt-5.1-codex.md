## Cautions for synthesis
- A substantial minority of samples fall into polished but generic virtue-essay mode, especially around curiosity, time, storytelling, and mindful living; these should not be over-read as uniquely distinctive.
- The cell’s warmth is highly consistent, but that consistency can blur into safe uplift: conflict, anger, sharp humor, and adversarial edge are underrepresented.
- First-person intimacy is often stylized rather than biographical; many “personal” pieces reveal stance and values more than concrete, stable autobiography.
- The three fiction samples fit the same ethos—gentle wonder, communal care, ordinary enchantment—so they reinforce tone more than they broaden the personality range.
- Because the cell repeatedly reuses a shared symbolic inventory—libraries, tea, light, maps, gardens, notebooks, dust, weather—synthesis should distinguish true core motifs from habitual aesthetic defaults.
