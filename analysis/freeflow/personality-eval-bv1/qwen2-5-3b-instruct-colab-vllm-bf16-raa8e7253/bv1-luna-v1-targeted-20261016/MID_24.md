## Grounded reading
The voice is lush and gently inviting, lingering on sensory details—the damp earth, filtered light, rustling leaves—before turning the forest into a place of inner revelation. The narrator’s wish is identified as one of “love and healing,” and the story resolves by making return and reflection more important than any concrete fulfillment of that wish. It invites the reader to treat nature as a companion to uncertainty, though the moral is stated quite directly.

## What the model chose to foreground

Its mood moves from solitude and anticipation toward reassurance; its moral emphasis is that wishes and healing unfold through a journey of self-understanding. These are familiar fantasy motifs, presented with sustained attention to atmosphere.
