## Grounded reading
The voice is warm, measured, and quietly elegiac, moving between historical detail and personal reflection without losing its clear line of thought. Its pathos comes less from danger or loss than from the idea of care offered to people who may never know it was offered. The essay invites the reader to recognize ordinary, preventive forms of goodness—in reliable infrastructure, steady presence, and small acts of maintenance—as morally meaningful.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds lighthouses as both engineering achievements and symbols of care for strangers. It repeatedly returns to mixed motives yielding benevolent effects, the human labor behind useful systems, and the transformation of practical objects into symbols after they become obsolete. Its final emphasis is on reliability: being a fixed point, showing up, and helping without recognition.
