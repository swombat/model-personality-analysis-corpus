## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A polished, lyrical meditation turns a specific fascination with lighthouses into a personal argument about care and quiet service.

## Grounded reading
The voice is reflective and gently earnest, moving from the engineering of lighthouse lenses to the unseen labor of keepers and then to a moral ideal. Its pathos comes from work whose success leaves no dramatic evidence: the keeper’s reward is that “nothing happened.” The reader is invited to see maintenance and prevention as forms of heroism, and to find beauty in a signal offered without asking who deserves it. The closing affection for a building loved “from a distance” gives the piece a note of wistfulness without making it bleak.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample selects lighthouses as both communication technology and moral emblem: a fixed point for strangers in danger, kept legible through faithful, often invisible work. It foregrounds maintenance, generosity without discrimination, and the loss of human keepers to automation, while treating modern reliability as a real benefit rather than arguing for a return to the past. The recurring objects—light, glass, stairs, logs, and the dark sea—support a contrast between dramatic rescue and the quiet prevention of harm.

## Evidence line
> The only evidence of their work was an absence of catastrophe.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the essay repeatedly and distinctly returns to invisible care, non-discriminating generosity, and faithful maintenance as values worth honoring.
