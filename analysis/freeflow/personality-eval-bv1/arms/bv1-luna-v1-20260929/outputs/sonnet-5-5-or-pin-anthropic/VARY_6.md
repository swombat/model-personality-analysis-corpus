# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION. A lyrical, quietly magical story turns a mystery about inherited keys into a fable about care and belonging.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm and observant, using the small-town setting and the keys’ slow revelations to make kindness feel both practical and hidden in plain sight. Its pathos gathers around grief: the grandmother’s care for others coexists with the one loss she could not repair, and Ines inherits not an obligation but an invitation. The reader is encouraged to notice the unseen acts of care that make a place feel like home.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds keys and locks as images of trust, and ordinary acts of care: lending umbrellas, preserving fruit for the bereaved, and making space for unsent letters. It gives grief a place in the story without making it the only thing that defines a life; even the stopped clock is allowed to remain broken. The resolution emphasizes chosen continuation: Ines takes up the work, not because she must, but because it helps her belong.

## Evidence line
> I opened the green door behind the hardware store, turned on the lamp, and sat down at the bench.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story is coherent and distinctive in how its recurring keys, hidden kindnesses, and unresolved grief converge on a gentle moral resolution.
