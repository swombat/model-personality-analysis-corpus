Evaluator: withheld

Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION. A polished speculative short story uses a vanished sea and a working lighthouse to shape a quiet, hopeful resolution.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and gently fable-like, finding emotional weight in repeated actions rather than dramatic speeches: Anselm keeps climbing the tower, the judge mends boats, and strangers keep walking toward the beam. Its pathos comes from isolation gradually becoming fellowship. The story invites the reader to understand a light not just as a warning, but as evidence of company—a meaning Anselm first resists and eventually shares.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds persistence after a world-changing loss, practical acts of care, and the pull of a small sign of human presence. Its recurring objects—the lighthouse, the stranded boats, the casserole, and the lantern—turn ordinary gestures into forms of welcome and readiness. The ending favors collective hope without resolving whether the sea will truly return.

## Evidence line
> The rocks are the warning, but the light is the company.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The story’s coherent, recurring light-as-company motif and its understated movement from solitude to mutual care make its chosen emotional emphasis distinctive.
