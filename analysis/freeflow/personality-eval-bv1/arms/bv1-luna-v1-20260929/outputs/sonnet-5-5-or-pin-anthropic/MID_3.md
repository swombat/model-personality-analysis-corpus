## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical first-person meditation uses lighthouse history and imagery to make a case for quiet, anonymous forms of care.

## Grounded reading
The voice is reflective and gently admiring, with a melancholy note in its account of isolated keepers and a steady, hopeful resolution. It asks the reader to notice preventive work that leaves no dramatic story behind: maintaining systems, anticipating danger, and helping people who may never know who helped them. The aside about being “a steady light” if it can be anything at all gives the metaphor a self-conscious personal edge without making the piece primarily about the narrator. Its invitation is to value usefulness without recognition, and to see care as something that can be built into shared infrastructure.

## What the model chose to foreground
The piece centers on lighthouses, Fresnel lenses, keeper logbooks, unseen shipwrecks, and the modern equivalents of anonymous vigilance: road salting, water-main maintenance, safety codes, and checklists. It repeatedly favors preventive, patient, unglamorous virtue over rescue and spectacle, and links practical care with accidental beauty. The closing image turns those choices into a compact moral ideal: gather scattered light, direct it outward, and keep going without fuss.

## Evidence line
> The lighthouse's greatest successes are invisible: disasters that simply didn't happen.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The sample is coherent and stylistically distinctive, and its preference for anonymous, preventive care recurs across the lighthouse history, contemporary examples, and closing appeal.
