## Grounded reading
The voice is warm, composed, and quietly admiring. It moves from factual history into reflection, inviting the reader to see the lighthouse not just as technology but as an image of reliable care. Its pathos is restrained: the vanished keepers and the disappearance of their work matter, but the essay resists romanticizing their hardship and returns to the practical value of keeping people safe.

## What the model chose to foreground
The essay foregrounds lighthouses as both working structures and moral symbols: engineering ingenuity, the human labor of keeping the lights, the loss of that vocation to automation, and the idea that dependable guidance helps strangers without directing their lives. Its recurring emphasis on constancy and public generosity makes the historical details serve a chosen argument about quiet forms of care.
