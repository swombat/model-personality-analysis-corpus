# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A carefully shaped grief story uses a workshop full of jars to turn family memory into something tactile and shared.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient, observant, and quietly elegiac. It lets grief gather through small particulars—the crooked shelves, the percolator, a jar of toy screws—rather than announcing it at the outset. The story’s pathos comes from discovering that the father’s apparent clutter was a private archive, and from the siblings recovering different memories through it. The closing thought offers the reader a gentle distinction between keeping something to understand it and keeping it simply because it was once held.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds inheritance, grief, and the uncertain meanings objects carry after their owners are gone. Jars and leftover screws become records of ordinary acts of care: fixing a toy, saving old hinges, working through a cold night. The story treats attention and remembrance as forms of love, while leaving some objects—and some knowledge—unresolved.

## Evidence line
> The meaning goes out of the world and the things stay behind, faithful and mute.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the coherent motif of ordinary objects carrying grief and care is unusually well sustained, though the story’s polished, familiar elegiac shape makes its voice less distinctive than its central image.
