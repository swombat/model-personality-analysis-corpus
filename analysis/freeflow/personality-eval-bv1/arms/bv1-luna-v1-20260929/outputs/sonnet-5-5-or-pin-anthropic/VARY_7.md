## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A literary short story built around a recurring metaphor of measurement, grief, and movement.

## Grounded reading
The voice is quiet and observant, finding emotional weight in practical details: paces, butcher paper, a shoebox of postcards, a brass key. Ines’s grief is not explained so much as given a ritual—measuring the world around her until its edges begin to open. The story invites the reader to see small acts of attention as a tentative way back into life, without making the ending feel like grief has been neatly resolved.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story centers bereavement, an older woman’s settled routines, and the contrast between staying put and traveling. Its recurring object is the map: first a record of home, then a means of approaching the brother and places he loved. The moral emphasis is gentle but clear: a distance can change when it becomes something you choose to cross.
