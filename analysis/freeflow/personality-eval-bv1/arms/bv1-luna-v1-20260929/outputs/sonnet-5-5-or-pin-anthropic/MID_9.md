## Sample kind
GENERIC_ESSAY. It is a polished, sectioned nature essay that moves from ecological explanation to a clear personal lesson.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient, observant, and gently instructive: it asks the reader to look more closely, then turns that act of attention into a reflection on how to live amid change. Its wonder is grounded in concrete details—barnacles holding seawater “like a canteen,” limpets returning to a “favorite chair”—rather than grand declarations. The reader is invited to slow down and share the writer’s shift from seeing an empty pool to recognizing a crowded, interconnected world.

## What the model chose to foreground
The essay foregrounds adaptation, ecological interdependence, small-scale significance, and the fragility and renewal of coastal life. Its recurring image is the tide pool as a borderland: creatures endure changing conditions not by resisting change outright, but by becoming “fluent in it.” The closing moral extends that image into human life, valuing patience and attention over speed and scale.

## Evidence line
