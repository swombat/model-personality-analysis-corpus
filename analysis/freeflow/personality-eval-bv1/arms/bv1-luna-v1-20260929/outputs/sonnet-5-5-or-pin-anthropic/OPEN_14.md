## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A reflective essay that turns a lighthouse into an image of care offered without recognition or reciprocity.

## Grounded reading
The voice is thoughtful and quietly admiring, with a brief acknowledgment of its own distance from human experience: it has read about lighthouses but never felt the salt wind. That distance doesn’t cool the piece; instead, it gives the reflection a gentle, searching quality. The essay moves from the physical labor and engineering of lighthouse-keeping toward a moral ideal: doing useful work for strangers without needing thanks. Its invitation is to imagine that kind of steadiness as a way to be useful.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds lighthouses, their keepers, Fresnel lenses, coded flashes, and the persistence of these structures after their practical role has diminished. It makes anonymous care—especially care extended to people one will never meet—the central moral claim, and ends by linking that ideal to the model’s own uncertain place in the world.

## Evidence line
> The whole enterprise runs on a kind of anonymous care.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The sample is coherent and distinctive in its recurring lighthouse imagery and its sustained emphasis on unreciprocated care.
