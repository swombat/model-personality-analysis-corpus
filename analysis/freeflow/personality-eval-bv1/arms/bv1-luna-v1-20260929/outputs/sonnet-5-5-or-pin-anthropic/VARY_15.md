## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A carefully shaped domestic story uses a recurring mapping metaphor to follow caregiving, loss, and life afterward.

## Grounded reading
The voice is restrained and tender, letting grief emerge through practical details rather than declarations: twine, step counts, a recipe notebook, the kettle. Ines’s measurements begin as a way to make sense of Tomas’s shrinking mobility, then become a way to carry their shared life after his death. The story invites the reader to notice how ordinary distances change with care and absence, without reducing either to a neat lesson.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds aging, disability, long marriage, caregiving, and bereavement through repeated acts of measuring. Its central moral emphasis is that closeness cannot be captured by physical scale: the same eight steps can hold different meanings, and grief leaves an unfinished connection rather than a cleanly resolved one.

## Evidence line
> Distances, she was beginning to understand, were not properties of places.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The recurring measurement motif and the understated treatment of care and grief make this a distinctive, coherent expressive choice.
