## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A reflective essay with a distinctive first-person voice, using lighthouse history to build a sustained metaphor for care and reliability.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm, measured, and quietly admiring. Its pathos comes less from dramatic rescue than from the unseen labor that prevents disaster: keepers tending a flame, builders learning from failure, and sailors who pass safely without knowing who helped them. The essay invites the reader to value steady, respectful help—showing people what they need to see, then trusting them to act—rather than taking over or seeking recognition.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds lighthouses as care for strangers, accumulated knowledge, and constancy under difficult conditions. It gives special weight to the Fresnel lens as an image of focusing what already exists, and to the keeper’s repetitive work as a quiet contribution whose success is measured by tragedies that never happen. Its moral claims favor useful limits, trust in others’ competence, and unglamorous faithfulness over grand gestures.
