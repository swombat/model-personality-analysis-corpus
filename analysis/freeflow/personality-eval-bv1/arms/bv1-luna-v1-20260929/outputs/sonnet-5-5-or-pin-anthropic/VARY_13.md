## Grounded reading
The voice is restrained and attentive to small physical details: striped light, a ticking pipe, a dented kettle, a sprinkler’s slow arc. Its grief is conveyed less through declarations than through distances that stand in for failed or renewed connection. The story invites the reader to notice how care can persist in ordinary records and gestures, while leaving room for loss that cannot be undone: the notebooks preserve the grandmother’s longing, but they do not make the past move closer.
