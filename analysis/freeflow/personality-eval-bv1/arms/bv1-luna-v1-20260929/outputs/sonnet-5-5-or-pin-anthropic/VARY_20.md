## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A carefully shaped, emotionally resolved story in which a widow’s map of neighborhood details becomes a practice of attention and connection.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and observant, finding emotional weight in modest particulars: a fern in pavement, a door’s sigh, a cat’s daily route. Grief is present but not treated as something to overcome; Marguerite’s attention gives it room to widen into companionship and a renewed sense of love. The story invites the reader to notice what ordinary routines conceal, and to understand close attention as a way of caring for someone.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds small acts of noticing, the material map that gathers them, and the way private observation becomes shared neighborhood knowledge. Its moral emphasis is that attention can itself be love: Teodor listened to Marguerite, and Marguerite’s map helps neighbors see one another. The mood moves from quiet bereavement toward warmth without erasing the loss.
