## Grounded reading
The voice is warm, contemplative, and gently persuasive. It finds pathos not in dramatic rescue but in the quiet prevention of harm: the keeper’s work matters precisely when nothing happens. The essay balances affection for the human presence of a keeper with a clear-eyed refusal to romanticize isolation and danger. Its invitation is to value steady, often unnoticed acts of care—and to offer that kind of dependable presence to others.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds lighthouses, the elegant subtraction of the Fresnel lens, and the characteristic rhythms by which lights identified themselves. These details support a moral emphasis on prevention, maintenance, and reliable attention, extending from lighthouse keepers to infrastructure workers, friends, colleagues, and neighbors. It also dwells on the felt difference between human vigilance and machine reliability, without declaring one morally superior.
