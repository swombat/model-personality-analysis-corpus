## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A quiet, carefully structured short story about a woman who maps small acts of kindness and eventually passes that practice on.

## Grounded reading
The voice is tender and attentive to ordinary places: a bakery’s warm exhaust, a bench, afternoon light on a church wall. Marta’s grief gives the story its ache, but the emotional center is not loss alone; it is the way brief, unassuming kindness can become a form of memory and care. The ending invites the reader to notice and carry forward small kindnesses rather than make them public or grand.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds unofficial knowledge, grief, and the emotional lives hidden inside familiar places. Its recurring objects—the hand-drawn maps, three dots, the bench, and the changing town—turn observation into a modest moral practice: record what helped, then make it available to someone who might need it. The final act of drawing gives that practice a sense of inheritance rather than closure.

## Evidence line
> It stopped being a set of addresses and became a set of moments, some hers and some borrowed from strangers.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The story sustains a distinctive focus on small, place-bound kindnesses, with its recurring map symbols and final image reinforcing the same emotional concern.
