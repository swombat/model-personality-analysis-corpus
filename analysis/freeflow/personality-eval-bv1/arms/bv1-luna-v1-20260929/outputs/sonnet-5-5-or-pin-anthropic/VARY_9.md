# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld
Source model: `withheld`
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A lyrical magical-realist story uses the sea’s returned objects to bring a grieving woman into contact with a long-buried part of her past.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and quietly uncanny, with grief carried through ordinary things rather than explained at length: a folded jacket, a piano key, a suitcase of unopened letters. Odalys’s discovery does not erase her love for Ansel or resolve the mystery of the sea; it complicates what she has been searching for. The ending offers hope as a loosening of certainty, inviting the reader to regard not knowing what comes next as a form of possibility.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds bereavement, forgotten attachments, and the emotional lives objects can hold. Its recurring image of the sea as a ledger makes recovery less like a tidy reward than an unsettling return of what was once left unresolved. The moral emphasis is on treating one’s former self gently and allowing grief to coexist with new uncertainty.

## Evidence line
> The shelf was a museum of other people's absences.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the interlocking objects, restrained grief, and hopeful but unresolved ending make this a distinctive, coherent expression of its chosen themes.
