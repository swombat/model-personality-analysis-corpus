The closing image invites the reader to recognize ordinary acts of care as meaningful even without thanks or applause.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds lighthouses, maintenance, danger and safety, and invisible labor—from checking bridge bolts to texting a friend. Its moral emphasis is that careful, uncelebrated acts can hold life together, and that one may keep doing them without knowing who benefits.
