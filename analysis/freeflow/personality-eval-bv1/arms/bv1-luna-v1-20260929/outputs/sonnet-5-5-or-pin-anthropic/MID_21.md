It resists easy nostalgia: the keeper’s dangerous, lonely labor should not return, but the attentiveness behind it deserves to survive. The essay invites the reader to see maintenance—in relationships, institutions, and ordinary shared systems—as care made visible through repeated action, even when nobody notices.
