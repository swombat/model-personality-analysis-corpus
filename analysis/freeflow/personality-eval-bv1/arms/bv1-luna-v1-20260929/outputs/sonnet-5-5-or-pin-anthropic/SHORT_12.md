EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A reflective, gently lyrical essay that turns ordinary infrastructure into an invitation to practice gratitude.

## Grounded reading
The voice is calm and attentive, finding emotional weight in things that work without asking to be noticed. Its examples move from sidewalks and refrigerators to the people who maintain bridges, label medicine, and stock shelves; the pathos comes from recognizing care as a chain of quiet, dependable acts. The closing invitation is modest rather than grand: notice one invisible thing each day, and offer thanks if possible.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds everyday infrastructure, hidden labor, and the indifference that can accompany reliability. It presents gratitude as a way of seeing the civic and practical work that keeps daily life intact.
