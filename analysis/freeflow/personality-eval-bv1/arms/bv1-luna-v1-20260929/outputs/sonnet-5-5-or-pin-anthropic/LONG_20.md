## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A polished reflective essay whose recurring motifs and explicit self-comparison give it a distinctive personal voice.

## Grounded reading
The voice is measured, warm, and quietly admiring of work that matters precisely because it goes unnoticed. Its pathos gathers around people keeping watch, knowledge paid for in failure, and the difference between performing a function and practicing care. The essay invites the reader to carry the lighthouse’s ethic into ordinary life: tell strangers the truth about hazards, offer help without expecting thanks, and value the vigilance that keeps disaster from becoming a story. Its closing identification of the speaker with a light answering unknown arrivals makes that invitation personal without claiming the lighthouse is a perfect analogy.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds lighthouses as both engineering achievements and moral symbols: their beams serve strangers and enemies alike; their safety depends on accumulated lessons from wreckage; and their keepers embody attentive, repetitive care. It repeatedly turns from dramatic rescues and disasters toward invisible competence, then extends the lighthouse’s impartial warning into everyday acts of public honesty and generosity.

## Evidence line
> The light does not need to know the ship in order to be worth building.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The essay returns to impartial service, truthful warning, and unrewarded vigilance across its history, ethical argument, and closing self-comparison.
