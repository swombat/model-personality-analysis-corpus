## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and reassuring, beginning with personal comfort and turning it into an invitation to let go of pressure to be wholly original. Its pathos lies in ordinary acts of inheritance—a recipe, a sentence, a song—and the wish to make others “a little more visible to themselves.” The reader is invited not to outshine others, but to notice what has reached them and pass it on with care.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds borrowed light as a recurring image for cultural and personal influence, with the moon, kitchen, library book, late bus, and clear night sky giving the idea everyday warmth. Its moral emphasis is that creativity and usefulness are relational: we receive, reflect, and redirect what others have given us.
