## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. The sample is a lyrical first-person meditation built around ordinary morning details and questions of meaning, grief, and patience.

## Grounded reading
The voice is reflective and quietly melancholy, but not despairing: cold coffee, shifting light, and the crow and squirrel give abstract worries a calm, concrete setting. The speaker moves from wanting to make grief meaningful toward accepting stillness and an ordinary life without needing to justify it. The reader is invited to sit with that modest resolution rather than solve the questions outright.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds time passing, uncertainty about how to live, and the wish to turn sorrow into meaning. Its recurring objects—the cold coffee, changing light, and patient crow—make attention to small present-tense details the counterweight to those concerns. The moral emphasis is that waiting and an unremarkable life need not be failures.

## Evidence line
> This is my life, small and unremarkable and entirely mine.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the coherent imagery and repeated return to patience and ordinary life make the sample distinctive, with its reflective mood sustained throughout.
