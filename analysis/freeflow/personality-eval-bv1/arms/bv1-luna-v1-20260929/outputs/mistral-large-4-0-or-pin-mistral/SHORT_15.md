## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A reflective first-person vignette uses ordinary morning details to explore waiting, solitude, and a modest form of peace.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and composed, finding emotional weight in rain, coffee, toast, and a clanking radiator rather than in dramatic events. Its pathos comes from divorce, meals alone, and the sense of postponed hopes, but the ending offers a quiet acceptance instead of triumph or despair. The reader is invited to see small routines and irritations as part of a life already being lived.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds waiting for an ideal future, anonymity as “a small mercy,” and everyday frustrations displaced onto objects like a parking meter and a Wi-Fi router named “Betrayal.” Its moral emphasis is that peace may mean accepting the present rather than waiting for life to begin.

## Evidence line
> Maybe the waiting was the life, and I've been living it all along, one small morning at a time.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the recurring everyday objects and the shift from restless waiting to acceptance make this a coherent, distinctive sample, though its reflective literary style is familiar.
