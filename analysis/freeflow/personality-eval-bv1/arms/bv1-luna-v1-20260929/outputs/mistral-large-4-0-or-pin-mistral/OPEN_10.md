## Grounded reading

Its pathos comes from a mild ache about missed moments and the wish to be fully present rather than remembered. The grandmother’s idea that houses remember, and the speaker’s small greetings to a tree, invite the reader to treat attention—not grand events or answers—as a form of connection.

## What the model chose to foreground

It foregrounds presence over remembrance and gives quiet significance to noticing what is still there, even as the closing uncertainty about whether the dust is old or new softens any tidy resolution.
