## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A first-person reflective essay turns private regret into a small, tentative commitment to reconnect.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and self-questioning, using unsent drafts, a saved voicemail, and a grandmother’s letters to give silence a physical shape. Its pathos comes from missed chances and the speaker’s fear that delay has made repair impossible; its ending offers no guaranteed reconciliation, only the dignity of trying. The reader is invited to recognize the distance between rehearsing words and saying them, without being told that every silence must be broken.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds unsent messages, family memory, grief, miscommunication, and the strain of feeling alone amid constant connectivity. It repeatedly makes silence architectural—a graveyard, rooms, a language—and gives the essay a modest moral movement from avoidance toward action. That choice makes emotional hesitation, rather than technology itself, the central concern.

## Evidence line

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
