# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: OPEN

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. The piece uses a remembered coffee shop to explore change, attachment, and the quiet grief of places and versions of life that no longer fit.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and observant, building feeling from precise ordinary details: the third table from the back, the golden light, familiar regulars, and the baristas who knew the narrator’s order. Its pathos is restrained; the shop is still thriving, so the loss is not a clean ending but a mismatch between a place and the memories attached to it. The narrator does not settle the question of whether to return or move on, inviting the reader to recognize that unresolved choice in their own routines and remembered places.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds change that happens without a definitive rupture: renovation, people moving on, retirement or death, and the narrator’s own aging. Light, a lost seat, and the image of visiting a ghost carry the emotional weight. The moral emphasis is gentle rather than prescriptive: showing up again and finding somewhere new can both be answers.

## Evidence line
> It just became a different place, and I'm still trying to find my seat in it.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — the coherent, recurring imagery of light, seats, and places that have moved on makes this a distinctive expression of wistful attention to ordinary loss.
