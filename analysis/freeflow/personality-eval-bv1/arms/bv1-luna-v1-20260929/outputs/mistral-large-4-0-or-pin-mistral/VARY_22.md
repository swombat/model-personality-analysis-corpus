## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A reflective first-person meditation built around the recurring image of unsent messages.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate, self-questioning, and precise about the gap between feeling something and being able to say it to another person. Its pathos comes from grief and regret, especially the father’s death, but also from smaller failures of connection: a mother’s comment, a date that felt like nothing, an ended relationship. The writing invites the reader into the narrator’s private archive without insisting that keeping the drafts is healing; they preserve both tenderness and avoidance.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds drafts as an archive of love, grief, and unfinished communication. It returns to phones, text boxes, kitchens, and the folder of forty-seven messages, while circling the wish to be understood and the impossibility of finding words that can undo loss or arrive at the right time. Its closing emphasis is not release but keeping: the unsent words still testify that the narrator paid attention and cared.

## Evidence line
> The drafts are more honest than I am in person.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the recurring draft-and-archive imagery gives the piece a distinctive, coherent emotional center, while its reflective literary voice remains closely tied to this particular scenario.
