## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. It is a sustained, intimate meditation on grief and memory, shaped by recurring sensory details rather than a public-facing thesis.

## Grounded reading
The voice is reflective and elegiac, but its grief is made concrete through ordinary particulars: lavender, a scar, soup, a fitted sheet, the posture of hands on hips. The essay moves from the ache of remembering incompletely toward a gentle resolve to pay closer attention to the living. Its invitation to the reader is not to overcome loss but to notice and gather the fragments of people while they are still here.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds imperfect memory, the partial versions of people we know, and the ways the dead persist in habits, gestures, and phrases. It repeatedly returns to kitchens, light, dreams, photographs, and family stories, with the moral emphasis that attention and listening matter before opportunities are gone. The ending turns remembrance into a modest practice: call the mother, ask about her day, and listen.

## Evidence line
> We are all, I think, walking archives of the people we've loved and lost.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the recurring fragments, domestic objects, and movement from loss toward attentiveness give the sample a coherent and somewhat distinctive expressive shape.
