## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A meditative first-person piece uses domestic observation and remembered loss to give emotional shape to ordinary time.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and attentive, lingering over worn linoleum, cold coffee, afternoon light, and the sounds of an old house. Its grief for the dead cat is quiet rather than dramatic: loss appears in changed routines and in a familiar patch of light that no longer has the same purpose. The piece invites the reader to slow down and notice how memory, aging, and affection accumulate in small, ordinary objects.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model chose to foreground the passage of time as something lived through household routines; the afterlife of a beloved pet in a bowl, a resting place, and an empty patch of sunlight; and the value of noticing an unremarkable afternoon. Its recurring objects and closing image make attention itself a modest moral claim: an ordinary moment deserves to be witnessed.

## Evidence line
> The ones that were ordinary, which is to say, most of them, which is to say, the ones that actually constitute a life.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The sample is coherent and distinctive in its recurring domestic details and its sustained emphasis on grief, time, and attentive presence.
