## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. It is a lyrical first-person meditation that turns insomnia and nighttime solitude into a reflective account of attention, endurance, and meaning.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and composed, using sensory details—the refrigerator’s hum, pipes, birds, streetlamp light—to make loneliness feel inhabited rather than abstract. Its pathos lies in the gap between wanting rest and discovering a strange companionship in wakefulness: the narrator’s remembered insomnia becomes evidence that frightening thoughts can ease, and that the body eventually finds its way back to sleep. The reader is invited to regard small acts of noticing as a modest form of solace, not as a cure or grand revelation.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model chose nocturnal consciousness, insomnia, private rituals, and fleeting observations as its central material. It repeatedly pairs isolation with connection: people around the world are imagined awake in separate rooms, while the narrator finds kinship with them and with the ordinary sounds of a waking house. Its moral emphasis is gentle acceptance—stop fighting every thought, let sleep arrive in its own time, and value having been present.

## Evidence line
> We are all awake together in our separate rooms, connected by this shared condition of consciousness in the dark, and yet utterly alone.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the recurring sensory motifs and carefully sustained movement from loneliness toward quiet consolation make this a distinctive, coherent expression of reflective attention.
