## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A lyrical meditation on ordinary life, shaped by recurring personal-seeming images rather than a detached public argument.

## Grounded reading
The voice is tender, reflective, and deliberately attentive to physical detail: late-afternoon light, an unfinished coffee, a jar’s weight, the texture of quilts. Its pathos comes from ordinary objects becoming keepsakes for grief and proof of a life lived. The piece invites the reader to resist busyness and notice what they might otherwise pass over, presenting attention not as productivity but as a quiet way of inhabiting one’s days.

## What the model chose to foreground
It foregrounds accumulated fragments as evidence of existence: the “evidence jar,” a grandmother’s chair and quilts, a woman paused in a doorway, and light across a kitchen. These images support a moral claim that presence and attention give a life weight, while constant striving and optimization can make it pass without touching us. The mood is elegiac but reassuring, ending in a modest affirmation of enoughness.

## Evidence line
> The small things aren't small because they're insignificant.
