## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A quiet, scene-driven meditation uses the library’s closing routine to carry a reflective mood.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and gently lyrical, attentive to small routines and people who might otherwise go unnoticed. Its loneliness is explicitly “more like... permission”: the day is over, the public space releases its visitors, and nobody has to explain why they came. The closing image offers the reader a kind of shelter in that patience—not a cure for loneliness, but a space that holds people without judging them.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model selects a library at closing time, its regulars, its workers, and the traces of private difficulty left in a public place. The detail of a security guard checking that a bathroom is locked sits beside the person who used the library because their apartment had no heat, making hospitality and dignity quiet moral centers of the piece. The mood is tender, subdued, and accepting; its resolution is the building waiting for morning.

## Evidence line
> The library doesn't judge any of it.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sustained attention to overlooked people and the recurring idea of the library as a nonjudgmental refuge make this a coherent, distinctive expressive choice.
