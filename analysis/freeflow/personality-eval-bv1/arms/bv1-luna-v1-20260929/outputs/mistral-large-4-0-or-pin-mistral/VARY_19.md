# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld
Source model: `withheld`
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A first-person literary meditation turns a recurring digital object into a sustained account of grief, avoidance, and intimacy.

## Grounded reading
The voice is self-aware and rueful, with humor that keeps the confession from becoming solemn: the narrator writes a 2,000-word analysis of nodding in therapy, then recognizes that this is exactly the habit under discussion. The folder of drafts gives the piece its emotional architecture: writing offers control and shelter, but also preserves distance from the people the narrator loves. The father’s death and the missed chance with Clara sharpen that cost. The ending does not resolve the conflict; it leaves the narrator writing into silence, inviting the reader to feel both the comfort of private expression and its limits.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds unsent messages, the gap between feeling and speech, and the way avoidance can become a relationship of its own. It pairs small, concrete details—the 47 drafts, a Tuesday glass of wine, a three-word reply—with family estrangement, bereavement, and romantic hesitation. Its moral pressure is gentle but clear: withholding may protect the speaker from immediate exposure while gradually making connection harder.

## Evidence line
> The thing about unsent messages is that they become their own kind of relationship.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the piece has a coherent central metaphor and distinctive, recurring details, with its emotional emphasis sustained from the opening folder to the unresolved final line.
