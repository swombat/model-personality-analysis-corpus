## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — This is a first-person meditation on grief, shaped by recurring domestic images and an intimate, reflective voice.

## Grounded reading
The voice is candid and elegiac, presenting loss as an ongoing weight to carry rather than a problem time will solve. Its pathos comes through ordinary spaces made newly charged—the unused side of the bed, an empty chair, a familiar photograph—and through the speaker’s effort to keep moving without claiming recovery. The loss is left open to several possibilities, including death or separation. The piece invites the reader less to offer a fix than to witness the grief and recognize small acts of presence and kindness.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample dwells on absence, memory, and the routines of living alone: rooms, thresholds, a photograph, a notebook, a clicking radiator, and a cat’s steady company. It returns to the idea that grief changes shape but does not simply disappear, and ends by treating continued living as a modest, hard-won form of persistence.

## Evidence line
> The grief doesn't diminish; you simply develop muscles for it.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: grief recurs throughout the piece through a coherent set of domestic images and a consistent emphasis on endurance, making those choices especially prominent in this sample.
