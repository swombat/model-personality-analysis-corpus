## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical personal essay that turns unsent messages into a meditation on memory, intimacy, and the changing self.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and reflective, making room for ambivalence rather than resolving it: writing can be both a retreat from the risks of contact and a way to feel less alone. The recurring images of birds, stones, drafts, and layers of old selves give the piece a quiet ache. It invites the reader to recognize private, unfinished expression as meaningful even when it never reaches its intended recipient.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds unsent messages and letters, changing versions of the self, longing for lost or imagined connection, and writing as a form of attention. Its moral emphasis is gentle: expression need not be published or received to count, and making something can lighten the weight of what cannot be changed.

## Evidence line
> The point is the rehearsal of intimacy without the risk of it.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the essay has a coherent, distinctive motif system, with the drafts folder and the image of trapped birds returning to shape its emotional resolution.
