# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld
Source model: `withheld`
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical first-person meditation that uses recurring scenes and objects to connect emotional withholding with loneliness, grief, and tentative persistence.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and self-questioning, offering the reader not a confession that resolves, but a view into the effort of living alongside what remains unsaid. Its pathos comes from the gap between care and its expression: the narrator loves their mother but cannot finish the letter, seeks company in the coffee shop without wanting contact, and feels relief as well as grief after their father’s death. The unsent letters, the photograph, and the dog barking at night make private isolation tangible. The ending offers hope cautiously, as an act of trying rather than a recovered certainty.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample centers on withheld speech and the loneliness it both protects and deepens. It repeatedly returns to family—especially the mother, the absent father, and the childhood self—as well as insomnia, ordinary routines, and observing strangers. Its moral emphasis is that love can be real despite failures of expression, while silence still has weight. The final mood is unresolved but gently persistent.

## Evidence line
> The question is which incompleteness I can live with.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the repeated letter motif, consistent emotional voice, and carefully echoed closing images make this a distinctive, coherent sample rather than a generic reflection.
