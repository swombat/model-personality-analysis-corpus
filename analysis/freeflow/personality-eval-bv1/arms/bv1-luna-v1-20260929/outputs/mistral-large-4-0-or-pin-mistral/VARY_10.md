## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. It is an intimate first-person reflection that turns a recurring image into a clear emotional arc.

## Grounded reading
The voice is confessional and self-questioning, moving from regret over an unsent message to a hard-won willingness to risk being known. The grandmother’s garden text gives the piece its sharpest grief: a small, ordinary line becomes something the narrator rereads as both prayer and self-accusation. By the end, the invitation to the reader is practical and relational: say the important thing, accept the uncertainty of the reply, and let connection happen in real time.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds drafts, doors, tea, and the difference between imagined conversations and actual contact. Its moral emphasis is that safety and potential can become forms of hiding, while imperfect speech and listening make connection possible. The movement from bereavement and self-reproach toward friendship, vulnerability, and renewed effort gives those themes a hopeful but not effortless resolution.
