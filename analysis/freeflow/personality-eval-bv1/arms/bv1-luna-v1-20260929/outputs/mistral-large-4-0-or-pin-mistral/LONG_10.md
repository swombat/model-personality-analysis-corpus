## What the model chose to foreground

Its recurring objects—the tree outside the window, coffee, the grandmother’s kitchen, walking, and the dawn—turn presence into a repeated practice. The moral emphasis is on accepting impermanence without withdrawing from love or wonder, and on resisting productivity as the measure of a life.
