EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A reflective personal essay that uses the phone’s “Drafts” folder and the grandmother’s letters to explore unfinished communication.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and gently self-questioning, turning a familiar habit—saving a message and not sending it—into a meditation on vulnerability, regret, and care. The unsent drafts carry pathos because they hold not just missed words but imagined versions of relationships that might have gone differently. The piece invites the reader to treat imperfect communication as an act of connection, while acknowledging that silence can leave people wondering what they never got to hear.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds digital drafts, handwritten letters, family memory, and the moral weight of deciding whether to speak. Its central claim is that reaching out imperfectly is often better than letting fear turn an “almost” into a never. The repeated cursor and letter imagery make connection feel like a practice that requires deliberate effort.
