Evaluator: withheld

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical first-person meditation turns domestic details and memory into reflections on grief, attention, and how to live.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and unhurried, finding meaning in ordinary sensory moments: honey-colored light, coffee, a dog settling, soup steaming. Its tenderness carries quiet grief, especially in the memory of the grandmother and the reflex to reach for the phone. The piece invites the reader to slow down and notice what the body holds, without insisting that grief be resolved.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds light, small household sounds, an oak tree, a grandmother’s photograph, and the making of soup. Its moral preference is for “less noise” and “more presence,” with nature’s patient continuation offered as an example; grief appears as something embodied and woven into everyday life.

## Evidence line
> The body remembers what the mind tries to protect.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sample coherently sustains a distinctive sensory, contemplative voice, and its repeated turn from ordinary objects toward grief and presence makes those choices unusually legible.
