# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical first-person meditation turns unsent messages into a sustained reflection on fear, responsibility, and connection.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and self-questioning, moving between a present-day drafts folder and the grandmother’s box of letters. Its pathos comes from the gap between love that is felt and love that never reaches its recipient. The piece makes room for both restraint and disclosure, but ultimately invites the reader toward the risk of sending: not because it guarantees forgiveness or understanding, but because it makes connection possible.

## What the model chose to foreground
It foregrounds drafts, letters, a blinking cursor, and the repeated image of weight as ways to consider communication and responsibility. The mood shifts from regret and imagined loss toward cautious resolve. Its moral emphasis is that imperfectly expressed care may be better than care kept safely private, even when the outcome is uncertain.

## Evidence line
> The weight of unsent messages is a particular kind of grief.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The recurring weight, letter, and sending motifs give the sample a distinctive, coherent emotional argument rather than a generic reflection on communication.
