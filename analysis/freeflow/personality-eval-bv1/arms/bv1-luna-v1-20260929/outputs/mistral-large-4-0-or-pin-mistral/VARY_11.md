## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A reflective first-person meditation that turns an ordinary drafts folder into a sustained image of intimacy, hesitation, and memory.

## Grounded reading
The voice is candid and self-questioning, moving between concrete family details and abstractions about what it means to communicate. Its pathos comes from the gap between wanting to be understood and fearing what words may do once they leave the writer’s control. The grandmother’s letters, grandfather’s notebook, and small texts from a faraway friend give the piece warmth and texture; the repeated uncertainty keeps it from resolving into easy advice. It invites the reader to recognize their own unsent words without insisting that silence is either cowardice or wisdom.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds unsent messages as a private archive: evidence of love, grief, self-protection, and unfinished versions of the self. It contrasts the permanence and exposure of sending with the freedom of keeping words private, while complicating that contrast through family records, therapy, and the fragile maintenance of friendship. The chosen mood is tender and melancholic, but not wholly bleak: writing and small gestures can preserve connection even when they cannot restore what was.

## Evidence line
> I wanted to say something true, but I also wanted to be understood, and those are different projects.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sustained central metaphor, recurring concrete objects, and nuanced treatment of honesty make this a distinctive, coherent expression of inwardness and relational uncertainty.
