## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. The piece is a lyrical personal meditation whose recurring images and emotional arc make it more distinctive than a generic essay.

## Grounded reading
The voice is contemplative and intimate, finding grief and memory in ordinary scenes: a geranium’s shadow on a kitchen floor, weeds in an empty lot, an evening walk by the river. Its pathos comes from wanting to attend fully to beautiful things while knowing they cannot be kept. The repeated tension between looking and capturing, presence and permanence, invites the reader to slow down and notice what is already passing.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model chooses late-afternoon light as its central object, linking it to the grandmother’s kitchen, a burned-down house, the father’s scientific explanations, and recurring figures by the river. It foregrounds grief, the unreliability of the present, regret over inattention, and a moral preference for witnessing experience rather than fixing or possessing it.
