## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A quiet, lyrical meditation turns a library’s closing routine into a reflection on solitude, memory, and transition.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and observant, building feeling from small sensory details: softened lights, squeaking cart wheels, old paper, and a lock turning. Its pathos comes from the contrast between loneliness as hunger and chosen solitude as “a kind of fasting.” The reader is invited to linger in the in-between moment, where a public place briefly feels both empty and shared.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds libraries as containers for anonymous human lives, though the building and its furnishings do not literally remember them. It gives weight to ordinary closing sounds and routines, and frames solitude, return, and unfinished stories as quietly meaningful rather than dramatic.

## Evidence line
