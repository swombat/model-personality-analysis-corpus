# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A reflective first-person piece that links ordinary objects and withheld conversations to impermanence, self-narration, and attention.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and self-questioning, but not confessional for its own sake: the speaker keeps testing what a detail might mean, then allows uncertainty to remain. The heron, apartment, plant, and drafts folder give grief and change a physical texture. Its pathos is gentle, with humor and small moments of warmth—especially the friend’s laugh—keeping the meditation from becoming solemn. The invitation to the reader is to notice what is passing while it is still here, without pretending that noticing can make it last.

## What the model chose to foreground
Under the freeflow condition, the sample foregrounds unsent words, remembered rooms, a dying plant, ordinary narration, and the bittersweetness of endings. It repeatedly treats attention as a way of staying present, while acknowledging that the speaker does not always know what to say or how to act. The moral emphasis is modest: people are still becoming, and paying attention can be enough.

## Evidence line
> The way a room holds the shape of what happened in it.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the recurring objects and linked reflections give the piece a distinctive, coherent preoccupation with memory and impermanence rather than a single polished thesis.
