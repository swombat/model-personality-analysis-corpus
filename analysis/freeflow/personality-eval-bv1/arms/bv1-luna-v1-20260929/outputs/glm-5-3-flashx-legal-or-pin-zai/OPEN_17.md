## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. This is a lyrical first-person meditation whose recurring images and emotional movement make it more distinctive than a generic thesis-driven essay.

## Grounded reading
The voice is tender and elegiac, attentive to small sensory details and the ache of losing what cannot be preserved. It moves from grief over forgotten days toward a gentler acceptance: memory is partial, photographs capture only surfaces, and other people may carry fragments of our lives. The invitation is to arrive for ordinary moments rather than trying to possess or document them.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds the passing of time through kitchen light, a half-remembered summer afternoon, photographs, and the river-and-soil imagery of forgetting. Its moral emphasis is that attention can be a form of love, while a day’s worth does not depend on being remembered. It also gives weight to the idea that our lives persist partly in other people’s memories.

## Evidence line
> The forgetting is not the opposite of the living.

Medium — the sustained sensory imagery and recurring movement from loss toward acceptance make this a strong, coherent expressive choice.
