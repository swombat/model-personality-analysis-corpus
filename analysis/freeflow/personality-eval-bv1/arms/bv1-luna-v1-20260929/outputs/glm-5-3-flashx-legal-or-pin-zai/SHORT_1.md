## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A compact domestic meditation uses an invented “maybe drawer” to turn clutter into a tender reflection on memory and uncertainty.

## Grounded reading
The voice is playful and observant, then quietly elegiac. Its metaphors—especially the flashlight that works “only if you shake it” and the screw-sized gap between function and ruin—make ordinary household disorder feel like a human condition without losing the humor of the objects themselves. The final image invites the reader not to sort or judge, but to pause with what has been kept and wonder about the lives attached to it.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds saved objects, deferred decisions, family traces, and the fragile persistence of memory. Its moral emphasis is gentle: usefulness and clear explanations are not the only reasons to keep something; uncertainty itself can preserve a path back to the past.

## Evidence line
> The drawer is a museum of postponed decisions.
