## Grounded reading

Its grief is deliberately quiet: the moon’s departure is not an emergency, but a loss so gradual that people must choose to notice it. The grandmother’s phrase gives that cosmic scale a personal foothold, while the eclipse crowds become an invitation to the reader: attend to what is passing, and share the act of looking.

## What the model chose to foreground

It repeatedly frames distance as a form of farewell, then counters that distance with attention and communal witnessing. The moral emphasis is that slow, non-urgent departures deserve notice.
