## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A reflective literary meditation built around a sustained metaphor for private, unsent communication.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and gently elegiac, moving from familiar scenes—washing dishes, hovering over a deleted text—to the grief and tenderness attached to letters that cannot reach the dead. Its central insight is double-edged: imagined conversations can offer perfect understanding, but that very perfection marks their distance from real exchange. The essay invites readers to recognize their own unsent words without condemning restraint, then to risk passing along one true, kind sentence while it can still be heard.

## What the model chose to foreground
It foregrounds the “silent mail” of rehearsed arguments, withheld love, belated explanations, and words addressed to the dead. The grandmother’s drawer of letters, the blank text field, the swallowed sentence, and the gravestone make private speech tangible. The mood holds mercy and tragedy together, while the moral claim is measured: not every unsent word should be spoken, but some should be forwarded.

## Evidence line
> Everyone is a postmaster of this silent mail.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sample sustains a distinctive metaphor across intimate, concrete scenes and returns to a clear but restrained invitation to speak.
