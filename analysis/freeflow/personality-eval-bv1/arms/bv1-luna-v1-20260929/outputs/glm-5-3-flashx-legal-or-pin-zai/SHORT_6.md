## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical personal reflection that builds its meaning through concrete objects and a wistful imagined connection.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and gently intimate, turning ordinary signs of use—a water ring, a crossed-out grocery item, a note in a book—into evidence of lives that have brushed against one another. Its pathos is quiet: the “call her, you coward” note opens a small unresolved human story, while the ending offers companionship across time rather than closure. The reader is invited to notice books not only as containers of words but as objects bearing other people’s habits and histories.

## What the model chose to foreground
The piece foregrounds used books, marginal notes, worn pages, and the traces strangers leave behind. Its mood is tender and lightly elegiac, with a moral emphasis on shared attention: readers who never meet can still sit with the same sentences and leave something for one another.
