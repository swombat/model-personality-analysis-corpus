## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A sustained first-person farewell turns a fire lookout’s retirement into a meditation on attention, care, and what machines cannot see.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and elegiac, but its grief stays grounded in work: reading smoke, tracking a bear’s habits, and noticing when familiar lights disappear. The narrator resists the idea that solitude is simply loneliness; attention gives her a relationship to the landscape, and the loss of that role is also the loss of a particular way of loving it. The piece invites the reader to value not just detection but patient, invested looking.

## What the model chose to foreground
It foregrounds the intimate knowledge built through repetition: fog in a draw, cattle-truck dust, a bear’s lateness, and the Hendersons’ lights. Against the camera’s scale and efficiency, it sets human care, grief, and responsibility—especially the lingering guilt over a missed fire. Its ending leaves the future unresolved, with hope that the next watcher will want to be there.
