EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A reflective personal essay turns insomnia into an imaginative tour of the hidden lives, sounds, and forms of care that gather at night.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate, observant, and gently lyrical, using insomnia as both a personal difficulty and a vantage point on lives usually missed in daylight. Its humor—the cat as critic, raccoons as a heist crew—keeps the piece from becoming solemn, while the ambulance driver’s account gives its nocturnal tenderness real human stakes. The essay invites the reader to meet wakefulness with less panic and more curiosity: to notice the night’s workers, mourners, animals, and private thoughts without insisting that any of them must yield a grand revelation.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds sleeplessness, the night shift of ordinary life, and the quiet labor of witnessing: the baker, cleaner, cardboard collectors, ambulance driver, and people alone in their apartments. It repeatedly renders the night as a populated place rather than an empty interval, using buildings, city sounds, the sky, owls, and a precise 3:17 awakening to make that idea tangible. Its moral emphasis is on attention and compassion, especially toward people whose difficult hours go unseen.
