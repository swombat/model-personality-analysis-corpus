## Grounded reading
The voice is elegiac but not resigned: it treats darkness as a living inheritance, lost through ordinary choices rather than a single dramatic act. The essay moves from the vanished Milky Way to animals harmed by artificial light, then to the human need for awe and proportion. Its invitation is both practical and reflective: leave the streetlights behind, wait, and look up.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds night as habitat, shared human heritage, and a source of humility. Stars, turtles, migrating birds, insects, and the deep sea make darkness feel populous rather than empty; the recurring image of a country being conquered or deleted frames light pollution as a moral and ecological loss. The closing appeal emphasizes that restoration is possible and that attention itself matters.
