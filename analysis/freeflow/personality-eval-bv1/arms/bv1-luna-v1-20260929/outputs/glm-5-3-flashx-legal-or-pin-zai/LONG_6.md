# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: LONG

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical, first-person essay that turns an ordinary walk into an argument for purposeless attention and a more receptive way of inhabiting the world.

## Grounded reading
The voice is reflective and inviting, moving between cultural criticism and intimate observation without losing its sense of play. Its pathos lies in the fear that efficiency and constant distraction have narrowed our attention—and in the hope that small, unproductive encounters can widen it again. The concrete details—a blue garden, a chalk jellyfish, an owl shared silently with a stranger—make the argument feel observed rather than merely asserted. The essay invites the reader not just to take a walk, but to become temporarily available to what they might notice.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds purposelessness as a quiet resistance to optimization, walking as a setting for thought, and attention as a route to knowing places and other people. It repeatedly returns to the phone as an interruption, the child’s stick as a symbol of absorbed attention, and small urban encounters as sources of imagination and mercy. Its moral claim is gentle but clear: not every hour needs to justify itself by producing an outcome.

## Evidence line
> The aimless walk is, among other things, an attempt to get the stick back.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The essay sustains a distinctive first-person lyric voice and repeatedly returns to the stick, owl, phone, and unscheduled attention, making its chosen moral emphasis unusually legible.
