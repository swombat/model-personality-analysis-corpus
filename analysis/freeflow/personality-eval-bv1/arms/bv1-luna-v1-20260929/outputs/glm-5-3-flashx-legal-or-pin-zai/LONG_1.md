## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical first-person essay turns nocturnal waiting into an inquiry about grief, honesty, and the quiet forms of care.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and image-rich, moving from the fatigue of hospital light and vending-machine food to a hard-won tenderness toward people awake at difficult hours. Its pathos comes from specific scenes of waiting for a father, listening to a stranger’s story, and failing at first to comfort an insomniac friend. Rather than making the night simply beautiful, the essay keeps its pain in view and finds meaning in staying present through it. It invites the reader to see waiting not as empty time but as a place where people can face truths, share stories, and keep one another company.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample repeatedly foregrounds hospital corridors, sleeplessness, night workers, family illness, and small acts of vigil: holding a coat, making tea, listening, holding a hand. Its moral emphasis is on patience and attention over easy consolation or grand wisdom. The chosen mood is elegiac but not despairing; the closing image of keeping the light on makes care feel modest, practical, and sustaining.

## Evidence line
> The waiting was not wasted time.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The recurring images of waiting and night, paired with specific scenes and a consistent ethic of attentive care, make this a distinctive and coherent expressive choice.
