## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A lyrical first-person meditation turns sleepless hours into a space for reflection and permission not to have everything settled.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate, contemplative, and gently reassuring. It finds emotional weight in ordinary nighttime details—the ticking radiator, a shadowed chair, waves—and uses them to make uncertainty feel less like a problem to solve than a place one can inhabit. Its pathos comes from the contrast between daytime roles and the temporary relief of being “nobody” in particular. The reader is invited to slow down, sit with unresolved questions, and carry a little of that looseness back into ordinary life.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample centers on liminal time: night turning to morning, endings becoming beginnings, and a self moving between who it was and who it is becoming. It repeatedly sets the “wilderness” of unstructured time against the managed routines of daylight. Its moral emphasis is modest but clear: not knowing what one is or where one is going can be tolerable, even generous; being alive does not mean being in charge.
