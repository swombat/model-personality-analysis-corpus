## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION. This is a literary first-person story built around an invented postal job and a series of undeliverable letters.

## Grounded reading
The narrator’s restrained, matter-of-fact voice makes the story’s tenderness feel earned: rules, dates, and procedures sit beside private acts of care. Its pathos gathers around messages that cannot reach their intended recipients, especially Wren’s letter to the North Star. The narrator invites the reader to see attention itself as a kind of delivery—to keep looking, reading, and honoring what others hoped would arrive.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds failed communication, persistence, and the ethics of handling other people’s private words. Its recurring objects—letters, a recipe, a pocket, a drawer, the river, and the North Star—give shape to the tension between letting go and preserving hope. The moral claim is quiet but clear: some messages matter even when they cannot receive an answer.

## Evidence line
> We are the terminal of wanting.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story’s distinctive motifs recur and converge on a consistent emphasis on care for unsent or undelivered messages.
