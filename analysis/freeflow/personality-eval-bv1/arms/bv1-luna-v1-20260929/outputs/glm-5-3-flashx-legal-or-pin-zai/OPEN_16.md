## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A polished essay, but its recurring dark/light imagery and intimate turn toward the act of writing give it a distinctive, expressive voice.

## Grounded reading
The voice is assured and lyrical, moving from everyday scenes to broad claims about how people think and speak. Its central pathos is that being unseen can make honesty possible: people want connection, but another person’s gaze can feel like a demand to perform or explain. The closing first-person turn makes the essay itself an example of that idea, inviting the reader to consider what they can say more freely when no face is watching.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds cars, fire, letters, phone calls, and the page as settings where people can speak without facing one another. It contrasts darkness and motion, associated with producing difficult truths, against light and eye contact, associated with checking and verification. Its moral claim is not that looking is bad, but that intimacy sometimes needs the shelter of looking away.

## Evidence line
> Writing is the perfected form.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The road, fire, page, and dark/light contrast recur throughout, and the explicit first-person turn makes the chosen preoccupation especially coherent and distinctive.
