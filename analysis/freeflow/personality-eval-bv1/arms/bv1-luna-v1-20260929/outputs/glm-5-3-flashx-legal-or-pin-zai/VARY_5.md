## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A polished short story uses piano tuning as the frame for a quiet encounter with grief and remembrance.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and technically grounded, letting the tuner’s knowledge of strings, pins, and pitch carry emotional weight without turning the scene sentimental. Its pathos comes through small acts of restraint: the narrator looks away while Mrs. Okonkwo plays, and answers her reflection about prayer with practical talk about the next appointment. The story invites the reader to see care as patient attention to what remains, even after neglect or loss.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds silence, gradual change, and the possibility of recovering an instrument’s “center.” The star on middle C, the husband’s monthly tuning, and the widow’s return to the keyboard bind technical repair to grief and remembered intimacy. Its moral emphasis is modest: listening carefully and showing up can matter, though the closing thought that most people receive no such help gives the ending a darker edge.

## Evidence line
