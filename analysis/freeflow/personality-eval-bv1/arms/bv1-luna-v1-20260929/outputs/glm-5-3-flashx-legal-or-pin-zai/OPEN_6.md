## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A reflective, lyrical essay turns a scientific and communicative metaphor into a meditation on meaning and care.

## Grounded reading
The voice is thoughtful and gently intimate, moving from the oddness of perception toward the difficulty—and value—of understanding another person. Its pathos is restrained: the speaker acknowledges distance and not being able to meet the reader, then finds a kind of peace in the imperfect crossing of words. The invitation is to treat interpretation not as a failed transfer of thought but as work that can resemble caring.

## What the model chose to foreground
The essay foregrounds gaps: the eye’s blind spot, the lag in experiencing the present, and the difference between a thought and the words another person reconstructs from it. It chooses to make those gaps sources of possibility rather than only loneliness, linking them to writing, form, and the love involved in anticipating who a reader may become.

## Evidence line
> The gap is the space between one mind and another.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the essay sustains a distinctive metaphor across perception, language, and relationships, with a coherent turn from isolation toward care.
