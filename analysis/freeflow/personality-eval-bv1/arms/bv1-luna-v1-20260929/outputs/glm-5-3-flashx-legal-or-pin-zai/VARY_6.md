# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld
Source model: `withheld`
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A literary first-person monologue uses the transit lost-and-found as a setting for sustained reflection on grief, memory, and what people leave behind.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant, restrained, and quietly tender. Its sadness gathers through concrete details—the funeral program, the suitcase of letters, the star-shaped hair clip—rather than through grand declarations. The narrator is both caretaker and witness, attentive to the private meanings attached to ordinary objects, and candid about the small trespass of reading the letters and keeping the clip. The piece invites the reader to look more carefully at what gets dismissed as lost or replaceable, while its repeated language of waiting gives that care a cost: most people do not return, and the narrator stays with what remains.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds lost belongings as vessels for grief and attachment, especially objects connected to the dead: a wedding dress, a dead man’s suitcase, a mother’s coat, and a brother’s hoodie. It gives dignity to small, easily discarded things and to the person who keeps watch over them. The recurring images of doors, glass, warehouses, and waiting make preservation feel provisional rather than triumphant.

## Evidence line
> The waiting is the whole of it.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The sample is coherent and stylistically distinctive, with recurring objects and a consistent moral attention to grief, memory, and quiet acts of care.
