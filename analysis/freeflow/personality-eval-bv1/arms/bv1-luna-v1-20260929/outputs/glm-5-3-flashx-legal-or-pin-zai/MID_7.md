# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld
Source model: `withheld`
Condition: MID

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A polished, gently sentimental short story built around a lost-property office and an unexpectedly recovered book.

## Grounded reading
The voice is attentive and tender, finding emotional weight in ordinary objects without making Vera’s care for them feel grandiose. The lost book becomes a meeting point between public rules and private memory; the husband’s distinction between letters written to him and a note written in a book gives the grief its particular shape. The closing image of Vera making room on her shelf invites the reader to see keeping and remembering as small acts of care.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds objects as carriers of personal history: umbrellas, a postcard, a wedding ring, a book, and a ticket stub. It places bureaucratic time—the ninety-day rule—against the longer time of love and remembrance, and resolves that tension through Vera’s discreet kindness and the ledger’s final word, “Found.”

## Evidence line
> Vera believed every object was a sentence someone had stopped speaking.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The story is coherent and distinctive in its recurring attention to ordinary objects as vessels for memory, culminating in a quiet act of recognition rather than a dramatic resolution.
