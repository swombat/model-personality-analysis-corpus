# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A polished short story built around a dead father’s unsent letters and his daughter’s reply.

## Grounded reading
The voice is tender and measured, letting grief emerge through practical details rather than grand declarations: a dead flashlight, incorrect postage, a washed car, a drawn diagram of a pan. The daughter’s discoveries reframe her father’s quietness as a form of keeping, without pretending that his silence caused no hurt. The story invites the reader to recognize how love can be present in forms that are hard to read until later.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds letters, handwriting, stamps, household tasks, and remembered phrases as containers for affection across time. It favors a moral distinction—“I wasn’t hiding. I was keeping”—and resolves the gap between father and daughter not by mailing a reply, but by placing it in the box with his.

## Evidence line
> Some people love out loud, and some people love in a copy-paper box at the bottom of a desk, and you don't get to choose which one you get, and you don't get to choose which one you are.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story’s recurring objects and carefully echoed phrases make its emotional design unusually coherent and distinctive.
