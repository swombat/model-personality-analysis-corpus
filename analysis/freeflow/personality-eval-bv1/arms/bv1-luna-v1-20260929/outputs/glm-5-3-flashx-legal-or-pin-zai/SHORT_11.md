## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A reflective miniature that turns a personal encounter with marginalia into a warm meditation on reading as a conversation across time.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and gently lyrical, moving from the unknown reader’s penciled note to the speaker’s changed view of writing in books. Its pathos comes from the smallness of the trace left behind: a phrase beside a passage about guilt, or an arrow beside a line that mattered. The reader is invited to see marks in books not as damage but as evidence of other lives briefly meeting on the page.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds used books, handwriting, marginal notes, and the persistence of feeling between strangers separated by time. It favors warmth and connection over preservation and pristine order, ending with the hope that a future reader might inherit the meaning of a mark without knowing its maker.

## Evidence line
> They'll know only this: that a sentence was important once, to someone, and might be again.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The piece has a coherent, distinctive image system, with marginalia and traces of readers recurring to carry its emotional and moral claim.
