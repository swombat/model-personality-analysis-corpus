## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION. A polished, emotionally resolved short story built around a piano tuner’s hidden notes and a long-delayed return to playing.

## Grounded reading
The voice is tender and quietly theatrical, making practical details—the wobbly middle C, the pencil on iron, the folded chairs—carry emotional weight. Its pathos comes from Emil’s retreat after public failure and from the private kindness he offers others without taking credit. The story invites the reader to see craft as a form of care, and to regard vulnerability not as something overcome but as something that can be carried into action. The ending grants him a small, earned opening rather than a triumphant cure.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds hidden acts of encouragement, the dignity of skilled work, and the way an old fear can shape a life without fully closing it. Music appears both as a physical instrument that needs patient attention and as a metaphor for courage: the piano’s “shaking” and imperfections are not obstacles to expression but part of it. The chosen resolution is relational—Emil is recognized by someone he helped, then invited to play—rather than a solitary victory.

## Evidence line
> The shaking is a kind of music too.
