## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. It is a polished reflective essay, but its recurring images and intimate, questioning voice make it more distinctive than a generic thesis-driven essay.

## Grounded reading
The voice is attentive and gently self-correcting: it starts from a chipped mug, builds toward a claim about wonder, then resists turning that claim into a lesson. The pathos is quiet rather than mournful—the sense that efficiency and familiarity cost us something, alongside the pleasure of noticing what remains available. The reader is invited not to perform mindfulness but to become curious about one specific thing, without making attention another duty.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds ordinary objects and places—the mug, a city block, morning light, leaves, a bird—as densely particular rather than interchangeable. It centers attention and curiosity as ways of encountering the world, and treats growing up and efficiency as trades that can narrow perception. Its moral emphasis is on attention as a freely given gift, not an obligation.

## Evidence line

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the essay coherently returns to attention, ordinary beauty, and resistance to turning wonder into a duty, with distinctive images carrying the argument throughout.
