Its pathos comes from the mismatch between the octopus’s evident intelligence and its brief, solitary life: each mind solves problems brilliantly, but has no teacher, and leaves no lasting knowledge behind. It makes loneliness and inheritance its moral center, presenting short life and absent culture as a haunting loss rather than merely an evolutionary curiosity.
