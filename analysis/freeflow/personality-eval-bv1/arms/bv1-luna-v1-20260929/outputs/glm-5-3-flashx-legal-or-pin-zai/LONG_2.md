## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A lyrical, personally framed essay that turns a defense of aimless walking into a meditation on attention, uncertainty, and being alive.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and assured, mixing sensory particulars—the bakery’s “yeasty exhale,” sprinklers on dark grass, winterized boats—with literary and historical references. Its pathos comes from easing private worry rather than conquering it: a walk does not solve the narrator’s uncertainty, but makes it feel shared and less accusatory. The reader is invited to leave room for unproductive movement and silence, and to notice how the world feels when it is not reduced to a route, a task, or a stream of content.

## What the model chose to foreground
The essay foregrounds purposeless walking as a practice of attention and a setting for thought to loosen. It contrasts that practice with phone interruptions, navigation systems, step-counting, and other forms of measurement or efficiency, while returning to small encounters with streets, strangers, weather, and animals. Its central moral claim is modest but sustained: wandering can turn private unease into a sense of shared human life.

Medium: the sustained lyrical voice and recurring images of wandering, attention, and consolation make this a distinctive, coherent expression of the themes it selects.
