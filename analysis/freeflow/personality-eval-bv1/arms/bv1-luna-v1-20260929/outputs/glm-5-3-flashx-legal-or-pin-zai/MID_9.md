# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld
Source model: `withheld`
Condition: MID

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A lyrical speculative story built around a magical archive and a visitor’s recovery of a small, intimate memory.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and elegiac, finding emotional weight in sounds that might otherwise go unnoticed: a chair creaking, a cane tapping, a door closing. Its central pathos is that grief can attach to details a person never knew to treasure until they were gone. The story invites the reader to listen more closely to ordinary life, while its resolution offers not restoration but a limited return: the man can carry the sound back only through his own memory.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds recognition over mere identification, and treats a sound’s meaning as inseparable from the person who heard it. Longing, ordinary domestic noises, and the fragility of memory recur throughout. The archive’s purpose is not to preserve loss forever but to let remembered sounds return to life; the closing image extends that concern to Marguerite herself, who fears she may be waiting to be remembered.

## Evidence line
> The point of an archive of vanished things is to empty it.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: The piece is coherent and distinctive, with its attention to ordinary sensory details and its repeated movement from loss toward release giving its elegiac preoccupations unusual clarity.
