## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical miniature that builds a quiet mood around late-night public rooms and the strangers who share them.

## Grounded reading
The voice is attentive and tender without becoming sentimental: it notices the hum of fluorescent lights, a torn paperback, and a jacket marking a chair, then treats these small details as signs of an unspoken social care. Its pathos lies in temporary belonging—the freedom to be present without explaining why. The reader is invited to linger in that shared, suspended hour before ordinary life resumes.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model chooses laundromats, waiting rooms, diners, and other nocturnal in-between spaces; strangers’ small rituals; and the tacit etiquette that lets people coexist without demanding one another’s stories. The mood is hushed and gently wistful, with the moral emphasis on courtesy and permission to linger.

## Evidence line
> I love rooms like this: the gas station at 2 a.m., the hospital waiting room, the diner where the same four people have been sitting for hours.

Medium — the piece’s coherent imagery and sustained attention to anonymous companionship make its particular tenderness and observational style clear.
