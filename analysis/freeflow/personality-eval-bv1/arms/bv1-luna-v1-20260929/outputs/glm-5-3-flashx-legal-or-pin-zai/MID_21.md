## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A polished literary short story whose quiet, carefully paced narrative follows a piano tuner through a final job.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and attentive, finding emotional weight in the practical details of tuning: a pin turned by millimeters, a wavering note, a scratch left by a child. Walter’s impending retirement and the daughter’s absent father echo one another without being spelled out. The story’s pathos rests on how music lets the living encounter the dead, and how an ordinary act of care can make that encounter possible. It invites the reader to listen for what objects preserve, and for the tenderness in maintaining them.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds aging, skilled work, listening, and the persistence of memory in objects. Its central objects—the scratched Baldwin, the tuning lever, the fallboard—carry the emotional meaning, while the piano’s silence and its return to tune shape the story’s movement from loss toward a small, provisional consolation. The closing image presents waiting and faithful response as qualities shared by instruments and human relationships.

## Evidence line
> They hold their hundreds of pounds of tension in the dark for years, and then, if someone sits down and lifts the fallboard, they give back exactly what was asked of them, note for note, as if no time had passed at all.

Medium: the story is coherent and distinctive in its attention to sound, patient craft, and objects as vessels of memory.
