## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A lyrical first-person essay turns nocturnal city scenes into a sustained meditation on attention, solitude, and feeling real without an audience.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and carefully composed, moving between sensory observation and reflective claims. Its pathos comes from the tension between feeling invisible and discovering that solitude can be a form of freedom: the young narrator’s lonely walk becomes an encounter with a self worth inhabiting. Recurring details—the fox, the stranger’s cake, the buoy bell, the lit windows—keep the essay’s abstractions tethered to particular acts of looking. The invitation is gentle rather than prescriptive: step outside the usual performance of daily life and give attention room to wander.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds the empty city at night as a place where everyday performances fall away. It dwells on strangers’ unknowable inner lives, the consolations of being among people without knowing them, and attention as a way of meeting the world. Its moral emphasis is that an unobserved experience need not be wasted or lonely; it can be valuable precisely because it is not turned into performance or content.
