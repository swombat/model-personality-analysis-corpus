## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical, persuasive essay that combines environmental argument with personal reflection and a sustained sense of loss.

## Grounded reading
The voice is elegiac but not resigned: it mourns the disappearance of the night sky while resisting a simple call to reject modern lighting. Vivid images—the city mistaking its own galaxy for an emergency, hatchlings drawn inland, moths orbiting a bulb—turn the argument into felt loss. The reader is invited to see darkness not as emptiness or backwardness, but as a shared inheritance that can still be recovered through deliberate choices.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample centers light pollution as ecological harm, cultural amnesia, and the loss of a common source of wonder. It foregrounds the Milky Way, animal navigation, bodily rhythms, and the contrast between thoughtless glare and purposeful light. Its moral emphasis is measured: artificial light has brought real benefits, but those benefits do not justify illuminating without care. The ending chooses conditional hope, resting on the claim that darkness can return when lights are switched off.

## Evidence line
