EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical first-person meditation turns sleepless city walks into a practice of attention and quiet fellowship.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant, intimate, and gently elegiac, finding meaning in details rather than dramatic events: a heron, a street cleaner’s “slow, looping arithmetic,” a dog learning to sit. Insomnia is acknowledged as unwanted, but the walks give it a counterweight: a way to notice the city’s hidden labor and imagine the lives behind its windows. The invitation is not to romanticize sleeplessness so much as to witness what ordinary daylight overlooks.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds the midnight city, insomnia, working people, strangers glimpsed through windows, and the small acts that keep life going. Its moral emphasis is on attention and mutual presence: even people who never speak can make the night feel inhabited. The recurring transformation of solitude into a quiet society gives the piece its warmth.

## Evidence line
> There is a society of the sleepless, with no charter and no meetings, and its only requirement is that you show up, that you walk, that you witness.
