## Grounded reading

Its pathos comes through restraint: Marta does not fill Mr. Halloran’s silence, and his grief turns on the recognition that the woman was looking at him. The story invites the reader to look closely while accepting that an image can suggest a life without containing it.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds restoration as an ethical practice: making damage visible without “improving the past.” Repeated images of light, silver, and faces connect Marta’s craft to memory and inference, while the emotional center is the intimate space just outside the frame—the person the woman is looking at. The ending favors care and attention over certainty about what a photograph can preserve.
