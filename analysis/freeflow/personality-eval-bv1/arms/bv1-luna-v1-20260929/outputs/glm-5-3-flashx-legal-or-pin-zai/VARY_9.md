## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A literary short story built around a mystery of family loss and a river that returns what it has kept.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and quietly elegiac, letting grief emerge through recovered objects rather than explanation. The magnet’s finds draw a town’s private losses into view, then turn Milo’s search toward his sister and a memory he had not known he carried. The story invites the reader to sit with partial recovery: the past cannot be restored whole, but an object or a detail can make room for what was missing.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds water as both keeper and giver, ordinary objects as vessels of grief, and the difficulty of living with an incomplete family story. Its recurring tally, license plate, and recovered car door make counting and recognition part of the emotional resolution. The town’s shared ritual of bringing lost things back also gives private mourning a communal form.
