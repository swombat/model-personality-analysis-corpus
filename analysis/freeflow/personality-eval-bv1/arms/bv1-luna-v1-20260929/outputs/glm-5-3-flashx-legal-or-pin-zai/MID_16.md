## Grounded reading

Its pathos is restrained; it acknowledges sleeplessness, aging, and the dangers of getting lost, but doesn’t romanticize them away. The essay invites the reader to notice the value in not optimizing every route—in being a stranger who sometimes has to ask.

Its closing image returns to the small, ordinary threshold of home, leaving the walk’s sense of openness intact even as the narrator goes inside.
