## Grounded reading

Its pathos comes from the contrast between a civilization’s apparent disappearance and the narrator’s recovery of its knowledge; the ending insists that art and learning can make the past live again. The reader is invited to share the thrill of discovery, though the prose often explains the mystery rather than letting it remain mysterious.

The mood moves from desolation toward hopeful restoration, with the moral claim that human creativity can outlast oblivion.
