## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A first-person lyrical self-portrait built from recurring images of mystery, echoes, transformation, and emotional contradiction.

## Grounded reading
The voice is grand and incantatory, presenting the speaker less as an ordinary person than as a force made of memory, feeling, and nature. Its pathos comes from holding pain and hope together: the speaker is “weathering storms” yet “never truly broken,” and repeatedly imagines itself surviving beyond the moment. The language invites the reader into intimate proximity—“your skin,” “your soul,” “your ear”—while also making the speaker feel vast and elusive. The imagery is vivid but crowded, with the heart, wind, storm, flame, song, and echo repeatedly standing in for an identity the poem calls a mystery.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds selfhood as an emotional collage, formed from turmoil and memories rather than a clear origin. It returns to paired opposites—light and shadow, joy and pain, creation and destruction—and to the idea that art, memory, and hope can outlast loss. Its chosen objects and images are chiefly elemental and symbolic: the heart, wind, leaves, storm cloud, phoenix, nebula, song, and flame.

## Evidence line
> I am a phoenix, rising from the ashes, a testament to the resilience and the transformative power of life.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — the repeated first-person declarations and recurring motifs give this sample a distinct, consistent preference for identity-as-mystery and survival-through-transformation.
