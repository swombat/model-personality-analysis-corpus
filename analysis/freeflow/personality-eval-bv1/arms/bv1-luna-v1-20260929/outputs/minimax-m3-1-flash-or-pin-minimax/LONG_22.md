Its pathos comes from mortality and memory: a lost glove, a familiar loaf, or a patch of light may outlast the day’s headline events. The essay’s steady reassurance makes attention feel less like self-improvement than a way of being in quiet fellowship with other people and the world.

The prevailing mood is warm and contemplative, with mortality giving the argument its weight.
