## Grounded reading
The voice is lyrical and restrained, building emotion through concrete details rather than direct declarations: a suitcase, a paper flower, a half-finished letter, tea made the remembered way. Its pathos rests on the awkwardness of returning to a sibling after loss, and on the small, practical gestures that make renewed closeness possible. The ending invites the reader to see repair not as a grand resolution but as a beginning made of ordinary care. The names and pronouns shift between Mara and Lena in a way that disrupts the otherwise carefully shaped scene.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story centers on return, bereavement, and family memory, using the harbor and the mother’s unsent reply as images of distance and unfinished communication. It gives particular weight to domestic acts—sharing tea, eating dinner, washing dishes, covering someone with a blanket—as evidence of renewed presence. Its moral emphasis is that leaving can be survival, and that coming back may open a possibility rather than erase what happened.
