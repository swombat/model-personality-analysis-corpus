## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — An atmospheric supernatural story uses a grieving daughter’s discovery of her mother’s sewing room to turn memory into an uncanny mystery.

## Grounded reading
The voice is measured and quietly uncanny: ordinary household details—clocks, pipes, a toaster, a sewing machine—become instruments for grief. The story’s pathos rests on memories that are incomplete or painful, especially the daughter’s guilt about her brother and the cryptic warning not to forgive “him” yet. The mother appears less as a source of explanation than as a tender guide who accepts the daughter’s uncertainty. The reader is invited to feel that remembering may be gradual and embodied, like finding a button in one’s palm.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds maternal care, grief, memory, and unresolved family hurt, expressed through sewing, blue thread, a missing button, and a house that seems to keep time. Its moral emphasis is gentle: the daughter need not force a complete account of the past before she can begin to face it.

## Evidence line
> The old metronome in the hallway refused to beat above sixty, as if the house had settled on a slower heart.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The story’s coherent recurring imagery and distinctive blend of domestic detail, grief, and supernatural suggestion make its chosen emotional register clear.
