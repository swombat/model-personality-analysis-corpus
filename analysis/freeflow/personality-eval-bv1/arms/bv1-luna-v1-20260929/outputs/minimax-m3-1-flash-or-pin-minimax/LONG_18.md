## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. This is a lyrical, first-person meditation whose recurring images and personal memories give it a distinct voice beyond a general essay thesis.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and tender, drawn to lives the city’s daytime bustle makes easy to overlook: sleepers, delivery drivers, cleaners, and strangers passing in crowds. Its pathos comes through small, tactile objects—a chipped cup, two stones, a green door, a bridge—that become vessels for grief, memory, and imagined lives. The piece invites the reader to slow down and notice others without claiming to know their stories; attention becomes a modest form of care. Its closing uncertainty between speaking and remaining silent keeps that invitation intimate rather than resolving it into a lesson.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds predawn urban life, overlooked labor, loneliness amid crowds, memory held in ordinary objects, and the crossings—bridges, art, books, self-invention—that let people approach what feels distant. It repeatedly values attention to particular people over the city’s polished, functional image.

## Evidence line

Medium — the sustained lyric voice, first-person reflection, and recurring object motifs make this a notably distinctive and coherent expression of attention and memory.
