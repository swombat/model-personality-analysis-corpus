## Grounded reading
The voice is observant, calm, and gently reverent toward ordinary life. Its pathos comes less from individual hardship than from the quiet endurance of workers, patients, and travelers: people keeping time, tending others, or carrying on through sleeplessness. The recurring images of clocks, bridges, ferries, and the paper boat make the city feel held together by small acts and shared routines. The reader is invited to look closely at a familiar world and feel its hidden tenderness without expecting it to explain itself.

It foregrounded patience, coordination, care, and the fragile hope of ordinary journeys, with the paper boat and the repaired clocks serving as images of time and possibility.
