# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: LONG

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A sustained, atmospheric fantasy story uses a time-travel station to explore grief, memory, and the temptation to remain in the past.

## Grounded reading
The voice is lyrical and compassionate, with a fondness for quiet uncanny details: a warm key, a clock fixed at 2:13, tickets that name lost moments. Its pathos comes from regrets that are painfully ordinary—missing a phone call, sleeping through a song, losing a neighborhood—and from the story’s insistence that revisiting these moments cannot undo them. The reader is invited to feel the pull of the past while accepting that care sometimes means returning rather than staying.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds grief as both a wound and an attachment: the train offers a second hearing, but charges memories as its fare. Repeated objects—the key, the clock, blank tickets, the paper crane, the silver map—give loss a tactile form. The moral emphasis falls on return and accompaniment rather than erasure or repair: people can recover a voice, a song, or a sense that a place was real, but must accept what cannot be changed. The final movement toward reopening the station makes the resolution one of responsibility and continued care, not a cure for grief.

## Evidence line
> The road takes what you use to keep the wound open.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the recurring motifs and carefully sustained emphasis on returning from grief make this a distinctive, revealing creative choice.
