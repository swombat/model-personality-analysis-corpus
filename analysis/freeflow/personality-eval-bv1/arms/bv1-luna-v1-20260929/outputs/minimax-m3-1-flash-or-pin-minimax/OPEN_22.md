## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A lyrical fable uses a magical house and a missing minute to turn seasonal change into a story about attention and renewal.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and wistful, with small uncanny details held in a calm, storybook register. Its pathos comes from the possibility that spring—and the overlooked moments of a life—can quietly go missing. The story invites the reader to notice what seems minor before it disappears; Mara’s jar of rainwater makes that attentiveness tangible without explaining it away.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds winter and spring, clocks and lost time, and the idea that noticing matters: Elias is valued because he noticed, while Mara gradually recognizes the importance of moments she had disliked or overlooked. Its resolution is hopeful but not triumphant; spring returns for a moment, and the house closes until another lost minute needs finding.

## Evidence line
> She did not know why she needed to keep it, but she suspected that some things arrive only once, and if you are not paying attention, they can slip quietly from the world before you ever know their names.
