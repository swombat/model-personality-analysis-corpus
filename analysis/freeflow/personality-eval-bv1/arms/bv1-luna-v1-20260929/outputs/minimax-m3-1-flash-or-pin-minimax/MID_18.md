# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: MID

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A cohesive magical-realist story turns grief and family estrangement into a journey through a museum of lost things.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle, dreamlike, and attentive to small sensory details, but its central ache is specific: a father vanished, and the narrator has also been estranged from their mother. The fantastical museum offers not a literal reunion or a recovered past, but a way to carry one changed understanding back into ordinary life. Watches, jars, soup, and bread make grief tangible without making the story harsh. The reader is invited to see remembering as painful and unfinished, yet capable of opening a path toward contact.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds loss, memory, and belated family connection through recurring objects: a stopped watch, a green button jar, and a blue door that appears for people missing something essential. Its moral emphasis is on partial repair rather than undoing loss: the father is not returned, but the narrator reconnects with their mother. The mood stays wistful and compassionate, with everyday details grounding the magical premise.

## Evidence line
> The museum had not returned me my father; the watch on my wrist began ticking again.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the recurring lost-object imagery and emotionally restrained resolution make this a distinctive, coherent sample, though its fable-like form carries familiar magical-realist conventions.
