## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical, carefully structured meditation on urban life, organized around a recurring contrast between maps and the lives they leave out.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and generous, finding emotional weight in ordinary urban details rather than in dramatic events. Its pathos is quiet: places change, memories distort, and strangers remain unknowable, yet attention and small acts of care make shared life meaningful. The essay invites readers to slow down and notice what official maps, schedules, and ideas of ownership omit.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds dawn streets, trains, bakeries, staircases, shops, parks, rain, pigeons, and lit windows as evidence of the city’s unseen human life. It repeatedly favors shared space and everyday care over efficiency, ownership, and grand claims of progress. The recurring map metaphor makes attention itself a moral practice: noticing details is presented as a way to care for a place and its people.

## Evidence line
> Attention may be the rarest and most radical thing a person can offer a place.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the coherent map-and-attention motif recurs throughout, and the sample consistently gives ordinary shared life a humane, quietly moral emphasis.
