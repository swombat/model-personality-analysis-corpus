Evaluator: withheld
Source model: `withheld`
Condition: MID

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION. A lyrical magical-realist story uses a mysterious door to stage Mara’s encounter with memory, loss, and the choice to return to the present.

## Grounded reading
The voice is tender and elegiac, with tactile details—the tarnished handle, warm toast, silver yarn—making an otherworldly premise feel intimate. The story treats grief not as something to defeat but as something to welcome without being ruled by it: Mara can visit the past, hear what she needs, and still close the door. Its invitation is gentle rather than didactic, asking the reader to regard sorrow as a visitor that can be met with care.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds discarded doors, lost places and relationships, unfinished things, and the pull of imagined second chances. Its central moral emphasis is that returning to the past can offer recognition, but holding on would prevent change. The ending turns that idea into a domestic ritual—making tea and leaving a door open—rather than a triumphant cure.

## Evidence line
> From then on, whenever sorrow knocked quietly, Mara made tea, welcomed it, and left the door open behind her.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sample is coherent and distinctive, and its recurring door imagery carries the same compassionate emphasis on remembering without remaining trapped in the past.
