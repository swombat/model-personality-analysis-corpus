# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld
Source model: `withheld`
Condition: LONG

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A sustained, lyrical fantasy built around a mysterious museum and the keeper who must decide what its collection is for.

## Grounded reading
The voice is atmospheric and gently mournful, making ordinary keepsakes feel charged with memory: a shoe, a ticket, a key, a suitcase. The story’s wonder is repeatedly tempered by grief and unease, especially in its images of people surrendering parts of their lives. Its moral turn is from protecting the past by locking it away to helping people choose what to remember and release. The reader is invited to treat beginnings not as guarantees of a particular future, but as doors that leave responsibility for what comes next.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story centers on beginnings, thresholds, and the objects that preserve them. It foregrounds memory as both shelter and deprivation, and develops a clear tension between safety and stillness: the museum protects people from having their past used as a weapon, but its protection also denies them ownership of their own transitions. The resolution favors shared remembrance, choice, and the imperfect freedom to continue.

## Evidence line
> The museum collected beginnings.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sustained system of recurring objects and thresholds supports a coherent moral emphasis on memory, agency, and change, while the voice remains within recognizable lyrical fantasy conventions.
