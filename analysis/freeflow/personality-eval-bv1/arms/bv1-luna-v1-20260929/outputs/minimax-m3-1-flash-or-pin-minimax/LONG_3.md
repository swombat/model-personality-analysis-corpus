# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld
Source model: `withheld`
Condition: LONG

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A sustained, lyrical fantasy fable built around an archive that preserves mornings and a city trapped in repetition.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and wonder-oriented, turning civic machinery—drawers, vessels, clocks, gates—into the furniture of a myth. Its pathos comes from the cost of preserving what should be allowed to change: citizens unknowingly repeat their lives, while Elian must accept that a genuine beginning will alter what everyone remembers. The story invites the reader to value attention and stewardship without confusing them with possession. Its resolution is hopeful but not frictionless; the city becomes alive again through uncertainty, loss, and the willingness to let a day end.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds stored light, rivers, bells, archives, and the tension between preservation and release. It gives moral weight to witnessing ordinary life and sharing what is kept, while casting manufactured permanence as a form of theft. Under the freeflow condition, it chooses a world in which memory matters, but renewal requires surrendering the claim to repeat or hold on to every moment.

## Evidence line
> Every morning in the city was supposed to be kept.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the coherent, distinctive fable repeatedly returns to preservation, memory, and change, and makes those choices central to its moral resolution.
