## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A polished, atmospheric short story about estrangement and a tentative return to family.

## Grounded reading
The voice is quiet and lyrical, building feeling through coastal weather, repeated routines, and objects that carry emotional weight. Elena’s unopened letter makes avoidance tangible; the eventual key offers a path toward reconciliation without pretending that the years of absence are resolved. The story invites the reader to share her hesitation and to regard taking the first step—not certainty or forgiveness—as a form of courage.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds waiting, solitude, and the possibility of reconnection, rendered through recurring objects and images: the letters, the kettle, the lighthouse, fog, the ferry, and the key. Its moral emphasis is gentle: one can move toward an unanswered past while still carrying questions.

## Evidence line
> She carried a key, some questions, and the courage to ask them.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story sustains a coherent pattern of restrained longing and symbolic objects, though its coastal imagery and hopeful resolution are familiar genre choices.
