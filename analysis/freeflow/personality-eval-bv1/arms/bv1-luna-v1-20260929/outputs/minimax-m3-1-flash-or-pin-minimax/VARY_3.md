# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld
Source model: `withheld`
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A polished portal fantasy in which a woman’s journey through a rain-soaked city becomes an encounter with memory, grief, and possible futures.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle, dreamlike, and carefully composed, building wonder through precise sensory details: rain that smells “of iron, roses, and the sea,” clocks that keep different times, and a bookshop whose books bear names. The story’s pathos gathers around Mara’s absent mother and the limits of recovering the past. Rather than offering reunion as a cure, it lets recognition arrive through remembered gestures and leaves Mara with a choice about what to carry forward. The reader is invited to experience mystery as a route toward reflection, not merely a puzzle to solve.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds doors, clocks, tickets, books, and other threshold objects as ways of moving between memory and possibility. Its moral emphasis is that grief and love need not be discarded, but considered for what they teach or enable. Curiosity opens the journey; the resolution turns on choosing what to carry, while leaving the future unresolved.

## Evidence line
> “Memory is not a road back,” she said.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story coherently returns to memory, loss, and chosen inheritance, though its portal-fantasy imagery and consoling moral are familiar rather than highly distinctive.
