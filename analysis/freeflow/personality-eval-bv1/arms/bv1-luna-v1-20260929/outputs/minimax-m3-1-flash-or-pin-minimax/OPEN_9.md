# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: OPEN

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A gently uncanny fantasy story built around a clear choice between returning to the past and moving forward.

## Grounded reading
The voice is quiet, inviting, and gently wistful, using whimsical details—the paper moon, shops selling lost buttons and second chances—to make grief and renewal feel approachable rather than stark. Mara’s choice gives the story its emotional weight: she misses what is gone, but chooses not to live by returning to it. The ending offers comfort without undoing that choice, suggesting that something new can rise from what has been lost.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds inherited space, everyday mystery, and the pull of the past, then centers a moral choice about whether to revisit what is gone or accept what comes next. Bells, bread, keys, and gates carry the story’s sense of transition; its mood stays tender and hopeful rather than frightening.

## Evidence line
> But the man had said *choose wisely*, and Mara had never been good at returning to what was gone.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story is coherent and its recurring magical objects reinforce a distinctive, tender emphasis on grief, memory, and choosing forward motion.
