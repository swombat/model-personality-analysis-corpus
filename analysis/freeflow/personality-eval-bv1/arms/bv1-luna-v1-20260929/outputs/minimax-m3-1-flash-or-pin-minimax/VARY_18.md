## Grounded reading

The closing invitation is to notice ordinary acts of care and let uncertainty, even having no destination, feel sufficient.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds harbor and street life, weather, books, remembered voices, and encounters between strangers. It repeatedly turns everyday objects into carriers of memory or connection, while giving special attention to quiet kindness, perseverance, and the question of whose stories are remembered.
