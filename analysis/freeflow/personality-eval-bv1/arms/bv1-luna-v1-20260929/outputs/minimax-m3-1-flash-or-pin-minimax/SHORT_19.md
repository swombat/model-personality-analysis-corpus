## Grounded reading
The voice is playful and gently self-deflating: the narrator briefly calls the clock’s claim profound, then undercuts it with the practical detail of the sloping roof. The clocks’ homesickness gives the story a tender pathos, while the mayor’s clock that points “toward whatever mattered most” leaves the reader with an invitation to consider purpose beyond keeping time.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds clocks displaced from their original homes, the different contexts that shape what counts as “correct,” and restoration through returning things to where they belong. Its moral emphasis is that usefulness may come from attending to what matters, not simply from measuring time.
