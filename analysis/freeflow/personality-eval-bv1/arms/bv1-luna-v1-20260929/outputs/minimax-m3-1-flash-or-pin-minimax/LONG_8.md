# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: LONG

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A sustained, lyrical speculative story built around a museum of lost, unrealized, and remembered hours.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and contemplative, inviting the reader to move slowly through rooms where ordinary sensory details—a bowl of soup, rain on a roof, a broom on a street—carry the emotional weight of loss and care. Its pathos is gentle rather than tragic: the narrator feels regret for roads not taken and grief for people gone, but finds consolation in the fact that memories and acts of maintenance keep something present. The reader is invited to treat attention itself as a form of preservation, and to see a city not as a finished monument but as something continually repaired and remade.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds counterfactual lives, anonymous labor, family memory, vanished places, unsent words, and the fragile persistence of names. Its recurring objects—rain jars, tickets, cards, bread, a blue broom—turn memory into something tangible that can be tended, carried, or released. The moral emphasis falls on care over possession: the narrator ultimately keeps one hour, not every lost possibility, and chooses an hour shaped by workers preparing the city for others.

## Evidence line
> The city belonged to those whose work began before other people woke.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The story’s coherent, recurring attention to memory, ordinary care, and repair makes these choices unusually clear, while its polished speculative frame gives them the form of a crafted narrative.
