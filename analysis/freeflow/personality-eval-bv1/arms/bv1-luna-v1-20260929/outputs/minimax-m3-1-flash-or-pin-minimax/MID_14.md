# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: MID

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A symbolic literary story in which estranged siblings revisit family history through a museum of ordinary objects.

## Grounded reading
The voice is quiet and lyrical, giving emotional weight to small, handled things: a missing button, a cold cup, an old compass. Its pathos comes from regret and delayed understanding rather than an explosive confrontation; the siblings approach each other through objects and half-told truths before they can speak plainly. The story invites the reader to treat memory as something assembled from imperfect clues, and reconciliation as an opening rather than a clean resolution.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds humble objects as vessels for unfinished intentions, especially the compass as a shifting symbol of direction, inheritance, and the need to face the past. It chooses estrangement, family silence, and the possibility of love persisting alongside failure. The museum’s invitation not to discard anything becomes the story’s moral center: even awkward, painful, or seemingly useless remnants may still matter.

## Evidence line
> The truth was smaller and more painful: he had failed, but he had not ceased loving us.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The recurring object imagery and restrained movement toward reconciliation make this a coherent, distinctive piece, while its symbolism and resolution follow a familiar literary-fable shape.
