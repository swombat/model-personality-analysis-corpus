## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A polished fantasy fable uses a lighthouse and its lost objects to explore memory, grief, and acceptance.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and atmospheric, building wonder through precise, tactile details: pipe smoke and oranges, a red umbrella, a jar holding a miniature house. Its pathos comes from losses that are not simply recovered but given a place to be held. The story invites the reader to see remembering not as undoing grief, but as choosing how to carry it; its ending offers comfort without pretending that the sea—or the past—can be made to disappear.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story centers on loss, memory, and care: a keeper receives objects that have nowhere else to go, while the lighthouse illuminates the future and eventually brings Mara to terms with her uncle’s absence. It gives special weight to the idea that tending to loss is different from preventing it, and resolves the mystery through acceptance rather than escape or reunion as a cure.
