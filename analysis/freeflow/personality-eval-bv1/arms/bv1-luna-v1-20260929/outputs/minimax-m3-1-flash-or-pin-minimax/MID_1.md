# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: MID

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A lyrical fantasy story turns a lighthouse-keeping ritual into a choice about inherited memory and communal history.

## Grounded reading
The voice is atmospheric and gently portentous, building wonder through tactile details—the iron-tasting wind, salt-stained cottages, barnacled underground walls—rather than through fast action. Its pathos comes from a keeper inheriting a ritual whose purpose has been forgotten, and from the town’s comfortable distance from its own past. The invitation is to see tradition not merely as superstition or duty, but as a form of care that can preserve what a community no longer knows how to remember.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds inherited ritual, family memory, the sea as an immense and listening presence, and the tension between safety and remembrance. Its central moral choice is whether the keeper will keep the light turned outward or illuminate the town’s past; the final act favors history, even though the story does not resolve what that revelation will bring.

## Evidence line
> It was habit, and habit had become a kind of civic tenderness, a shared forgetting that kept the town from feeling too safe.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sample’s recurring imagery and its distinctive emphasis on ritual as civic tenderness give the fiction a coherent moral and emotional signature.
