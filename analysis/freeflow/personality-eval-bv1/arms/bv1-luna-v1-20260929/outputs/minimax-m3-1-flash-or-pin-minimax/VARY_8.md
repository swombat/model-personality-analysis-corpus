# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A polished speculative mystery built around a hotel that preserves alternate moments and selves.

## Grounded reading
The voice is atmospheric and patient, using rain, clocks, keys, photographs, and doors to make uncertainty feel tangible. Its pathos comes from the narrator’s encounter with younger and older versions of themself, and from the choice to give up a promised past rather than accept a possibly false one. The reader is invited to follow a puzzle, but also to weigh the story’s final preference for an unknown future over comforting certainty.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds recursive time, memory, and the instability of identity, embodied in the house and its mirrored rooms. Its central moral claim is that choosing uncertainty can be more honest than recovering a past that cannot be verified. The rain-soaked setting and the repeated objects—envelopes, clocks, keys, doors—give that choice a sustained, dreamlike mood.

## Evidence line
> I stepped into morning carrying an unwritten warning and the comfort of choosing uncertainty over a lie.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story develops a coherent set of recurring images and resolves them in a clear moral choice, though its themes and atmosphere are shaped by familiar speculative-fiction conventions.
