# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A polished supernatural grief story that uses an impossible railway station to bring Mara toward a gentler understanding of her brother’s disappearance.

## Grounded reading
The voice is tender and atmospheric, building sorrow through concrete objects—the photograph, red cap, two cups, and backward clock—rather than through abstract reflection. Mara’s guilt gives the story its emotional weight, while the woman’s gentle correction invites the reader to distinguish love from self-punishment. The ending offers release without erasure: Mara keeps her brother as a memory and returns to ordinary life.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds bereavement, unresolved guilt, and the risk of turning grief into a life of waiting. Its supernatural imagery serves a clear moral claim: remembering someone need not mean preserving the wound or blaming oneself. The chosen resolution is small and physical—Mara takes the cap and walks home—rather than a return of the dead or a definitive explanation of what happened.

## Evidence line
> “You left a hat,” the woman corrected gently. “People leave hats in thousands of places. It does not mean they intend to disappear.”

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story is coherent and its recurring objects reinforce a distinctive emphasis on forgiveness and continuing life, though its emotional arc follows familiar supernatural-grief conventions.
