# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: LONG

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A sustained literary-fantasy story uses a museum of suspended possibilities to frame a sibling’s estrangement and Mara’s decision to reach out.

## Grounded reading
The voice is atmospheric and patient, finding emotional weight in objects that are broken, misplaced, or left unfinished: a stopped clock, a missing bridge span, a key with no known lock. Its central pathos is the cost of avoiding hurt by keeping decisions—and relationships—suspended. The supernatural archive makes that avoidance visible, but the resolution stays modest: Mara writes a letter, travels to the observatory, and waits without demanding a guaranteed reunion. The story invites the reader to regard uncertainty not as a puzzle to solve but as a condition one can enter willingly.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds interruption, deferred choices, memory, and estrangement, giving them physical form in the museum’s exhibits and the mysterious seventh floor. It places particular moral weight on the difference between preserving possibility and hiding from risk. Rain, doors, keys, thresholds, and unfinished drawings recur as images of both separation and potential reconnection; the ending favors an open door and patient hope over certainty or neat closure.

## Evidence line
> The museum taught her that it might merely be fear.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the recurring objects and threshold imagery consistently serve the story’s emotional argument, and the ending resists a tidy guarantee of reconciliation.
