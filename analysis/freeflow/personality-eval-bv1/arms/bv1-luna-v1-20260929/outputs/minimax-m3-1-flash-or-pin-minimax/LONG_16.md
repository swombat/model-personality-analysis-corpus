## Grounded reading
The voice is reflective and gently exhortative, using sensory scenes—the rain-dark street, a repaired book, a familiar museum—to make attention feel like care rather than self-improvement. Its pathos comes from the fear that usefulness, speed, and consumption make people and particulars fade into a blur. The reader is invited to slow down, notice, listen, and let encounters alter their judgments.

## What the model chose to foreground
Under the freeflow condition, the sample foregrounds attention as generosity, relationship, ethical responsibility, and practical love. It returns to ordinary objects and acts—umbrellas, books, tools, walks, listening, mending—as places where wonder and care can be recovered. It also critiques productivity culture and performative mindfulness, while insisting that attention matters most when it leads to humility and action.

## Evidence line

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
