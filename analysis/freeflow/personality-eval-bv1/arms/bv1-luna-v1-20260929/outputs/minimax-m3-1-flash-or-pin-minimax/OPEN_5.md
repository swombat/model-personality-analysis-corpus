## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and dreamlike, with a wistful attention to things left incomplete: unsent letters, unfinished buildings, and a museum that keeps its collection from wandering away. The story’s pathos comes from the possibility that abandoned efforts may still have a future. Its invitation to the reader is hopeful but not effortless: tomorrow is made “clumsily, bravely, one decision at a time,” and Mara must choose to take part rather than merely observe.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds incompletion as potential rather than failure, using the museum, the future-facing subway map, and the child’s red stick as recurring images. It gives moral weight to making and choosing: tomorrow is not waiting to be found, but is built by people who carry bricks, questions, courage, and unfinished plans. The mood shifts from quiet strangeness and hesitation to communal possibility.

## Evidence line
