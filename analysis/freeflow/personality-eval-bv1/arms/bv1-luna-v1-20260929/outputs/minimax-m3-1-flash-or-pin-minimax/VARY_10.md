## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A lyrical fantasy story built around a lighthouse, a red boat, and a mysterious inheritance of names and light.

## Grounded reading
The voice is hushed and dreamlike, turning ordinary details—bread, cats, a rusty ring—into the threshold of a myth. Its pathos lies in the tension between being chosen and being free to refuse: Mira is offered a role, but fears what it may ask of her. The ending invites the reader to see courage less as finding a route than as choosing what deserves attention.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds the sea’s mystery, inherited identity, memory, fear, and the possibility of becoming through practice. Its recurring objects—the lantern, linen names, mirrors, and red boat—make responsibility feel at once intimate and ancient. A notable moral choice is the village tale that lets the sea be cruel without assigning blame; the resolution shifts from passive explanation toward Mira’s willingness to carry the light.

## Evidence line
> Mira liked that story, because it allowed the sea to be cruel without making anyone responsible.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story is coherent and has distinctive recurring imagery, with a clear emphasis on identity, responsibility, and chosen courage.
