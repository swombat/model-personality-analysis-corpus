## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and observant, finding emotional weight in small sensory details: flour on bread, a bicycle bell, a key, a worn map. Its pathos comes from the quiet disappearance of places and the partial, object-bound nature of memory, balanced by ordinary kindness and continuity. It invites the reader to slow down and treat the familiar world as worthy of notice, without demanding a grand revelation.

## What the model chose to foreground
