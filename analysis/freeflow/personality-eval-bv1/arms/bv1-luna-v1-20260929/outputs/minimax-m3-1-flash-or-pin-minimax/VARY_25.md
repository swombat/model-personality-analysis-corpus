## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A folkloric mystery unfolds through recurring magical objects and resolves in a quiet act of return.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and atmospheric, building wonder through sensory details—salt-crusted paint, a lantern without a flame, a compass pointing toward darkness—rather than urgency or explanation. Mara’s curiosity draws the reader through the clues, while the story’s pathos gathers around a community that has forgotten what it owes. Its ending turns the mystery toward restitution and remembrance, offering release without making the sea’s power feel tame.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds the lighthouse, red door, key, photograph, map, and book as linked objects of inheritance and debt. Its chosen mood is hushed and uncanny; its moral emphasis is that promises and communal sacrifices persist even when people forget them. The final return of the light and the villagers’ lost names makes restoration an act of acknowledging what was taken, not simply solving a puzzle.

## Evidence line
> They were the villagers who had given their names, their laughter, and their love of the light to the sea.
