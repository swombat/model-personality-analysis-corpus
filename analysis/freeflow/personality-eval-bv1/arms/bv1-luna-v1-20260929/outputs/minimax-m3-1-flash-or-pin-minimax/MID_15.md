## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A lyrical coastal mystery uses grief, family secrets, and uncanny clues to draw Mara toward an old lighthouse.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and atmospheric, lingering over gulls, rain, harbor light, and domestic traces rather than rushing to explain the mystery. Its pathos comes from Mara’s bereavement and the possibility that her mother’s silence concealed love rather than abandonment. The reader is invited to follow small material clues—the compass, letters, map, and iron marker—and to share Mara’s gradual sense of being understood.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds the harbor and its weather, the mother’s legacy, and objects that seem to hold knowledge: a cracked compass, letters with impossible stamps, a map, and bells labeled by year. Its mood is wistful and quietly uncanny. It also favors continuity and preservation, culminating in the claim that an old light can matter beyond its practical usefulness.

## Evidence line
> Mara sat beside the marker and cried, not from sadness, but from being understood at last.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sample sustains a distinctive, coherent atmosphere through recurring coastal objects and a clear emotional resolution, though its mystery-fiction structure is familiar.
