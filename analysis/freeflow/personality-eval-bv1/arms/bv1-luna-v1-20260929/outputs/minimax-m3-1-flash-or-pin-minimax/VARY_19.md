Evaluator: withheld

Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION. A sustained surreal fairy-tale quest follows Mara through a chain of impossible places and choices.

## Grounded reading
The voice is lyrical and gently uncanny, making impossibility feel matter-of-fact: a fountain flows upward, a newspaper is dated tomorrow, and a librarian is made of paper. Beneath the whimsical imagery is a wistful concern with what is owed, what can be returned, and what a person chooses when offered familiar ways home. Mara’s disappointment that wonder needs witnesses gives the story a small ache amid its inventions. The reader is invited to follow the images as clues, while the ending leaves the journey open rather than resolving it into a settled homecoming.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds borrowed time, keys and maps, memory, and the tension between returning to what is familiar and choosing an unknown destination. Its recurring objects—paper birds, glass fruit, books, tickets—turn abstract questions of obligation and possibility into things Mara can hold or follow. The final choice of an unknown coastline gives the narrative’s wonder a note of deliberate uncertainty.

## Evidence line
> Wonder needed witnesses.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sample sustains a distinctive, coherent pattern of surreal objects and wistful choices, while its lyrical fairy-tale mode is familiar rather than sharply idiosyncratic.
