## Grounded reading
The voice is lyrical and gently melancholy, turning cartography into a way of noticing what official accounts leave out. Its emotional center is Mara’s long grief over her vanished brother, but the story makes room for other kinds of unfinished business too: regrets, unrealized ambitions, and lives that do not fit a tidy public story. Its invitation to the reader is to treat uncertainty and hesitation not only as errors to correct, but as places where people may need time and choice.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds maps, clocks, rain, thresholds, and the small intervals that escape measurement. Its central moral claim is that visibility can offer recognition without demanding control: Mara preserves Aster Lane by mapping what it means to its inhabitants, and lets Tomas choose whether to return. The ending favors reconciliation and shared memory, while leaving a little mystery alive in the clocks’ disagreement.
