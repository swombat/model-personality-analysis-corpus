Evaluator: withheld
Source model: `withheld`
Condition: MID

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical meditation whose recurring images and intimate sensory details give it more character than a thesis-driven public essay.

## Grounded reading
The voice is contemplative and gently wistful, finding wonder in familiar streets after dark. It moves from night walking into memories of family, home, and departure, then offers the reader a way to see ordinary places as layered with other people’s lives and the past. Its invitation is not to solve those mysteries, but to pay attention and keep looking.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds nighttime city details—laundromats, streetlamps, windows, puddles—and treats them as openings into memory and imagined lives. It links private recollection with shared urban space, then broadens toward a moral claim that history contains silences and should remain open to conversation. Walking becomes both the image and method for reading a changing world.

## Evidence line
> Walking home after midnight feels like crossing a border without a passport.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the piece sustains a distinctive, recurring weave of city imagery, memory, and attentive wonder, though its polished lyrical reflections are familiar in form.
