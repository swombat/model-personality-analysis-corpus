## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION. This is a compact, atmospheric fable built around a supernatural call and a final sacrifice.

## Grounded reading
The voice is quiet and solemn, using fog, bells, and the failing lighthouse to make grief feel like a duty inherited from Mara’s father. The ending leaves her fate unexplained: the village is safe, but she has gone, and only she understood the promise. It invites the reader to weigh the cost of keeping others safe rather than spelling out what Mara’s choice means.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds inherited responsibility, a father’s idea of light as a promise, and the possibility that a community’s safety depends on one person’s departure. Lighthouse, logbook, bell, and boat recur as linked objects of duty and mystery. The mood is mournful but not despairing; the final calm harbor offers resolution at a personal cost.

## Evidence line
> She lit the lantern with a page from her father’s logbook.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the coherent recurrence of light, inheritance, and sacrifice makes the choices distinctive within the sample, though the fable’s spare, familiar structure keeps the voice restrained.
