## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A lyrical first-person reflection turns an ordinary walk in the rain into a modest lesson in attention and renewal.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle, observant, and lightly intimate, moving from sensory details—warm pavement, mirrored streets, the “small crown” of a footstep—to a personal change in attitude. Its pathos is quiet rather than dramatic: rain interrupts plans, but that interruption becomes a chance to notice people and places more fully. The closing invitation is to slow down and meet the ordinary with openness.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds rain as both physical weather and a pause in routine, with puddles, umbrellas, traffic, a café, and a garden as objects of attention. It favors restoration, playfulness, and noticing over efficiency, and ends with the moral suggestion that interruption can make room for renewal.

Medium: the reflective, sensory voice is coherent across the piece, though its rain-as-renewal imagery and closing insight are familiar rather than unusually distinctive.
