## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A cohesive magical-realist fable about a woman following a cat into a hidden district of lost places.

## Grounded reading
The voice is dreamlike and gently wistful, building wonder from ordinary urban details: cold coffee, a clicking radiator, a late bus. Its invented places give shape to feeling—departures, changed plans, and the sense of being unfinished or lost. The story invites the reader to treat uncertainty less as failure than as movement toward a place not yet known; its ending offers reassurance without insisting that Alma has arrived.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story centers on lostness and departure, expressed through maps, blank street signs, photographs, and a paper river. It selects small, tender guides—the orange cat, the girl sorting photographs—and gives moral weight to changing direction and continuing on. The closing image of the blue cup and white paw turns the surreal journey into a quiet reminder that the mysterious may persist in ordinary life.
