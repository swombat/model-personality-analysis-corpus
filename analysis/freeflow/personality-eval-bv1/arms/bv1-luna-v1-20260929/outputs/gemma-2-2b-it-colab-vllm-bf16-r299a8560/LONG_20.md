## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION. A mythic coastal horror story follows a returning storyteller through an island haunted by unresolved histories.

## Grounded reading
The voice reaches for lush, ominous lyricism: brine, bruised skies, skeletal beaches, and a map that seems to bind the narrator to the island. Its pathos comes from the damaged bond with the grandfather, whose warnings and lost stories now pull Alexandra back toward a place she once left. The story invites the reader to treat storytelling as both inheritance and obligation—to listen to what has been lost rather than simply turn it into material for fame. That thread is vivid, though the passage often breaks its own spell with abrupt, hard-to-parse images and shifts in meaning.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds stories as things that can be inherited, erased, or stubbornly left unfinished; the sea and island become keepers of that history, but also threats that swallow people and narratives. Grief, regret, and the pull of home recur alongside maps, whispers, bones, and storms. The ending leans toward shared ownership of stories and warnings, without resolving whether listening will save Alexandra or trap her.
