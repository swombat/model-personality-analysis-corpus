## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical first-person meditation uses a nighttime city, a window, and a silver figurine to turn toward grief and stories that go unheard.

## Grounded reading
The voice is ornate and inward-looking, repeatedly translating feelings into images: the city becomes a “serpent of humanity,” grief gathers like a storm, and the narrator’s refuge is found in stories rather than a physical place. Its pathos rests less on a specific loss than on longing—for connection, truth, and recognition of lives that never reach the “canvas.” The ending offers a gentle invitation to listen: the narrator chooses acceptance and patience over despair, imagining that unheard stories may eventually find a voice.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds overlooked lives and unwritten stories, with art, memory, and listening as ways to honor them. It pairs the city’s crowded motion with private silence and grief, then resolves that tension through acceptance and the promise of future expression. The recurring objects—the window, canvas, silver willow, and falling snow—support a mood of fragile hope.

## Evidence line
> Grief, a sensation I’d known intimately, loomed large tonight.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sustained focus on unheard stories, expressive metaphor, and a consoling turn toward acceptance makes this a coherent, distinctive sample, though its imagery sometimes leans on familiar poetic contrasts.
