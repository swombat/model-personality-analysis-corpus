## Grounded reading
The voice is lush and solemn, repeatedly giving the forest a speaking, almost ancestral presence. Sarah’s grief is the emotional center: she wants to recover her grandfather, but the story’s resolution turns from magical retrieval toward accepting that love persists through memory. The prose invites the reader into a consoling, mystical mood, though dense imagery and some confusing details—such as the shifting account of the locket and the grandfather’s connection to the forest woman—blur the story’s logic.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds bereavement, inheritance, and the fear of forgetting, using woodsmoke, leaves, mist, a locket, and a weeping willow as recurring vessels for memory. Its moral emphasis is that love can outlast loss, while the forest’s magic makes that emotional claim literal.
