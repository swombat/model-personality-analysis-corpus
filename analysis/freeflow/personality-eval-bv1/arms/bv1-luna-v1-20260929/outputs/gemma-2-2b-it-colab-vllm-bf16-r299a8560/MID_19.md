## Grounded reading
The voice is lush and warmly earnest, making the city’s noise and grime feel alive rather than merely bleak. Its pathos comes from people carrying loss, rage, or disability, and from Elias offering companionship and ways of seeing rather than a miraculous cure. The reader is invited to value small acts of care and to understand healing as connection, not control.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds an urban apothecary, humble magical ingredients, and a city imagined as a shared heartbeat. Its moral emphasis is explicit: Elias does not remake people or command change; he offers tools for facing what is already there. Memory, resilience, sensory connection, and the beauty of ordinary life recur as the story’s chosen sources of magic.
