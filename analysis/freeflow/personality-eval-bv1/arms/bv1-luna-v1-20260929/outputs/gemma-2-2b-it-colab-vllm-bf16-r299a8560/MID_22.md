## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical prose meditation uses a small-town setting and a few characters more as vessels for mood and memory than for a developed plot.

## Grounded reading
The voice is lush and elegiac, layering images of dust, weathered wood, flowers, and dusk to make time and loss feel almost physical. Its sadness is softened by a persistent reach toward renewal: the town’s burdens remain, but hope waits “just beyond the horizon.” The reader is invited to linger in a shared, half-spoken melancholy rather than follow a clear sequence of events; the prose’s emotional pull comes from that atmosphere, though its many overlapping metaphors sometimes blur the scene.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds memory, longing, inherited sorrow, and the pressure of time, set against the rituals and material textures of a rural town. It also chooses a small current of hope: wildflowers and the closing image of freshly turned soil suggest possible renewal amid the town’s accumulated loss.

## Evidence line
