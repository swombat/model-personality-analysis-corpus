## Grounded reading
The voice is lyrical and melancholic, drawn to weathered places and the emotional weight they gather over time. Its pathos comes through Alistair’s fading beacon and dream of making it shine again: hope is presented not as mastery over the sea, but as a small act of guidance within its dangers. The prose leans heavily on metaphor, sometimes crowding its images, while inviting the reader to linger over how a place can hold memory, loss, and companionship. The sudden shift to a cliffside conversation and its opaque final line leave the piece more suggestive than resolved.

## What the model chose to foreground
The chosen materials are the lighthouse, its failing machinery, the stormy sea, shipwrecks, old stories, and the keeper’s stubborn hope. The sample repeatedly makes the light a symbol of refuge and resilience while treating the sea as beautiful, perilous, and indifferent. Its closing coda turns toward shared stories and mortality, though it leaves their meaning unsettled.
