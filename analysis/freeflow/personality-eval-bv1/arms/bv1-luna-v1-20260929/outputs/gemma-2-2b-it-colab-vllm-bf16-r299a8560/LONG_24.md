## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A vignette about a café owner whose quiet attention helps strangers find comfort and hope.

## Grounded reading
The voice is lush and melancholy, leaning on sensory detail and repeated images of music, warmth, and shelter. Its pathos comes less from any one visitor’s story than from the idea that ordinary listening can make grief bearable. Layla is presented as a quiet witness and caretaker, and the reader is invited to see the café as a refuge where private stories can be held without judgment.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds a rain-soaked café, chai and lantern light, and strangers carrying grief, anxiety, and longing. It treats attentive presence and shared understanding as small acts of transformation, setting the café’s warmth against an indifferent, image-conscious city. Repeated tapestry and symphony metaphors make connection and imperfect human lives its central moral emphasis.

## Evidence line
