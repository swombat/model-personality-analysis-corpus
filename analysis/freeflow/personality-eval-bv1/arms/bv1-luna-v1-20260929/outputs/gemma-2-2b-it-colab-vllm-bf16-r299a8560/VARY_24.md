## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical grief vignette with a clear emotional arc from mourning to consolation.

## Grounded reading
The voice is solemn and lush, turning a dusty room and its objects into a vessel for memory. Its pathos comes from the narrator’s sense that grief has made the grandmother’s love feel unreachable, then from the recognition that her stories and courage can still guide the narrator. The piece invites the reader to treat remembrance not as simply looking backward, but as carrying love forward.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model selects dust, a stopped grandfather clock, a worn book, and the grandmother’s singing as anchors for absence and memory. Stars, light, and the lantern image gradually shift the mood toward hope; the moral emphasis is that love and courage can persist as a legacy after loss.

## Evidence line
> This was the room where my grandmother used to sing.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — the repeated dust, clock, voice, star, and lantern imagery makes the sample’s elegiac-to-hopeful choices coherent, though its emotional language leans on familiar sentimental conventions.
