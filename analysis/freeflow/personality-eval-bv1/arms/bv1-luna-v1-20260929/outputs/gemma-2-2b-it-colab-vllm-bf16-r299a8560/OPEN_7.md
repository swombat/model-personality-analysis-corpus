## Grounded reading
The voice is lush and mournful, building its atmosphere through familiar gothic images: the ticking antique clock, dusty window light, howling wind, and a woman described as a ghost. The prose makes grief feel shared rather than solitary; the narrator finds comfort in her stories and presence even while sensing the emptiness between them. The closing lines invite the reader to sit with that ambivalence: memories can fade or wound, yet a fragile connection may still touch something alive.

Its recurring objects—the clock, the window, the chair, and the woman’s veins—give the scene a domestic closeness beneath the supernatural atmosphere. The moral emphasis falls on shared sorrow as a form of solace, with the final whisper left uncertain rather than fully resolved.
