## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — Lyrical, reflective prose turns a grandmother’s cottage into a setting for remembering her and making peace with time’s passage.

## Grounded reading
The voice is wistful and heavily sensory, lingering over the cottage’s smells, textures, and sounds as if its objects can preserve a person’s presence. Grief is present but softened by a movement toward acceptance; the self-opening fairy-tale book adds a faintly magical comfort. The reader is invited to treat memory as something carried by ordinary things—the clock, table, shawls, and wind—rather than something fully left behind.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds loss, childhood memory, and the passage of time through recurring images of the ticking clock and restless wind. It gives special weight to domestic keepsakes and sensory traces—lavender, cookies, wallpaper, a picture frame, and a feather duster—and ultimately frames the cottage as a place where mourning can become solace. The repeated “dance,” “song,” and “rhythm” imagery makes that consolation feel cyclical, though some of the metaphors grow crowded.

## Evidence line
> My grandmother's cottage held a quiet awe.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the repeated clock, wind, and memory motifs give the sample a coherent reflective emphasis, while its familiar sentimental imagery makes the voice less individually distinctive.
