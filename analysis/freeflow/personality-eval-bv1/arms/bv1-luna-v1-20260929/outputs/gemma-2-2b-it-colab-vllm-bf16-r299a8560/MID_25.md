Evaluator: withheld

Condition: MID

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A lyrical speculative-fiction vignette follows a curator preserving fragments of human experience in a forgotten digital archive.

## Grounded reading
The voice is atmospheric and openly tender: the warehouse, humming server, and “ghostly” records make preservation feel like an act of mourning and care. Celia’s work gives the story its emotional center; she hopes that attending to lost messages can give their makers’ voices life again. The reader is invited to treat overlooked scraps—letters, calls, forum posts—not as disposable data but as fragile connections across time.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds forgotten digital memories, especially love, grief, fear, and longing, and casts their preservation as a moral good. Its recurring images of ghosts, echoes, bridges, and melodies make the archive feel less like a database than a vessel for human connection. The mood is elegiac but hopeful: the stories may be lost, yet someone can still listen and carry them forward.

## Evidence line
> These stories, she felt, were not just fragments of memories; they were fragile bridges across time.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story sustains a distinctive emphasis on memory, loss, and care through recurring archive-and-echo imagery, though its prose sometimes piles up mixed metaphors.
