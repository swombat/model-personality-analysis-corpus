## Grounded reading
The voice is mournful and heavily sensory, lingering over rain, rust, dust, stale tobacco, old paper, and the sewing machine as if each object could hold a fragment of a vanished life. Its pathos comes from the speaker’s estrangement from their own memories and from the sense that the future offers only more storms. The ending, though, invites the reader to see beauty not as a return to what was lost but as something that can endure in damaged places.
