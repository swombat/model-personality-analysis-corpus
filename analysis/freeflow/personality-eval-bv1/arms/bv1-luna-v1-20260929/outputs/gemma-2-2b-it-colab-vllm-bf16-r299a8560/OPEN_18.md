## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical meditation turns an imagined attic into a place where worn objects hold traces of human lives.

## Grounded reading
The voice is hushed and sentimental, building pathos from the contrast between decay and the lives it seems to preserve. It personifies the attic and its contents as witnesses, inviting the reader to look at relics not as clutter but as evidence of feeling and memory. The final shift from “tomb” to “garden” offers a gentle, if wistful, invitation to dwell with what is lost and still felt.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds dust, rust, faded fabric, chipped porcelain, and frozen time, then gives these objects an imaginative emotional life. Its central claim is that ordinary remnants can carry the echoes of laughter, fear, tears, and promises; its chosen mood is melancholy softened by refuge and welcome.

## Evidence line
> Maybe the attic wasn't a tomb, but a garden - a hidden haven overflowing with memories and loss and things we miss, and still feel.
