# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: SHORT

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A first-person scene in an archive uses a discovered journal and portrait to suggest a lost, yearning life.

## Grounded reading
The voice is hushed and reverent, treating old writing as something almost physically alive: pages whisper, history reaches out, and a portrait seems to reflect the narrator’s own longing. The pathos comes from imagining a vivid life constrained to the past, while the invitation is to read forgotten stories with empathy and wonder.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds dusty books, faded writing, a storm-eyed woman, and fragments of private feeling—wishes, inner struggles, unrequited love. It makes uncovering the past a way of recognizing shared human yearning, especially a desire for freedom.

## Evidence line
> In her gaze, I saw a reflection of myself, a yearning for a world left behind, a spirit yearning for a flicker of freedom.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: The scene coherently returns to forgotten lives and yearning, though its ornate archive-and-portrait imagery is familiar rather than highly distinctive.
