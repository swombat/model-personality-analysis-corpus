## Grounded reading
The voice is lush and personifying, turning small urban remnants into carriers of loss: the cigarette becomes a “lost promise,” while the broom seems to guard a forgotten corner. Its pathos is subdued and elegiac, but the closing emphasis on survival keeps the scene from settling into pure desolation. The reader is invited to linger over the city’s overlooked details and imagine the lives and feelings they hold.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds a nocturnal city through streetlamp, beer glass, cigarette, broom, and distant music. It connects urban decay with resilient life, and gives the scene a mood of suspended longing that opens, at the end, toward dawn and survival.
