## Grounded reading
The voice is weary and self-questioning, moving among the cafe, the city, and the speaker’s own stalled ambitions. Repeated images of ghosts, echoes, mirrors, and anchors give the loss of creative drive an atmosphere of haunting and entrapment. The emotional turn is modest rather than triumphant: the speaker cannot name what comes next, but still feels “quiet yearning.” The direct questions invite the reader into that uncertainty, especially the question about whether one ever stops writing.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds urban alienation, artistic frustration, social-media approval, and the gap between ambition and lived experience. The cafe and its stale coffee and fireplace set a scene where former student energy has given way to absence; screens, likes, and paychecks become measures of worth. The city is both a trap and an object of fascination, while the ending preserves a small, unresolved impulse toward creative renewal.
