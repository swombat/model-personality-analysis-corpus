## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A lyrical first-person meditation turns a researcher’s uncertainty into a reflection on the comfort and limits of seeking truth.

## Grounded reading
The voice is earnest and atmospheric, leaning on sensory and musical imagery: ticking clocks, dusty books, whispers, symphonies. Its pathos comes from the loneliness of pursuing answers that may not exist, then finding some consolation in the shared, imperfect work of searching. The ending invites the reader to value that search—and the stories and perspectives gathered along the way—rather than demand one final truth.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds the library as refuge, research as a struggle with doubt, and knowledge as provisional rather than absolute. It moves from scientific certainty and solitary obsession toward a plural, collaborative view of truth, with books and stories serving as the bridge. The chosen mood is wistful and consoling, though the repeated metaphors and abstract conclusions sometimes make the prose feel overworked.

## Evidence line
> The library was my haven, a symphony of whispers and forgotten histories.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
