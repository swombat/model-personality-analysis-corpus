## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A first-person meditation moves associatively from the cosmos and nature through humanity and abstract questions, then returns to the act of writing.

## Grounded reading
The voice is earnest and expansive, inviting the reader to share in wonder about large subjects before settling into the small sensations of a desk and keyboard. Its pathos comes less from personal conflict than from the aspiration to find meaning and connection in creation. The repeated movement from vastness back to the present gives the piece a gentle, reassuring resolution.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds cosmic mystery, ecological beauty, humanity’s capacity for kindness and harm, and questions about time, truth, and consciousness. It also makes writing itself a theme: a way to explore the self and glimpse “the infinite within the finite.” The choices favor wonder and harmony over concrete events or sharper tensions.

## Evidence line
> I realize that writing is not just an act of expression but also an act of discovery, a journey into the depths of my own consciousness.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Low — The piece is coherent and recurrently oriented toward wonder and meaning, but its broad images and familiar phrasing make that orientation only weakly distinctive.
