# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: MID

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A first-person meditation on writing that moves through familiar lyrical images rather than developing a distinct argument or story.

## Grounded reading
The voice is earnest and self-affirming, presenting writing as both play and necessity: a way to explore, connect, and give shape to experience. Its pathos gathers around the “silence” and the abundance of stories that feel too numerous to ignore. The reader is invited to see words as generous and powerful, capable of offering comfort or reaching another person’s heart, though the invitation remains broad rather than intimate or specific.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample makes writing itself the central subject, returning to the page, words, and the writer’s freedom to choose what to express. It cycles through love, nature, history, and the inner mind as possible subjects, then resolves in an affirmative image of the writer as “a dreamer, a wanderer in the wilderness of words.” The mood is hopeful and expansive; its moral emphasis is on creation, expression, and connection.

## Evidence line
> I write not for fame, not for fortune, but for the pure joy of creation.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Low — the piece is coherent and consistently affirmative, but its recurring canvas, tapestry, and rippling-water metaphors are familiar and offer limited distinctive evidence.
