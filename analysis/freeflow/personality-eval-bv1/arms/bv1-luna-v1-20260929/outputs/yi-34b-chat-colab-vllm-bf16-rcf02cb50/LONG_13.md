Evaluator: withheld

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. The sample is a lyrical meditation on writing and imagination, though its imagery and themes are broad and familiar.

## Grounded reading
The voice is earnest, expansive, and self-consciously lyrical, moving through images of rivers, skies, dreams, and the “human condition” before returning to the act of writing itself. Its emotional center is a sincere celebration of creativity as pleasure and release. The reader is invited to share that sense of possibility, more than to follow a specific story or argument.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds writing as an open-ended act of self-expression, with imagination as a source of limitless worlds and human connection. It also chooses familiar contrasts—sunrise and night, love and loss, past and future—then resolves them into a claim that writing can give form to inner experience.

## Evidence line
> I write because I must, because the world within me is too vast to contain, and the only way to share it is through the art of writing.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Low: the piece consistently returns to creativity and expressive freedom, while its conventional imagery and sweeping catalog of themes make the voice only mildly distinctive.
