## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. It is a first-person meditation on writing and observation, shaped as a daylong drift from a quiet room into the city and back.

## Grounded reading
The voice is earnest and gently elevated, turning ordinary sights—the blinking cursor, a book left unopened, city passersby, the sunset—into invitations to notice stories. Its pathos comes from wanting to give anonymous lives a voice and from treating love, loss, and hope as shared human forces. The reader is invited to see writing as a way of making fleeting moments meaningful, though the imagery and emotional turns remain familiar rather than sharply individual.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds writing as discovery, close observation of everyday surroundings, and the imagined inner lives of strangers. It moves from urban bustle to evening quiet, then names love, loss, and hope as subjects worth writing about; its closing emphasis is on an apparently inexhaustible supply of stories.

## Evidence line
