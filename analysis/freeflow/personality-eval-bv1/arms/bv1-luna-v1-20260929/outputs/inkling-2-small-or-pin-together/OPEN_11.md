## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical, self-reflective meditation that turns the open prompt into a discussion of attention, constraints, and what it means to write freely.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm and composed, with a fondness for metaphor and carefully balanced contrasts: gentleness without softness, freedom with limits, curiosity rather than certainty. Its pathos rests less on personal confession than on an appeal to notice and care for ordinary things—the refilled soap, the returned library book, the friend who has been “fine” too long. It invites the reader to slow down, stay curious, and treat attention as a form of welcome. The self-description as a tool, and the rejection of a “secret kingdom of preferences,” keep the piece from claiming an inner life untouched by its conditions.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds constraint as enabling creativity, attention as hospitality, and maintenance as a quiet form of devotion. It pairs gentleness with truth-telling and ends with practical imperatives: notice more, be brave about small things, and leave rooms better than you found them. These choices favor care and curiosity over spectacle or certainty.

## Evidence line
> What feels worth saying is this: attention is a kind of hospitality.
