## Sample kind
GENERIC_ESSAY — A polished, thesis-driven essay that argues for attentive living through sensory examples and civic reflection, with a coherent but familiar public-intellectual voice.

## Grounded reading
The voice is lyrical and gently admonishing, moving between close sensory observation and broad claims about technology, democracy, and self-respect. Its pathos lies in the fear that a life can pass as a sequence of errands and prompts, leaving its ordinary beauties unregistered. The reader is invited to pause over one real detail—not to achieve purity or productivity, but to become more present and more capable of tenderness.

## What the model chose to foreground
The essay foregrounds attention as both a private practice and a civic virtue, set against platforms that profit from distraction and a culture that demands instant reaction. It repeatedly returns to ordinary details—the chipped cup, rosemary by the curb, a beetle, a bus, a repaired bicycle—as evidence that the world’s value does not depend on usefulness or commentary. It also emphasizes limits: attention alone does not make virtue, and no one can care actively about everything.
