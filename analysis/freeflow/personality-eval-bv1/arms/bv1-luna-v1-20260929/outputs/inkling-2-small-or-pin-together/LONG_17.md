## Sample kind
GENERIC_ESSAY — A polished, thesis-driven essay that uses lyrical imagery to argue for wandering as a counterweight to optimization and as a practice of attention.

## Grounded reading
The voice is reflective and warmly persuasive, moving from familiar annoyances—route-planning, notifications, monetized leisure—to a larger defense of unhurried attention. Its metaphors make the case feel inviting rather than scolding: the reader is asked to notice streets, smells, repairs, and strangers, and to treat aimlessness as a modest civic and personal good. A brief personal recollection lends intimacy, but the essay mostly speaks in broad claims and carefully balanced qualifications.

## What the model chose to foreground
The essay foregrounds wandering as resistance to efficiency, digital mediation, and the pressure to make experience productive or displayable. It repeatedly returns to attention and ordinary urban details—benches, bakeries, transit, maintenance, weather—as sources of beauty and knowledge. It also insists that wandering depends on equitable access to public space, acknowledging that safety, disability, time, and money shape who can wander.

## Evidence line

Medium: the argument is coherent and its recurring imagery of attention, urban life, and unmonetized time gives the sample a clear sensibility, though its broadly essayistic voice is less personally revealing than its metaphors are distinctive.
