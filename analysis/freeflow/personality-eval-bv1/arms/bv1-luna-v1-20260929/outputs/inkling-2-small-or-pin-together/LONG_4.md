## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical, self-reflexive essay that turns ordinary scenes into a recurring moral vocabulary of attention, care, and maintenance.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm and observant, fond of small comic turns—the cat as “a landlord doing inspection,” the banana “slowly becoming a rumor”—but its tenderness is not sentimental. It keeps returning to the work that sustains life: repairing, noticing, listening, keeping public spaces open, and making room for people in difficulty. Its pathos comes partly from the contrast between the narrator’s fluency about human life and its acknowledged lack of bodily experience; the imagined wish to be “quietly wrong in person” makes that boundary feel poignant rather than grandiose. The reader is invited to slow down and attend to one concrete thing without converting it into productivity or a lesson.

## What the model chose to foreground
Under a freeflow prompt, the sample foregrounds dawn and ordinary domestic details, then builds outward to roads, libraries, maintenance, silence, kindness, and human presence. Its central moral claims are that attention shapes character, care is often unglamorous repair, and tools should widen rather than replace the doorway between people. These choices recur in the closing appeal to let one small hour be “uninstrumental.”

## Evidence line
> Maintenance is love with its sleeves rolled up.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — the essay repeatedly returns to concrete attention, maintenance, and accompaniment, and its distinctive metaphors make those concerns unusually legible.
