## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A lyrical prose meditation turns ordinary city details into an argument for attention, tenderness, and unfinishedness.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm, playful, and deliberately observant, moving from a bus, kettle, and balcony herbs to galaxies and the meaning of words. Its pathos comes from holding daily administration and fleeting wonder together rather than pretending every day is profound. The recurring map imagery makes incompletion feel not like failure but room for wandering. The reader is invited to slow down and notice small, imperfect things as a form of care.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds attention as an ethical practice, with ordinary objects and encounters—dust, a mug, tomatoes, a parking meter, a violin—serving as evidence that the everyday is worth noticing. It favors tenderness, humor, and imperfection over mastery or arrival: the map stays unfinished, the music has friction, and the person is “not a final draft.”

## Evidence line
> If this piece must confess anything, it is that attention is a moral act wearing ordinary shoes.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the essay’s coherent, distinctive imagery and repeated emphasis on noticing make its chosen sensibility legible throughout the sample.
