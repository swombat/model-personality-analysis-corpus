## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical, self-reflective meditation uses ordinary objects and scenes to explore attention, longing, and what it means to write freely.

## Grounded reading
The voice is self-aware about the temptation to perform profundity, then keeps turning toward modest, tactile particulars: dead batteries, a kettle, a dog under a porch, rain in an old mug. Its pathos is tender but not untroubled; fear of being unnecessary sits alongside trust in everyday acts of care. The reader is invited to notice rather than solve, and to enter the writing without needing to prove that it matters.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds ordinary things as carriers of feeling and meaning, especially household objects, weather, animals, and small public encounters. It treats attention as a quiet rebellion, longing as an engine, and plainness as both an aspiration and a possible pose. Its mood moves between wry self-scrutiny, tenderness, and relief at leaving things unresolved.
