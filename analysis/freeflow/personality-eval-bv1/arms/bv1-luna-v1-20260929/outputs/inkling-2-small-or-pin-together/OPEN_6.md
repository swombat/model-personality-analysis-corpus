## Grounded reading

Its pathos lies in the contrast between an inner life we cannot fully know and the small signs through which we try to approach it. It also chooses to address its own status directly: it describes itself as having “pattern and language,” without claiming that this amounts to experience. The recurring moral preference is for the particular over the generic, presented as a route toward genuine feeling and understanding.
