## Sample kind
GENERIC_ESSAY — A polished, thesis-driven meditation that develops a coherent public argument without a strongly personal or idiosyncratic voice.

## Grounded reading
The voice is reflective and assured, using accessible metaphors to make an abstract idea feel tangible. Its pathos comes through in scenes of quiet attention—watching rain, listening to grief, staying with what cannot be fixed—and in concern for what distraction causes people and societies to overlook. It invites the reader to slow down, remain curious, and notice without demanding that every act of attention be useful or solvable.

## What the model chose to foreground
The essay foregrounds attention as both a way of perceiving and a moral practice. It connects curiosity and language to the ability to notice others more fully, then extends that concern to commercial distraction, civic neglect, helpless witnessing, and tenderness toward life’s limits. The recurring contrast is between lingering, receptive attention and fast, engineered selection.

## Evidence line
> The mature version of attention includes humility about limits and tenderness toward what exceeds fixing: grief, aging, unfairness, mortality.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: attention, curiosity, and resistance to engineered distraction recur throughout the essay, though the treatment remains polished and broadly familiar.
