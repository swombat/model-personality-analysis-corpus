Evaluator: withheld

Condition: MID

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. It is a lyrical, thesis-driven reflection whose repeated images and turns of phrase make it more stylistically distinctive than a generic essay.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gently persuasive and observant, making a case for attention without scolding readers for distraction. Ordinary details—the kettle, bus seats, bread, a friend mid-sentence—carry the essay’s pathos: noticing them offers not escape from trouble, but a wider frame and a more inhabited life. The reader is invited to try small rituals rather than pursue purity or dramatic renunciation.

## What the model chose to foreground
The essay foregrounds attention as both a way of experiencing time and a moral offering to other people. It contrasts depth with stimulation and agency with device-driven habit, then links noticing to listening, gratitude, art, and consolation. Its chosen objects are deliberately commonplace: sunlight on a table, trees, tea, bread, and the kettle.

## Evidence line
> The modern world is arranged to loot our eyes and leave us insisting we enjoyed the robbery.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: attention recurs across the essay as its central value, expressed through distinctive everyday imagery and a consistent refusal of purity-based advice.
