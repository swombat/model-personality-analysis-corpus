## Grounded reading
The voice is calm and encouraging, with an intimacy suggested by “a small piece I've been wanting to write” and the conversational “Anyway.” It offers reassurance without pretending that uncertainty is easy: drafts, gardens, friendships, and careers remain open, and that openness can be lived with rather than treated as failure. ## What the model chose to foreground

Its moral emphasis is patience and kindness toward unfinished work, including the “metaphorical” drafts of becoming.

## Evidence line
