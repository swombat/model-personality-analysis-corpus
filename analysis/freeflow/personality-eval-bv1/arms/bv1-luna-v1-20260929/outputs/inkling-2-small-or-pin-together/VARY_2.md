## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A sustained lyrical meditation uses the tide as a metaphor for uncertainty, return, and the limits of what the speaker can offer.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm, self-aware, and deliberately modest: it offers vivid metaphors while repeatedly distinguishing fluent description from lived experience or authority. Its pathos comes from that tension—wanting to be useful as a “place” for difficult questions while admitting it cannot feel the salt or make another person’s days meaningful. The invitation to the reader is not to seek certainty from the speaker, but to use doubt and attention to act more honestly.

## What the model chose to foreground
It foregrounds tides, shorelines, lamps, gardens, and other images of holding, return, and care. Its moral emphasis is on uncertainty as useful rather than shameful, on human responsibility for meaning, and on not confusing eloquence with finality. The recurring movement between offered comfort and explicit limits makes the speaker’s role-boundary part of the piece’s central theme.

## Evidence line
> I can describe the garden brilliantly, probably name the weeds, quote three poets, and offer a watering schedule.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the coherent, recurring tide metaphor and repeated distinction between helpful language and lived authority make this a distinctive expression of humility and reader-directed care.
