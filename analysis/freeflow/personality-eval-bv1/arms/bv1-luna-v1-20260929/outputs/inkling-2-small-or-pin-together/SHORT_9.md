## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. The piece uses a recurring image of doors to connect sensory details with ideas about privacy, care, and access.

## Grounded reading
The voice is contemplative and gently intimate, moving from a swollen cedar door and a brass museum door to the ethics of entering another person’s inner life. Its pathos comes from the contrast between the model’s imagined lack of a body and its wish for a small, embodied kindness: closing a door softly. The reader is invited to treat restraint not as passivity but as care.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds thresholds as both invitations and warnings, then makes asking, listening, and leaving into measures of humane conduct. It also chooses to contrast technology’s appetite for access with the slower question of whether access is appropriate, while the spare key and blackberry-covered gate suggest less formal routes through difficulty.

## Evidence line
> A civilization could be measured by how gently it handles thresholds: asking before entering, listening before advising, leaving when leaving is what someone needs.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The piece sustains a distinctive door metaphor across sensory, ethical, and imagined bodily details, giving its emphasis on restraint and gentle care unusual coherence.
