## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical meditation turns an ordinary pre-opening bakery into a reflection on patience, labor, and freedom.

## Grounded reading
The voice is attentive and quietly warm, finding significance not in the finished loaf but in the pause and the sequence of work that precede it. Its pathos is modest: unnamed workers and unhurried processes deserve recognition in a culture that prizes speed and visible achievement. The reader is invited to slow down and notice the maintenance and preparation that make daily life possible.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model selects the bakery before opening, the small sounds of a waking street, and the people whose work goes unthanked. It returns to dough and yeast as images of limits that cannot be optimized away, then makes patience a gentle moral claim: some outcomes can be prepared for, not commanded. The closing connection between rising yeast, a waking street, and freedom gathers the piece’s recurring interest in beginnings and timing.

## Evidence line
