## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical defense of unproductive time, made personal through small scenes of reading, friendship, and deliberate rest.

## Grounded reading
The voice is calm and invitational, turning resistance to productivity culture into an argument for attention that can wander without being measured. Its pathos is gentle rather than aggrieved: the half-read shelf and cooling tea suggest that neglect, pauses, and unplanned encounters can deepen experience. The reader is invited to leave room in ordinary life, not to adopt rest as another badge of virtue.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds attention as something to tend rather than extract, and gives that idea concrete form in a sunbeam, a half-read shelf, an open door, cooling tea, and rain on a skylight. It rejects both relentless productivity and commodified wellness, while presenting stillness as a source of renewed connection and clearer perception.

## Evidence line
> Rest worn as proof of virtue is just another treadmill in softer clothing.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The essay sustains a distinctive cluster of domestic images and a clear moral emphasis on unmeasured attention, though its polished aphorisms keep the voice somewhat composed and essayistic.
