## Grounded reading
The voice is conversational but carefully shaped, moving from Freud’s failed eel dissections to the long scientific pursuit of the eel’s spawning grounds. Its pathos comes from a recurring mismatch between what people can observe and what they want to know: the eel is familiar as food, yet its adult life remains out of reach. The piece invites the reader to reconsider ignorance not as simple neglect, but as something built into the animal’s life cycle—and to feel the melancholy of a creature whose decisive journey ends in death.
