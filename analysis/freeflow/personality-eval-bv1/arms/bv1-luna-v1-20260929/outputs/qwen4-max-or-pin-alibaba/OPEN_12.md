## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A polished essay whose distinctive, intimate voice turns word histories into a meditation on inherited ways of thinking.

## Grounded reading
The voice is curious and self-questioning, delighted by etymological oddities but careful not to mistake origins for present meaning. Its pathos emerges in the sense that language carries old worldviews we never chose, while the closing admission makes that inheritance personal: the speaker presents itself as shaped by the very “fossil record” it is examining. The reader is invited to notice the hidden assumptions in ordinary words without imagining that noticing them offers easy escape.

## What the model chose to foreground
The essay foregrounds words as durable containers for discarded cosmologies: stars, bodily fluids, spirits, weather, and money remain embedded in everyday speech. It also highlights the difference between true and invented etymologies, treating false origins as evidence of what people wanted language to say. The moral emphasis is on intellectual humility: origins can illuminate the “instrument” of thought, but they do not settle what words mean now.

## Evidence line

Medium: the essay repeatedly joins concrete verbal curiosities to a broader, self-reflexive argument, and its closing attention to the speaker’s own language gives that pattern unusual definition.
