## Grounded reading

Its pathos gathers around a particular absence—the speaker’s detailed knowledge of smell without a sensory referent—and around the human loss of smell as a loss of connection to the world. The essay invites the reader to treat ordinary perception as something both biological and culturally shaped, and to notice how words can bridge gaps without fully closing them.
