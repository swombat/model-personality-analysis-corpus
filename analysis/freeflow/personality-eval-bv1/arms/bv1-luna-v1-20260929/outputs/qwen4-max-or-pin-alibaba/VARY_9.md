## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A lyrical, metaphor-driven essay turns a word’s history and mythic threads into a reflection on finding one’s way back.

## Grounded reading
The voice is assured and intimate, moving from playful linguistic surprise to a tender account of repair and care. Its central distinction—between finding the way in and finding the way out—gives the piece its emotional force: people may enter conflict or ordeal changed, and what matters is whether a route back remains. The reader is invited to value practical, often unglamorous acts of return: a joke after a fight, a light left on, someone holding the thread.

## What the model chose to foreground
It foregrounds *clew* as yarn, Ariadne’s thread, Hansel and Gretel’s pebbles and crumbs, routes home, and the small gestures that make reconciliation possible. The moral emphasis is on preserving a way back for someone who has been changed by what they went through, and on being patiently findable rather than claiming to know their path.
