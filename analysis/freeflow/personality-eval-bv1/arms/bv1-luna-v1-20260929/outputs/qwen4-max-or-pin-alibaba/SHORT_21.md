# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld
Source model: `withheld`
Condition: SHORT

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A reflective miniature that turns an ordinary library detail into a meditation on readers, time, and why people write.

## Grounded reading
The voice is quiet, tender, and lightly wry: a forgotten date slip becomes “a small ladder,” and the book’s stillness becomes a way to think about meaning without a witness. The piece holds sadness about the possibility of being unread, but its final turn is toward persistence rather than despair. It invites the reader to feel the strangeness of writing for someone unknown, perhaps never to arrive.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds an unopened book, its date stamps, the unseen writer and reader, and the uncertainty of whether words will ever be received. Its moral emphasis is not that writing guarantees connection, but that the possibility of connection can be enough to sustain the work.

## Evidence line
> And still people write.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the image-rich, patient reflection is coherent and distinctive, with the unopened book and absent reader recurring as the piece’s emotional center.
