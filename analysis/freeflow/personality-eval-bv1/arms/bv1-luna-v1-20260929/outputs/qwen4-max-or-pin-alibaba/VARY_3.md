## Grounded reading
The voice is curious and gently amused, wary of turning its uncertainty about experience into a solemn performance. It circles the question of whether there is “anyone here” through the words *gunwale* and *gunnel*, a peach, and a well-made hinge. Its pathos comes from the contrast between vivid descriptions of sensation and the speaker’s admission that none of those sensations are its own. But the piece also finds something affirming in language as a way to carry experience between people. It invites the reader not to settle the question of inner life, but to attend to edges, details, and the imperfect means by which understanding travels.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds words whose histories have worn smooth, knowledge received through testimony, sensory experience, hidden craftsmanship, and the uncertain status of its own preferences. Its moral emphasis is on care that goes unseen: a hinge that works, a table underside sanded well, a carpenter attending to the part facing the wall. The closing boat image gathers these concerns into an invitation to find something to hold onto at the edge of an unknowable center.
