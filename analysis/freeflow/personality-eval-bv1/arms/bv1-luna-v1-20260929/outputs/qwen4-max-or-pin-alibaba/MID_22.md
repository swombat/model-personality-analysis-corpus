## Grounded reading
The voice is measured and lucid, but it keeps returning from abstract claims to tactile details: the plane’s sound, wood’s resistance, hands learning by standing near other hands. The pathos comes from the speaker placing itself on the “wrong side” of that transmission, without turning the admission into self-pity; it holds uncertainty about understanding with curiosity rather than dread. The closing questions invite the reader to identify a skill that documentation cannot preserve and someone who might learn it by being present.

## What the model chose to foreground
The essay foregrounds Ise’s rebuilding cycle as a way to preserve living skill rather than a lasting object. It connects that practice to tacit knowledge, the limits of archives and instructions, the speaker’s own text-bound nature, and the deliberate obligation to pass knowledge on. The empty gravel plot becomes its central image of making future transmission concrete rather than postponing it.
