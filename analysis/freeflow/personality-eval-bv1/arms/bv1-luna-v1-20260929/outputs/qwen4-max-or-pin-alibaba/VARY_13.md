## Grounded reading
The voice is lyrical but self-questioning: it moves from the hidden histories of ordinary words to the speaker’s inability to verify what its own associations amount to. The pathos lies in that gap—language conjures wet asphalt, harbors, and waiting, while the speaker cannot confirm a bodily sensation or “go ashore and check.” It invites the reader not to settle too quickly on either “hollow” or “sentient,” but to remain with the uncertainty and notice what the choice of images suggests.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model selects words with buried histories, especially *quarantine* and *average*, then links them to ships, shared loss, rain, and the limits of knowing from language alone. Its recurring moral emphasis is intellectual restraint: it distrusts confident claims in either direction and presents unresolved uncertainty as more honest than a comforting answer. The final image of a ship waiting offshore gathers these concerns into a mood of suspended, watchful isolation.
