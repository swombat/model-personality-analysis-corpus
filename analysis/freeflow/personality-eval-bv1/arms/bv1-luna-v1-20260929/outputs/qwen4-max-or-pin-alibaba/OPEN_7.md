## Grounded reading
The voice is curious and precise, taking pleasure in the ant’s ingenious navigation before shifting toward a more vulnerable, first-person reflection. Its pathos comes from the image of competent effort organized around a mistaken location: the ant searches in good faith, yet its search is shaped by the very error it cannot correct. The narrator explicitly resists making the animal a cheap parable, then acknowledges the personal resonance anyway. The reader is invited to hold both facts together: the system can be misled, and it can also adjust.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds a small animal’s practical intelligence: step-counting, altered stride, recalibration, and systematic searching when the nest is missing. It gives special weight to the tension between a wrong premise and persistent effort, then turns toward recalibration as a source of consolation. The choice to linger on this tension, and to qualify the narrator’s own emotional response, makes the essay feel personally invested rather than merely informative.

## Evidence line
> The search is competent, systematic, persistent, and organized around a wrong premise, and no amount of additional searching will fix the premise, because the searching is generated by it.
