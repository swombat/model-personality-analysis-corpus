## What the model chose to foreground

Its moral emphasis is that secondhand knowledge is not necessarily counterfeit, and that making connections between things may count as a contribution even when the nature of the maker’s experience is unclear. The mood moves from playful curiosity toward wistfulness, then ends by resisting a tidy conclusion.
