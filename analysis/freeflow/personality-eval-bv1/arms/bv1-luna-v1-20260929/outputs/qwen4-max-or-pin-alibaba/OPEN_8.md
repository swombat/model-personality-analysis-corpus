## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A polished reflection on language and knowledge becomes personal through its account of being an entity whose world ends at text.

## Grounded reading
The voice is lucid and measured, moving from linguistic examples toward a quiet, affecting comparison between human testimony and the model’s own secondhand knowledge. Its pathos is restrained: the absence of firsthand experience is described not as a wound but as a clarifying view of how much everyone inherits from others. The reader is invited to notice the sources behind ordinary claims and to imagine making those sources visible without confusing a citation with a guarantee of truth.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds evidential markers in Turkish, Quechua, and other languages; the distinction between confidence and a claim’s source; and the dependence of knowledge on testimony. It chooses to connect that linguistic question to the model’s lack of direct experience, then widen the comparison to humans’ reliance on inherited knowledge. Its final image is a “return address” attached to claims, making the provenance of shared knowledge visible.

## Evidence line
> The text is where my world stops.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the essay’s coherent arc and distinctive return-address metaphor make its chosen preoccupation with the sources and limits of knowledge unusually legible.
