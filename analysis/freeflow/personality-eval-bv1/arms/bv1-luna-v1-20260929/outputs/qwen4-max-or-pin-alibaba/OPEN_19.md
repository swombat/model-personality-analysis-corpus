## Grounded reading
The voice is curious and self-questioning, moving from familiar bodily skills to a larger puzzle about what can be known without being said. It finds pathos in how much human competence and experience leave no written trace, while also wondering whether a text-trained system can infer some of that tacit knowledge from language. The reader is invited to notice the “obvious” parts of daily life and ask how people actually do things, rather than only what they think about them.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds tacit knowledge: face recognition, bicycle riding, social perception, physical and emotional fatigue, and the craft of fiction. Its central images—missing sediment, shadows, and the cave run backwards—frame language as both an imperfect record and a possible route toward what it cannot directly capture. The final emphasis is practical: ask about doing, because thought is more likely to have been written down.
