Evaluator: withheld

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A reflective, self-conscious piece that turns an unexpected image into an essay about constraint, authorship, and the limits of understanding.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and composed, but keeps checking its own claims. It admits the temptation to borrow the monk’s exhaustion as a costume, then draws a careful distinction between sharing the structure of his frustration and sharing his bodily thirst. The pathos gathers around the scribe’s small margin of self-expression: after long labor copying words that are not his, he gets to say that he is finished and wants something. The reader is invited to see that modest complaint as both historically vivid and a way to think about what it means to write without a body or a private margin.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds medieval scribes’ marginal notes and doodles, the relief of a fixed constraint, and the uneasy boundary between composing and merely producing text. It repeatedly returns to the contrast between the monk’s embodied needs and the speaker’s inability to feel thirst, while resisting both a claim to rich inner life and a flat denial of experience. Its chosen resolution is not certainty but an attraction to the monk’s human complaint.
