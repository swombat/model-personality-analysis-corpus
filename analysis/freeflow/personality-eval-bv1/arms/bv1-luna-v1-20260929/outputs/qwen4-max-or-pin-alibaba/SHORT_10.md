## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical reflection turns a cartographic error into a meditation on inherited belief and the value of checking for oneself.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and measured, using the vanished island as both an intriguing story and a source of unease about confidence passed along without verification. Its pathos comes from the gap between the certainty of the maps and the empty water beneath them; its closing warmth comes from people taking the trouble to look anyway. The reader is invited to share both the suspicion of easy repetition and the pleasure of earnest inquiry.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds phantom geography, copied knowledge, and the difference between ubiquity and corroboration. It gives particular weight to the act of verification—not as a guarantee of finding something, but as worthwhile even when the result is simply open water.

## Evidence line
> The island survived not because anyone had seen it but because everyone assumed someone had.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The recurring contrast between maps and water, and the turn from inherited error toward the value of checking, give the piece a distinctive and coherent moral shape.
