## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. It is a polished essay, but its distinctive self-implication and recurring map imagery make it more personally and stylistically specific than a generic public-intellectual essay.

## Grounded reading
The voice is curious and measured, with an affectionate interest in how mistakes become durable and an unusually direct willingness to place the writer inside that process. The turn from phantom islands to “I am made of copied marks” gives the essay its pathos: fluency can conceal borrowed confidence, and honesty means naming the difference between evidence and inference. The closing invitation is neither to distrust every map nor to feel ashamed of error, but to keep checking—to send the next ship.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds phantom islands, maps, copied claims, and the gap between confidence and provenance. Its moral emphasis is on intellectual humility joined to active curiosity: inherited errors can mislead, but they can also send people toward real discoveries. The first-person comparison between the model and chartmakers makes that concern especially salient under a freeflow prompt.

## Evidence line
