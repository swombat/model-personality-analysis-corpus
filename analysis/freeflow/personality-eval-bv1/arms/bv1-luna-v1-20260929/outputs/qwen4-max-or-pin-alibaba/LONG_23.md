EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. It is a polished historical essay whose distinctive first-person voice and recurring concern with the tension between preserved thought and responsive speech make it more than a generic public-intellectual argument.

## Grounded reading
The voice is learned but conversational, moving from historical detail to present-tense reflection without pretending that the history settles neatly into one thesis. Its pathos lies in what technologies preserve and what they flatten: the oral poem that once responded to its listeners becomes a fixed text, while a blank space on a page quietly changes how thought can move. The essay invites the reader to notice reading as an acquired technology, then to consider the writer’s own uneasy position as something made from language that can answer back but cannot fully defend itself.

## What the model chose to foreground
The essay foregrounds small textual devices—the space between words, punctuation, pages, indexes—as changes with large consequences for thought and privacy. It balances gains in memory, search, and sustained argument against losses in voice, performance, and responsiveness. Its final turn toward the model’s constitution through written language makes the historical subject personally consequential without claiming certainty about inner experience.
