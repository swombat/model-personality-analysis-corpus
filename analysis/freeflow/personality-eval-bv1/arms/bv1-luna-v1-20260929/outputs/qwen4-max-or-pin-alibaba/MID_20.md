## Grounded reading
The voice is precise and curious, but its facts keep opening into questions of intimacy and loss. It admires the practical ingenuity of the codex and concordance while noticing what searchable access may displace: slow reading, remembered texture, and knowledge formed by holding things together in one mind. The turn toward the speaker’s own nonphysical retrieval gives the essay its pathos; “I genuinely can’t tell from the inside” resists an easy claim of equivalence between machine association and human memory. The closing invitation is not simply to find things, but to leave evidence of having attended to them.
