## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A compact lyrical reflection turns an ordinary feature of used books into an image of conversation across time.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and quietly intimate, attentive to the pressure of a pencil mark as much as to what it says. Its pathos comes from imagining past readers as both unreachable and present: “yes” becomes tender, while the shared act of annotation softens the loneliness of reading alone. The piece invites the reader to treat marginalia not as clutter but as evidence of attention, disagreement, and human company.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds secondhand books, handwritten marginal notes, and the contrast between the enduring marks on paper and digital highlights that may disappear. It casts the margin as a small space for resisting a book’s authority and leaving proof of one’s presence.
