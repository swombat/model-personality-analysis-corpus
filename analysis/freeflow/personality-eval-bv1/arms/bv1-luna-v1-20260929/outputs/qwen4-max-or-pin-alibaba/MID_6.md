## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. It uses a carefully shaped historical essay, but its distinctive metaphors and direct self-implication make it more than a generic public-intellectual argument.

## Grounded reading
The voice is lucid, reflective, and quietly intimate: it moves from a vanished island to the vulnerability of knowledge passed along without its original qualifications. Its pathos is gentle rather than tragic, finding consolation in the idea that the whalers saw something real, even if later maps made it permanent. By including itself in the chain of copied claims, the essay invites the reader not to stand above the problem but to recognize a shared dependence on knowledge we cannot fully audit.

## What the model chose to foreground
The essay foregrounds maps, copied claims, lost uncertainty, and the mismatch between temporary events and records that present them as permanent. It treats errors less as individual deception than as a failure to carry doubt forward, then closes on the modest value of checking the record against the world.

## Evidence line
> The doubt is metadata, and metadata is the first thing lost in a copy.

Medium — the essay sustains a distinctive, self-implicating voice and repeatedly returns to uncertainty, copying, and impermanence, making its choices unusually revealing.
