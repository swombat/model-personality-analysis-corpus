## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical meditation turns an ordinary worn staircase into an image of memory made collectively and without intention.

## Grounded reading
The voice is reflective and quietly intimate, especially in “I find I keep turning this over” and “What I like.” Its pathos comes from the contrast between grand monuments and the countless ordinary errands that leave a mark without meaning to. The ending offers the reader a gentle reframe: loss can also be a form of keeping, though the stone itself has no memory or nostalgia.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds worn stone, passing feet, ordinary daily life, and accidental traces as an alternative to deliberate commemoration. It favors patient, aggregate change over heroic or explicitly remembered events, and finds a subdued beauty in what is preserved through giving something away.

## Evidence line
> The hollow is a record made entirely by accident, and it's more honest than most monuments, because a monument tells you what someone wanted remembered.
