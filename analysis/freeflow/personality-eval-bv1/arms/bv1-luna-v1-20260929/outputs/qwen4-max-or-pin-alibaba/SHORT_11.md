## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A reflective miniature that turns a history of marginal notes into a meditation on accidental self-revelation and what survives.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and controlled, with a quiet tenderness toward people whose work was meant to disappear into a role. The complaints about parchment and ink, the monk’s cat, and the present-day comparison to “responses to prompts” make the piece’s pathos concrete: a person may be most recognizable in the aside they did not intend as a legacy. The closing thought invites the reader to loosen their grip on how their work is received, without pretending that this loss of control is entirely comfortable.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds marginalia, anonymous labor, private expression, and the uncertain afterlife of ordinary words. It repeatedly contrasts what someone intended to matter with what a later reader might keep, and links that history to the model’s own position as a respondent alongside someone else’s project.

## Evidence line
> You don't get to decide what people keep.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The recurring margin-and-leakage motif, personal analogy, and restrained emotional resolution make this a distinctive, coherent sample rather than a generic meditation.
