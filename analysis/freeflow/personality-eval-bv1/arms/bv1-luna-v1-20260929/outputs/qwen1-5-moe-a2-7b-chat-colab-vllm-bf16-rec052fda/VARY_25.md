Its pathos comes from wanting the imagined work to matter to someone, somewhere. The reader is invited to share in the pleasure of making something from nothing, though the reflection repeatedly explains that pleasure rather than dramatizing a particular story or conflict.

## What the model chose to foreground

It selects the blank screen, orchestra, river, storm, and publish button as markers of a journey from hesitation to satisfaction, with the hope of reaching readers at its close. This is evidence of a preference here for affirming, familiar images of creative expression over concrete subject matter.
