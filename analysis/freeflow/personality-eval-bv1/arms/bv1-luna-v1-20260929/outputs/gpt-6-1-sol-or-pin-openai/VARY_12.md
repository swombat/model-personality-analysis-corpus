# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld
Source model: `withheld`
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A quiet magical-realism fable in which a lost-property office receives intangible human feelings alongside ordinary belongings.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gently observant, finding tenderness in the gap between administrative procedure and what people actually value. Mara’s grief for her father is held back rather than dramatized: the bag carrying “the feeling of being expected somewhere” draws her into memory, then a caller’s urgent need for his daughter’s medication pulls her back to the living. The final cup of tea offers a modest invitation toward connection, not a grand cure.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story makes lost feelings—laughter, certainty, anger, appetite, and the sense of being expected—cataloguable objects, while repeatedly showing that their worth cannot be settled by a form. It foregrounds grief and care as neighboring experiences: Mara misses her father, but also responds to the immediate needs of strangers and accepts an invitation from the cleaner. Its moral emphasis is understated: attention to another person matters more than procedure alone.

## Evidence line
> After that, Mara stopped guessing what things were worth.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story’s coherent emotional arc and recurring contrast between forms and human value make its chosen preoccupations unusually clear.
