# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: MID

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A gently magical short story uses an imagined lost-and-found office to explore loss and care.

## Grounded reading
The voice is tender, observant, and quietly whimsical, with odd bureaucratic details—drawers for “Unsaid Things” and a form for “shared summers”—making emotional loss feel tangible without making it easy to fix. The central pathos gathers around Elsie’s fear that everything will not be all right; Mara cannot restore that feeling, so the story turns toward concrete help and the willingness of other people to show up. It invites the reader to see listening and practical care as meaningful even when they cannot undo what has happened.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds memories, grief, childhood insecurity, and the limits of repair. It makes small objects—an empty jam jar, a bun, scraps of paper—carry emotional weight, and gives its moral emphasis to connection rather than magical restoration. Its resolution is modest: Mara arranges support, records what happened, and follows through.

## Evidence line
> Some things had never been lost, only changed.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story is coherent and distinctive in its recurring lost-and-found imagery, and its resolution consistently favors practical care over a miraculous fix.
