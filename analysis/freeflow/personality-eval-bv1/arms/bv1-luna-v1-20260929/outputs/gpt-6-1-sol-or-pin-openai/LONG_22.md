# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: LONG

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A quiet speculative story uses a hidden archive of ordinary Tuesdays to explore grief, memory, and the limits of preserving the past.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and gently wry, letting municipal paperwork and small practical details sit beside the impossible without much fanfare. Its grief is specific: Mara wants to recover her mother not as an emblem but in the unremarkable act of peeling potatoes, and the story makes that ordinary presence more affecting than any grand reunion. The ending invites the reader to loosen their grip on recovered moments and notice the present instead, through small acts like peeling an orange and filling a glass.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds unnoticed days, the objects and routines that carry memory, and the tension between cataloguing experience and letting it go. Its recurring objects—the envelopes, the glass of water, the orange peel—keep the uncanny tethered to everyday life. It also gives bureaucratic language a double role: a source of dry humor and a means for Mara to protect something that official categories cannot quite name.

## Evidence line
> There was no box for a mother peeling potatoes.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The coherent, distinctive use of mundane objects and restrained grief makes this a revealing expressive choice, especially as those concerns recur through the story’s imagery and resolution.
