# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A quiet allegorical story uses a lost-property office to make regret and memory tangible.

## Grounded reading
The voice is tender and restrained, giving emotional weight to small gestures rather than dramatic revelations: a coat taken off in a doorway, tea made for someone with no claim, bread carried to a brother’s house. Its sadness is rooted in ordinary missed chances, but the story invites the reader toward repair rather than self-punishment. Mara’s work of returning lost things gradually becomes permission to attend to her own relationships.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds lost time, remembered voices, family estrangement, and the difference between something gone and something genuinely lost. Envelopes, a grocery receipt, bread, tea, and the station’s passing trains make memory feel both concrete and fleeting. Its moral emphasis is modest: regret cannot undo the past, but it can prompt a visit, a call, or a promise kept.

## Evidence line
> The envelope grew slightly heavier in her hand.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story sustains a distinctive pattern of making emotional absence tangible through ordinary objects, then resolving regret through small acts of connection.
