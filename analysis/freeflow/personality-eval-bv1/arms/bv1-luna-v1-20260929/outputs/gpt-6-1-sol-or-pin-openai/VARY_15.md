# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A quietly magical short story uses a lost-time office to shape a plot about grief, memory, and belated connection.

## Grounded reading
The voice is tender and controlled, making its strange bureaucracy feel ordinary enough that the emotional turns land without fanfare. Its pathos gathers around moments people cannot recover: a missing mother, a father Mara postponed visiting, and the small everyday scenes preserved in the stones. The story invites the reader to treat memory not as a way to undo loss, but as a reason to reach toward someone who is still here. Mara’s final call gives that invitation a modest, practical resolution rather than a grand revelation.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds lost afternoons, forms and drawers, time made tangible as pears, birds, water, stones, and a cup. It attends to regret without making it punitive: the form’s question about whether time was lost “willingly” troubles people because it sounds like blame. Its moral emphasis is on the difference between keeping a memory and retrieving the dead, and on acting in the present when the past cannot be changed.

## Evidence line
> The cup warmed in her hand.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The story’s recurring objects, restrained grief, and turn toward ordinary human contact form a coherent, distinctive emotional pattern.
