## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A quiet, fable-like short story built around a fictional museum and the small things people nearly did or chose to keep.

## Grounded reading
The voice is tender and restrained, finding emotional weight in humble objects rather than explaining it at length. Its pathos gathers around unfinished gestures and the possibility of letting something go without erasing its significance. The reader is invited to linger over what might otherwise seem trivial, and to see returning something home as a meaningful kind of care.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds near-misses and suspended possibilities: the uncompleted letter, the unsaid words, the journey not taken, and the stone almost thrown away. It gives special attention to careful handling, temporary keeping, and the trace left after an object departs. The closing paperwork makes “Taken home” feel less like a loss than a quiet resolution.
