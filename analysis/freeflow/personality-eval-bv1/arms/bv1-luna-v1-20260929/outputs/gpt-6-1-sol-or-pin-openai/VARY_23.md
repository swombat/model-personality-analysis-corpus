## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A restrained literary story uses a bench facing a wall to connect an ordinary worker with an old man’s memories and unfinished grief.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and dryly tender, finding pathos in practical details: coffee sweet enough to hurt, repaired shoes, a wet empty bench. Daniel’s connection with Arthur grows through repeated visits and small gestures rather than explicit declarations. The story invites the reader to treat ordinary objects as places where private histories gather, while leaving some of that history unresolved.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds benches, walls, photographs, coffee, and drawn doors as modest objects that hold memory and invite interpretation. Its moral emphasis is gentle: attentive care can mean choosing not to erase something, or simply making room for another person’s story. The recurring door and the final exchange give the ending a quiet sense of possibility without turning the loss into a tidy lesson.
