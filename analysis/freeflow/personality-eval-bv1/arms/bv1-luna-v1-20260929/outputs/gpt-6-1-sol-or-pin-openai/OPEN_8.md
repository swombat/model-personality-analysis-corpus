## Grounded reading
The voice is wistful without becoming bleak: it lingers on ordinary near-misses—a letter never sent, a train missed by eight minutes, a gathering that simply fades from the calendar. Its tenderness comes from treating these small absences as worthy of attention. The final turn offers the reader a gentle invitation toward action, especially the courage to begin imperfectly, without promising that the choice will be easy or safe.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample centers on counterfactual lives and everyday moments that quietly acquire emotional weight. Its recurring objects—glass cases, a lit train, a set table, a paper bag—make uncertainty tangible, while the contrast between preserved past possibilities and things that “still might happen” gives the piece its hopeful moral emphasis.
