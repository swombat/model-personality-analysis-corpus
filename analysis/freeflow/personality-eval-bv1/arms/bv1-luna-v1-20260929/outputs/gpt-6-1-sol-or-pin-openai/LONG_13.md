# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

## Sample kind
GENERIC_ESSAY — A polished, thesis-driven essay develops a clear argument through familiar domestic and civic examples, with limited personal or stylistic idiosyncrasy.

## Grounded reading
The voice is calm, humane, and gently corrective: it asks readers to stop treating every unfinished thing as a failure and to distinguish what needs completion from what needs ongoing care—or release. Its pathos comes through ordinary objects and tasks rather than confession: the cold cup, the public bench, the patched sleeve, the difficult conversation. The reader is invited to make more room for repair, slack, attention, and revision without turning that invitation into a demand to preserve everything.

## What the model chose to foreground
The essay repeatedly foregrounds incompletion as a condition of shared life: gardens, friendships, cities, relationships, character, and the future all remain open to interpretation and change. It links that idea to public space, resilience, unstructured attention, repair, inheritance, and letting go. The recurring moral claim is that care requires room for work to continue, change direction, or end—not simply pressure to produce a clean finish.

## Evidence line
> The world is less like a worksheet than a kitchen.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the essay coherently returns to unfinishedness, repair, and making room for others, while its polished public-essay voice and familiar examples make the choices less distinctive.
