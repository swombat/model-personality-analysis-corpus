# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A quietly magical short story uses a fantastical archive to revisit grief through a small, intimate memory.

## Grounded reading
The voice is tender and restrained, letting grief surface through concrete details: damp grass, a missing shirt button, a spoon dropped on kitchen tiles. The story’s pathos comes from restoring the mother and father to an ordinary morning rather than trying to undo the loss. Its invitation is to make room for memories that are not defined entirely by what happened afterward.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds bereavement, the way later catastrophe can overwrite earlier memories, and the value of recovering an ordinary moment without changing its outcome. Jars of light, a blade of grass, and the father’s missing button give the magical premise a tactile, domestic scale; the ending turns remembrance into a small act of renewed participation.

## Evidence line
> For seventeen years she had remembered herself as a child waiting unknowingly for disaster.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story is coherent and distinctive in its recurring concern with grief and ordinary remembered details, though its emotional choices are expressed within a familiar magical-realist form.
