# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: OPEN

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A compact, gently symbolic story about unfinished work, grief, and the ways other people can help carry something forward.

## Grounded reading
The voice is quiet and tender, finding meaning in plain objects rather than explaining its themes outright. Its pathos gathers around the clock made by a dead father: the story lets the daughter ask for only his name, then gives her a shared task with the watch repairer. The invitation is to see incompletion not only as failure or loss, but as something that can be held, altered, or continued without pretending the original is restored.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story centers unfinished things—a one-sleeved sweater, an unsent letter, a repeatedly sanded boat, and a clock missing its numbers. It gives special weight to small acts of repair and to the difference between finishing an object and honoring the person who left it unfinished. Its closing image preserves the spare parts, suggesting that care does not require a perfectly resolved ending.

## Evidence line
> Without numbers, it was difficult to read, but people learned.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story has a coherent, distinctive pattern of treating incompletion with tenderness, and returns to it through several concrete objects.
