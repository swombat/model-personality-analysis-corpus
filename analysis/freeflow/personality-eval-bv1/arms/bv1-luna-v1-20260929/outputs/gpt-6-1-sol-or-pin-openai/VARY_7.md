# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION. A polished speculative short story uses an impossible archive to bring a daughter face to face with the choices and conversations she still has time to make.

## Grounded reading
The voice is quiet, precise, and gently uncanny: a missed train opens into a bureaucracy of unlived lives, where details like the knitting, the oranges, and the damp crescent on the counter keep the fantasy grounded in ordinary feeling. Its pathos comes from regret without easy absolution; Mara sees that other versions of her life might have offered comfort, but the ending turns toward the imperfect present rather than a repaired past. The story invites the reader to recognize how small exchanges can carry what people have not managed to say.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds missed chances, family grief, imagined alternatives, and the possibility of acting before time runs out. Its recurring objects—the orange, the photograph, the card—make memory tangible, while the department’s files suggest that not choosing can have consequences too. The moral emphasis is modest: seeing another possible life may help someone return to the one they have.

## Evidence line
> We keep the consequences of choices nobody made.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story’s distinctive archive metaphor, recurring objects, and consistent movement from regret toward a present-tense act make its chosen emotional concerns unusually clear.
