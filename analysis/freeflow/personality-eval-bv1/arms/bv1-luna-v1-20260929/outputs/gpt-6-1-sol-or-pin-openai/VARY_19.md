## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A quietly magical realist story that gives a missing day the weight of an ordinary lost object.

## Grounded reading
The voice is restrained and tender, letting an impossible premise unfold through practical details: a cardboard box, regulations, a ledger, a stiff office window. Its pathos comes from people recovering not grand events but the small, sensory traces that make a day theirs. Mara’s own memory of breaking her mother’s bowl brings the story’s public mystery down to personal grief. The reader is invited to treat remembrance as both intimate and shared: the Tuesday cannot be returned person by person, but people help bring it back together.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds lost time, the fragility of memory, bereavement, and ordinary moments that acquire meaning through recollection. Its chosen objects—the blue mitten, the fish-patterned tie, the broken bowl, and the ledger—keep the surreal premise rooted in everyday life. Its moral emphasis is quiet and communal: facing painful memories matters, and recovering them can be an act people undertake together.

## Evidence line
