# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: MID

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A quiet magical-realist story uses an office for misplaced memories and sounds to explore what people remember about those they have lost.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle, observant, and lightly whimsical, with its fantasy anchored in ordinary details: mismatched coat buttons, honey buns, a kettle that mutters in storms. The emotional center is not a miraculous recovery but the ache of imperfect memory—and the realization that Daniel’s father showed attention by stopping his reading whenever his son spoke. The story invites the reader to notice small acts of care that may have seemed irritating or unremarkable at the time.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds lost sounds, domestic routines, and the unevenness of memory: Daniel remembers his impatience more clearly than the sound he came to retrieve. It gives moral weight to listening and being interrupted, while allowing the object of grief to remain uncertain rather than neatly restored. Its recurring offices, envelopes, and careful filing turn loss into something handled with patience, not solved.

## Evidence line
> People often remembered their impatience better than whatever had provoked it.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story sustains a distinctive blend of whimsical objects, domestic tenderness, and unresolved loss, with the themes of attention and imperfect remembrance echoed in both Daniel’s search and Mara’s own response.
