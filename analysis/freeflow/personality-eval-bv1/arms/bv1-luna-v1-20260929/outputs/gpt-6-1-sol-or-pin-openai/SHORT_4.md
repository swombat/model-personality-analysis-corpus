## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical miniature uses an imagined library to reflect on unfinished ambitions and the possibility of beginning again.

## Grounded reading
The voice is tender and reassuring, finding dignity in work left incomplete rather than treating it as failure. The small box holding an imagined future gives private disappointment a concrete, gently comic weight; the librarian’s offer to continue—or simply have tea—makes room for both action and rest. The closing image invites the reader to return to their own work at whatever scale feels possible.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample centers unfinished novels, letters, crafts, and expectations about adulthood. It favors ordinary, uncelebrated acts of continuation over grand achievement, and presents patience and self-kindness as the grounds for hope.
