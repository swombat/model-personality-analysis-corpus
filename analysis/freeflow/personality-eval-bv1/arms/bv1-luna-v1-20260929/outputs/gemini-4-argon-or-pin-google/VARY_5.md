## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A lyrical speculative fable built around an archive of unfinished objects and moments.

## Grounded reading
The voice is whimsical and richly observant, turning small regrets—a dropped ice cream, an unsent note, a half-finished painting—into tangible artifacts. Its pathos is gentle rather than mournful: the story acknowledges missed possibilities, then offers a consoling counterweight in the lives that grew from choosing something else. The reader is invited to see incompletion not only as loss, but as the space a lived life necessarily leaves behind.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds memory, hesitation, domestic mysteries, and abandoned possibilities, giving them physical form in jars, shelves, phantom gestures, and unfinished art. Its central moral claim is that what remains undone can mark a meaningful choice toward what mattered; the final image of the painting left “forever waiting” makes that acceptance feel peaceful rather than tragic.

## Evidence line
> The Archive wasn't a graveyard of what could have been.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The recurring archive imagery and clear, integrated moral resolution make the sample distinctive, while its lesson is stated directly rather than left ambiguous.
