## Grounded reading

Its pathos comes from exhaustion and displacement rather than overt distress. The closing image of the terminal waking invites the reader to share a brief pause between destinations, when ordinary time seems temporarily suspended.

## What the model chose to foreground

It also chooses a gentle egalitarian claim: at this hour, different kinds of travelers are brought together by exhaustion.
