## Grounded reading
The voice is lyrical and explanatory, inviting the reader to look closely at ordinary remnants—a photograph, a stone wall, a cassette tape—and feel both their mystery and their tenderness. Its pathos comes from the gap between the vividness of lived moments and how easily their stories disappear. Rather than treating that loss as purely tragic, the essay turns toward acceptance: forgetting makes room for the present, and impermanence gives ordinary experiences their value.
