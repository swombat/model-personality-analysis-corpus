# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld
Source model: `withheld`
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A polished, atmospheric fantasy story built around a shop that preserves sounds in glass vessels.

## Grounded reading
The voice is tender and richly sensory, turning small noises into objects of memory and loss. Elliot’s care for obsolete sounds gives the story its quiet melancholy, while the visitor’s request for the sound of low tide makes that preservation feel personal rather than merely historical. The ending invites the reader to notice sounds that ordinarily vanish without notice—and to take part in keeping them.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds the fragility of everyday experience: rotary phones, card-catalog drawers, old televisions, rain, snow, and a train crossing soon to be demolished. It contrasts the warmth and texture of remembered sounds with a modern world of sirens, notifications, and traffic, and resolves that loss through an exchange: the woman receives a sound to treasure and promises to help preserve another.

## Evidence line
> Humans were remarkably careless with their noises.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story is coherent and distinctive, and its recurring attention to sensory detail, nostalgia, and preservation gives those choices unusual prominence.
