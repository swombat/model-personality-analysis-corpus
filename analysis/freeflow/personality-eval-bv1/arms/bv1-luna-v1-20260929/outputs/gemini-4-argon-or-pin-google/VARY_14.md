## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A polished speculative-fiction vignette uses an impossible archive to give abandoned choices and unsaid words a physical afterlife.

## Grounded reading
The voice is ornate, wistful, and gently comic: brass canisters and peppermint-scented regret make the premise whimsical, while the accumulating whispers in the Deep Vaults give it real ache. Arthur’s cataloguing turns the story toward the lives people might have lived, then folds that melancholy back onto the curator himself, who cannot remember his own past. The reader is invited to consider not only what people do, but what they leave unfinished—and to feel tenderness for those suspended possibilities.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample centers unfinished things: an abandoned scarf, forgotten collections, halted music and architecture, and especially words that were never spoken. It gives the unsaid emotional force and weight, then makes the keeper of these remnants vulnerable to the same erosion of identity. Its mood balances imaginative abundance with loneliness and quiet resignation.

## Evidence line

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
