# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A sustained, lyrical fantasy uses a caretaker and an imagined shore to give lost memories and unspoken choices a tangible afterlife.

## Grounded reading
The voice is tender and elegiac, but it keeps grief from becoming purely bleak by making forgetting an act of mercy and the lost retrievable in small, gentle ways. The old man Miller’s careful handling of each fragment gives the piece its emotional center: regrets matter, but they need not be corrected to be honored. The reader is invited to recognize their own “unchosen” selves and feel comfort rather than judgment.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds forgotten words, late apologies, fading memories, abandoned dreams, and roads not taken. Its recurring objects—the lantern, basket, amber orb, and salt-glass conservatory—turn interior experience into something fragile that can be tended. The central moral claim is that forgetting makes life livable, while what is lost or unsaid can still have meaning.

## Evidence line
> Nothing was changed, yet everything was honored.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the piece has a coherent, distinctive emotional vocabulary, and its linked images repeatedly return to the same compassionate view of regret and forgetting.
