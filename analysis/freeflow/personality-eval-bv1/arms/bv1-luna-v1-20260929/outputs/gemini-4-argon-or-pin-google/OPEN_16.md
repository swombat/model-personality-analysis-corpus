EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical reflection that turns a predawn city into a refuge from the demands of daytime life.

## Grounded reading
The voice is quiet, observant, and gently enchanted. Sensory details—the neon sign “buzzing like a trapped insect,” steam becoming a “miniature, fleeting cloud”—make the empty city feel alive without making it threatening. The piece finds comfort in solitude and in a pause from social performance, then gives that reprieve a tenderly melancholy ending as the city resumes its routines. It invites the reader to share the stillness rather than solve or explain it.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model selects the city at 4:00 AM as a liminal place inhabited by overlooked workers, insomniacs, and imagined ghosts. It foregrounds silence, small urban details, and a contrast between private, unallocated time and the daytime demands of productivity and performance. Its moral emphasis is not that the city is bleak, but that temporary release from those demands can feel comforting.
