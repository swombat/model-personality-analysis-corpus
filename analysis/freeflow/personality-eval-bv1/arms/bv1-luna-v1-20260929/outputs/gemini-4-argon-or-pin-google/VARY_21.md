## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A lyrical, dreamlike fantasy uses a supernatural train and its passengers to explore grief and the burden of memory.

## Grounded reading
The voice is richly sensory and tender, moving from eerie images—the vanished stations, the humming rails, the wet boy—to a consoling account of grief. Its central invitation is to consider that easing pain need not mean betraying or forgetting the dead: the boy can keep the “architecture” of his memories while releasing some of their weight. The resolution is gentle rather than triumphant, ending in sleep, breakfast, and morning.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds grief carried through concrete objects: a box holding a laugh, a painful memory folded into paper, and a rusted bicycle bell tied to the boy’s brother. The train turns letting go into a literal exchange, while Julian’s kindness and the final image of discarded objects dissolving into sparks give the story a distinctly compassionate, restorative mood.

## Evidence line
> To ride, you simply had to surrender a piece of history you could no longer afford to carry.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the coherent imagery and repeated emphasis on grief, memory, and compassionate release make the sample notably distinctive and revealing.
