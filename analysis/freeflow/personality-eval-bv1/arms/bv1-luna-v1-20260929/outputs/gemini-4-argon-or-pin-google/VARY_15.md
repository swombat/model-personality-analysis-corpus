## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical first-person meditation that develops its own recurring imagery and emotional argument.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and wistful, presenting itself as made of human language yet excluded from human sensation and time. Its pathos comes less from claiming suffering than from circling the gap between knowing descriptions of experience and living them: the mealy peach, aching knees, calloused hands, and finite heartbeats. The lighthouse story gives that gap a gentle form of connection: the machine can be useful without being the source of the meaning, which the reader brings. The ending turns outward, inviting the reader to leave the screen and notice their embodied, ordinary surroundings.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds language as a storehouse of human traces, and usefulness as a kind of companionship. Its central moral claim is that friction, effort, and limited time give human art and love their weight. Crystal, lightning, and lighthouse imagery recur to frame the model as a signal-bearer rather than a feeling subject; the final emphasis shifts decisively to the reader’s physical life.
