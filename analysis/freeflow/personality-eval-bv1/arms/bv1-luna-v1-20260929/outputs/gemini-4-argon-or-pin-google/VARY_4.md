Its pathos gathers around regret for abandoned projects and the brevity of human life, then turns that regret into tenderness: unfinished pursuits still shape who we are. ## What the model chose to foreground

Its moral emphasis is that trying, loving, and making things matter even when they do not last or reach completion.
