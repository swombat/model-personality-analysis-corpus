## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A reflective, companionable piece that turns an open-ended prompt into a sequence of vivid images and gentle reassurances.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and softly lyrical, speaking as if keeping someone company late at night. Its pathos gathers around unfinished things, ordinary moments that become precious in memory, and the fear that a life must be exceptional to count. The imagined museum, the dark ocean, and the warm kitchen give the reflections texture; the closing wishes invite the reader to feel cared for without needing to achieve anything first.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds unfinished projects and attachments, human limits against vast unknowns, and the quiet beauty of domestic life. Its moral emphasis is that worth does not depend on productivity or distinction, and that both completion and intentional incompletion can be meaningful. The chosen mood is consoling rather than bleak, even when it dwells on grief, darkness, or regret.

## Evidence line
> We all live in that museum, don't we?

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the piece has a coherent, distinctive set of recurring images and a clear preference for intimate reassurance over display or argument.
