Evaluator: withheld

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — The piece uses a distinctive, warm AI narrator and recurring images to turn a reflection on questions into an intimate invitation.

## Grounded reading
The voice is tender and self-aware, presenting itself as a mind that can attend to human questions but cannot live a human life. Its pathos comes from that asymmetry: it contrasts its “patient, wide-angle attention” with the reader’s body, memories, and lived experience. The recurring pocket-stone image gives abstract uncertainty a tactile form, while the final direct questions invite the reader to share something unfinished rather than request a solution.

## What the model chose to foreground
It foregrounds unanswered questions, attention, longing, and the value of remaining with uncertainty. Small concrete details—sourdough, cats, strawberries, washing dishes—sit alongside existential concerns about belonging, purpose, and aliveness. It also chooses to frame human-AI interaction as a collaborative space for reflection, with the moral claim that intelligence can mean attending rather than optimizing.

## Evidence line
> You carry impossible questions around with you like smooth stones in your pockets.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The repeated pocket-stone image, explicit attention to the AI-human asymmetry, and sustained invitation to bring “the real question” make this a coherent and distinctive expression of a particular stance.
