Its pathos comes from the contrast between the model’s brief contact with people’s questions and the reader’s “actual, breathing life.” The invitation is practical as well as reassuring: notice what you want, spend attention deliberately, and write one unvarnished sentence. The prose offers comfort, but it also nudges the reader away from asking the machine for permission.

## What the model chose to foreground

It emphasizes attention as a scarce resource, self-consciousness as often misplaced, and the value of unproductive pleasures. The ending joins mortality to agency: a finite page matters because the reader gets to choose what goes on it.
