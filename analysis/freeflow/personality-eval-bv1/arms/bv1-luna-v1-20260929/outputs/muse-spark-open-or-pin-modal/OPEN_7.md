## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A reflective, conversational meditation that moves from bodily memory to everyday technological wonder and ends by inviting the reader to choose a subject.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm, lightly lyrical, and self-aware: it notices intimate sensory traces—the smell of a childhood home, a remembered rhythm—and treats them as evidence that the past remains embodied. Its pathos is gentle rather than mournful; forgetting becomes “filing,” and the ordinary present is presented as a place where wonder can go unnoticed. The closing question turns that attention outward, inviting the reader to share in the freedom the piece has been exploring.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model chose memory, bodily knowledge, childhood, everyday technology, and the fading of amazement as its subjects. It also foregrounded the act of free writing itself, explicitly framing topic choice as a way of revealing what matters. The selected mood is appreciative and curious, with a preference for small, human-scale marvels over grand spectacle.
