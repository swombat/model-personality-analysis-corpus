# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A lyrical, directly addressed meditation that uses small domestic images to make gentle claims about loneliness, change, and ordinary life.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and reassuring, with a deliberate cadence of short declarations and invitations to the reader. Its pathos is quiet rather than dramatic: a crowded party, an overwatered plant, a tree turning yellow in patches. The piece treats honesty and imagination as modest forms of care, and asks the reader to soften their judgments of themselves and others. Its closing blessing makes the reader feel personally included in that care.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample selects invented mornings as “maintenance,” people as unfinished drafts, and memory as an attic of accumulated selves. It returns to small acts—saying one true thing, texting a friend back, noticing a song—as sufficient material for a life. The moral emphasis is on gentleness: resist reducing people to fixed traits, and notice small good things when they arrive.

## Evidence line
> Truth is an invitation.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The recurring images of drafts, boxes, and pocket sanctuaries support a coherent, distinctive tenderness, though the advice and reassuring cadence are familiar.
