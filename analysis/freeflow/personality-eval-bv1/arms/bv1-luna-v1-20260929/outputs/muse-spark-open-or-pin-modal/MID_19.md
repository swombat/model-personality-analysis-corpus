## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A reflective, image-rich meditation sustains one central metaphor while moving through memory, forgetting, and self-reinvention.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm, contemplative, and gently elegiac. It invites the reader to treat memory not as a complete record but as a living collection: shaped by childhood, sorted by identity, and continually revised through forgetting. The affection for ordinary details—light on a kitchen floor, a smell that summons a memory, an old textbook kept from guilt—gives the piece its pathos. Its closing gesture turns that tenderness outward: a life’s value lies partly in leaving room and light for someone else.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds libraries, shelves, cataloguing, and weeding as images for memory and identity. It emphasizes the mercy of forgetting alongside grief for lost experiences, and presents curiosity and the freedom to reorganize oneself as forms of wisdom. The recurring librarian, working quietly and without permission, gives the meditation a mood of acceptance rather than control.

## Evidence line
> Forgetting is mercy.

Medium: the sustained library metaphor, concrete sensory details, and gentle moral resolution make this a distinctive, coherent expressive sample.
