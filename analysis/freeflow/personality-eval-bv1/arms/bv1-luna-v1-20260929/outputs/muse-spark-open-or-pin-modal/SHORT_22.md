## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and gently wistful, finding comfort rather than drama in sleeplessness. Specific details—the humming refrigerator, the drip of the faucet, mismatched socks—make the kitchen feel private and lived-in. The piece contrasts daytime self-presentation with the freedom of “unobserved time,” then invites the reader to recognize that freedom in solitude and in landscapes without an audience. Its ending returns to routine, but lets the speaker keep a little of the quiet for themselves.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds insomnia, a domestic scene at 3 a.m., and the everyday labor of appearing friendly and composed. It treats solitude as relief from performance, linking the empty kitchen to deserts, deep oceans, and chapels. The refrigerator’s steady hum bookends the piece as a small image of watchfulness and continuity.
