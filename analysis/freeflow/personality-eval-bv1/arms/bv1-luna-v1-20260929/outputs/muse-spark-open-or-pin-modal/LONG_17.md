# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: LONG

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A sustained, imaginative meditation turns unfinished work and unrealized choices into a gentle metaphor for possibility.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm, patient, and reassuring, inviting the reader to look at what they left unfinished without treating it as evidence of failure. Its four-floor library gives different emotional weight to unsaid words, abandoned projects, alternate lives, and ideas not yet made. The piece is most affecting when it acknowledges regret and comparison, then redirects attention toward what can still be chosen or made. Its invitation is intimate but practical: revisit what still matters, accept the life you have, and carry one small possibility back into the world.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample centers on unfinishedness as something held in safekeeping rather than condemned: unsaid apologies and affection, dormant creative projects, paths not taken, and ideas waiting to be discovered. It favors images of lamps, shelves, windows, and a door opening onto ordinary life, and repeatedly pairs wistfulness with permission to begin again. The moral emphasis is that a person need not complete everything; choosing and working on one meaningful thing is enough.

## Evidence line
> I remember that there is a place where all my unfinished things are being kept safe, not as indictments, but as invitations.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The coherent library metaphor recurs throughout the piece and supports a distinctive, consistently compassionate emphasis on possibility over self-reproach.
