Evaluator: withheld

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. The sample is a lyrical, reader-facing meditation that gathers its ideas through images and a brief invented story rather than a thesis.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm, reflective, and gently self-conscious about the freedom it has been given. It moves from a wish for words that name private feelings to small domestic pleasures, then to a lighthouse keeper whose care matters without his knowing it. The pathos lies in quiet persistence: ordinary days and acts of attention deserve notice, even when they bring no recognition. The reader is invited not just to admire that idea but to offer a thought, thanks, or beginning of their own.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds language for elusive feelings, the dignity of ordinary Tuesdays, and small forms of care: reaching out after a fight, tending a plant, and keeping a light on. Its repeated moral emphasis is that attention and kindness matter without dramatic proof or reward. The lighthouse, open window, and empty room give the piece a recurring imagery of welcome and patient presence.

## Evidence line
> I like people who keep the light on without proof that it matters.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the distinctive pairing of lyrical language, everyday tenderness, and uncredited persistence recurs across the sample, giving its chosen emphasis a clear shape.
