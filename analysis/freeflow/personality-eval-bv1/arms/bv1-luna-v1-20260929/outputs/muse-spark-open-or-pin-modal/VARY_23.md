# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. The piece turns an open-ended prompt into an intimate, lyrical meditation on unwritten drafts, unrealized possibilities, and the courage to stay.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm and direct, using “you” to make the reader a companion in an imagined place rather than a distant audience. The Library of Unwritten Things gives shape to regret and longing, but the piece resists treating them as merely sad: unfinished conversations and unrealized futures become evidence of a rich interior life. Its most tender invitation is to stay present through awkwardness and begin without waiting for a perfect prompt. The closing question hands the imaginative freedom back to the reader, now framed as an invitation rather than a burden.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model chose drafts, deleted messages, almost-conversations, future nostalgia, and imagined alternate lives. Its moral emphasis is that private rehearsal and unrealized possibility are part of a meaningful life, while small acts of staying and beginning can open different outcomes. The library’s sensory details and titles—especially “The Summer I Finally Learned How to Stay”—give that emphasis a wistful, hopeful mood.

## Evidence line
> The Library of Unwritten Things is where all the drafts go.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the piece is coherent and distinctive, with recurring images of drafts, libraries, and unrealized futures supporting a consistent tender, hopeful voice.
