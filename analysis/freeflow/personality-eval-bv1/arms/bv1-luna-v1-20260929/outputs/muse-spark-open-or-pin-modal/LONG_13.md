The reader is invited to slow down, tolerate not knowing, and treat their own unfinished life as a place still worth exploring.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds incompletion over achievement, boredom as a condition for imagination, abandoned books as records of change, and attention to small, ordinary wonders. Its moral emphasis is that a worthwhile life need not be polished or fully mapped; generosity can mean leaving something open for oneself or someone else to continue. The choice to return repeatedly to maps, libraries, stars, and seeds makes uncertainty feel not merely unavoidable but potentially fertile.
