# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: LONG

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A polished, sustained literary story built around a narrator’s return to his childhood home.

## Grounded reading
The voice is measured and observant, letting grief emerge through ordinary details: a changed garden, a treehouse that is gone, the taste of tomatoes, and the mother who no longer remembers the house. Its pathos comes less from confrontation than from the narrator’s gradual recognition that he cannot reclaim the place or share its memories with his parents as they were. Ellen’s recollection of him reading in the tree gives the visit a human witness, and her offer to welcome him back makes the ending feel less like closure than a small permission to return. The story invites the reader to see memory as something that shapes a life without granting ownership over the places where it began.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds the passage of time through altered domestic details: the removed cherry trees, replaced fence, changed house, and surviving maple planted by the narrator’s father. It gives particular weight to childhood solitude and reading, to the losses of a dead father and a mother with memory impairment, and to the difference between remembering a home and possessing it. Its central moral emphasis is that the past persists in the person who remembers, even when the physical place belongs to others.

## Evidence line
> The memories, the light of the old house, the light of the summer afternoons filtering through the leaves of the maple—that light belonged to no one.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The coherent, recurring use of ordinary objects to carry memory and grief makes those preoccupations a strong feature of this sample.
