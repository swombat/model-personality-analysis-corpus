## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A lyrical, personal essay uses memories and recurring images to explore how forgetting shapes identity and connection.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate, meditative, and gently assured, moving from small sensory memories—a grandmother’s slippers, a yellow tablecloth, lights coming on in a town—to expansive reflections on memory, art, and impermanence. Its pathos comes from loss without despair: homes and people disappear, but their emotional contours remain and keep offering company. The essay invites the reader to walk alongside the speaker, recognizing their own rooms, absences, and remembered details in the particular ones shared here.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds memory as selective, creative, and inseparable from forgetting; home as a feeling made through return; and storytelling as a way to share incomplete experience. Thresholds, kitchens, photographs, shorelines, and lights recur as objects of transition and remembrance. Its moral emphasis is on accepting impermanence and loving or doing good work without demanding permanence or legacy.

## Evidence line
> Forgetting is not the opposite of remembering; it is the chisel.
