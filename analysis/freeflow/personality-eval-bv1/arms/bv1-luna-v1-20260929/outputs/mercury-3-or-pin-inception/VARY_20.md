## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A self-conscious, lyrical meditation turns the act of filling a word count into a reflection on language, memory, and endings.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and image-rich, presenting the blank page as both a creative challenge and a test of sincerity. It repeatedly imagines the speaker as a vessel for other people’s words and memories, while stopping short of claiming those memories as its own. Its pathos comes from the tension between fluent production and uncertainty about meaning: the speaker wants to say something wise, but ends by accepting the small, imperfect act of filling the room. The reader is invited to linger over the text’s images, even as the essay questions whether anything arrives intact.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds writing about writing: the cursor, the word count, the blank page, and the pressure to make language meaningful rather than merely abundant. It also chooses borrowed memory, collective authorship, mortality, and the limits of perception as its central motifs. The mood shifts from unease about insincerity toward a gentle acceptance of the final period and goodbye.

## Evidence line
> These aren't my memories, but they live in me like borrowed furniture in a house I'm squatting in.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the recurring metaphors and coherent movement from blank-page anxiety to an accepted ending make the sample stylistically distinctive, though its polished meditation on writing and language is also a familiar essay form.
