Evaluator: withheld

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical, self-reflective meditation turns the act of writing under an open-ended prompt into its subject.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and gently earnest, moving between intimate sensory details—the blinking cursor, overcast sky, hands on keys—and broad reflections on time, choice, and what a page leaves out. Its central pathos is the effort to trust that attention and presence are enough, even when there is no clear destination. The reader is invited not so much to follow an argument as to linger alongside the speaker’s process: watch the clouds, let thoughts shift, and accept an ending that arrives by constraint rather than final resolution.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds writing itself: its constraints, its omissions, the uncertainty of an open prompt, and the hope of reaching a reader. Clouds recur as an image of unhurried attention, while streams and rivers frame thought as movement. The moral emphasis is on staying present and continuing without needing to control where the process leads.

## Evidence line
> The point is the willingness to stay with something, to follow it, to let it unfold.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: The piece is coherent and stylistically distinctive, with recurring images and a sustained emphasis on presence, though its self-reflective subject confines what it reveals.
