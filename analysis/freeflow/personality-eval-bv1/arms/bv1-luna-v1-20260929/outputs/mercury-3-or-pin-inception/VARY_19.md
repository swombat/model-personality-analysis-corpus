# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A reflective first-person meditation turns an ordinary afternoon into a carefully shaped account of memory, aging, and attention.

## Grounded reading
The voice is quiet, intimate, and deliberately observant, finding emotional weight in dust, fading light, a tree, and an old failed raft. Its pathos comes less from dramatic loss than from the realization that time and relationships have changed without announcing themselves. The speaker invites the reader to slow down and notice what might otherwise pass unclaimed; the ending offers a modest comfort in holding the present moment briefly, rather than resolving the larger questions.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds forgetting, memory’s unpredictability, aging, people who have drifted out of reach, and the small sensory details that make a past moment vivid. Its mood is wistful but not despairing. It repeatedly elevates attention to ordinary things—leaves, hands, a glass of water—as a way to give fleeting experience “a little weight, a little permanence.”

## Evidence line
> You find that the big questions are too big to hold, and so you reach for the small things—the ordinary, the fleeting, the almost-forgotten—and you try to give them a little weight, a little permanence, before they drift away like the leaves on the sidewalk.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sample is coherent and distinctive in its sustained focus on sensory particulars as a way to approach memory and time, though its reflective, elegiac themes are familiar.
