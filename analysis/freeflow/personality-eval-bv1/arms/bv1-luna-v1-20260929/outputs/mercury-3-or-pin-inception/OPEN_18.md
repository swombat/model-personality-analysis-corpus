## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical meditation that turns the act of writing into its own subject, using recurring images of water, maps, silence, and travel.

## Grounded reading
The voice is composed and intimate, inviting the reader to treat free writing as a way of discovering thought rather than recording it. Its pathos lies in the gap between what is said and what remains unspoken; its advice is to attend closely to small, concrete details and to feelings that resist naming. The closing image makes writing feel like a modest act of creation and a gift sent toward an unknown future reader.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds writing as discovery, the weight of silence, the future self and reader, and the possibility of making something new through attention. Its chosen objects—the blank page, a crack in the sidewalk, cold coffee, a refrigerator’s hum, and seeds in an envelope—make its abstract claims tactile. The recurring water imagery gives the piece a mood of patient exploration rather than urgency or confession.

## Evidence line
