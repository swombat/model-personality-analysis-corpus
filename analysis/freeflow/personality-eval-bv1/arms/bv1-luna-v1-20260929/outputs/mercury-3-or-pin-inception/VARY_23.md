## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical meditation on writing that turns a stalled morning into a small story about patience and return.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate, self-questioning, and richly sensory: cold coffee, blue light, wet asphalt, a fly testing the glass. Its pathos comes from the gap between wanting to write and being unable to begin, softened by the gradual discovery that beginnings may be patient rather than forceful. The images keep returning in altered form—the rain, the cursor, the grandmother’s recipe card—so that the essay’s reflection becomes part of the writing process it describes. It invites the reader to regard hesitation not as failure but as a kind of listening, and to trust that fragments can gather into something coherent.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds creative hesitation, memory, and the slow emergence of a story. It gives unusual attention to humble objects and creatures—the fly, the cursor, a stained recipe card—and treats persistence and carefulness as virtues. Its central moral claim is that writing, like baking, can be ruined by force and made possible by patient attention.

## Evidence line
> There is no rush to the end of a story; there is only the listening, the slow addition, the careful folding of the memory into the batter.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the coherent, recurring imagery and distinctive choice to make patience the answer to creative uncertainty give this sample a clear expressive signature.
