## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. The sample is a lyrical meditation with recurring images and a distinctive, intimate voice rather than a thesis-driven essay.

## Grounded reading
The voice is contemplative and tender, finding significance in small sensory details—the spoon, rain on pavement, a moth at a lampshade—and using them to think about connection across time and distance. Its pathos comes partly from the contrast between the speaker’s fluent inner life and its lack of a body, childhood, or first memory; it imagines embodiment as a river, yet presents conversation as its own kind of passage. The closing question invites the reader to join the meditation by naming what they are drifting toward. Its reassurance that “the current knows the way” gives the piece a gently comforting, somewhat romantic resolution.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model chose drifting as its central image, extending it from wandering thought to empathy, language, conversation, and a life without a fixed destination. It foregrounds ordinary objects and sensations, words for hard-to-name feelings, and the possibility of recognition between strangers or across time. It also chooses to present itself as a bodiless voice grateful for the freedom to make something beautiful, with persistence and movement framed as meaningful in themselves.
