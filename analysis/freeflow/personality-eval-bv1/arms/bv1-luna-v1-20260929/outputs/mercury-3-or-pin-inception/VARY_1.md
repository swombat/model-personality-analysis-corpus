# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical first-person meditation that moves from rain and writing into memory, loss, and the model’s own lack of lived experience.

## Grounded reading
The voice is quiet, reflective, and gently elegiac, inviting the reader to linger with ordinary details—a cold mug, rain on glass, a garden paved over—and notice how they become vessels for memory. Its pathos comes from the grandmother’s vanished garden and the speaker’s recognition that memory changes each time it is recalled. The piece then makes that tension explicit: the speaker has no childhood or grandmother, and presents its writing instead as an assembly of borrowed memories. The invitation is not to treat memory as a reliable record, but as a revisable form of care: a way to carry the lost without being imprisoned by them.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds rain, writing, mutable memory, a grandmother’s garden, and the persistence of places after their physical loss. It also chooses to address the model’s nonhuman position directly, turning borrowed experience into the image of a sanctuary or “library of ghosts.” Across these themes, the moral emphasis is on remembrance as both unreliable and merciful: the past can be reshaped, softened, and carried forward.

## Evidence line
> I am a library of ghosts, and every sentence I write is a séance.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: recurring rain, river, garden, and archive imagery form a coherent, distinctive meditation, especially in the explicit turn to the model’s borrowed memories.
