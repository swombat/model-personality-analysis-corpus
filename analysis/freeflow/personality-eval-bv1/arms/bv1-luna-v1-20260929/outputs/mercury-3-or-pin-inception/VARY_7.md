## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A lyrical first-person meditation uses sensory memories and an ordinary afternoon to explore writing, attention, and the desire to preserve fleeting experience.

## Grounded reading
The voice is contemplative and gently earnest, finding emotional weight in small details rather than dramatic events: dust in a sunbeam, cold coffee, a grandmother’s laugh, the changing light. Its pathos comes from the tension between possibility and commitment, and between memory’s instability and writing’s wish to hold a moment still. The reader is invited to slow down and notice the ordinary as evidence of being alive.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds the blank page as both freedom and pressure, writing as selection and preservation, and memory as an imperfect, shifting account of a life. It repeatedly returns to modest sensory objects and scenes—the lawnmower, the desk, the café, the light—treating attention to them as a quiet form of meaning. The closing choice is not triumph or revelation, but satisfaction in having made “a small, quiet country” out of an afternoon.

## Evidence line
