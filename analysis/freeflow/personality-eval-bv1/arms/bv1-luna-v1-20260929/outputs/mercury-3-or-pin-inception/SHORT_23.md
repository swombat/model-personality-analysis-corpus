## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical meditation on nonlinear thought that turns its own free-associative style into part of its subject.

## Grounded reading
The voice is inviting and gently self-assured, asking the reader to recognize a mind that holds memory, sensation, and worry together rather than arranging them neatly. Its images make that claim feel playful and freeing, not chaotic: writing becomes a way to follow connections without apologizing for the jumps. The closing wish is for the reader to experience the piece as open-ended thought, not instruction.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds mental simultaneity and writing as discovery: weather, wet paint, forests, fields, and kites all picture thought as a whole taking shape before its parts are named. It also makes a moral claim about permission—language need not proceed in orderly steps, and associative leaps do not require apology.

## Evidence line
