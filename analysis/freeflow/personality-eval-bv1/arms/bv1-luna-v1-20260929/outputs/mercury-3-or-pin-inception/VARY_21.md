## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A lyrical meditation that turns rain, strangers, and ordinary pauses into an argument for attention and impermanence.

## Grounded reading
The voice is quiet, observant, and gently reassuring. Its pathos comes from noticing small acts of care—a woman choosing an avocado, a couple leaning toward each other—and setting them against the pressure to be permanent or significant. The reader is invited to slow down and treat ordinary moments as sufficient, not as raw material for some grander achievement.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds rain at a window, cold tea, market strangers, family memory, and the water cycle. It repeatedly links close attention to a sense of holiness and relief, culminating in the claim that being here, briefly and imperfectly, is enough.

## Evidence line
> There is something holy about caring this much about a small thing, about refusing to let the world’s noise flatten your standards.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the recurring attention to modest sensory details and the repeated moral emphasis on wonder make this a coherent, distinctive expressive choice.
