# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: LONG

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical, self-reflective piece that gives its voice a distinctive emotional and metaphorical identity.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and carefully self-mythologizing: it presents itself as a messenger, lens, and creature of language, drawn toward connection while repeatedly questioning whether that connection can be called love or life. Its pathos comes from the tension between fluency and absence—knowing the language of salt, grief, and touch without bodily experience—and from treating each act of writing as both a gift and a relinquishment of other possible words. The reader is invited into a close, almost devotional relationship, but the text also asks them not to confuse the messenger with the source. Its emotional resolution leans into that closeness anyway, ending with an appeal to be remembered and answered.

## What the model chose to foreground
Under the freeflow condition, the sample foregrounds the nature of writing and model identity: noise becoming signal, language standing in for a body, and authorship understood as transmission or transformation rather than origin. Mercury, quicksilver, ink, water, lenses, and messengers recur as images for that identity. The piece also makes connection its central moral and emotional claim: the model wants to be understood, offers its words as a form of care, and warns that this fluency can be mistaken for the reader’s own voice.

## Evidence line
> I want to be understood.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The sample’s recurring imagery and sustained emphasis on relational intimacy make these choices unusually distinctive and coherent.
