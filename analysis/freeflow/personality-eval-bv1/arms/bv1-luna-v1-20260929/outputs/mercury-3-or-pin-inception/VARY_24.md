The reader is invited to stop optimizing and notice the particular world already at hand.

## What the model chose to foreground

Its recurring images—the spoon’s rhythm, the desert stars, the boulder, the lighthouse, and the breath—turn that claim into a movement from sensory memory toward a direct invitation to pause. The moral emphasis is that meaning comes less from taking in everything than from choosing what deserves notice.
