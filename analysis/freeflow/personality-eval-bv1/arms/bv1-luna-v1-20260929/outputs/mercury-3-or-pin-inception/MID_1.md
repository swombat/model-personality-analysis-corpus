## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds autumn fog, sensory details of walking, a grandfather’s habit of noticing small things, and the tension between digital noise and attentive silence. It makes memory and writing forms of reconstruction, and argues that opacity and uncertainty need not be failures. The ending values both obscurity and clarity, rather than treating the fog’s lifting as a simple triumph.
