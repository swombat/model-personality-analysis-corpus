## Grounded reading
The voice is contemplative and gently admonishing: it notices small sensory details, then uses them to argue that attention is a form of care. Its pathos comes from the contrast between vivid ordinary life and the devices that draw people away from it. The essay criticizes attention-harvesting design without rejecting technology wholesale, and ends by inviting the reader to practice noticing rather than simply lament distraction.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds dawn streets, puddles, wind through grass, the blank page, and other minor details as evidence of a world worth attending to. It links distraction to grief and civic erosion, while presenting close attention as intimacy, empathy, and a possible source of hope. The movement from urban observation to cultural critique and back to the blinking cursor makes this chosen theme feel deliberate and sustained.
