# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: SHORT

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical reflection that turns the act of writing without a plan into an invitation to loosen control and follow thoughts wherever they lead.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle, intimate, and encouraging, with a taste for metaphors that make creativity feel like a landscape or natural force. Its pathos is mild rather than anguished: freedom means setting aside judgment and allowing messy, unfinished thoughts to be alive. It invites the reader to try a deliberately pointless act of writing, not to produce something impressive but to rediscover ease and possibility.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds wandering, inner voice, ordinary prompts such as dust and old books, and natural images of clouds and rivers. Its moral claim is that creativity flows better when people stop trying to control or judge it; the repeated emphasis on freedom and “being human” gives that claim a warm, affirming mood.

## Evidence line
> The words can be messy, unfinished, contradictory—alive.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sample sustains a coherent preference for gentle encouragement and nature-based metaphors, though the imagery and affirmations are familiar rather than highly distinctive.
