## Grounded reading
The voice is tender and sensory, lingering over small rituals and objects—the goose-shaped teapot, the half-filled cup, the moving patch of sunlight—as though attention itself is a way of preserving someone. Its pathos comes from the contrast between the child’s vivid summers and the adult’s ordinary kitchen, with the faded, repainted door marking what time has taken away. The reader is invited to treat memory not as a perfect record but as a place where warmth can remain vivid.

## What the model chose to foreground

Its central objects—the humming red door, dreaming steam, and teapot—make arrival and homecoming feel emotional rather than merely practical. The closing emphasis is on keeping an inner version of the lost house alive.
