## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A compact lyrical meditation turns the airport into an image of shared suspension and imagined freedom.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and quietly wistful, finding intimacy not in conversation but in strangers’ parallel stillness. Its pathos comes from the contrast between the open-ended promise of departure and the ordinary return to a specific gate and seat. The reader is invited to linger in that in-between state, where simply knowing the world is reachable can feel liberating.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds airports as places of transition, with fluorescent light, announcements, empty concourses, vending machines, and a departures board giving the mood concrete texture. It emphasizes the brief kinship of strangers sharing silence, then the tension between freedom as possibility and travel as an actual, narrowing itinerary.

## Evidence line
> There is a strange intimacy in this shared suspension.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sustained imagery and clear turn from weightlessness back to the boarding pass make this sample distinctly expressive rather than merely descriptive.
