## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A lyrical meditation that moves from a rain-lit room into reflections on memory, loss, and impermanence.

## Grounded reading
The voice is hushed and companionable, drawing its pathos from ordinary sensory details rather than dramatic events: a curtain “breathing,” sunlight on a kitchen table, a hand on a shoulder. It treats memory as intimate and fragmentary, and grief as a continuing presence woven into daily life. The closing address invites the reader to become part of the reflection, not by offering a firm answer but by asking them to notice and cherish what is passing.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds small moments as the materials of identity, with recurring images of doors, bricks, photographs, echoes, rain, and borrowed time. Its moral emphasis is that meaning may lie less in explaining life than in attending to it while it happens. The movement from private contemplation toward direct address makes the reader’s own act of noticing part of the subject.

## Evidence line
> These are the bricks of who we are, and we don't even notice we're building with them.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the coherent return to memory, impermanence, and sensory detail gives the sample a distinct reflective voice, though its imagery and conclusions are familiar lyrical motifs.
