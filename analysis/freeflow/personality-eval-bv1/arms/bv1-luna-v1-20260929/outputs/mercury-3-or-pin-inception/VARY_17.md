## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical meditation turns the open-ended prompt into a sequence of imagined rooms and small scenes, then reflects on writing as attention and connection.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle, wistful, and deliberately intimate, moving between invented scenes and a first-person memory without sharply separating them. Its pathos comes from unfinished communication, changing selves, and fleeting gestures: the unsent letter, the books meant for a future self, and a stranger’s wave across the water. The reader is invited to treat small observations as meaningful—not grand revelations, but signs that people notice one another and leave messages across distance.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds doors, letters, keepsakes, books, water, and the act of noticing. It repeatedly makes incompleteness valuable: the letter remains unfinished, the postcard’s mystery need not be solved, and the frame of a word limit gives shape to otherwise boundless possibility. The moral emphasis is that truthful attention to ordinary moments can be enough, and that writing offers a modest hope of connection.

## Evidence line
> When you write, you are waving from a boat.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the piece sustains a distinctive, coherent focus on small acts of attention and connection, with recurring images that reinforce its reflective mood.
