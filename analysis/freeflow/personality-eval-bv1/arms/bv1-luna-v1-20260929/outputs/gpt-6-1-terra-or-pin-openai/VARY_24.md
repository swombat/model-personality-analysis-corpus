# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld
Source model: `withheld`
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION. A quiet, compact story uses an ordinary public park to build a small mystery about lost keys and shared care.

## Grounded reading
The voice is restrained and gently observant, letting the objects carry much of the feeling: the tea tin, the keys, the tree stump, and finally the box of gloves. Its pathos comes from small acts of attention rather than revelation or sentimentality. The story invites the reader to see a humble civic ritual as a form of connection, while leaving the keys’ histories and the participants’ motives partly open.

## What the model chose to foreground
It foregrounds overlooked public space, lost objects, and the quiet ways strangers can make room for one another. The moral emphasis is on keeping a place for what is unclaimed, without insisting that every mystery be solved; the ending extends that practice from keys to gloves.

## Evidence line
> By winter, a second box had appeared beside it.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story’s recurring object motifs and understated communal resolution give its choices a clear, distinctive shape.
