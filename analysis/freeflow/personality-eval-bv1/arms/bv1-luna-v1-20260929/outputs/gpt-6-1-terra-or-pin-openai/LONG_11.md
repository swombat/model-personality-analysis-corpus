# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld

Condition: LONG

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A quietly realist story uses a repair shop and its objects to carry a sustained narrative about grief, work, and making room for change.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and gently wry, finding tenderness in practical details rather than grand declarations: a lamp’s dent, a wrong-colored cord, a robot repeating “Please stand by.” Mara’s grief initially seeks a task she can finish, but the story lets repair remain both useful and limited: it cannot explain her father’s death or keep Ivo from getting tired. Its pathos comes from people helping one another without turning that help into a cure. The reader is invited to value care that is modest, shared, and honest about what cannot be fixed.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story repeatedly makes repair a way of handling memory, not just machinery: Mara brings objects connected to her father, and their flaws preserve particular stories rather than needing to be erased. It foregrounds economic change and aging, too, but resists a triumphant rescue; Ivo’s shop changes scale, and he is allowed to admit he is tired. The ending’s repaired robot, now saying something different, gathers the story’s preference for adaptation over restoring things exactly as they were.

## Evidence line
> The first thing Mara brought to the repair shop was a lamp that did not need repairing.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The story sustains a distinctive, recurring preference for practical acts of care and imperfect repair, with a coherent emotional resolution that avoids easy consolation.
