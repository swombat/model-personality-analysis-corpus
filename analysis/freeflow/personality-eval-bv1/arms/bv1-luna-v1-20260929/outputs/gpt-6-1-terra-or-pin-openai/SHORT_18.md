## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle, observant, and quietly sentimental, finding emotional weight in ordinary gestures: a book returned, a child leaving with extra stories, a librarian stamping a due date. Its pathos is modest rather than dramatic; the library offers a place where private journeys briefly share a room. The closing image invites the reader to see books as waiting companions, made vivid by the attention of their readers.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds libraries at closing, books and hands, rain, and the small transitions of people at different stages of life. It favors patience, curiosity, and the possibility of unexpected discovery, presenting reading as a quiet human connection rather than an achievement or argument.

## Evidence line
