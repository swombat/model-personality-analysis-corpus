## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and restrained, letting the odd objects—the changing-sky photograph and the jar labeled **TUESDAY**—sit beside ordinary umbrellas and gloves without explanation. Its emotional center is Mara’s quiet care: she checks for a man’s missing courage, then lets him leave without demanding a lesson or offering false comfort. The ending leaves loss unresolved, with the jar’s bright rattle giving the farewell a small, uncanny aftersound. It invites the reader to sit with the limits of helping someone and the dignity of not forcing an explanation.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds lost courage, attentive but limited care, and the choice to leave before one feels ready. It gives these themes a whimsical frame, but its moral emphasis stays with Mara’s discreet compassion and the man’s agency rather than with a neat recovery or redemption.
