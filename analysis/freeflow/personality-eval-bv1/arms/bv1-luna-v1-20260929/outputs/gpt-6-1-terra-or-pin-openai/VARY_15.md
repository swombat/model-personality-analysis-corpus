## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and patient, letting small discoveries—a bent spoon, a note, a photograph—carry the emotional weight. Mara’s grief appears in the way the house still seems to expect her mother’s return, while the bird story gives her and her brother a way to remember their mother together. The ending offers connection rather than certainty: they do not learn whether the bird lived, but Mara notices her brother’s hand reaching toward the box. The story invites the reader to look closely at ordinary keepsakes and the incomplete memories they can bring back.

## What the model chose to foreground
Under the freeflow condition, the sample foregrounds bereavement, childhood memory, family care, and the mysterious afterlife of household objects. Its mood is wistful but not bleak; its moral emphasis falls on attention and shared recollection, rather than on solving the mystery completely.
