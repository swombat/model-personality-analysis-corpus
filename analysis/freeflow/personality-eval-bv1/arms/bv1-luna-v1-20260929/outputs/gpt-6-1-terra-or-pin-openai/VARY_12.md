# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A quiet, carefully shaped story about an unremarkable chair becoming a place where grief and hospitality can meet.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and observant, finding emotional weight in small, ordinary details: a hot-pan groove, worn pencils, stacked bakery trays, rain at the door. Its pathos is restrained; loss is present in the aunt’s mourning, the widower’s visit, and the memories visitors attach to the chair, but the story avoids explaining grief into a lesson. It invites the reader to see how an object can matter not for its history or beauty, but for the welcome people find around it.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story centers an ordinary blue chair, a small town, the smells and routines of the bakery, and visitors carrying private memories. Its moral emphasis is on making room for people: the museum’s purpose gradually shifts from preserving an object to offering a place to sit, remember, and come in from the rain. The resolution favors openness over ceremony, with the velvet rope removed and a second chair placed by the window.

## Evidence line
> “She said there was a chair in a museum. I thought there must be more to it, but that was all she told me.”

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story’s coherent, distinctive use of an ordinary object to hold grief, memory, and welcome makes its chosen emotional register clear.
