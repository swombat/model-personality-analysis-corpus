## Sample kind
GENERIC_ESSAY — A polished, thesis-driven essay uses repair to make a broad case for care, shared knowledge, and maintenance.

## Grounded reading
The voice is calm, explanatory, and gently persuasive. Its pathos comes from the modest value of keeping ordinary things—and shared systems—working, rather than from dramatic transformation. It invites the reader to notice hidden labor, approach problems patiently, and see repair as a practice that can connect people. It also resists turning that practice into a personal virtue test, acknowledging limits of time, money, product design, and safety.

## What the model chose to foreground
The essay foregrounds familiar objects—a wobbling chair, a toaster, a jacket, a bridge—as ways to think about attention and maintenance. It emphasizes repair as collective knowledge-sharing, while also naming the structural conditions that make repair possible or difficult. Its moral claim is that progress can mean sustaining what people rely on, not only inventing or building something new. The relationship metaphor is deliberately bounded: listening may help, but some harm calls for walking away.

## Evidence line
> It would be unfair to turn every broken appliance into a test of individual virtue, or to romanticize unnecessary hardship.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the essay sustains a coherent emphasis on patience, shared skill, and structural access, though its polished civic argument is not highly stylistically distinctive.
