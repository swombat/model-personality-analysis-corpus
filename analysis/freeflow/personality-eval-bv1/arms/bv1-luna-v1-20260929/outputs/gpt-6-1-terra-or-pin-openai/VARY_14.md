# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A restrained literary fable uses an absurd bureaucracy to give shape to grief and unfinished emotional business.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle, dryly observant, and emotionally controlled: the painted door that becomes real and the department’s forms and stamps make the magical premise feel ordinary, while the man’s memory of his brother supplies the quiet ache. The story invites the reader to sit with the difference between receiving an answer and finding a way to carry an unanswered question. Its small resolution is neither proof of the supernatural nor a grand reconciliation; the man recognizes that he wrote the reply, and still takes both slips home.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story centers on unfinished conversations, bereavement, and the stubborn afterlife of small childhood conflicts. It selects mundane objects—the bicycle, paper slips, an envelope, a photograph—as containers for memory, and gives special weight to the modest act of keeping the question rather than declaring it resolved.

## Evidence line
> After he left, Mara stamped the empty envelope *RETURNED TO OWNER*.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the controlled magical premise, recurring paperwork imagery, and modest, emotionally unresolved ending make the sample distinctly shaped around grief and the limits of closure.
