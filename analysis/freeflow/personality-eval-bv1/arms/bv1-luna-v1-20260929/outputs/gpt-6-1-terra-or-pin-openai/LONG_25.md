# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: LONG

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A quietly magical short story follows municipal sound-retrievers through a case that turns into a portrait of grief.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle, dryly observant, and attentive to small disruptions in ordinary life: a late bakery, a man who sits too long in his chair, a station waiting room with an uneven radiator. Its magic is treated as practical work, but the emotional center is not the recovered bell so much as Lewis’s difficulty parting with his mother’s things and his hope of hearing her voice. The story invites the reader to approach grief without demanding a tidy explanation or cure. Mara’s restraint—she does not promise that the department can recover the mother’s voice—keeps the tenderness from becoming sentimental.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds lost sounds as vessels for memory and attachment: a church bell, a mother’s voice, rain on a childhood window. It pairs municipal routine and understated humor with grief, and gives weight to ordinary acts of care: listening, asking what someone remembers, and leaving room for what cannot be restored. Its resolution returns to work rather than offering a grand revelation: another caller begins describing a sound, and Mara opens a fresh file.

## Evidence line
> The department could not promise any of those things.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the recurring sound-and-memory motif, restrained treatment of grief, and practical, humane resolution make this a distinctive and coherent expressive choice.
