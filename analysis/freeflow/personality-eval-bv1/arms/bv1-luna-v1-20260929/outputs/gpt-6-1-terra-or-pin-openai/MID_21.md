## Grounded reading
The voice is warm and observant, finding emotional significance in practical details: the loose screw, the crooked stitches, the old belt kept for an unknown future use. Its pathos is gentle rather than dramatic; setbacks are met with patience, and the story makes room for things to remain imperfect. The reader is invited to see repair as shared attention and to value the confidence that grows from trying, even when the first attempt does not work.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds broken household objects, hands-on knowledge, and a library table that becomes a small community. It gives moral weight to patience, teaching without humiliation, and usefulness that does not depend on perfection. Its closing image of Eli setting up the table and beginning to open the radio turns his learning into a quiet invitation to continue the practice.
