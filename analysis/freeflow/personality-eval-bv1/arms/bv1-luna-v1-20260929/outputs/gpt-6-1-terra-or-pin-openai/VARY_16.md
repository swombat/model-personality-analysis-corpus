# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A quiet, carefully resolved short story about an unwanted piano becoming a point of connection between neighbors.

## Grounded reading
The voice is restrained and observant, building feeling through ordinary gestures rather than declarations: a tarp tied down, flour on a wrist, a familiar exercise remembered. The piano holds grief for the man who has lost his mother, while its imperfect keys echo Mara’s own habit of accommodating what no longer works. Their conversation turns this shared damage into a small opening: Mara considers teaching again, and the man recalls his mother playing. The story invites the reader to see care not as restoration to perfection, but as giving something—and someone—another chance to be used.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds discarded objects, imperfect instruments, bereavement, memory, and renewed connection across generations. Its moral emphasis is gentle: an object need not be useful as it is, and a person’s old capacity can return through an invitation. The piano’s broken keys recur as more than a practical flaw, linking the mother’s imperfect playing, Mara’s habits, and the tentative start of a new lesson.

## Evidence line
> At the stuck keys she stopped, pressed them firmly, and waited for them to rise.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the coherent, repeated use of the piano and its stuck keys gives the story a distinctive emotional structure, though its understated resolution is familiar in tone.
