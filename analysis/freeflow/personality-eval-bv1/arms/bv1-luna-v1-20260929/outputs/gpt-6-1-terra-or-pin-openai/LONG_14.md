# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld
Source model: `withheld`
Condition: LONG

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A sustained literary story uses a small-town library and its collection of unfinished scraps to explore uncertainty, repair, and the possibility of continuing without a neat resolution.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and observant, finding emotional weight in modest details: a radiator that sometimes starts working, a boy’s repaired bridge, a leak that gets fixed, and letters that turn out to contain ordinary memories rather than revelations. Mara’s return home and her hesitation over her father’s letters give the story its quiet ache. The reader is invited to treat incompletion less as failure than as a condition of living: some things are resolved, some are still in progress, and some endings simply remain unknown.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story repeatedly returns to objects that hold unfinished effort or partial knowledge: the box of scraps, the unsent postcard, the broken bridge, the anonymous progress cards, the cropped photograph, and the unopened letters. Its moral emphasis is gentle but specific: don’t mistake what you cannot see for what did not happen, and offer practical help without pretending it solves everything. The mood stays tender and lightly melancholy, with community and small acts of repair supplying its hope.

## Evidence line
> She had been treating the scraps as evidence of abandoned plans when they were mostly evidence of the limits of what she knew.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sustained recurrence of unfinished objects, modest repair, and compassionate restraint makes these choices unusually coherent and distinctive within the sample.
