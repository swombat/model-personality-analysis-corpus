## Grounded reading

Its pathos is understated; the woman’s rest and the unclaimed food suggest kindness without resolving who it was meant for. The reader is invited to accept pause and uncertainty rather than seek a lesson or tidy ending.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds permission to rest amid the pressure to hurry, and a modest, ambiguous act of generosity. It treats the chair as a public invitation and lets its ordinary objects carry the moral claim that rest and kindness need not justify themselves.
