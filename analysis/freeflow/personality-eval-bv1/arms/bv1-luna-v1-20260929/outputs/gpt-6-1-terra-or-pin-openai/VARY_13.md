GENRE_FICTION — A lyrical fable built around an invented lost-and-found for intangible things.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle, whimsical, and quietly aching. Its invented objects make emotional losses tangible, but the story avoids a grand recovery: the girl gets only a small chance to be silly, and the narrator helps by looking away. That discreet kindness gives the ending its warmth. The reader is invited to notice how play and self-expression can return through a modest gesture, especially when someone makes room for it without demanding a performance.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds forgotten capacities—courage, patience, laughter, silliness—as objects that can be returned only when someone is ready to ask. It gives particular weight to childhood spontaneity and to the privacy that lets it emerge: the narrator pretends not to watch, and the girl laughs. The final label, “RETURNED,” frames recovery as small, tentative, and worth recording.
