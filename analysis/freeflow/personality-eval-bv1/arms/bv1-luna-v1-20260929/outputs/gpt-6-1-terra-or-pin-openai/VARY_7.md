## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A quiet, gently uncanny story in which a town’s geography slips out of alignment and draws its residents together.

## Grounded reading
The voice is calm and observant, letting impossible details arrive without alarm: a shifted moon, a vanished street, and a bakery door that opens onto another version of the town. Its pathos is modest and communal rather than dramatic; people are separated by a strange disruption, then briefly brought together over tea, buns, and a practical need for a stamp. The ending leaves the mystery unresolved, inviting the reader to share Mara’s uncertainty rather than solve it.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds ordinary places and errands—streets, a bakery, a telephone, an envelope—as the surfaces through which reality can quietly change. Its chosen moral emphasis is on neighborly accommodation: when the world becomes confusing, people make room, offer tea, and help one another carry on. The final return to the moon gives the communal episode a lingering note of uncertainty.

## Evidence line

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story is coherent and has a distinctive, recurring blend of everyday kindness and low-key unreality, though its gentle resolution keeps its emotional register narrow.
