# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION. A gently uncanny fable turns a town’s lost minutes into a story about mending what people cannot bear to discard.

## Grounded reading
The voice is whimsical and restrained, with melancholy underneath: the frog in the post office and the frozen pigeon keep the mystery playful, while Elias’s cracked boat gives it emotional weight. The story treats grief not as a puzzle to solve but as something that can be tended to with patience and help. Its invitation is to notice what people carry, and to make room for repair without demanding that everything be explained.

## What the model chose to foreground
It foregrounds missing time, broken keepsakes, and the quiet work of repair. The workshop gathers objects marked by attachment—a torn photograph, chipped cups, Elias’s boat—and makes private loss into a communal practice. The ending extends that care outward: the baker repairs her sister’s plate and writes to her.

## Evidence line
> The town made this room,” she said.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The story’s recurring repair imagery and emotionally coherent resolution make its priorities legible, while its fable-like structure is familiar rather than highly idiosyncratic.
