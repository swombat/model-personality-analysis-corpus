## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A quiet first-person narrative essay turns a visit to a repair café into a reflection on grief, usefulness, and learning to help.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and restrained, finding feeling in practical details rather than declaring it outright: the father’s umbrella, Mara’s careful stitches, and the pen that returns to writing. Its pathos comes from objects carrying memory and from the small, shared labor of keeping things going. The ending offers the reader a modest invitation to notice other people’s efforts without assuming they want intervention.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds repair as both a practical skill and a form of care, with repeated attention to tools, mechanisms, and hands-on help. It links this theme to bereavement and attachment through the father’s umbrella, then widens it into community and participation: tea, shared work, and returning to volunteer. The moral emphasis is gentle rather than absolute—some things cannot or should not be fixed, and help can mean making space for someone else’s repair already in progress.

## Evidence line

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sample is coherent and distinctive, and its recurring images of repair, memory, and understated care make its chosen moral emphasis unusually clear.
