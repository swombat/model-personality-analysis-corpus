# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld
Source model: `withheld`
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION. A quiet, tightly controlled short story uses a sudden change in the world to draw out its characters’ grief, attention, and connection.

## Grounded reading
The voice is restrained and attentive to small sounds and gestures rather than dramatic explanation. The story’s pathos gathers around ordinary absences: a dead wife, a failing kettle, the sea’s silence, and the neighbors’ previously unnoticed lives. It invites the reader to listen alongside Mara, finding intimacy not in a revelation or grand solution but in noticing, helping, and adjusting a whistle.

## What the model chose to foreground
The chosen images are repair work, water, and sound: Mara tries to mend a kettle while the sea’s silence makes familiar noises newly legible. The story gives grief a quiet presence, then turns listening into a form of renewed connection, from fixing a neighbor’s pipes to the man asking for a quieter whistle. Its resolution is modest and practical, not an explanation of the sea’s behavior.

## Evidence line
> Only when it stopped did she understand how much it had been saying.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the coherent pattern of quiet attention, understated grief, and care expressed through practical acts is distinctively sustained across the story.
