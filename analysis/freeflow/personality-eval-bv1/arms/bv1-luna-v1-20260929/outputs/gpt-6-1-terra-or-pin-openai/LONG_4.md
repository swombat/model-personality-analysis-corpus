# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld
Source model: `withheld`
Condition: LONG

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A quiet, observational slice-of-life story follows a librarian through a day of small acts of service.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and attentive to the work that makes public generosity possible: paper in the printer, a password reset, a fine quietly cleared, a lost mitten moved where it can be found. Its pathos stays restrained; people’s difficulties appear in practical details rather than dramatic revelations, and Mara helps without claiming to know more about them than she does. The recurring lost-and-found objects give the story a gentle, accumulative tenderness. It invites the reader to notice care as ordinary labor, and to see a library not just as a storehouse of ideas but as a place where people can ask for help without being made to feel small.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds public access as something built through uncelebrated maintenance and human attention. It pairs civic ideals—knowledge, reliable information, opportunity—with concrete obstacles such as rain, overdue charges, forgotten passwords, and the need for a printed form. Its moral emphasis is on patient assistance, privacy, and modest acts of leniency; its recurring objects—the mitten, the hold-shelf books, the printed confirmation page—make those themes tangible.

## Evidence line
> It is hard to exchange ideas freely when the printer has run out of paper and you need a form before noon.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sustained attention to practical care and understated dignity is distinctive and reinforced across many scenes in this coherent story.
