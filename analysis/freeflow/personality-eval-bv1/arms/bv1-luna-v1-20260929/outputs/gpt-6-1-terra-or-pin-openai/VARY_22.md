# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A quietly magical short story, built around a librarian who helps people recover losses that cannot be entered on ordinary forms.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle, whimsical, and attentive to how grief can change shape without simply disappearing. Ada cannot return the husband’s laugh or the boy’s vanished sense of summer, but she offers care, a photograph, and a way for people to become useful to one another. The mayor’s lost town becomes the story’s hinge: belonging is recovered not through authority or nostalgia, but by listening to neighbors and gathering their imperfect accounts. The reader is invited to treat small acts of attention as a practical kind of repair.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story selects lost intangible things—laughter, a feeling, a town—and gives them the homely containers of jars, labels, shelves, and boxes. Its mood is tender rather than sorrowful; loss leads toward shared work and local memory. The ending returns to the jar, now empty and ready for someone else’s need, making care feel ongoing rather than conclusive.

## Evidence line
> After that, people began coming into the library with stories about the town.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: The recurring lost-and-found imagery and the resolution through patient, communal attention make this a distinctive, coherent sample of a quietly humane imaginative mode.
