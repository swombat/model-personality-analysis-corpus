# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld
Source model: `withheld`
Condition: SHORT

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION. A compact, gently fable-like story centered on a librarian and a boy who make meaning together.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm and quietly contemplative, finding emotional weight in ordinary things: a worn library, a gray stone, rainwater in a vacant lot. Its pathos comes from the suggestion that people arrive with questions they cannot yet articulate, and that a patient exchange can help them imagine a way forward. The reader is invited to value curiosity and shared storytelling over a thing’s conventional worth.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds the library as a place of welcome, the librarian’s attentive generosity, and the boy’s shift from asking whether a stone is valuable to deciding it is “worth a story.” Its mood favors imaginative renewal: even the town’s puddles become windows, and the ending imagines the whole town as newly available to possibility.

## Evidence line
> The stone had fit his palm, survived a storm, and made him curious enough to visit the library.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story coherently and repeatedly favors curiosity, gentle care, and imaginative transformation, though its fable-like style is familiar rather than highly distinctive.
