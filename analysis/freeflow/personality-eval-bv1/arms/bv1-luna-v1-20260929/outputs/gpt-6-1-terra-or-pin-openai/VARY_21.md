Evaluator: withheld

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A compact, gently fable-like story built around a town’s slow clock and the person who finally repairs it.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and restrained, finding emotional weight in practical objects and small local habits. Mara’s precision meets a town that has made peace with lateness; the anonymous note quietly reveals that the clock’s delay may once have mattered to someone waiting for a loved one. The story does not explain who wrote it or resolve the loss. Instead, Mara keeps the blue buttons and gives the clock an easy, unremarkable explanation. This invites the reader to notice how private grief can persist inside ordinary things, without turning the story into a lesson about it.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds communal accommodation, precision, and the traces people leave behind: the bent mechanism, the note, and three blue buttons. Its mood is gently whimsical at first, then subdued. Its moral emphasis is understated: Mara repairs the clock, but does not pretend to know the story behind its relics or make that story public.

## Evidence line
> The town clock was seven minutes slow, which suited almost everyone.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The story’s coherent object motifs and restrained handling of implied grief make its choices distinctive, while the emotional resolution remains deliberately quiet.
