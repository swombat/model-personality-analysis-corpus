## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A compact literary vignette builds toward a tender homecoming through a recurring image of sound and color.

## Grounded reading
The voice is quiet and economical, letting the father’s longing show through a small ritual rather than explaining it outright: he opens the window to listen for his daughter’s bell. The pathos comes from years of waiting and the gentle contrast between his teasing and his faithful attention. The ending invites the reader to see home not just as a place, but as something made recognizable through a shared, idiosyncratic memory.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds patient parental devotion, the passage of years, and the power of a private childhood invention to endure. Its central objects—the automated lighthouse, the painted bell, and the boat returning to the pier—turn waiting into an act of listening and make homecoming feel intimate rather than grand.

## Evidence line
> “The bell still sounds blue,” he told her.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the vignette is coherent and distinctive in its linked bell-and-color motif, while its brevity and familiar homecoming arc make the evidence less revealing than a more developed piece.
