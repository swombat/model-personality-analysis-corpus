## Grounded reading
The voice is calm, attentive, and gently reverent toward ordinary labor. Warmth, scent, and small sounds make the bakery feel sheltered from the city, while the speaker’s solitude reads as chosen ease rather than loneliness. The closing image invites the reader to share in the small wonder of work done before anyone else wakes.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds nocturnal quiet, practiced craft, and bread-making as transformation: flour, water, and yeast become food for a waking neighborhood. It sets the bakery’s predictable, fragrant interior against brief signs of the outside world—a truck, a cat, approaching commuters—and treats patient work as a steady source of comfort.
