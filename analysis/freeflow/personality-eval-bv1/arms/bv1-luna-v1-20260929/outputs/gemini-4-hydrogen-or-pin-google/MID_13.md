## Grounded reading
The voice is calm, observant, and gently persuasive. Sensory details—the cobalt dawn, damp wool, diesel exhaust, and socks tumbling in a laundromat—give the essay’s affection for overlooked moments a lived-in texture. Its pathos lies in the worry that constant stimulation erases the quiet intervals where attention and memory can wander. The reader is invited not to reject modern life, but to let a delayed flight or a few minutes in the rain remain unfilled.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds thresholds: dawn, transit, waiting rooms, and the stretches between major life events. It contrasts these unowned, passive moments with calendars, productivity, and phone-driven stimulation, then gives boredom a positive moral and imaginative value. The returning blue-light image makes noticing the brief and unpossessable feel central.
