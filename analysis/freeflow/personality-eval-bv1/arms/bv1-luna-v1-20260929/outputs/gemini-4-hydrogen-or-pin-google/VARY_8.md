## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical, carefully observed night-time vignette that turns a diner scene into a meditation on temporary freedom and quiet human care.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and contemplative, attentive to small sensory details: humming fluorescent lights, wet pavement, coffee gone sharp, and a waitress who refills a mug without demanding conversation. Its pathos is gentle rather than dramatic. The travelers and the narrator appear briefly unmoored from ordinary identities, and the diner offers them warmth without questions. The reader is invited to notice the mercy in pauses and in care that asks nothing in return.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds night, rain, fluorescent light, coffee, waiting, and people passing through. Its central moral claim is that spaces between destinations can offer relief from the demands of identity and productivity. It also treats unobtrusive service and temporary shelter as forms of kindness, then resolves with the narrator returning to the road more at ease.

## Evidence line
> There is a particular mercy to a twenty-four-hour diner.
