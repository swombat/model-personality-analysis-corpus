## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical, sustained meditation on evening light, worn objects, memory, and the value of unhurried attention.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and sensuous, dwelling on how ordinary surfaces can hold traces of time: scratches in a floor, a polished banister, notes left in an old book. Its pathos is quiet rather than mournful; things and memories fade or pass between people, but leave small marks that make continuity feel possible. The reader is invited to slow down, notice what utility and speed obscure, and accept an evening without turning it into a task or lesson.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds the amber-to-blue shift of late afternoon, aging household objects, secondhand books and their accidental keepsakes, and the uneven persistence of sensory memory. Its moral emphasis is on attention, material history, and resisting a culture of constant productivity—not as a grand argument, but as a defense of inhabiting ordinary hours.

## Evidence line
