EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical, scene-driven meditation turns an ordinary night ferry crossing into a reflection on rest and life between destinations.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and observant, building feeling through precise sensory details rather than dramatic events: diesel vibrations, wet wool, a lukewarm coffee, a seagull briefly suspended in the updraft. Its pathos is gentle—the passengers are tired and anonymous, but their shared passage feels quietly humane. The essay invites the reader to slow down and notice the interval itself, where the usual demands of arriving and performing briefly fall away.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds the ferry’s physical textures and sounds, the anonymity of nighttime travel, and the relief of being temporarily “between shores.” Its moral emphasis is that small, unrecorded moments matter even without an audience, and that not every meaningful part of life is an arrival.

## Evidence line
