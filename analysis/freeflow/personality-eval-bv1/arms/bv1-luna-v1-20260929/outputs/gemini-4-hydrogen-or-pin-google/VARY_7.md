## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and sensuous, attentive to the building’s sounds, temperatures, and smells as much as to Mara’s actions. Its pathos is understated: the moth has awakened into a world without food, the plants need shelter, and Mara works alone to keep a small pocket of warmth alive. The reader is invited to see maintenance not as drudgery but as a form of care, and the conservatory’s isolation as both refuge and fragile human invention.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds winter, glass and iron, failing heat, and the minute acts that preserve living things. It pairs practical repair with tenderness toward the plants and moth, then gives the scene a moral center in the idea that people build green sanctuaries because they cannot bear a season without green. The mood is hushed and melancholy but ultimately consoling: the pipes warm, the boundary fogs over, and Mara continues her rounds.
