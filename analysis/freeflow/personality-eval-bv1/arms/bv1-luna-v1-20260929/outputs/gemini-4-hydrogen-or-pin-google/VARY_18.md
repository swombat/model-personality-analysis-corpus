## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical meditation on ordinary time, built from domestic details and gentle reflections on memory and loss.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and contemplative, making modest scenes feel emotionally charged without turning them into dramatic events. Its pathos comes from how easily everyday life disappears: a lukewarm cup, keys by the door, a stranger’s small routines. The piece invites the reader to pause and notice, offering attention itself as a quiet response to impermanence. Its tenderness is deliberate but restrained, ending not with a revelation but with breathing and letting evening arrive.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds late-afternoon light, household objects and habits, unrecorded lives, imperfect memory, and writing as an effort to preserve what cannot be kept. It repeatedly treats ordinary scenes as worthy of care—the dog inspecting fence posts, the refrigerator hum, the water glass—while emphasizing that their value does not depend on becoming a turning point or a story. The moral emphasis is gentle attention rather than achievement or control.
