## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and reverent, finding emotional shelter in sensory details: cool asphalt, a kettle’s hum, coffee, birdsong, and the changing sky. Its pathos comes from the contrast between this unhurried interval and the obligations that will soon return. The reader is invited to share a brief permission to exist without demands, and to regard ordinary morning quiet as something almost sacred.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds dawn as a threshold between private stillness and public routine. It selects domestic rituals and natural sounds as anchors for calm, and closes on renewal: the day is “still unwritten,” even as the peace is explicitly temporary.

## Evidence line
