## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical reflection built around the tactile details of secondhand books and the imagined lives they carry.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm, intimate, and gently contemplative. It turns ordinary marks—a receipt, a leaf, a coffee ring, an underline—into openings for imagining another reader’s life. The pathos is quiet rather than mournful: people remain anonymous, but their traces allow a brief sense of connection. The closing gesture invites the reader not to erase those traces, but to add their own and pass the book onward.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds physical objects as records of lived experience, especially worn books and the evidence left by previous readers. It contrasts the texture and aging of print with the cleanliness of digital reading, and favors continuity over ownership: a book becomes a shared conversation that can outlast any one reader.

## Evidence line
> Things absorb the lives lived around them.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the recurring attention to tactile traces, anonymous connection, and the deliberate choice to preserve rather than erase them gives the sample a coherent and distinctive emotional center.
