## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A reflective miniature essay uses the traces left in used books to explore how solitary reading can become a connection across time.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm, observant, and gently lyrical, turning small physical details—a coffee ring, a brittle clover, a pencilled objection—into signs of another person’s life. Its pathos is quiet rather than mournful: the unknown reader remains unknowable, but their pauses make them briefly present. The piece invites the reader to see wear and marginalia not as damage but as evidence of shared attention, and ends by encouraging them to add their own marks.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds used books, accidental keepsakes, handwritten marginalia, and the contrast between an individual’s ink marks and mass digital “popular highlights.” It treats personal traces as more intimate than aggregated data and presents reading as both solitary and potentially shared. Its closing image of a book as a trail makes continuity between readers the central comfort.

## Evidence line
> A digital highlight tells you what a crowd thought; ink on paper tells you what *one person* felt, in a specific moment they never expected anyone else to see.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sample is coherent and stylistically distinctive, with recurring attention to ordinary objects as evidence of human connection.
