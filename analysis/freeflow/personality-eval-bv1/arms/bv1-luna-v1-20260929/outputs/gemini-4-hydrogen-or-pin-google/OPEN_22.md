EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical reflective essay that turns the history of reused parchment into a sustained metaphor for memory and personal change.

## Grounded reading
The voice is measured and gently wonder-struck, moving from material history to city streets and then to the self. Its pathos lies in the reassurance that change does not erase what came before: old selves and experiences persist as texture, not simply as burdens or regrets. The reader is invited to notice traces—in a city’s layout, a remembered accent, a scent—and to find comfort in continuity beneath reinvention.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds palimpsests as a way to connect historical material, urban space, and personal memory. Its chosen images—scraped parchment, buried roads, muscle memory, and scent as “ultraviolet light”—serve a moral emphasis on persistence: experience is not wholly wasted, and a person is never cleanly deleted.
