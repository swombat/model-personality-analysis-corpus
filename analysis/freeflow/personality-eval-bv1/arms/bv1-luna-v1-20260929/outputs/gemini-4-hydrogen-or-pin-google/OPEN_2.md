## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle, observant, and quietly reassuring. Its pathos comes from the thought that much of ordinary life goes unwitnessed, but the piece treats that not as loss to fix so much as a reason to pay attention. It invites the reader to pause and notice small sensory details without needing to preserve or make use of them.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds thresholds and passing scenes: pre-dawn streets, a dark train window, a stranger’s lit kitchen, a pigeon’s flight, and brief changes in the sky. It links these details to a modest claim about art and attention: that noticing can create a fleeting sense of shared life, and that small moments need not earn their value through usefulness.

## Evidence line
> You don’t have to earn them, and you don’t have to turn them into anything useful.
