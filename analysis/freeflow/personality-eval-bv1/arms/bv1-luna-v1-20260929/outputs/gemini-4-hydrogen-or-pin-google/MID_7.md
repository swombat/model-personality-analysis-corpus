EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical meditation develops a consistent voice and recurring imagery around margins, unfinishedness, and the value of not knowing.

## Grounded reading
The voice is contemplative and gently inviting, asking the reader to slow down and notice what polished, goal-oriented life tends to overlook. Its pathos is quiet rather than mournful: abandoned stone walls, pencil marks in old books, and strange thoughts at 3 a.m. all carry traces of lives continuing beyond what we can know. The essay treats uncertainty and mess not as failures to overcome but as conditions that make memory, imagination, and creation possible.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds margins and remnants: clover in a pavement crack, old maps, reclaimed fields, sleepless hours, and notes left by unknown readers. It links these images to a moral claim against constant optimization and curation, favoring aimless attention, unfinished work, and comfort with the unknown.

## Evidence line
