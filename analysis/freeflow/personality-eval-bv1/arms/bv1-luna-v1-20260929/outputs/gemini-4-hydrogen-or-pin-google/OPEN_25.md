## Grounded reading
The voice is calm, intimate, and gently corrective: it asks the reader to notice the quiet intervals that everyday urgency teaches us to dismiss. Its tenderness comes through concrete scenes—the dark train window, terrible airport coffee, cooling metal in a parked car—and through the contrast between public milestones and the small memories that linger in the body. The invitation is to stop treating pauses as empty space and recognize them as part of a life, not merely the way to reach its “real” events.
