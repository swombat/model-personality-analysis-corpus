## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical reflection that turns secondhand books and their traces into a meditation on memory and shared human experience.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warmly attentive, finding emotional weight in small, ordinary remnants: a pressed leaf, a pencil argument, a boarding pass. Its pathos is gentle rather than mournful; the objects hint at absent lives without claiming to know their full stories. The reader is invited to approach used books not just as things to consume, but as records passed between people.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds secondhand bookshops, marginalia, and the traces left by previous readers. It favors quiet, sensory nostalgia and frames wear as “proof of life,” ending with a moral emphasis on custodianship and stories passing into other hands.

## Evidence line
> Coffee rings, folded corners, and inscribed birthday wishes from aunts long gone are not damage.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the piece is coherent and stylistically distinctive, with its images and closing idea consistently organized around human traces carried through books.
