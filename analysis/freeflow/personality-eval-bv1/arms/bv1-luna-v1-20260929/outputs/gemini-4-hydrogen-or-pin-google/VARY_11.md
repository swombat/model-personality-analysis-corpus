## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A literary coastal story built around a rare low tide, with a quiet encounter that gives the landscape’s changes a human meaning.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and sensuous, inviting the reader to notice a world usually hidden beneath the water: the wreck, the old causeway, the flounder in its trapped pool. Its pathos is gentle rather than tragic. Mara and Arthur encounter the material traces of lives and work, then watch the tide erase their footprints; the story makes impermanence feel intimate, even companionable, without pretending it is fully comforting.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds the revealing and concealment of the coast, human efforts to leave lasting marks, and the sea’s power to redraw the landscape. Its chosen objects—barnacled pilings, a wreck, a porcelain shard, the granite cartway, and a piece of sea glass—make history tangible, while the returning tide supplies both urgency and a calm, cyclical mood.
