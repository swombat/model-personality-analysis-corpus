## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical personal essay that turns a familiar object into a meditation on how readers leave traces for one another.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm, observant, and quietly sentimental, finding emotional weight in modest details: a coffee mark, a pencilled line, a ticket tucked into a book. Its pathos comes from the possibility of intimacy across time rather than from loss or drama. The reader is invited to treat books as objects that hold evidence of ordinary lives, and to see leaving a mark as a generous way of joining that chain.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds secondhand books, marginalia, and the physical memory of paper against the “clean, uniform” quality of screens. It chooses connection across generations as its moral center: private reading can become a shared encounter, and small traces can outlast their makers. The essay repeatedly returns to marks left in books, building its emotional argument through those concrete objects.

## Evidence line
> A pristine book is an unread monument; a marked-up one is a lived-in house.

Medium — The sample sustains a distinctive, tender voice and develops its chosen objects into a coherent reflection on memory and human connection.
