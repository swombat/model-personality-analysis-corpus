## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A quiet, atmospheric story turns a night clerk’s routine handling of lost objects into a meditation on memory, care, and return.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and observant, finding tenderness in practical details: damp wool, the green ledger, a tag tied carefully around the flute. Its pathos comes from holding back rather than resolving the flute’s mystery; the clerk imagines possible histories, then respects the limits of what can be known. The reader is invited to share that attention—to see an ordinary act of cataloguing as a small form of care.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds objects people leave behind, the bodily traces they carry, and the stories that cannot be recovered from them. Harbor fog, night work, and the repeated routines of the lost-property office create a subdued mood. Its moral emphasis is modest: handle what is entrusted to you gently, and recognize the value of helping something—or someone—find its way back.

## Evidence line
> And on those mornings, handing it back across the scratched Formica feels less like clerk work and more like returning a piece of the world to its proper weight.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the recurring attention to tactile objects and quiet acts of care gives this sample a distinctive, coherent sensibility.
