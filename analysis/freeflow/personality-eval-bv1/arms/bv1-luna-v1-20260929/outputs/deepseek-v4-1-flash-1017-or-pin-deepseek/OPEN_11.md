## Grounded reading

The piece also turns that attention toward the model itself, but carefully: it acknowledges the temptation to claim experience while distinguishing what it can describe from what it cannot verify. The ending offers a modest invitation to notice transitions rather than hurry through them.

It also foregrounds a tentative account of AI selfhood, locating the model “between prompt and response” while resisting a confident claim about experience.
