## Grounded reading

A coin that buys nothing, a locked library, a kitchen after dinner: ordinary scenes become ways to think about attention, love, and grief. The emotional center is a wish to notice and honor what might otherwise pass unseen; the reader is invited to slow down, look closely, and treat everyday acts of care as meaningful.

## What the model chose to foreground

The images of windows, doors, libraries, light, and the passing dog recur as ways to frame attention and connection.
