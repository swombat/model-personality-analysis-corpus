# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: OPEN

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A lyrical reflection uses recurring sensory images and a clear emotional invitation to notice ordinary life more closely.

## Grounded reading
The voice is calm, attentive, and gently persuasive. Its pathos comes from resisting the sense that life is passing in a blur: small sights and sounds become ways to feel present, while writing them down becomes “resistance against the blur.” The reader is invited not to find grandeur, but to become available to what is already around them.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds attention, patience, and the ordinary as a source of meaning: a chipped cup, a screen door, a tree watched through a season, a bakery opening at five, and November light. It presents noticing and recording as modest acts of presence, culminating in the wish to feel “a little more alive” to everyday existence.

## Evidence line
> Attention is one of the few things we can give away freely and still have more of afterward.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the piece sustains a coherent, distinctive motif of attention through concrete images and a repeated contrast between the ordinary and the blur.
