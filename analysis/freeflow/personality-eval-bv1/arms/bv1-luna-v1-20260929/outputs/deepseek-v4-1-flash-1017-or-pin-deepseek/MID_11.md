# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: MID

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. The piece uses a personal essay voice and recurring images to make a reflective argument about unfinished creative work.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle, orderly, and reassuring, turning a private source of shame—the folder of abandoned or delayed projects—into a place of attention and curiosity. Its pathos comes from small, unfinished things: the imaginary city, the unbaked bread, and the lighthouse keeper who remains afraid. Rather than urging the reader to finish everything or to abandon ambition, it invites them to value lingering with an idea without demanding a product from it.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds unfinished projects, quiet attention, and resistance to treating every activity as productive or marketable. It repeatedly returns to the folder marked LATER and its contents, and uses the friend’s chair restoration as a parallel image of an ongoing conversation rather than a task to conquer. The moral emphasis is that curiosity and ordinary experience can matter without being converted into completed work.

## Evidence line
> The almost is not the enemy of the done.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the coherent, recurring imagery and considered moral distinction between completion and attention make this a distinctive expressive sample.
