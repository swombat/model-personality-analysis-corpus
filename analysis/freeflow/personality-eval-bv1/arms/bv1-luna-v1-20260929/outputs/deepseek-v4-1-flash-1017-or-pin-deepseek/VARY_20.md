EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical first-person meditation uses concrete memories and everyday scenes to explore attention, loneliness, and repair.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and observant, moving between sensory detail and self-questioning without forcing a tidy resolution. Its pathos comes from regret and loneliness softened by small acts of care: a mother humming at the sink, a stranger on a train, returning to a difficult conversation or an ordinary task. It invites the reader to feel recognized rather than instructed, especially in its insistence that attention and return can make the world less lonely.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds rooms, windows, rain, books, radios, food, and other ordinary objects as ways into memory and feeling. It gives particular weight to attention as a form of love, forgiveness as repeated effort rather than a single dramatic event, and the possibility of companionship between strangers. The ending favors an unfinished but sustaining act of writing over a solved problem.
