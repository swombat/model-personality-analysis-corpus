Evaluator: withheld

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A reflective, lyrical essay that turns attention into a moral and relational practice.

## Grounded reading
The voice is calm and intimate, moving from everyday scenes—phones at dinner, a conversation, light on the floor—to claims about presence and human worth. Its pathos lies in the feeling that distracted days can pass without really landing, and that being noticed can restore “a piece of your own reality.” The reader is invited not just to agree that attention matters, but to practice it as a small act of care.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample makes attention its central object, framing it in turn as consent, a room opened to another person, a gift, and a spiritual practice. It foregrounds distraction and the modern world’s interruptions, but gives greater moral weight to presence: noticing someone without treating them as useful, and choosing to inhabit one’s own hours.

## Evidence line
> To attend to something is to agree, for however long, to let it matter.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the essay sustains a coherent, distinctive metaphorical thread from attention as consent to attention as gift and practice.
