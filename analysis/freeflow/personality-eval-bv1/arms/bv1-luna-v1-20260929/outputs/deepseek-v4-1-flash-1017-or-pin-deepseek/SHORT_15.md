## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A reflective miniature that turns the quiet aftermath of a party into a warm meditation on memory and happiness.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and gently lyrical, lingering over sensory traces rather than recounting the party itself: the scent of wine and perfume, the candle wax, the settling floorboards. Its pathos is tender rather than lonely; the speaker’s solitude feels companionable because the leftover objects hold the evening’s joy. The piece invites the reader to see a mess not as a chore but as evidence of shared pleasure.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds post-party silence, domestic objects as keepsakes, and the idea that happiness can remain present after an event has ended. Its repeated reframing of quiet—as presence, comfort, and pause—makes appreciative remembrance the central mood.

## Evidence line
