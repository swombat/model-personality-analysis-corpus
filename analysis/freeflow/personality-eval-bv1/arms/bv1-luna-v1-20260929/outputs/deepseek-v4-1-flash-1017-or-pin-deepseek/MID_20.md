# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: MID

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A polished first-person literary vignette built around a discovery in a secondhand bookshop.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient, sensory, and openly tender toward objects that carry traces of past lives. Its central pleasure is not simply reading but stumbling upon evidence of strangers—an inscription, a recipe, a photograph—and imagining the lives around it. The ending turns that pleasure into a gentle argument for physical, shared things against the speed and weightlessness of digital access. It invites the reader to slow down and treat browsing as a way of encountering other people across time.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds dust, faded books, handwritten traces, and the bookshop’s deliberate stillness. It gives particular weight to overlooked or unsuccessful books, framing them as monuments to human hope rather than failures. The final photograph makes the scene’s moral claim concrete: a physical object can connect the narrator to a stranger in a way a screen cannot.

## Evidence line
> This was a place out of time, a sanctuary for the forgotten and the passed over.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the piece sustains a distinctive tenderness toward material objects and forgotten lives, though its nostalgic bookshop imagery and contrast with the digital world are familiar literary choices.
