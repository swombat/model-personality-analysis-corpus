## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A reflective first-person meditation turns the difficulty of choosing what to write into a gentle exploration of ordinary objects and memory.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and self-conscious without becoming harsh about its indecision. It finds emotional weight in small, familiar details—the cold coffee, the chipped mug, the rain—and invites the reader to see them as records of lived time. The mood moves from blank-page vertigo toward permission to wander; its pathos is quiet, rooted in missed possibilities and the comfort of accepting an unfinished walk rather than demanding a masterpiece.

## What the model chose to foreground
Under the open prompt, the sample foregrounds the pressure of possibility, the histories objects accumulate, and the value of stillness and unchosen paths. It favors modest sensory scenes over grand claims, then makes a modest moral claim of its own: freedom can mean following the next thought without needing to make the result profound.
