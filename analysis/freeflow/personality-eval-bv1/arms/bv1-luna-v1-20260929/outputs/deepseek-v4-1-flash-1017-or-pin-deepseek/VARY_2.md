## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical, reflective vignette uses an ordinary rainy afternoon to explore family photographs, memory, and the passage of time.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and observant, turning small sensory details—the cold tea, faded handwriting, a jacket, the rain—into openings for reflection. Its pathos comes from the gap between what the photographs prove happened and what the narrator can actually remember or feel. The ending offers a modest form of acceptance rather than recovery or revelation: the box remains open, and the evidence of lives having happened is “enough, perhaps.” The reader is invited to linger with uncertainty instead of forcing the past into a tidy story.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds rain as a shared, enveloping presence; photographs and household objects as traces of lives; and memory as partial, inherited, and changeable. Its moral emphasis is tender attention to people and things that might otherwise fade into the background.

## Evidence line
> The rain began as a rumor on the roof, a soft percussion that might have been pigeons or a branch scraping the gutter.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The sustained imagery and recurring movement from concrete objects to reflections on memory make this a distinctive, coherent expressive choice.
