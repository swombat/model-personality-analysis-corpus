## Sample kind
GENERIC_ESSAY — A polished, coherent meditation on writing, with a clear arc from creative paralysis to relief but familiar imagery and ideas.

## Grounded reading
The voice is earnest and reassuring, treating the blank page as both a source of pressure and an opening for discovery. Its pathos comes from the struggle to begin, then the relief of lowering the demand for profundity. The reader is invited to see writing—and, by extension, everyday storytelling—as a modest way to make meaning and connect with others.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds the blinking cursor, the blank page as untouched snow, the sensory bustle outside the window, and the act of writing as selection, discovery, and connection. Its moral emphasis is that beginning imperfectly matters more than waiting for a profound idea; the ending recasts the first step as a small victory.

## Evidence line

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
