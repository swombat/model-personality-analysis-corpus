# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical meditation on writing that uses recurring images and a personal memory to give its reflections emotional shape.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm, contemplative, and consciously poetic, moving between grand claims about writing and intimate details like a grandmother’s hands kneading dough. Its pathos comes from treating words as fragile bridges between minds and as voices that outlast their makers. The reader is invited into that bridge: not merely to observe the meditation, but to help bring its “ghost” to life.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds the cursor as both pressure and possibility, writing as communication across distance and time, and memory preserved in marks on a page. It also chooses tactile domestic imagery—yeast, sugar, dough, a kitchen—to frame patience and creative process, then returns to mortality, readership, deadlines, and the blank page. The closing image makes writing feel temporary yet renewing: words reach another mind, then the cursor waits to begin again.

## Evidence line
> The blinking cursor is a patient tyrant.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the piece is coherent and distinctive in its recurring cursor, ghost, and bread-making images, though its reflections on writing and connection are familiar in subject and tone.
