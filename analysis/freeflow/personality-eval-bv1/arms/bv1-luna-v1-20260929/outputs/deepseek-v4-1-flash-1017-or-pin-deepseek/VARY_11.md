# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. The sample is a lyrical meditation organized around ten imagined jars, with concrete scenes and a recurring reflection on memory, connection, and ordinary life.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm, observant, and gently wistful, making emotional meaning from modest details: a screen door, a lost glove, a child on a station platform, a sleeping dog. Its pathos stays low-key; it acknowledges grief and loneliness without turning them into spectacle. The jars give the reflection a playful structure, while the story about passing a note through the wall makes its central invitation explicit: to notice other lives and risk reaching toward them. The reader is invited to find significance not only in what is cherished or remembered, but also in empty space and everyday company.

## What the model chose to foreground
Under the freeflow condition, the model chose a sequence of ordinary sensory objects and scenes—jars, laundry lines, traffic lights, food, trains, dogs, and laughter—as containers for memory and feeling. It emphasizes brief, partly unknown moments shared with other people, the small losses that linger, and the value of leaving room for what has not yet happened. Its moral center is humane and ungrand: attention, companionship, hospitality, and beginning again.

## Evidence line
> The past is not a place we can return to, but it is full of doors that open when we are not paying attention.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — the sustained jar motif and repeated turn from commonplace detail toward connection and acceptance make this a distinctive, coherent expression of a humane reflective voice.
