## Grounded reading
The voice is warm and quietly persuasive, inviting the reader to notice meaning in ordinary, imperfect objects rather than in grand gestures. Its pathos comes from specific traces of use—a crossed-out name, uneven darning, a patched tire—and from the suggestion that repair can hold both affection and loss. The reader is not asked to fix everything; the ending offers a modest invitation to begin with one loose button.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds repaired household objects, the hands and effort behind their repair, and the moral claim that worn things—and, by extension, people and relationships—need not be discarded. Its recurring emphasis is on care made visible through small, imperfect acts.

## Evidence line
