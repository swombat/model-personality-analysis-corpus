## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical first-person meditation turns ordinary errands and household objects into a reflection on memory, attention, and presence.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle, observant, and quietly wistful, finding emotional weight in what might otherwise pass unnoticed: a faded receipt, tea left in a cup, a stranger’s umbrella, a man watering a fern. Its pathos is restrained; loss and uncertainty appear as traces rather than dramatic revelations. The narrator invites the reader to slow down and notice how small objects and gestures can serve as proofs of connection and ways of staying present.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample repeatedly foregrounds palm-sized objects and ordinary rituals as containers for meaning: the pear-shaped candle, the tea ring, the lemon, the spoon, the watering plant, and the soup-making at the end. Its mood favors patient attention over grand revelation, and its moral emphasis is that small acts and details help hold a life together. This is a distinctive choice of scale and feeling for the freeflow condition.
