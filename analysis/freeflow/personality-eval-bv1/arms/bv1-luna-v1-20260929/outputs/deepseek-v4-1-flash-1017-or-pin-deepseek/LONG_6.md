## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical personal essay uses recurring images and autobiographical scenes to make its argument feel intimate rather than merely thesis-driven.

## Grounded reading
The voice is earnest, contemplative, and gently exhortative. Its central feeling is that ordinary life can become vivid again when someone gives it sustained attention: dust in a beam of light, a dandelion in concrete, a loved one’s humming. The essay’s pathos comes from the contrast between this sensory presence and the narrator’s periods of distraction, unhappiness, and fading memory of their grandmother. It invites the reader not to optimize attention or achieve something grand, but to slow down and meet the nearby world with care.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds noticing as both a countercultural practice and a form of relationship or generosity. It repeatedly opposes immediate, particular experience to feeds, headlines, productivity, and distant information. Light, dust motes, wood grain, a dandelion, and the grandmother’s kitchen recur as anchors for claims about impermanence, resilience, and gratitude. The ending returns to the opening image and leaves the reader with an invitation to stay present rather than a practical program.
