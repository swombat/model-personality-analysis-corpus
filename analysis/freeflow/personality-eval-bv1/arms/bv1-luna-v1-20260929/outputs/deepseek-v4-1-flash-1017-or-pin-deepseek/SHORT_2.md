## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A reflective miniature essay that uses walking to explore attention, solitude, and freedom from productivity.

## Grounded reading
The voice is calm and gently intimate, inviting the reader to notice how a walk can loosen thought and restore a sense of space. Its pathos is modest rather than dramatic: ordinary details—a cat on a fence, bread in the air, light on brick—become small openings in routine. The reader is encouraged not to make walking another achievement, but simply to pay attention.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds walking without a destination, sensory attention to familiar surroundings, and the value of being unproductive. Its moral emphasis is that a person’s worth need not be measured by output, and its recurring mood is quiet permission: to be alone, idle, and briefly returned to oneself.

## Evidence line
> Walking does not solve life, but it makes life feel roomy.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the repeated movement from ordinary sensory details to a clear defense of unmeasured time gives the sample a coherent, recognizable sensibility.
