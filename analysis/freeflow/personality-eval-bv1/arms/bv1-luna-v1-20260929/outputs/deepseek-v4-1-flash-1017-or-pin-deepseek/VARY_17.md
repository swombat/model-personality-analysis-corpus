## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical meditation built from sensory details and ordinary scenes, with a clear but gently resisted theme of attention.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant, calm, and lightly self-questioning. It finds pathos in the passing quality of things—a childhood memory that surfaces and recedes, a cat that is not quite the narrator’s, a hot cup of coffee that requires waiting. The piece invites the reader to notice without turning noticing into a grand lesson; its repeated qualifications keep its tenderness from becoming too certain or sentimental.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds domestic morning rituals, small encounters on the street, unstable memory, and the effort to be present rather than caught in imagined arguments or futures. Its recurring objects—kettle, light, cat, squirrel, coffee, window—make attention feel tangible. The moral emphasis is modest: ordinary moments need not be important to deserve care.

## Evidence line
> The point is to notice it while it is there.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the piece sustains a distinctive, coherent focus on attention and impermanence, though its reflective lyrical style is familiar.
