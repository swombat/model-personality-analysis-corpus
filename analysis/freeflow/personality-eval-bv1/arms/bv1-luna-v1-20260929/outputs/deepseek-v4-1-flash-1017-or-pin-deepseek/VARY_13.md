# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A polished magical-realist story turns a fantastical corridor into a route toward apology, attention, and ordinary change.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and quietly instructive, inviting the reader to see everyday details as meaningful without making the magic grand or frightening. Mira’s regret about her mother and her habit of counting small things give the story its pathos; the warm teacup, sleeping fox, and rainy Tuesday make reconciliation feel tangible. Its resolution offers comfort, but not through a new life or a clean slate: Mira returns to her apartment, calls her mother, and begins to look up.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds unfinished business—especially a six-year-overdue apology—and the possibility of repair through attention and action. It gives unusual weight to small, ordinary objects and observations: mismatched teacups, cracked tiles, a chalk fox, and the details Mira has been counting. The moral emphasis is that being right or imagining another life matters less than noticing what is already here and choosing to act.

## Evidence line
> The corridor had grown longer.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story coherently returns to attention, regret, and small acts of repair through distinctive recurring objects, though its consoling magical-realism style is familiar.
