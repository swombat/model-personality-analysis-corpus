## Sample kind

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and contemplative, inviting the reader to slow down and notice how commonplace things gather meaning. Its pathos comes through memory’s imperfect hold on childhood and in the gentleness it asks us to extend to others. The prose keeps returning to images of rooms, weather, and windows, making the reader feel addressed not as an audience to persuade but as a visitor briefly sharing the writer’s view.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds attention to ordinary objects—the cold cup, spoon, chair, rain-streaked window—and the way memory transforms them. It also chooses courtesy, gentleness, and resistance to speed and algorithmic influence as moral concerns. Its recurring mood is calm and wistful, with a final image of writing as an offered window rather than a demand for agreement.

## Evidence line
> I think this is what writing is, in the end: a kind of trespassing with permission, a way of saying, Here is my window, here is my rain, here is the small arithmetic of my attention.
