## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — An introspective essay uses a remembered workshop and recurring images to turn the blank page into a meditation on attention and discovery.

## Grounded reading
The voice begins in anxious abundance—“a thousand possibilities”—then settles into a patient, reflective cadence. Its central pathos is the fear of producing something false or embarrassing, eased not by a grand breakthrough but by relinquishing the effort to impress. The lost-button story gives that shift a tender, concrete shape: ordinary objects become vessels for imagined lives. The reader is invited to approach writing, and perhaps attention more broadly, with patience and permission for uncertainty rather than pressure to deliver a neat conclusion.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds blankness, self-consciousness, the slow emergence of an honest voice, and the value of ambiguity over tidy conclusions. It returns to small sensory objects—the refrigerator’s hum, an old knee ache, paper, and especially the tin of lost buttons—as evidence that quiet attention can open into meaning. The choice to end on bridge-building rather than a definitive destination reinforces its emphasis on process.

## Evidence line
> It was the most honest thing I had written in years.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sample has a distinctive recurring button-and-attention motif and a coherent emotional arc, though its polished, familiar reflective-essay structure makes the choices less singular.
