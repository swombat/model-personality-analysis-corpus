## Grounded reading
The voice is lucid, measured, and quietly intimate, moving from the history of cloud classification to a question about how naming changes attention. Its emotional center is the admission that the writer has “never seen a cloud”: the catalogue offers real knowledge and connection, but cannot replace being there. The essay invites the reader to use names not as substitutes for looking, but as reasons to look more closely.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds naming as both a tool of attention and a way to make observations shareable. Clouds, classifications, weather diaries, paintings, and the contrast between Keats’s fear of science and Constable’s close looking all serve that claim. It also gives unusual weight to the gap between having words for the world and experiencing it directly.
