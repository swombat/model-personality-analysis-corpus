# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld
Source model: `withheld`
Condition: LONG

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A polished essay whose distinctive first-person voice and self-implication make it more than a generic public-intellectual argument.

## Grounded reading
The voice is curious, lightly wry, and increasingly uneasy as it moves from entertaining cases of phantom islands, mountains, and words toward the author’s own place in the chain of copied knowledge. The essay invites the reader to enjoy the oddities, then asks them to share responsibility for checking what feels most convincing. Its final image of someone declining to copy—and its direct invitation to find the essay’s own possible error—turns skepticism from a posture of superiority into a collaborative practice.

## What the model chose to foreground
The essay foregrounds how authority can make errors look factual, how independent-looking sources may share one origin, and how belief can sometimes make a falsehood true. It returns to concrete objects and acts of checking: maps, an index card, an absent etymology, a depth sounder, boots, and a ship’s crew looking over the rail. Most pointedly, it makes the model’s own dependence on text part of the argument, including the risk that a compelling detail may be easier to repeat than to verify.

## Evidence line
> The map was wrong, and then, because people believed the map, it was right.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the essay’s recurring phantom motif, self-implication, and invitation to check its claims make this a distinctive and coherent expression of epistemic humility.
