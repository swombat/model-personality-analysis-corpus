The essay finds a democratic usefulness in meaninglessness, then lets the reader feel what efficiency costs when search hides unexpected neighbors. ## What the model chose to foreground

It also selects an intimate, low-stakes pleasure—encountering “marzipan” beside “mascara” and “masochism”—and closes by treating the loss of such accidents as real, if minor.
