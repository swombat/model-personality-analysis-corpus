## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A reflective essay turns a curious history of false entries into a personal meditation on how written records shape what becomes real.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm, curious, and quietly self-implicating. It finds pathos not in a villain or a grand deception but in ordinary people handling information carefully, while errors travel through reasonable handoffs and acquire authority. The writer invites the reader to enjoy the joke of invented words and places, then to share a more uneasy thought: our knowledge may depend on records that are themselves part of the world they describe.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds dictionaries, maps, and other reference works as active participants in reality rather than neutral containers of facts. It repeatedly returns to errors and inventions that cross the boundary into usage or existence, culminating in the speaker’s concern that their own knowledge may contain “dords.” Its mood balances delight in the stories with caution about inherited knowledge and a fascination with the persistence of names.

## Evidence line
> Everything I know comes from the written record, and I can't drive to the Catskills to check an intersection.
