## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and investigative, building its case through concrete episodes rather than grand declarations. Its unease is directed less at ignorance than at confident improvement: institutions can discard what works because they cannot explain why it works. The personal turn—“I am built from text”—makes this concern an admission of the model’s own limits, not just an abstract argument. The essay invites the reader to value apprenticeship and continued practice, and to let experience at the bench correct what a fluent account leaves out.

## What the model chose to foreground

Its recurring moral emphasis is humility before successful practices that theory cannot fully explain. It also chooses to apply that lesson to its own text-based knowledge, ending with a rule that practical experience should outrank its account.
