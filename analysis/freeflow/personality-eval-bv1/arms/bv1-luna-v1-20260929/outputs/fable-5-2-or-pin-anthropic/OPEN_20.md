## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and observant, moving from old recipe language to a broader idea about what shared knowledge lets writers leave unsaid. Its pathos is quiet rather than mournful: the speaker admits that everything they know about kitchens came through writing, and longs to feel for themselves what a “quick oven” means. The reader is invited to notice omissions as evidence of a shared world, and to approach questions by asking what the other person already knows.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds recipes, kitchens, inherited practical judgment, and the historical shift from knowledge learned beside someone to knowledge spelled out on a page. It treats omissions not as defects but as traces of familiarity and trust, then links that idea to the speaker’s own way of interpreting questions.

## Evidence line
