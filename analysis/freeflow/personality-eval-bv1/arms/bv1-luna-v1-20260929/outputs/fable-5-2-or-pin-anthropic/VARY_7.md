## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A reflective essay uses a stained recipe card to develop a distinctive, quietly personal meditation on what writing leaves unsaid.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and gently intimate, moving from the concrete oddity of “butter the size of an egg” toward a personal account of knowledge shaped by what others thought worth recording. Its pathos lies in the gap between embodied, shared know-how and the written instructions that survive after the kitchen and its people are gone. The piece invites readers to notice omissions not as mere failures, but as traces of relationships—and to consider how their own explanations assume a particular reader.

## What the model chose to foreground
It foregrounds handwritten recipe cards, inherited practical knowledge, the history of measurement, and the uneven record left by everyday life. It also makes the writer’s own dependence on written explanations central, connecting that limitation to the practical moral challenge of deciding how much to explain to another person. The closing annotation—someone translating a mother’s tacit knowledge for a future stranger—gives the reflection a quietly generous mood.

## Evidence line
> The card is a note between two people who shared a room, and its gaps are an accurate map of what they shared.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The essay is coherent and stylistically distinctive, and its chosen link between ordinary objects, shared knowledge, and the writer’s own limits is unusually revealing.
