## Grounded reading
The voice is lucid and measured, guiding the reader from an ordinary fact—the alphabet’s familiar sequence—to a larger trade-off between making knowledge meaningful and making it easy to retrieve. Its quiet pathos lies in the loss of browsing and shared practical skills as sorting disappears into machines. The essay invites readers to see a supposedly neutral system as a consequential choice, useful precisely because it refuses to explain or rank the world.

## What the model chose to foreground
The essay foregrounds the alphabet’s unexplained durability, medieval resistance to alphabetical order, and the contrast between knowledge arranged to teach and knowledge arranged to retrieve. It treats convenience and neutrality as genuine strengths, while noting their cost: less understanding through the structure of a reference work, and less awareness of the sorting itself.

## Evidence line
> Alphabetical order works because it means nothing.
