Its invitation is to notice the philosophical stakes hiding inside technical history, without treating the shift as a crisis; the ending leaves the cylinder in its vault, still worth watching.

The moral emphasis falls on the gap between universal access in principle and practical access in reality. The essay’s chosen bridge from metrology to broader human systems is understated but deliberate.
