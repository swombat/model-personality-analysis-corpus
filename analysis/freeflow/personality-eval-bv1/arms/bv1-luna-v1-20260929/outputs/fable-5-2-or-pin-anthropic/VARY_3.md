## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A literary vignette uses an essay about pictures and words to frame a quietly affecting scene of siblings negotiating an old hurt.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and gently self-aware, attentive to small domestic details without making them feel ornamental. Its pathos comes through restraint: the woman lifts the kettle so her brother can sleep, then corrects the crossword without undoing his work. The piece invites the reader to notice how care can take the form of leaving room for another person, and how words make that meaning visible by choosing what to linger on.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds the difference between seeing a scene and knowing its history: the chipped kettle, the brother’s glasses, the wrong crossword answer, and the years of estrangement behind them. It favors quiet gestures over confrontation, and presents attention—the choice of which chair, object, or detail matters—as a way of giving meaning.

## Evidence line
> A picture could never tell you which chair matters.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the scene is coherent and stylistically distinctive, with its recurring attention to ordinary objects carrying emotional weight.
