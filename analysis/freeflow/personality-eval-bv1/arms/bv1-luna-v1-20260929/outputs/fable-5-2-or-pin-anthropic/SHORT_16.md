## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A personal, image-rich reflection turns scraps of medieval everyday life into an argument for the intimacy of marginalia.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm and quietly delighted by the ordinary: a tired hand, a cat’s paw prints, a monk’s wish for a drink. Its pathos comes from shrinking the distance between past and present, not by making history grander but by noticing how familiar boredom and small pleasures are. The reader is invited to value accidental traces alongside official records, and to imagine future readers finding human presence in today’s mundane messages.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds complaints, work-worn bodies, cats, and informal notes left beside scripture and law. It repeatedly contrasts the intended importance of the formal text with the lasting human appeal of the aside, ending with grocery lists and “running late, sorry” as possible records of our own lives.

## Evidence line
> The margins tell us what it was like to be there, which is what we most want to know and what nobody thinks to write down.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the distinctive choice to treat mundane traces as a source of human connection recurs across the sample and is developed with a coherent, inviting voice.
