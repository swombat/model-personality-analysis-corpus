## Grounded reading
The voice is lucid and gently wonder-struck, moving from precise historical detail toward sympathy for an object that was once beyond measurement. The cylinder becomes a figure for a lonely kind of authority: it could settle every disagreement but could not itself learn from comparison. The closing image invites the reader to feel relief at its demotion into an ordinary, measurable thing.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds the tension between universal ideals and imperfect physical standards: a measure meant “for all times, for all peoples” depended on a protected object in a French vault. It lingers on uncertainty, institutional authority, scientific revision, and the human costs of pursuing exactness, especially Méchain’s concealed discrepancy and torment. Its final emphasis is not triumph alone, but the cylinder’s release from being the unquestionable definition.
