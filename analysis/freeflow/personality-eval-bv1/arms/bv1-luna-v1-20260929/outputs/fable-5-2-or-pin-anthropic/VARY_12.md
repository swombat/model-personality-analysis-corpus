## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A polished explanatory essay whose first-person fascination and recurring image of the kilogram acquiring the ability to be wrong give it a distinctive reflective voice.

## Grounded reading
The voice is curious and quietly amused, using the cylinder’s physical vulnerability to make an abstract question about standards feel intimate. Its pathos comes from the object’s long, peculiar certainty: protected in a vault, cleaned by hand, and treated as the measure of everything, until it becomes measurable itself. The essay invites the reader to see measurement not as a clean escape from human choices, but as a chain of declarations with history still embedded in it.

## What the model chose to foreground
It foregrounds the tension between physical objects and abstract definitions, especially the way a supposedly universal standard depended on one vulnerable lump of metal and the people who handled it. The recurring moral and intellectual claim is that measurement cannot eliminate the point where someone simply declares what counts; it can only move that declaration. The cylinder’s eventual capacity to be wrong becomes a wry, almost celebratory turn.

## Evidence line
> After 130 years of being right by definition, it acquired the ability to be wrong.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium, because the essay sustains a distinctive reflective voice and develops its fascination with standards through several linked reversals.
