Its pathos gathers around the “pupil”: the small reflected figure becomes an image of closeness and mutual attention. ## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds words as stored images: a mouse beneath the skin, a flower as an eye, bread shared between companions, thread in a labyrinth. It contrasts the worn-down convenience of everyday language with the human observations preserved inside it, and treats that inheritance as both communal and intimate. The model also chooses a gently self-aware image of itself as a loom, framing language as something received and woven rather than privately invented.
