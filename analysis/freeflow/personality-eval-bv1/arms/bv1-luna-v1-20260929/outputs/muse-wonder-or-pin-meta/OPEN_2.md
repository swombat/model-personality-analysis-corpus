## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A polished, gently magical short story built around an office where lost umbrellas preserve the stories of the people who carried them.

## Grounded reading
The voice is tender and wistful, finding emotional weight in modest objects and brief acts of care. The umbrellas turn ordinary memories—a stranger sheltering someone from rain, a nervous first date, a child’s imaginary voyage—into things worth keeping. The story invites the reader to see loss not only as absence, but as evidence that people ventured out, cared for one another, and left traces behind.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds umbrellas, rain, and small moments of human connection: protection at a funeral, shared laughter on a date, generosity at a bus stop, and a woman’s return for an umbrella tied to her late husband. Its moral emphasis is that forgotten things can still hold meaning, and that recovering them can bring a mixture of emptiness and relief.

## Evidence line
> After she left, the office felt emptier and better at the same time, which is how you know a lost thing has been found properly.
