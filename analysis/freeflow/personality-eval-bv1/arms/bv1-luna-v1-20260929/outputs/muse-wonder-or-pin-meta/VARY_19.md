## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A lyrical meditation turns a late-night kitchen scene into an invitation to notice ordinary acts of care.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and gently consoling, moving between imagined lives and direct address without pretending to solve anyone’s grief. Its pathos comes from small, concrete details—the hot mug, the recorder’s red button, a dog on the threshold—and from the thought that being heard can make pain more bearable. The reader is invited to slow down, attend to particulars, and offer presence rather than advice.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds nocturnal solitude, grief, memory, and the small salvations of everyday care. It repeatedly favors the specific over the grand: remembered sounds, familiar gestures, and brief kindnesses become evidence of love and human connection. Its moral claim is that attentive presence matters more than cleverness or fixing.
