## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A compact, fable-like story built around recurring characters and a quiet emotional resolution.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and lightly whimsical, finding pathos in the keeper’s fear of leaving a question unanswered rather than in danger alone. Its refrain of listening turns uncertainty into a form of care; the reader is invited to see humble attention and continuing curiosity as meaningful ways to remain present.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story centers on a lighthouse, a ledger, bread, chalk questions, and the repeated act of listening. It favors gratitude, honest uncertainty, intergenerational continuity, and ordinary life’s “miraculous” quality over dramatic triumph.

## Evidence line

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
