## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION. A compact, lyrical story follows a lighthouse keeper whose garden and habits outlast his working life.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and attentive to humble particulars: oil drums, a tin cup, stolen hats, soup, and logbook entries. Its pathos comes from quiet displacement—the keeper retires inland, where the air feels empty, while the garden carries his presence forward. The story invites the reader to find a life’s meaning not in grand events but in patient routines and what they leave behind.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds stubborn growth in an inhospitable place, habitual attention, and a community’s small acts of care. It treats repetition as a way a whole life accumulates, and ends with renewal through seeds that outlast the keeper’s tenure.
