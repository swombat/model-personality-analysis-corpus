## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A polished, intimate meditation whose recurring images and personal voice make it more distinctive than a thesis-driven public essay.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and gently self-correcting: it begins with affection for the quiet hour before a town wakes, then acknowledges how memory edits the past and how age changes what deserves attention. Its pathos is restrained, rooted in ordinary losses and textures rather than grand tragedy—the closed bakery, a grandmother’s radio, a book’s weight, a plant that remains alive. It invites the reader to notice and value the uneventful parts of life, while making room for sadness and wonder without insisting either cancels the other.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds early-morning streets, strangers’ rituals, memory’s selective editing, handmade objects, finite attention, and small acts of kindness. Its moral emphasis is that everyday life deserves attention and gratitude, and that tenderness and brutality can coexist without being flattened into a reassuring story.

## Evidence line

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
