## Grounded reading
The voice is wry and intimate, with affection for small, slightly ridiculous objects and the people who cannot quite discard them. Its pathos comes from letting the drawer hold ordinary memories—an old key, a dead charger, a vanished restaurant—without insisting that grief must be grand to matter. The reader is invited to recognize their own clutter not as mere disorganization but as a quiet, imperfect archive.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds household detritus, hesitation, and the persistence of past lives in practical objects. Its central moral claim is that seemingly useless things can preserve evidence of ordinary days and earlier versions of ourselves; the drawer’s eventual inheritance extends that history beyond its current keeper.
