## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A polished, lyrical fable uses a magical house and its jars to explore memory, attention, and grief.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and patiently observant, finding emotional weight in small sensory details: cut grass, library glue, bread rising, the sound of a key. Its pathos comes from people carrying unfinished apologies, bereavement, and heartbreak without being “solved.” The story invites the reader to treat attentive recollection as a kind of mercy—and to begin living before waiting to become someone else.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds memory as something personal rather than collectible: the jars prompt each visitor’s own forgotten experiences instead of preserving a fixed past. It gives particular moral weight to attention, shared quiet, and ordinary specificity, while leaving Mara’s own-name jar unopened until she is ready to meet the present. The resolution favors a continuing practice over revelation or cure.

## Evidence line
> The jars, Mara realized, were not recordings.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The sustained, distinctive emphasis on attention as mercy and practice makes this a revealing expressive choice, while its polished fable form shapes how directly the voice comes through.
