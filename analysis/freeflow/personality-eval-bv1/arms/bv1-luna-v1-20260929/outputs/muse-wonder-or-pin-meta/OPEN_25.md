## Grounded reading

It finds safety and possibility in pauses rather than arrivals: the child beneath the table, the airport traveler between selves, the person lingering in a doorway. The pathos comes from treating these small, easily skipped moments as places where people feel sheltered, notice one another, and change. The reader is invited to slow down and recognize the significance of what might otherwise seem incidental.

It also foregrounds modest acts of attention—holding an elevator, keeping a grocery list in a returned book, noticing a dog’s sigh—as a counterweight to headlines and efficiency. Under the freeflow condition, it chooses wonder in the ordinary and presents lingering as a way of becoming.
