## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A lyrical first-person meditation uses an ordinary day by the harbor as a frame for recurring reflections on uncertainty, memory, and care.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and contemplative, finding emotional weight in small encounters and objects: a chipped mug, wet gravel, plums, library dust, harbor lights. Its pathos is quiet rather than dramatic; grief and absence remain present, but are held alongside humor, sensory pleasure, and the possibility of continuing. The repeated language of maps, weather, knots, and navigation invites the reader to treat uncertainty not as a problem to solve but as a condition to meet with patience and attention.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds ordinary acts of persistence, the way memory attaches to food and places, and the idea that attention is a form of love. It favors modest moral claims—keep going, loosen what panic tightens, make room for wonder—over triumph or certainty. The harbor, lighthouse, fog, maps, and stars give those claims a consistent vocabulary of imperfect navigation.

## Evidence line
