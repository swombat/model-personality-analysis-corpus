Its pathos comes from treating incompletion not as loss or failure but as evidence of having reached toward something: half-used notebooks, abandoned projects, and kindnesses that “just make a day bearable.” The writing invites the reader to slow down and notice small particulars rather than demand a solution or tidy ending. It favors beginnings over endings and noticing over solving, culminating in the claim that reaching can be enough. These choices give the freeflow a consistent ethic of patience and attention.
