## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A lyrical meditation built around an imagined museum gives the piece a distinctive, sustained emotional frame.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and wistful, but it resists treating unrealized plans as tragedies. The imagined exhibits make room for paths not taken, abandoned inventions, and feelings that resist naming; the museum’s curator simply nods at them rather than forcing them into a moral. The invitation to the reader is to regard unfinished possibilities with tenderness and to feel less alone in having them.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds alternate lives, hesitation, unnamed feelings, and the value of not forcing a choice simply because it has begun. Its recurring objects—a train ticket, a wedding dress, blueprints, slips of paper—make imagined possibilities tangible, while the moral claim is that an unrealized path can still matter without being a failure.
