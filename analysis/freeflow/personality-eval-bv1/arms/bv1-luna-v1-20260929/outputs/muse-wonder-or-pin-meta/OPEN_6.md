# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld
Source model: `withheld`
Condition: OPEN

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A reflective, metaphor-rich piece that turns ordinary repairs into a warm meditation on care and shared competence.

## Grounded reading
The voice is affectionate and conversational, with a self-aware streak (“if I'm allowed to get sentimental”) that makes its sentiment feel offered rather than imposed. Its pathos comes from imagining the history held in worn objects: a chipped teapot, a darned sock, a radio that belonged to someone’s father. Repair becomes both practical and relational—people learn together, share skills, and keep what has mattered to them. The reader is invited to regard wear not as evidence of failure but as a record of use and attachment.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds mending, repair cafés, visible signs of wear, and the difference between replacing something and learning to care for it. It gives moral weight to shared competence and to keeping objects—and, by extension, people—worthy despite their breaks. The mood is tender and quietly defiant: a mend is a “refusal” to discard a good story.

## Evidence line
> The break is almost never random.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sustained movement from repaired objects to intimacy, community, and human vulnerability makes this a distinctive and coherent choice of subject and mood.
