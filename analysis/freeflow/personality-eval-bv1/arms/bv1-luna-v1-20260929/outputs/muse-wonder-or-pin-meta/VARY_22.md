## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. The sample is a lyrical meditation that moves through invented scenes and ordinary objects toward an intimate account of attention and care.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm, observant, and gently self-conscious: it acknowledges that its imagined childhood is “borrowed,” then uses that limitation to approach human experience without claiming it outright. Its pathos comes from small, recognizable gestures—a light left on, a door held, the last plum—and from the hope that words can make someone feel specifically noticed. The ending turns that hope directly toward the reader, offering companionship without demanding a response.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds useless collections, half-heard conversations, an unimportant city, and objects imagined as carrying feelings. These scenes repeatedly elevate maintenance, accidents, and ordinary tenderness over importance, efficiency, or grand transformation. Under the freeflow condition, it chooses to spend its imagined “thousand words” on noticing and on language as a way of caring for someone.

## Evidence line
> Love is often just maintenance, performed without applause.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sample’s distinctive focus on ordinary care recurs through its city, inventor, remembered phrases, and sea imagery, giving the choice a coherent expressive shape.
