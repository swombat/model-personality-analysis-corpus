## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A reflective, sensory piece that turns an unhurried city walk into an invitation to practice attention.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and observant, finding warmth in ordinary urban details: a swept step, pigeons treated as colleagues, a runner’s scarf. Its pathos is modest rather than mournful; the piece suggests that noticing small kindnesses makes strangers feel less strange and daily life easier to meet. The closing invitation asks the reader to share this practice, with a warm drink as company.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds quiet mornings, patient movement, handwritten noticing, and uncelebrated acts of care. It gives more moral weight to these small gestures than to “grand speeches,” presenting attention to the ordinary as a way of changing how one moves through the day.

## Evidence line
> These moments rarely make headlines, but they stitch a place together more honestly than grand speeches ever could.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the piece coherently returns to attention, patience, and small kindnesses, though its polished, familiar imagery makes the choices less individually distinctive.
