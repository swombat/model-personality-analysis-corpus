## Grounded reading

Its pathos is quiet rather than sorrowful; the speaker takes comfort in incompletion, imagining that pausing is not failure and that a story remains open to return. The reader is invited to share this gentle attentiveness and to regard ordinary rituals as shelter.

## What the model chose to foreground
