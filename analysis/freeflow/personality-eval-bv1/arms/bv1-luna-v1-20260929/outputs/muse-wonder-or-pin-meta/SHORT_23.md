## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. The sample is a lyrical first-person meditation built around a library’s closing hour and the imagined selves held within books.

## Grounded reading
The voice is tender, observant, and quietly enchanted: ordinary details—the baker’s floury sleeves, empty chairs, a librarian’s tap—open into metaphors without losing their everyday texture. Its pathos comes from the selves we might have become and the familiar choices that keep us from them, softened by the image of each person carrying a small, local light. The reader is invited to see kindness and routine not as solutions to darkness, but as ways of moving through it.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds libraries, unfinished possibilities, and small acts of care. Its mood is wistful but not despairing; the moral emphasis is on carrying light without requiring darkness to justify itself. The recurring movement from imagined wonder to ordinary departure gives the closing line its gentle sense of resolution.

## Evidence line
> It seems like honest work, carrying a small lamp through enormous darkness without demanding the darkness explain itself first.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sample sustains a distinctive, coherent pattern of translating ordinary scenes into gentle moral imagery, especially through its recurring contrast between darkness and small lights.
