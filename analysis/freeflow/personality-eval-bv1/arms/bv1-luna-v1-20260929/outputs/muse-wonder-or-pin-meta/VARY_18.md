Its pathos comes from Mara meeting her dead father and younger self without being asked to stay in the past. The story invites the reader to treat unfinished possibilities with tenderness, but also to return to the life they actually have.

## What the model chose to foreground
The chosen images—an inherited house, a hidden garden, family dead, doors to postponed lives, and a seed carried back—make grief and unrealized possibility the story’s central concerns. Its moral emphasis is that love and memory can be carried forward without turning them into a refuge from living. The ending favors a small, practical act of renewal over escape or grand transformation.
