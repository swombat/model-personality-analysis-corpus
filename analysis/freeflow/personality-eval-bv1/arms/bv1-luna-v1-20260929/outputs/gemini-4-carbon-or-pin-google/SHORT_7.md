EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical meditation turns a rainy city at night into a refuge from the demands of the day.

## Grounded reading
The voice is atmospheric and gently consoling, moving from vivid sensory detail—neon reflected on wet pavement, the hiss of rain, a passing car—to emotional release. Its central distinction is between loneliness and chosen solitude: the reader is invited to experience being alone as a brief, private pause rather than an absence.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds the contrast between urban pressure by day and quiet at night, with rain transforming familiar streets into something luminous and alive. It gives moral weight to rest and solitude, treating them as temporary reprieves from deadlines, regrets, and the return of ordinary demands.

## Evidence line
> There is a profound difference between being lonely and being alone, and midnight rain teaches you the beauty of the latter.
