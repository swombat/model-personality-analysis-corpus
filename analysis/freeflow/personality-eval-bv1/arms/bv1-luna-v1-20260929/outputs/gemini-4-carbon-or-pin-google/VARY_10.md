## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A polished, lyrical short story uses a sound designer’s work as the frame for a quiet encounter with grief and change.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and sensory, making small sounds—pipes sighing, a leaf skittering, a floorboard groaning—carry the emotional weight that a more dramatic scene might place on speech or tears. Clara’s recordings turn the empty house from a symbol of absence into a place still alive with ordinary continuities. The story invites the reader to listen alongside her, and to see grief not as something resolved, but as a silence that still contains the world.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds room tone, domestic sounds, and the textures of spaces, then connects them to a daughter’s final visit to her childhood home after her mother’s death. Its central moral claim is that apparent emptiness is not absolute: change and loss leave room for ongoing life, new voices, and continuity. The mood is tender and elegiac, but its ending leans toward acceptance rather than despair.

## Evidence line
> Silence, it turns out, is full of life.
