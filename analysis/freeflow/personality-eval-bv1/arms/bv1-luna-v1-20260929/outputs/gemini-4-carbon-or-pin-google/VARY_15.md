## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A polished speculative-fiction scene built around a lighthouse keeper’s encounter with rain in a dried-up sea basin.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and tactile, making routine labor—the winding gears, polishing glass, guarding the cistern—feel as consequential as the storm. Elias’s solitude is not framed simply as misery: he likes a silence that “didn’t ask anything of him,” then finds himself present for a startling return of life. The passage invites the reader to share his bodily wonder at water, while keeping that wonder grounded in fear, scarcity, and the fragile work of watching over a changed landscape.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds a lighthouse repurposed for a world without a sea, the maintenance that keeps its warning light alive, and the preciousness of water. Its central movement is from drought and quiet toward rain and tentative renewal: the desert drinks, dormant life stirs, and a small green shoot begins to uncurl. The ending favors attentive hope over grand resolution, with Elias listening to the mud dry.

## Evidence line
> The sea had retreated three centuries ago, pulled back like a sheet from an unmade bed, leaving behind the Great Basin.

Medium: the sustained sensory detail and restrained emphasis on stewardship, solitude, and fragile renewal make the sample distinctive and coherent.
