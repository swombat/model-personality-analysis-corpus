## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical, highly structured essay that uses maps and paths to explore uncertainty, memory, grief, and the value of leaving some things unknown.

## Grounded reading
The voice is ornate and inviting, turning concrete scenes—a muddy trail across a lawn, a night train, a childhood home, a familiar face caught on Street View—into reflections on how people live with change and incomplete knowledge. Its pathos gathers around vanishing places and shared memories that can no longer be revisited or explained to anyone else. The reader is invited to notice the traces people leave behind, and to resist the urge to make every silence, route, or future fully legible.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample repeatedly links cartography to human efforts to manage uncertainty: dragons fill blank margins, desire paths resist official plans, memory distorts the past, and digital maps trade serendipity for convenience while accidentally preserving glimpses of the dead. It foregrounds grief, nostalgia, and the relief of temporary anonymity, then closes on a moral claim for patience with the unknown.

## Evidence line
