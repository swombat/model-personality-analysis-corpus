## Grounded reading
The voice is reflective and gently persuasive, inviting the reader to stop treating delay as wasted time and instead notice what uncertainty makes visible. Its pathos comes from ordinary transitions—a bare apartment, an empty terminal, the breath between inhaling and exhaling—rendered as both disorienting and full of possibility. The essay offers reassurance without denying grief or anxiety: the threshold is uncomfortable, but it can also be a place of attention and temporary freedom.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds liminal spaces and states: late-night terminals, blue hour, transit sites, moving house, changing jobs, and moments of suspended breath. It connects these images to a moral claim that efficiency and certainty can dull perception, while pauses sharpen the senses and make room for presence. The recurring opposition between destinations and the “in-between” is the essay’s central organizing choice.

## Evidence line
