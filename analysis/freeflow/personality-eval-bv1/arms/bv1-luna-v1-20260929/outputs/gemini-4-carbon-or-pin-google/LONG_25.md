## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical, highly structured essay turns the history of maps into a meditation on uncertainty, attention, and the value of not knowing.

## Grounded reading
The voice is learned and theatrical, moving from cartographic anecdotes into intimate claims about how people experience the world. Its pathos gathers around a quiet loss: convenience and total legibility, the essay suggests, can make travel and memory feel weightless. The recurring image of blank space gives that concern emotional shape, while the closing appeal to leave room for mystery invites the reader to treat uncertainty not as a defect to fix but as a place where attention and discovery can begin.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds maps as expressions of power and belief, phantom islands and other errors that persist through repetition, embodied navigation, GPS dependence, and the cognitive value of getting lost. It repeatedly sets systems of control and measurement against lived experience: the map against the terrain, the blue dot against the body, and certainty against the unknown. Its final moral emphasis is that some blank spaces—in the world and in people—should remain open rather than be filled with assumptions.

## Evidence line
