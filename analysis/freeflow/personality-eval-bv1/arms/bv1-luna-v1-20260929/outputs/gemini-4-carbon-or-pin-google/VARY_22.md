## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A polished supernatural diner parable turns memory into currency and grief into a transaction with moral weight.

## Grounded reading
The voice is atmospheric and tender, attentive to worn objects and small acts of care: Jonas sorts sugar packets to give the woman privacy, and he lets her sit without rushing her. The story’s pathos comes from making consolation costly: she can leave lighter, but only by surrendering a joyful memory that is also part of what made her grief matter. The ending offers relief without triumph; the reader is invited to feel both its gentleness and the unease of forgetting.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds loneliness, grief, and the intimate physical details of a late-night roadside diner: burnt coffee, cracked vinyl, fluorescent hum, pie crumbs, and the salt flats beyond the window. Its central moral claim is that joy and grief are bound together, expressed through the fantastical register that accepts only “gold”—good memories—as payment.
