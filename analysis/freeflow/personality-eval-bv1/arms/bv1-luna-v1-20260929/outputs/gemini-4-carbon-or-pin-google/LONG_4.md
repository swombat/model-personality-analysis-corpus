# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: LONG

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A sustained, atmospheric speculative story built around a hidden town and the people who choose not to expose it.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and richly tactile, making records, tools, weather, and old materials feel almost ceremonial. Its pathos comes from the contrast between Arthur’s quiet care for places that have vanished and Elena’s confidence in systems that claim to see everything. The story invites the reader to value names, memory, and mystery without assuming that discovery or preservation must mean public access. Its ending offers a gentle, practical form of loyalty: the town is protected not by solving its mystery for the world, but by agreeing to keep it out of the official record.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds maps and archives as acts of judgment, not neutral containers of facts; it repeatedly returns to vanished places, paper records, old glass, and the blind spots of modern sensing technology. Its central moral claim is that being overlooked can sometimes protect what is fragile from extraction and spectacle. The mood is elegiac but not bleak: the town’s survival depends on attention and care, and the final exchange leaves room for a new companionship formed around that work.

## Evidence line
> A map was an argument, and sometimes the earth was polite enough to agree with it.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — the recurring archive and mapping imagery, paired with the carefully staged choice to preserve a place through concealment, makes the story’s values unusually legible.
