The essay’s pathos comes from ordinary interruption and unfinished reading, not grand tragedy. It contrasts the permanence and privacy of marginalia with the performative noise of online comments, then finds comfort in the idea that strangers share familiar experiences of loneliness, boredom, wonder, and recognition.
