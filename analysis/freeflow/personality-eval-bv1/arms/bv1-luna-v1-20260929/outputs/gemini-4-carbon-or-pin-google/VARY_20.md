# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A quiet speculative story uses a municipal archive for lost feelings to give shape to grief and remembered sound.

## Grounded reading
The voice is tender and tactile, making the impossible feel like the night shift’s ordinary work: memories arrive in jars and envelopes, carrying smells, textures, and sounds. Elias’s careful restraint—he offers no apology, only the chance to hear the laugh again—keeps the story from treating grief as something he can fix. Its pathos lies in the woman’s fear that her brother’s laugh has disappeared, and its invitation is to see memory not as possession but as something we can learn to make room for again.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds grief, sensory memory, and the objects that carry feeling: a marble-like childhood memory, an umbrella, a ledger, and a jar holding a brother’s laugh. Its moral emphasis is gentle rather than restorative: the woman cannot take the sound home, but she can remember how it belonged in her life. The rain-dark setting, sleepy bureaucracy, and final shared laughter give loss a melancholy atmosphere that opens into warmth.

## Evidence line
> You don't take the sound home—you just relearn how to build the room it lived in.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: The sustained sensory detail, tender bureaucratic conceit, and elegiac yet reparative resolution make this a distinctive, coherent signal rather than generic story machinery.
