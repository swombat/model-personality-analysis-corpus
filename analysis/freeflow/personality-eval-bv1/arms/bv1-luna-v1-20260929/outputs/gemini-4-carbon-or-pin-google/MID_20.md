EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical first-person essay turns a found train ticket into a meditation on anonymous traces, small acts of care, and the desire to reach across time.

## Grounded reading
The voice is tender and observant, drawn to ordinary objects that carry an absent person’s life: a marked book, a grocery list, a lost mitten. Its pathos comes from imagining strangers without claiming to know their stories, then finding comfort in the possibility that brief, unnoticed gestures connect people. The closing act—writing a reply and returning the ticket to circulation—invites the reader not just to admire such traces but to leave one gently for someone else.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds fragile physical traces against digital self-presentation, anonymous kindness, and a softer idea of legacy: not being remembered by name, but leaving warmth in the world. Recurring images of margins, hands, lost objects, quiet spaces, and things passed along make these choices feel central to the piece.

## Evidence line
