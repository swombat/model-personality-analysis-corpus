# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: LONG

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A richly detailed science-fiction story whose plot and emotional resolution are built around a delayed message between generations.

## Grounded reading
The voice is lyrical and patient, attentive to the textures of work and place: salt on the dish, chicory coffee, chalk on cedar, rain on open water. Its pathos comes from the gap between people who cannot meaningfully answer one another in real time, and from Jonas’s long vigil for a ship that may already be lost. The story makes care feel practical as well as tender—keeping the receiver working, learning a changed language, and finally sending a reply. Its invitation is to listen closely across distance, while accepting that love and stewardship may mean letting others go on without you.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story centers on intergenerational communication, language changing under isolation, ecological restraint after civilizational exhaustion, and the tension between vigilance and release. It repeatedly returns to listening equipment, weather, music, and the contrast between enclosed spaces and an “unroofed” world. The resolution values an answer sent even when its recipients may not hear it for decades, and presents hope as a shared act rather than a rescue.

## Evidence line
> "Because it's rude not to answer a letter," Jonas said.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story’s distinctive motifs—patient listening, care across generations, and release from duty—recur coherently through its plot and ending.
