## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION. This is atmospheric speculative fiction, organized around a solitary lighthouse keeper and a moment of rescue.

## Grounded reading
The voice is richly visual and tactile, with technical detail that makes the ruined coast feel worked-in rather than merely imagined: mercury, clockwork weights, kerosene, barnacles, and a dented thermos. Its pathos is restrained. Mara’s life is marked by abandonment and loss, but the story turns her endurance into a practical kindness: she keeps the light going so a stranger can find a safe route. The closing image of her sweeping the new shoreline off her porch leaves that care embedded in ordinary routine. The reader is invited to feel both the scale of the catastrophe and the small, steady warmth of being seen.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds ecological collapse, obsolete infrastructure, and solitary persistence. Its central image is a lighthouse still serving its purpose after the ocean has vanished; the sandstorm briefly restores the sea’s force, while the red signal makes the old light a means of human contact. The moral emphasis falls on keeping watch and helping without needing recognition.

## Evidence line
> When a sandstorm hit the flats, the desert remembered how to be an ocean.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sample is coherent and distinctive, with recurring contrasts between ruined landscapes and practical acts of care.
