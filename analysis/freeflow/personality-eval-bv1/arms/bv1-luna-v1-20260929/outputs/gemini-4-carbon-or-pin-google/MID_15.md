## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical essay that uses vivid, recurring images to turn abandoned places into a meditation on memory and impermanence.

## Grounded reading
The voice is contemplative and richly sensory, drawn to the melancholy of spaces where human activity has stopped: empty malls, silent terminals, dead forums, and a game world still running for nobody. The essay moves from unease toward tenderness, finally treating forgetting and decay not only as losses but as what makes the present feel precious. It invites the reader to recognize their own abandoned “rooms” of memory and to feel gratitude for the temporary noise of living.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds places built for crowds after the crowds are gone, then extends that image to digital communities and personal memory. Its chosen moral emphasis is that impermanence can offer relief and grace, not merely erasure. Coins left in a fountain, an unused game lobby, a childhood treehouse, and a blurred house in memory make that claim intimate and concrete.

## Evidence line
> Impermanence is the oxygen that gives the present moment its fire.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The sustained imagery and repeated movement from loss toward grace make this sample a distinctive expression of reflective, elegiac sensibility.
