## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical miniature essay that turns secondhand-book details into a meditation on anonymous connection across time.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm, observant, and gently wistful. It lingers over physical traces—a boarding pass, coffee ring, pencil marks—and uses them to invite the reader to imagine the unknown person behind an annotation. Its pathos is quiet rather than mournful: a private moment of recognition becomes a small reassurance that our feelings are shared. The reader is invited to treat reading as a conversation not only with an author, but with strangers who have passed through the same pages.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds marginalia and the ordinary remnants of previous readers as evidence of lives briefly touching. It contrasts private, unmarked new books with used books as “a crowded room,” then closes on shared feeling and the passing along of stories. The moral emphasis is on recognition, intimacy, and leaving traces for others—not on ownership or literary authority.

## Evidence line
> In that moment, the solitary act of reading fractures into a three-way conversation across time.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the piece is coherent and its recurring images of traces, conversation, and shared feeling give it a distinct emotional through-line.
