## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. The polished essay uses a distinctively tender voice to turn traces left in books and everyday objects into signs of connection across time.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and gently lyrical, moving from sensory details—the softened corners, vanilla scent, and hard-pressed ink—to an expansive reflection on what it means to leave evidence of having lived. Its pathos comes from imagining the unknown reader behind “Still hurts,” while resisting the urge to resolve their story. The essay invites the reader to look differently at worn things and recognize, in them, other lives and feelings.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds marginalia, wear, and ordinary marks as material traces of feeling and use: handwriting in books, hollows in stone steps, shine on a handrail, and stains on a recipe card. It treats the desire to leave a mark not as vanity but as a bid for recognition and connection, culminating in the image of the world as a shared, worn book passed along.

## Evidence line
> To open a book filled with someone else’s handwriting is to stumble into a quiet conversation unfolding across time.
