Its pathos comes less from any one city than from the contrast between former human life and its underwater remains; the conclusion turns that contrast into a reminder of impermanence. The reader is invited to share the role of curious explorer, though the repeated framing and catalog of examples make the piece feel more like a packaged article than a distinctive personal voice.

The sample selects submerged cities, artifacts, archaeological discovery, and the ocean’s capacity to preserve or conceal human history. It gives the examples a mood of mystery and adventure, then closes on the moral claim that human achievement is fleeting and that the sea still holds things worth seeking.
