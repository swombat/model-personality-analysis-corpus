## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A self-contained fantasy allegory in which shared dreams warn a city of danger and help its people unite.

## Grounded reading
The voice is earnest and fable-like, building an ornate dream-city before turning its imagery toward collective fear and responsibility. Joris’s burden—carrying other people’s sorrows and deciding whether to speak about a dream that is not his—gives the story its clearest pathos. The ending invites the reader to see facing fear together as a way to shape the future, though the repeated affirmations make that moral unusually explicit.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds dreams and memories as both beautiful and perilous: they can create a city, carry private sorrow into public life, and become a warning that either divides or unites people. It chooses communal courage over prophecy or individual heroism as the resolution, with friendship, shared fears, and hope presented as the means of protecting the city.

## Evidence line
> They learned that dreams were not just fleeting images that lived in the depths of the mind, but powerful tools that could shape the world around them.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The sample has a coherent, repeated moral emphasis on shared imagination, courage, and unity, though its conventional fable-like voice is not highly distinctive.
