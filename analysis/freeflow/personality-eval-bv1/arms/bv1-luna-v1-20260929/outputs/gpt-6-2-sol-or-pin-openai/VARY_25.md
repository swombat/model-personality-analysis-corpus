## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A tightly composed literary short story whose domestic details and restrained dialogue carry its emotional weight.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and understated, letting grief surface through practical tasks rather than naming it. The door, marked with the siblings’ childhood heights, becomes both a piece of salvage and a record of the family they are leaving behind. The narrator’s dawning recognition that Ruth is trying to give them both something—while allowing them to resist it—gives the story its quiet pathos. It invites the reader to attend to the small acts of care embedded in ordinary objects and shared work.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds displacement by a reservoir project, the dismantling of a family home, and sibling intimacy expressed through disagreement, labor, and memory. It makes the kitchen door, height marks, coffee filters, and an uneven table bear the emotional history of the family. Its moral emphasis is subtle: care may arrive in a form that looks like a burden, and accepting it can mean holding something together.

## Evidence line
> I’d spent the week thinking she was giving me one more thing to carry.
