Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: SHORT

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A compact literary vignette uses an invented childhood memory to explore the small mysteries left behind in books.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant, gently amused, and touched with wistfulness. It finds emotional weight in ordinary things—a lopsided cake, mismatched mittens, a key—without explaining away their mystery. The reader is invited to share the narrator’s curiosity about the lives that briefly brush against ours, and to notice how a book can hold more than its printed story.

## What the model chose to foreground
The piece foregrounds books as accidental archives: grocery lists, photographs, and other lost objects that hint at absent people. Its recurring contrast between what can be retrieved (the key) and what remains harder to recover (the photograph) gives the vignette a tender preoccupation with absence, return, and the limits of knowing.

## Evidence line
> I was nine and had never considered that anyone could stop reading in the middle of a sentence to go buy eggs.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the imagery and emotional restraint are distinctive, and the recurring lost objects give the piece a coherent, revealing shape.
