# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld

Condition: MID

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A quietly shaped literary story, using a lost-property counter to bring grief, memory, and ordinary acts of care into focus.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and observant, letting feeling gather around objects rather than explaining it outright. Bea’s reluctance to sell the shoes or the tin, Martin’s repeated delays, and the loaf brought to the counter make care feel practical and imperfect: people keep things, put off returning, and still find ways to offer something. The story invites the reader to notice what can be hidden in an object’s small details, and to accept that endings may leave traces without supplying closure.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds found and kept objects—umbrellas, repaired dancing shoes, the apple-tree photographs, the loaf—and the human histories they carry. It favors patience over administrative efficiency, setting Bea’s handwritten *coming* column against the new office’s rigid categories. Its mood is tender and rueful, with loss conveyed through delayed calls, a disconnected number, and the narrator’s repeated Thursday rituals; the final image of a woman carrying a young tree offers a modest continuation rather than a neat resolution.

## Evidence line
> Bea added a fourth column and called it *coming*.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story’s distinctive object-centered tenderness and preference for quiet, unresolved gestures are sustained across its scenes and ending.
