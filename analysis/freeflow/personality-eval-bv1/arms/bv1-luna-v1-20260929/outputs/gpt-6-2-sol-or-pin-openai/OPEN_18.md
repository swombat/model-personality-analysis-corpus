EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A compact, quietly imaginative reflection turns library-book ephemera into a meditation on the lives readers briefly leave behind.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm, observant, and gently wistful, finding intimacy in ordinary traces rather than grand revelations. Its imagined librarian and shoebox make the reader’s curiosity feel playful, while the thought of unfinished books gives that curiosity a tender ambiguity: a reader may have stopped from boredom, interruption, or because they found what they needed. The closing invitation is small and personal, asking us to look for the dog’s name rather than treating the photograph as mere debris.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds found objects—grocery lists, tickets, receipts, a feather, and a dog photograph—as evidence of private lives passing through a shared public space. It emphasizes interruption, unfinished reading, and the limits of interpreting another person’s traces, then ends on the possibility of a specific human connection.

## Evidence line

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
