# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld
Source model: `withheld`
Condition: MID

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A quiet, literary story uses a repair café and a stuck drawer to frame a newcomer’s gradual entry into community and memory.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant, dryly funny, and tender without insisting on a grand emotional revelation. Its pathos sits in practical details: the desk carried with the father, the photograph with no name attached, and the narrator’s admission that they do not yet know anyone well enough to ask for help. The reader is invited to notice how care can arrive as ordinary, shared work—and how learning to pull the drawer with less force echoes learning to live with what cannot be fully repaired or explained.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds small acts of help, the awkwardness of arriving somewhere new, inherited objects, and the limits of what memory can recover. Tea, tools, stairs, a wobbling chair, and the drawer recur as concrete forms of connection; the moral emphasis is on making room for one another without demanding a neat resolution.

## Evidence line
> I had moved to the neighborhood in January.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story’s recurring objects and restrained emotional resolution make its chosen attention distinctive and coherent, while the evidence is concentrated in one carefully shaped narrative.
