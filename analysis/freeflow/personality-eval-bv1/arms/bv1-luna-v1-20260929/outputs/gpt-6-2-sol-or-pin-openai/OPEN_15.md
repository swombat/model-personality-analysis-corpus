## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A restrained domestic story turns an attempt to record silence into a way of noticing how grief remains in ordinary sounds and habits.

## Grounded reading
The voice is quiet, precise, and gently wry: the narrator keeps restarting the recording, then notices that the interruptions are what make the kitchen feel inhabited. The pathos gathers through small objects and actions rather than an explanation of grief—the grocery circular addressed to the dead mother, the birthday candle, and the drawer that needs lifting. The brother’s recognition gives the memory a shared human scale. The piece invites the reader to listen closely to mundane traces, and to see that what sounds like “noise” may be what someone wants to remember.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds domestic sound and the practical details that outlast a person: a refrigerator starting, mail through the slot, peaches on a windowsill, and a sticking drawer. It treats grief as embedded in routines and sensory memory, with the recorder’s “room tone” becoming a frame for what remains between explicit words.

## Evidence line
