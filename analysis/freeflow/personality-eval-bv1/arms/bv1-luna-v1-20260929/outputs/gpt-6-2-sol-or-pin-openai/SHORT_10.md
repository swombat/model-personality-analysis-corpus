Evaluator: withheld

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A compact literary vignette uses an ordinary laundromat encounter to build a quiet story about music and fleeting connection.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and restrained, finding tenderness in small gestures rather than explaining what they mean. A boy tests the piano’s keys, a woman and a stranger complete fragments of a half-remembered song, and the moment passes when the dryer buzzes. The ending turns that temporary openness into a gentle loss: at the music school, the piano is marked “Please do not touch.” The reader is invited to notice how easily people make a shared space feel less anonymous—and how quickly that possibility can be closed off.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds an out-of-place piano, the ordinary labor of laundry, and brief, tentative acts of connection across strangers. Its moral emphasis is quiet: shared music can bring people together without resolving their separate lives, while rules and ownership can end the encounter.

## Evidence line
> A week later the piano was gone.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the vignette’s distinctively gentle attention to small acts of connection and its resonant piano motif make those choices salient in the text.
