## Grounded reading
The voice is quiet, observant, and gently wistful. It finds loneliness in repeated calls to broken intercoms and in a man waiting outside a closed pool, then allows connection to arrive without explanation or cure: the woman asks to wait with him, and they watch strangers get off a bus neither needs. The piece invites the reader to notice how small acts of attention can make an otherwise empty ritual shared.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds listening, deferred hope, and companionship offered without pressure. Its selected objects—the broken intercom, faded shark towel, vending-machine almonds, and unused bus—make ordinary things carry the weight of isolation and tentative connection. The moral emphasis is modest: being present with someone can matter even when nothing is fixed.
