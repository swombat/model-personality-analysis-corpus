## Grounded reading
The voice is restrained and attentive to ordinary objects: chairs, stuck-together rolls, an empty dryer, a clock that has always been fast. The sadness of the closing is conveyed indirectly through people arriving without laundry and checking clothes that are not there, rather than through speeches or overt grief. The ending offers a gentle invitation to leave some imperfect things as they are: the clock’s error is a familiar part of the place, and correcting it would make someone miss the bus.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds communal attachment expressed through practical gestures, the tenderness of shared imperfection, and the passing of a local place. Its chosen mood is subdued and affectionate; even the final exchange treats a small flaw as something worth preserving.
