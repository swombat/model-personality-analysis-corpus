## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A quiet, miniature story uses an ordinary ritual to explore attention and the difficulty of doing nothing.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and gently amused, attentive to small sounds and objects without making them carry a grand lesson. Mara’s effort to sit still gives the piece its mild pathos: even a crumb can become a task when attention is trained toward usefulness. The ending leaves her practice unfinished rather than resolved, inviting the reader to notice the small pleasure and uncertainty of continuing.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model chose domestic objects and ordinary interruptions—the tomato timer, toast crumb, flute, truck, and rain—to foreground attention, idleness, and the pressure to finish or account for time. Its moral emphasis is understated: doing nothing need not be mastered to be worth attempting.

## Evidence line
> She had come for a desk, but the desk was already sold, and the timer had a strip of masking tape on its underside.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The story is coherent and distinctive in its careful attention to mundane details, with its unresolved ending reinforcing the same preoccupation.
