## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION. A quiet literary story uses a magical postal premise to bring an estranged childhood friendship into view.

## Grounded reading
The voice is restrained, observant, and gently funny, letting guilt and tenderness emerge through practical details rather than declarations. The narrator’s memories are precise in some places and self-serving in others; the letters to the school expose the gap between the version of the past they favored and what happened. The pathos rests in small acts of care—the fish rescued from the piano, the seat saved on the bus—and in the narrator’s late apology. The ending offers repair without a grand reconciliation: a postcard asking about bread is an ordinary, open-ended way to continue. The reader is invited to notice how much feeling can gather around overlooked objects and imperfect memories.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds closed buildings as keepers of memory, letters and stamps as a means of asking the past questions, and physical details that correct or complicate recollection: the turned sleeve, the fogged window, the uncomfortable backpack seat. It gives moral weight to the narrator’s betrayal of Mara’s confidence while also preserving the friendship’s small kindnesses. Its chosen mood is wistful but not solemn, with dry humor and domestic details keeping the movement toward reconnection modest and lived-in.

## Evidence line
> The pale shapes they’d left behind were easier to read than the letters had been.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the precise recurring objects, restrained humor, and moral attention to the narrator’s faulty memories make this a distinctive, coherent sample.
