Evaluator: withheld

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. It is a carefully shaped personal narrative whose recurring objects and understated emotional turns give it more individual character than a generic essay.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant, dryly funny, and tender without announcing its tenderness. The stopped clock and the repaired rabbit make room for stories about what people keep, what they replace, and what they leave unsaid; the mother’s five extra minutes and the narrator’s small acts of attention carry the pathos. The piece invites the reader to see repair less as returning something to perfect condition than as spending time with it—and with the person attached to it.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds repair cafés, household objects with histories, and the small gaps between what family members know and what they ask. Its mood is warm but lightly wistful: the clock can be fixed, yet it reveals new stories, and the chipped bowl remains in use despite the identical replacement. The closing lunch scene makes imperfect timing and ordinary care feel sufficient.

## Evidence line
> The sign promised nothing about success.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the recurring clock, rabbit, and bowl give the piece coherent, distinctive evidence of an interest in imperfect repair and understated family tenderness.
