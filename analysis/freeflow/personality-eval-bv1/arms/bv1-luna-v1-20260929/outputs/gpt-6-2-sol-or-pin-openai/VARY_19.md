## Grounded reading
The voice is calm, gently wry, and attentive to the small sounds that make a home feel inhabited: keys scraping, a bag landing, a door sticking. Its pathos comes less from grand declarations than from Irene’s remembered habits and the narrator’s quiet recognition of them. The story invites the reader to treat ordinary, even irritating details as part of what is loved and mourned.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds sound as both a record of intimacy and an imperfect object of recovery. Grief appears alongside mustard, groceries, and the ritual of listening for someone to reach the bowl; the ending links Irene’s remembered shove to the narrator’s own familiar action. The choice to resolve the scene through a stuck door rather than a speech keeps attention on how ordinary gestures carry attachment.

## Evidence line
