# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: LONG

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A carefully shaped short story follows Mara through the closure of a lost-property office, using linked encounters to build a quiet, bittersweet narrative.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant, dryly funny, and tender without insisting that its characters’ small disappointments become grand revelations. Mara’s work gives the story a humane premise: people often arrive asking for a missing object and, as they talk, reveal what they are really trying to recover. The anecdotes about the ladder, the goat, the bean, and the keys accumulate into a sense of lives connected by ordinary acts of attention. The ending invites the reader to see Mara’s practical help with the girl’s zip as meaningful—not a cure for loss, but a way of taking care of what is immediately in front of her.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds lost and mislaid objects as carriers of memory, guilt, affection, and incomplete stories. It dwells on small, specific things—the ladder with a split rung, the brother’s index card, the bean in its jar, the sack of keys—and on the people who handle them. Its moral emphasis is modest: patience and generosity can help people find what they need, even when the original object is gone or the larger problem remains unresolved. The closing movement from the office’s keys to the girl’s envelope and stuck zip extends that concern into a simple act of care.

## Evidence line
> She was good at persuading people to tell her what was actually missing.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story’s recurring lost-object motif, consistent understated humor, and repeated turn toward practical tenderness make its chosen emotional register especially clear.
