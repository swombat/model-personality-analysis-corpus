Evaluator: withheld

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A quietly magical short story uses returned sounds to explore what people remember, lose, and choose to listen for.

## Grounded reading
The voice is tender and restrained, letting the fantastical premise sit beside ordinary details: a questionable egg, a bus route, cherries, a pan on the stove. Mara’s grief is less about recovering a perfect final message than about her lingering uncertainty over what her father meant to teach her. The story invites the reader to notice how care can be present in small acts and imperfect memories, without resolving loss into a revelation.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds sounds as portable pieces of personal history: a father’s throat-clearing, a child’s laughter, an obsolete bus announcement, and the knocks of someone hanging a picture. Its mood is wistful but gently comic, and its moral emphasis falls on attention and ordinary kindness rather than recovering a definitive answer. The ending shifts Mara from listening for the past to listening to the life around her.

## Evidence line
> For thirty years that sound had meant he was about to say something difficult or ask where the scissors were.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story is coherent and distinctive, with its recurring sound motif and its gentle resolution giving clear shape to its chosen preoccupations.
