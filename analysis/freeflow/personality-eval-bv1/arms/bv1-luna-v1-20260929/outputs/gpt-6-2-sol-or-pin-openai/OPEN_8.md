## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A quiet literary vignette uses a repaired drawer and a kept screw to give grief and family habit physical form.

## Grounded reading
The voice is restrained and attentive to ordinary gestures rather than declarations of feeling. Mara’s grief comes through in the small pleasure of opening the drawer, her brother’s hip bumping it out of habit, and the screw she cannot quite discard. The story invites the reader to find emotional weight in objects that are neither precious nor broken, and in routines that persist after the person who shaped them is gone.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds domestic repair, inherited habits, the work of sorting a parent’s house, and the difference between something being defective and simply being wrong for its job. Its moral emphasis is gentle: what is useful or worth keeping may not be obvious, and letting go need not mean treating the past as worthless.

## Evidence line
> She had meant to throw it away.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the vignette is coherent and distinctive, with its recurring drawer, hip-bump, and screw making the emotional meaning unusually clear without spelling it out.
