Evaluator: withheld

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A quietly literary story uses the lost-property office as a recurring frame for reflections on care, memory, and imperfect family ties.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and restrained, finding emotional weight in practical details: fishing line on a briefcase, warm jars of beans, a mended glove. Its pathos comes through withheld feeling rather than declarations, especially in the narrator’s strained closeness with both parents and the small acts of care that bridge it. The recurring work of describing lost things becomes a way of noticing people without claiming to know their stories. The reader is invited to attend to those details, and to see ordinary gestures—offering socks, pulling down a sleeve, sharing an orange—as forms of connection.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds lost objects and the uncertain stories attached to them, alongside small acts of practical kindness. It returns to family distance, memory’s gaps, and the limits of knowing another person: the narrator and mother remember different parts of the man with the potatoes, while a phone can announce HOME without making anyone answer. Its resolution is modest rather than triumphant: the narrator calls his father, brings him an orange, and yields the fruit to his hands.

## Evidence line
> It seemed unfair that one of us should have the man and the other the beans, and that neither of us had kept his name.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the recurring objects, restrained emotional delivery, and repeated attention to care in small gestures make the sample unusually coherent and distinctive.
