Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION. A literary short story uses a surreal workplace premise to carry a quiet, emotionally restrained grief narrative.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gently wry and attentive to small, physical details: jars, flickering bulbs, wet newspapers, a dog refusing the rain. Its pathos comes from what the narrator cannot recover—the missed call from Nell—and from the modest, imperfect memory that does surface. The story resists a consoling revelation: the recovered sound is ordinary, and the narrator’s final act is simply to stand in the rain rather than move. It invites the reader to notice how memory and care can live in pauses, gestures, and unfinished moments.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds lost and unclaimed sounds, the bureaucracy of preserving them, and the gap between recovering a memory and resolving grief. Its recurring objects—the jars, the shopping bag, the rain—make ordinary life feel worth keeping without making it grand or complete. The moral emphasis is on patience and permission: Anita offers the narrator a choice, and the story leaves that choice open.

## Evidence line
> For a while I stood where I was.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sound archive, restrained grief, and attention to ordinary gestures form a coherent and distinctive expressive design.
