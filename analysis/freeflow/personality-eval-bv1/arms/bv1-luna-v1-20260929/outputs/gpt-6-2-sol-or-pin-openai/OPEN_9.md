GENRE_FICTION — A compact literary vignette uses a family clock to give grief an understated, gently comic shape.

## Grounded reading
The voice is restrained and observant, letting small household details carry the emotion rather than naming Mara’s grief directly. The father’s and mother’s notes make the clock feel like an object still holding their voices, while the ending invites the reader to see Mara’s choice to finish her coffee as a small reclaiming of time.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds time, inheritance, and the way family sayings can outlast their original meaning. The fast clock is both a running joke and a source of ambivalence: Mara once treated her father’s remark as wisdom, but now finds herself annoyed by the clock and then by her own reaction. Its resolution is modest and bodily—she takes off her shoes and stays for coffee.
