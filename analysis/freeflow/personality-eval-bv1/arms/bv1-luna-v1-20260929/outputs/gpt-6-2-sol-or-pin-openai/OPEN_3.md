# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: OPEN

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A compact literary story builds an understated emotional turn around a recurring lost-and-found peach.

## Grounded reading
The narrator’s dry, practical voice softens as the peaches become connected to the boy’s worry about his father. The story leaves that worry indirect, giving the spoiled fruit and the father’s “another week” a quiet pathos without spelling out what is wrong. Its closing image—eating the bruised peaches over the sink—offers no repair, only a small act of accepting what is imperfect and already changing. The reader is invited to notice how care can begin with taking someone’s seemingly trivial concern seriously.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds lost objects, delayed judgment, and decay: a station clock running slow, people returning—or not returning—for what they have lost, and peaches that cannot wait another week. It chooses ordinary, perishable fruit as the hinge between a worker’s routine and a boy’s private fear.

## Evidence line
> I’d learned not to guess what people would come back for.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The recurring peaches, slow clock, and restrained ending make the story’s attention to time and imperfect forms of care distinctive and coherent.
