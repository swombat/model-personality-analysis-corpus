# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: LONG

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A literary short story uses a speculative bureau for stray sounds to frame a narrator’s grief and changing understanding of her father.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant, gently wry, and emotionally restrained. Its grief arrives through small domestic particulars—the worn measuring cup, the moved bed, a fingertip traveling down a book’s edge—rather than dramatic revelation. The narrator’s memories of her father remain mixed: affection and regret coexist with irritation, distance, and the possibility that she has misread his silences. The story invites the reader to attend to details that seem incidental and to leave room for other people’s inner lives, even when a loss cannot be neatly resolved.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds misplaced sounds as vessels for memory, especially the ordinary noises of shared domestic life. It chooses to make grief less about recovering a definitive message from the dead than about revising assumptions: the father’s quiet may have held more than loneliness, and a former family home can become meaningful to its new occupants. Repeated gestures of accommodation—offering a question one can answer, inviting the narrator back, sitting beside a stranger—give the story a moral emphasis on making room for another person.

## Evidence line
> She was offering me a question I could answer without saying it.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — the sustained, distinctive sound motif and the recurring attention to quiet acts of care make this a strong signal of the sample’s imaginative and moral emphasis.
