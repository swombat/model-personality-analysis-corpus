## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A compact magical-realist story that uses an invented market for sounds to frame grief and memory.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and lightly wry, letting the strange premise feel matter-of-fact rather than grand. Mara’s grief comes through in the reversal of an old irritation—her father’s spoon tapping—and in her willingness to buy back a moment she once lived through without knowing it would matter. The ending offers comfort without erasing loss: after the crash and laughter, someone gets another plate and dinner goes on. It invites the reader to notice the ordinary sounds that become precious in retrospect.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story centers grief, memory, and the changing value of irritation over time. It selects small domestic sounds—spoons, mugs, dishes, a cupboard—as vessels for emotional meaning, and makes its final moral emphasis one of continuation: ordinary life resumes without making the past less dear.
