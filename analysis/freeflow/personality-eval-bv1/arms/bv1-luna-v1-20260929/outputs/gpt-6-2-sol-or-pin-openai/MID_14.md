# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: MID

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A carefully shaped literary story uses a family’s lost-property work to explore grief, attention, and the uncertain afterlife of ordinary things.

## Grounded reading
The voice is restrained and observant, letting grief surface through domestic details rather than declarations: peaches eaten with evaporated milk, a wooden leg mistaken for the father, a ledger whose crossed-out entries cannot tell us whether things were returned or discarded. Its pathos comes from the narrator’s late recognition of what the father noticed and preserved, especially the warmth of a child’s mitten. The story invites the reader to look closely at mundane objects and at acts of care whose meaning may remain partial or unknowable.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds lost objects, small acts of listening, family memory, and the practical interruptions that accompany grief. It repeatedly places tenderness beside routine: tea for a bereaved stranger, a sister’s wish to call old phone numbers, and the need to turn off the water before the washing machine is collected. The final emphasis is not on recovering what was lost, but on the father’s attention to a fleeting detail that could help no one identify the mitten.

## Evidence line
> He was good at noticing the point where a story stopped being useful without making the person telling it feel they ought to stop.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the coherent, recurring attention to ordinary objects and understated care makes this a distinctive sample, especially in the final image of the warm mitten.
