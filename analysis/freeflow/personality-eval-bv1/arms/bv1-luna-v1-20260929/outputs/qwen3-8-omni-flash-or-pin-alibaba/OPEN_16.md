## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical meditation on ordinary mornings that moves from sensory detail toward reassurance and an invitation to pay attention.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle, intimate, and deliberately unhurried. It finds emotional weight in small domestic scenes—the kettle, a cup, light on dust—and uses them to make uncertainty feel permissible rather than like a problem to solve. There is a quiet undertow of regret about time passing, balanced by gratitude that another day arrives. The reader is invited not to achieve or explain, but to pause and notice.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds ordinary rituals as meaningful in themselves, and morning as a space of renewal before demands set in. Its recurring images of light, windows, weather, and breath connect attention to a sense of belonging in the world. The moral emphasis is that life need not be earned or made extraordinary to deserve notice.

## Evidence line
> I like mornings because they feel like permission.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The piece has a coherent, distinctive pattern of turning everyday sensory details into gentle reassurance, though its imagery and closing invitation are familiar forms of reflective prose.
