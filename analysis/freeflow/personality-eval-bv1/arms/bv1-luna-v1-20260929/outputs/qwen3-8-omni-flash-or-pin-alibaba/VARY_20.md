EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A long prose-poem shaped by domestic details, linked images, and reflective aphorisms.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and attentive, finding emotional weight in ordinary rituals: boiling a kettle, washing a plate, mending socks, walking to market. Memory and grief appear, but the poem keeps returning to care, routine, and the possibility of small acts that repair rather than harm. Its invitation is to slow down and notice—the kettle, a neighbor’s laugh, one face in a crowd—as a way of staying connected to other people and to the living world.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds kitchens, gardens, streets, weather, birds, and the tools of writing: page, pen, sentence, and ink. These recurring objects bind reflection on memory, impermanence, and attention to a modest moral vision: routine can be chosen rather than confining, attention is a gift, and language can mend. The mood is patient and consoling, with repeated returns to dawn, home, and ordinary gestures.

## Evidence line
> I choose the roof and let the hearth glow low.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The repeated domestic imagery and consistent emphasis on attention, patience, and repair make the sample a clear expression of a particular reflective sensibility.
