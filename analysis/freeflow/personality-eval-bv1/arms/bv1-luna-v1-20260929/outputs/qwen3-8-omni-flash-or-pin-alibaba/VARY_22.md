## Grounded reading

Its pathos is quiet rather than tragic: work is routine, but the narrator keeps finding small forms of wonder and connection within it. The closing image of beginning “the long welcome” invites the reader to treat attention as a way of making room for life, not escaping it.

## What the model chose to foreground

Its moral emphasis is modest but clear: kindness, memory, and careful attention make ordinary life feel less mechanical. Writing appears as companionship with silence and as a small, fragile act of crossing toward someone or something.
