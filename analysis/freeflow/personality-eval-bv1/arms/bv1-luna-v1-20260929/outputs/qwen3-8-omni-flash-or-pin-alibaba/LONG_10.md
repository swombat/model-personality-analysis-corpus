Evaluator: withheld

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION. This is a sustained literary narrative whose invented family history and recurring lighthouse imagery resolve into an explicit meditation on care.

## Grounded reading
The voice is elegiac but controlled: grief is present in the clocks, the unopened letters, and the emptied lighthouse, yet the narrator keeps returning to practical details rather than dramatizing loss. The grandfather’s attention to weather and maintenance becomes the story’s language for love, and the repeated light becomes an image of care that persists without recognition. The piece invites the reader to treat ordinary reliability—not spectacle or declaration—as a form of tenderness and moral attention.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model selects inheritance, family silence, and a keeper’s repetitive duty, giving special weight to small acts of observation: logging the sea, polishing a lens, staying awake. Its mood moves from longing and abandonment toward a quiet sense of continuity. The closing claim that repeated faithfulness can become grace makes the moral emphasis unusually clear.

## Evidence line
> The world will always require people willing to remain awake, and those who believe ordinary faithfulness, repeated enough times, becomes grace.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the coherent, recurring lighthouse imagery and consistent emphasis on ordinary care make this a distinctive sample of morally attentive literary storytelling.
