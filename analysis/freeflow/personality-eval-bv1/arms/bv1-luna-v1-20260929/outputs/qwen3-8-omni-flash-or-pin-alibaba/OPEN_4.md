## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical meditation whose recurring images and emotional movement give it more individual texture than a thesis-driven essay.

## Grounded reading
The voice is calm, intimate, and gently elegiac: it lingers over ordinary sensory details, then opens them into reflections on regret, change, mortality, and connection. Its pathos comes from holding tenderness and loss together rather than trying to resolve them—the joy of fresh sheets sits beside the passing of seasons, and keepsakes become small acts of resistance to time. The invitation to the reader is to notice more carefully and to meet other people as inwardly complex, without demanding that every mystery be solved.

## What the model chose to foreground
The piece repeatedly returns to thresholds and unfinished things: doorways, dawn and dusk, half-read books, unsent letters, and alternate lives. It also foregrounds small domestic pleasures and keepsakes, framing attention as a form of love and impermanence as part of what makes experience meaningful. These choices make the freeflow less about grand achievement than about noticing, carrying, and letting go.

## Evidence line
> Attention may become a form of love, and love a form of attention.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The sample has a coherent, distinctive network of recurring images around attention, thresholds, and impermanence, though its polished lyrical voice is also familiar in reflective prose.
