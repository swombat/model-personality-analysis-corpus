## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical, reflective essay whose recurring images and gentle invitation give it a distinctive voice.

## Grounded reading
The voice is tender and unhurried, finding meaning in small sensory moments without pretending they solve life’s harder problems. Its pathos comes from the contrast between distracted, overmanaged living and brief experiences of genuine presence. The reader is invited not to achieve mindfulness as another task, but to notice what is already here: warm bread, rain, another person’s separateness, light moving across a wall.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds attention as a modest, almost freely available form of contact with the world. It repeatedly returns to ordinary sensory objects—water, a cup, a tree, an orange, laundry, a dog, the evening sky—and sets them against productivity, scrolling, and mental rehearsal. Its moral emphasis is on returning to people and to one’s own life with tenderness, even while recognizing that the world can be cruel and indifferent.

Medium: the piece sustains a distinctive tenderness through recurring everyday images and a consistent invitation to return to the present.
