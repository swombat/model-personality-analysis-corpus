## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and elegiac, keeping close to the keeper’s quiet routines and the losses he carries. Its pathos comes less from dramatic confession than from small acts of care: rescuing strangers, tending the lamp, and reading letters from a life that moved on without him. The story invites the reader to see steady, often unrecognized service as a form of companionship and communication.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds isolation, memory, and humble duty through recurring objects—the lamp, logbook, rosemary, letters, and fiddle case. Its central moral claim is that a person’s care can matter even when they are forgotten or cannot witness its effects. The ending gives that claim a gentle continuity: the keeper is gone, but the light remains.
