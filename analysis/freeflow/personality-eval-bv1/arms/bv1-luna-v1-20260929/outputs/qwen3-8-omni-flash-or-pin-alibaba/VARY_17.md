## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A lyrical coastal fable whose family mystery turns inherited grief into a ritual of remembrance and renewal.

## Grounded reading
The voice is solemn and gently enchanted, building pathos from the lighthouse’s faithful beam, the sea’s losses, and Elias’s long-held uncertainty about his wife. Rather than making grief vanish, the story lets it travel through generations and find expression in names, letters, and the scattering of ashes. Its invitation is to regard remembrance and care as forms of inheritance: Mara chooses to take up the light, while Elias finds peace in “enduring love.”

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds a lighthouse, a secretive and personified sea, lost family, a recovered chest, and the transmission of responsibility across generations. Its moral emphasis is that debts may be answered through remembrance rather than payment, and that inherited roles can become chosen ones. The repeated movement between darkness and light gives the story its mood of sorrowful wonder, with the ending favoring continuity over closure.

## Evidence line
> Some debts are paid not with coin but with remembrance.

Medium — the sustained lighthouse-and-sea imagery and intergenerational grief give the story a clear, coherent shape, while its resolution follows familiar sentimental-fable conventions.
