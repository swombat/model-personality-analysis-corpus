It also chooses moral themes—care, forgiveness, and ordinary courage—and frames them as practices that accept vulnerability rather than eliminate pain. This selection suggests a preference for humane, reflective consolation, while the essay’s broad and familiar formulations keep the emphasis more universal than personally distinctive.
