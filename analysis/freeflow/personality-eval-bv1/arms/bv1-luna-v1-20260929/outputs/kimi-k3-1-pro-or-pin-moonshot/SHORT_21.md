## Grounded reading

Its pathos comes from the narrator’s recognition that trying to restore things to their original innocence can mean abandoning them. Umbrellas and rain carry the reflection from a specific object to the narrator’s apologies and losses, with the red repair becoming a small emblem of gratitude rather than shame.
