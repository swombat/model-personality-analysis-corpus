## Grounded reading

The closing thought invites the reader to treat attention as a way of keeping ordinary life from being discarded.

## What the model chose to foreground
It foregrounds insomnia, fluorescent-lit everyday spaces, brief encounters among strangers, and the possibility that shared presence can ease loneliness without requiring familiarity. Its moral emphasis is that noticing ordinary minutes gives them value.
