# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION. A compact, gently magical story uses a lost-property office to give grief and memory a physical form.

## Grounded reading
The voice is whimsical but emotionally restrained: comic details—the coffee in “three degrees of disappointment,” the artificial teeth, the wedding cake—make room for the boy’s grief without making the scene sentimental. Ada’s kindness lies in listening rather than correcting or hurrying him toward a sensible explanation. The story invites the reader to treat remembered joy not as a possession to preserve untouched, but as something that can be returned to use.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds grief, sensory memory, and the small rituals that make loss bearable. Its recurring objects—tickets, labels, shelves, boxes, and the ledger—give the unmanageable feelings of absence a quiet, practical structure. The final change from “RETURNED TO OWNER” to “RETURNED TO USE” makes care, rather than ownership, the story’s central moral claim.

## Evidence line
> Ada did not tell him he was in the wrong place.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sustained blend of whimsical objects, restrained grief, and practical tenderness makes this a distinctive, coherent expression of its chosen concerns.
