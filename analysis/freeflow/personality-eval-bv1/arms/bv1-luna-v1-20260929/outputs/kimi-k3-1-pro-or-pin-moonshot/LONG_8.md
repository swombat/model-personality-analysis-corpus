## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A literary story narrated by a house across generations, with a clear arc from possessive memory toward acceptance of change.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant, patient, and gently wry: the house measures human lives through footsteps, weather, repairs, and the wear left on objects. Its pathos comes from what it cannot know—the contents of a letter, why someone weeps—and from its gradual recognition that it mistakes keeping for understanding. The story invites the reader to feel loss without treating return as restoration: Ruth revisits her childhood home, but neither she nor the house becomes what they were. Its ending offers shelter as a temporary, meaningful act rather than a promise of permanence.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds houses as witnesses, ordinary care as repetition, and memory as partial and sometimes corrected by the living. Recurring objects—the banister, pantry marks, pear tree, and blue marble—give change a material presence. Its moral emphasis is that love can preserve, repair, replace, or simply make room; none of these can reverse time.

## Evidence line
> A house’s memory is accurate about weight and evasive about meaning.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sustained house perspective and recurring attention to ordinary objects make the sample distinctly revealing, while its polished, familiar literary arc makes the evidence less singular.
