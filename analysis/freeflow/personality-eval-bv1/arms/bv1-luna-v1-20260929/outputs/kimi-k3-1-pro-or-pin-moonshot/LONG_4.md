## Grounded reading
The voice is reflective and measured, inviting the reader to loosen the grip of efficiency without turning aimlessness into a virtue. Its humane pathos is clearest when it rejects pressure to find a lesson in grief or illness, and when it notes that free time and safe places to wander depend on material conditions. The invitation is modest: notice the world beyond the route, without making every detour another self-improvement project.

## What the model chose to foreground
The essay centers on maps, phone directions, libraries, feeds, conversations, creative drafts, rivers, and ordinary streets as ways to examine efficiency, attention, and permission to wander. It repeatedly distinguishes useful detours from waste, avoidance, or coerced hardship, and argues that slack and curiosity matter without displacing responsibility.
