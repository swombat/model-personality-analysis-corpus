## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical personal essay that uses a recurring metaphor and small observed details to make a distinctive invitation to leave room for uncertainty.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant, gentle, and quietly self-questioning. It begins with a stranger’s pencil mark in a book, then lets that encounter open onto attention, unhurried time, and lives overlooked by the page’s “center.” Its pathos is modest rather than confessional: the speaker recalls having mistaken respect for leaving no trace, and finds meaning in small acts of noticing. The reader is invited to pause without needing to turn that pause into self-improvement, and to make room for other people’s stories as well as their own unfinished thoughts.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds margins as places of response, rest, and overlooked value: pencil marks in books, unassigned minutes, a field hedge, and people whose labor or stories go unseen. The mood favors patient attention over optimization, while carefully avoiding a simple rejection of technology or a demand that every quiet moment become morally useful.

## Evidence line
> A question mark can be a form of patience: not rejection, not agreement, just a door left unlatched.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the essay’s recurring margin motif, precise everyday observations, and patient moral emphasis make this a coherent and distinctive expression.
