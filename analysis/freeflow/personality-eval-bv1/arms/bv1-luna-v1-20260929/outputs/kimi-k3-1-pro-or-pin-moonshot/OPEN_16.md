## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and observant, making the ordinary sounds and objects of night feel companionable without turning them into symbols that promise a cure. Its pathos comes from the awake person’s isolation and rumination, balanced by recognition that others are also moving through the night. The reader is invited to ground themselves in small, concrete things and to resist treating every late-night thought as truth.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds insomnia, the mind’s tendency to magnify regret and uncertainty in darkness, and the quiet labor of people awake for different reasons. A chipped cup, kettle, chair, and table recur as ordinary anchors; dawn offers no solution, only a return to familiar shapes and names. The moral emphasis is modest: company can matter without being a message, and comfort need not contain a lesson.
