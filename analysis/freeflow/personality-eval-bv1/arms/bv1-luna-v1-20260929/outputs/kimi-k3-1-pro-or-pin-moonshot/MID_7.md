## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. It is a lyrical first-person meditation whose recurring images and shifts from noticing to practical care give it a distinctive voice.

## Grounded reading
The voice is attentive, gentle, and self-questioning, finding meaning in ordinary particulars without claiming they solve grief or hardship. Rain, a cake carried through drizzle, worn household objects, and the moon briefly exposed by a departing bus invite the reader to slow down and let things remain partly mysterious. The essay’s warmth is balanced by a practical insistence: attention matters most when it becomes useful to another person.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds attention as hospitality; small sensory details as a way to meet grief and resist vagueness; care for objects and strangers; and a measured ambivalence about devices and distraction. Its moral emphasis is that wonder should lead toward concrete acts of care, not become a substitute for them.

## Evidence line
> Wonder may open the door, but love has to learn the address and arrive with something genuinely useful.
