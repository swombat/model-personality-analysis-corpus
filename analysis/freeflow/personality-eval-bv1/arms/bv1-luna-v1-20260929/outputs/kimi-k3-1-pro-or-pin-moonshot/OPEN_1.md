## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and gently philosophical, finding meaning in concrete details without pretending to fully know the lives behind them. The contrast between a real window and a screen gives the piece a quiet ache: screens offer views without weather, while actual windows keep time and admit the world’s textures. The reader is invited not to break through every boundary, but to notice what is already there with patience.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds windows as selective boundaries: they let light and attention through without erasing separation. It dwells on train reflections, glimpsed domestic scenes, the physical signs of weather and time, and the limits of screens. Its moral emphasis is restrained attention—seeing the world clearly while respecting that a lit window is “not an invitation to know.”
