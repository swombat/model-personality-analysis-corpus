## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical, image-rich essay that makes a clear argument while giving ordinary domestic details a distinctive emotional weight.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warmly attentive but resists turning attention into another obligation. It lingers over kettles, chipped bowls, errands, and small acts of care, while making room for days marked by worry or pain. Its pathos comes from the thought that unremarkable hours are not empty or merely preparatory: they are where affection, quiet competence, and much of living take place. The reader is invited to notice without having to admire everything, and to let a plain hour be enough.

## What the model chose to foreground
The essay foregrounds ordinary domestic life, repetitive care, unseen maintenance, creative practice, and the dignity of mere survival. Its moral emphasis is two-sided: ordinary days deserve recognition, but people should not be pressured to find every day beautiful or turn every moment into a lesson. The recurring household objects and routines make this chosen attention feel central rather than incidental.

## Evidence line
> A life does not have to justify every one of its afternoons.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The recurring domestic imagery and consistent refusal of compulsory cheer give this sample a coherent, distinctive voice and moral emphasis.
