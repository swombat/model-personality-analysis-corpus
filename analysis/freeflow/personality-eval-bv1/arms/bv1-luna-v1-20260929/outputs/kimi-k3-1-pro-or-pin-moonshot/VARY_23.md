# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A fable-like short story uses a magical museum to turn regret into a concrete encounter and a call to reconnect.

## Grounded reading
The voice is lyrical but controlled, balancing wistful images with dry humor—the cat’s “permanent professional disappointment,” the almost-invented soup, the curator’s patient rebuke. Its pathos comes from an ordinary, unresolved estrangement rather than a dramatic revelation: the narrator has polished an apology until there is little room left for the sister to answer. The story invites the reader away from prosecuting past choices and toward a small, present act of repair.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story centers on unlived possibilities, self-recrimination, and the way rehearsing an apology can become a substitute for making one. It gives those themes memorable objects—the blue cup, the unsent letter, the wooden box—and resolves them not through certainty about the past but through bread, a phone call, and a sister’s plain request: “don’t make a speech.”

## Evidence line
> The museum does not prove you chose wrongly.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The sustained, distinctive metaphor and its resolution give clear evidence of a preference in this sample for humane, concrete reckonings with regret over grand declarations or punishment.
