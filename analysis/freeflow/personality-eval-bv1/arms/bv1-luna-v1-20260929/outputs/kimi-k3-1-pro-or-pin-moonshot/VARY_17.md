# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. The piece is a lyrical meditation that uses an invented courtyard to move from reading and imagination toward grief, ordinary care, and permission to pause.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and self-aware, fond of turning small observations into invitations without insisting they solve anything. Its pathos gathers around grief’s sudden intrusions—the familiar tea beside the rice, the room rearranged—then settles into practical gestures like washing a cup or opening a window. The reader is invited to notice what is already here and to resist turning every morning into an accomplishment or every experience into a lesson.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds windows, keys, cups, plants, and other modest household objects as openings into imagination and memory. It chooses grief and daily maintenance as its emotional center, while giving special weight to the possibility that care can be small, ordinary, and still merciful. Its closing emphasis is not transformation but attention: let the day begin before making it a list.

## Evidence line
> This is the private carpentry of reading: I bring a few crooked boards, and you build a room with weather in it.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sample has a coherent, distinctive voice, with recurring images and a consistent preference for quiet attention over self-improvement.
