## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical first-person meditation that builds its reflections through recurring ordinary objects and personal encounters.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and tender without making every loss sacred: it notices the glove’s odd “second career,” mourns a notebook, and admits that some things are better left behind. Its pathos comes from the gap between an object’s plain appearance and the private life it may touch. The piece invites the reader to pay attention without inventing stories about strangers—to leave room for what cannot be known.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds abandoned objects, everyday labor, lost attention, and small acts of care, from propping up a glove to returning a wallet. It repeatedly resists reducing people to passing impressions, while also making room for letting go: not every loss should be recovered, and not every mystery needs an explanation. The mood is reflective and gently hopeful, ending with the city as a place of possible encounters rather than an inventory of losses.

## Evidence line
> A single glove on a fence post can change the way I see a city.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the recurring object motif, distinctive lyrical voice, and consistent emphasis on attention and humane restraint make this a revealing sample.
