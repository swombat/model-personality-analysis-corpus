## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION. A polished, lightly surreal short story uses the narrator’s departing shadow to explore a life not lived.

## Grounded reading
The voice is dryly observant and tender, finding the uncanny in ordinary details: a shadow that smells of rain, a doctor’s practical advice, a drawer repaired with a spoon. The story gives real weight to the narrator’s longing for Mara’s imagined life, then turns gently away from the fantasy of a perfect alternate future toward the imperfect life at hand. Its invitation is not to stop wondering, but to let wonder coexist with attention to what remains.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds counterfactual longing, memory, and the small acts through which tenderness takes shape: a dish towel thrown, a drawer left as a shelf, two cups of coffee. Its recurring images of shadows, doors, and light make the alternate life feel close without treating it as a destination the narrator ought to claim.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
