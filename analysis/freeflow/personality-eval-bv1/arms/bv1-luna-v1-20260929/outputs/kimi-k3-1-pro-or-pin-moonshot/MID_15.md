## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A polished magical-realist story uses a mysterious museum to bring a man face-to-face with the apology he has postponed.

## Grounded reading
The voice is lyrical and carefully composed, making regret tangible through objects and rooms: the unsent letter, the empty chair, the cold cups, the umbrella. Its pathos comes from Elias’s recognition that his sister’s grief frightened him, and from the story’s refusal to make apology a shortcut to forgiveness. The reader is invited toward repair through modest action: say the plain thing, listen, and show up again. The supernatural museum gives shape to missed possibilities, but the emotional resolution rests on an ordinary second meeting.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds regret over delayed care, sibling estrangement, and the difference between possibility and promise. It gives special weight to domestic details—curtains, coffee, cups, a spoon—and to the moral importance of an imperfect but direct apology. Its ending favors continued, ordinary effort over grand absolution.

## Evidence line

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
