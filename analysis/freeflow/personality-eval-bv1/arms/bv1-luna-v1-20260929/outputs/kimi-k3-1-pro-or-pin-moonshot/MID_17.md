## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical, scene-led meditation that turns ordinary night work into a reflection on how a city is kept going.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and tender, attentive to small gestures rather than grand declarations: a baker testing dough, a nurse interrupted mid-sandwich, a cleaner adjusting her son’s drawing. Its pathos comes from the contrast between the city’s seamless appearance and the tired bodies and quiet acts that sustain it. The essay also resists easy reverence, making room for the cost of shift work and the wish not to have to do it. It invites the reader to notice what usually goes unseen, without insisting that hidden labor is noble simply because it is necessary.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds maintenance, night work, handoffs, and the ordinary infrastructure of shared life. Recurrent objects—the sandwich, bus, drawing, traffic lights, and rising dough—make care tangible and modest. Its central moral claim is that a city’s apparent self-sufficiency depends on thousands of largely unnoticed gestures, while acknowledging that the people performing them may reasonably resent the burden.
