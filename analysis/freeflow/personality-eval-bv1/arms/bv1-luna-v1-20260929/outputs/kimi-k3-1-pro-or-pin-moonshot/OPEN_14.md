EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical prose meditation uses small, concrete scenes to make an argument about the unseen work that keeps ordinary life going.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant, quiet, and gently elegiac, finding significance in people working without an audience: a baker, a bus driver, a nurse, and a man tending a dying plant. Its pathos is restrained rather than tragic; the plant’s possible green fleck and the city’s gradual dawn offer modest hope without promising a grand transformation. The reader is invited to notice the maintenance and care that usually disappear into the background.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds unseen labor, routine acts of care, and the fragile continuity of ordinary life. Repeated objects—the loaf, the chart, the plant, the lock, the wet street—make quiet maintenance feel both practical and morally meaningful. The central claim is that a world that appears uneventful is often being sustained by people whose work goes unnoticed.
