## Sample kind
GENERIC_ESSAY — A polished, thesis-driven essay that uses the public bench to argue for the civic value of rest and shared space.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant, measured, and gently persuasive, moving from small physical details—the pale slat, the smooth armrest, the bus stop—to claims about dignity, access, and who is allowed to remain in public. Its pathos is quiet: fatigue, grief, and exclusion appear in ordinary pauses rather than dramatic scenes. The essay invites the reader to notice benches as practical accommodations and as signs of a city’s welcome, while resisting easy sentimentality about public life.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample chooses an unremarkable piece of street furniture as a lens on permission, rest, accessibility, social judgment, and memorialization. It repeatedly values modest, unbought forms of hospitality, and argues that design choices—especially hostile seating and neglected maintenance—express judgments about whose presence is acceptable. The closing image returns to the bench’s patient availability, framing public space as a place held open for whoever comes next.

## Evidence line
