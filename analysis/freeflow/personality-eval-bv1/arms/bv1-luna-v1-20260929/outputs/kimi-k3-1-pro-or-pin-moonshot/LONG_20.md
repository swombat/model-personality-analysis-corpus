## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A polished speculative-fiction story uses first contact to explore translation, inheritance, and hospitality through a recipe.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and quietly lyrical, finding emotional weight in practical details: a softened recipe card, dough slowly recovering from a touch, a loaf divided into portions. Its pathos comes less from alien danger than from distance and absence—Mara’s dead mother, senders whose fate is unknown, and a reply whose audience will be unborn. The story invites the reader to treat understanding as something enacted imperfectly, with materials at hand, and to see hospitality as a gesture that can reach beyond certainty.

## What the model chose to foreground
Under the freeflow condition, the sample makes an alien message into a recipe, then repeatedly returns to bread, cooking, waiting, substitutions, and setting a portion aside. It foregrounds patient interpretation over confident mastery, and presents care for absent or future people as a small, meaningful act rather than a cure for human conflict.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The sustained, distinctive chain of recipes, waiting, inheritance, and provision for the absent makes the sample unusually revealing in its moral emphasis.
