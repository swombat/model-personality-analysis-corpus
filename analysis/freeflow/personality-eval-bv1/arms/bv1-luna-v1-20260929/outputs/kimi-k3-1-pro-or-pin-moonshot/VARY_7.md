## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A lyrical first-person vignette turns an ordinary call to an estranged sister into a meditation on courage and change.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and gently self-mocking, finding emotional weight in chipped paint, yellow socks, leaking taps, and rain. Its pathos comes from regret without melodrama: the narrator acknowledges remembered hurts, then makes a small gesture toward reconnection rather than demanding a grand resolution. The reader is invited to see ordinary actions as openings—toward other people, and beyond the limits of one’s own room.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds windows, rain, domestic details, and memory as recurring images for change. It treats courage as modest and practical, and reconciliation as a willingness to reopen contact without pretending the past has been repaired. The repeated movement from looking outward to calling Mara gives these choices a coherent emotional arc.

## Evidence line
> Perhaps courage is usually this unimpressive: a window lifted, a number dialed, two people discussing a tap while something enormous quietly changes its mind.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sample’s distinctive domestic imagery and recurring window motif make its emphasis on small acts of courage unusually coherent.
