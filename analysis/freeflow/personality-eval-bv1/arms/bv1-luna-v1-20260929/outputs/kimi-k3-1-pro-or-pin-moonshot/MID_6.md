# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: MID

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A polished fable-like story about a station lost-property clerk who helps a boy face his wrongdoing and confronts her own abandoned music.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and carefully composed, finding emotional weight in small, concrete objects: a warm matchbox, a trembling feather, a dusty violin case. The story’s pathos comes from the gap between fear and action, not from fear vanishing; Mara and Nico both move forward while still uncertain. Its invitation to the reader is humane but unsentimental: apologize without expecting instant forgiveness, and begin before feeling ready.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story centers on lost and recovered things, especially intangible losses—courage, music, and the chance to repair trust. It favors patient order and everyday care, then tests those values against the need to break a rule. Its moral emphasis is that courage means acting alongside fear, while accountability includes accepting that forgiveness may take time.

## Evidence line
> For the first time in seventeen years, she did not feel ready.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The recurring lost-object imagery and the violin case give the story a distinctive emotional pattern, while its controlled fable structure keeps the voice relatively restrained.
