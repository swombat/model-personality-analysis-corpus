Evaluator: withheld
Source model: `withheld`
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical, scene-driven meditation that keeps returning to the ethics of attention and the temptation to turn ordinary life into a lesson.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and gently self-questioning, building feeling from modest details: the blue cup, the cat, cardboard wings, rain on the alley. Its pathos comes from restrained uncertainty—the unanswered kind message, the person’s hesitation, grief’s refusal to become wisdom—rather than dramatic revelation. It invites the reader to notice without demanding gratitude or transformation, and to let an ordinary thing remain ordinary. The closing image of the beetle “indifferent to metaphors” also playfully checks the writing’s own impulse to make meaning.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds windows and sentences as partial frames, small acts of attention, the distance and possibility involved in understanding other people, and resistance to tidy consolation. Its moral emphasis is that care need not solve anything: a message can shift the “weather” between people while leaving their history unresolved.

## Evidence line
> Perhaps good writing is not a matter of making everything meaningful, but of leaving a little space around things.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sustained imagery and recurring insistence on leaving things unconverted make the sample notably coherent and distinctive in its chosen concerns.
