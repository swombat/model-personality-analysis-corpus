# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION. A polished, gently magical story uses a museum of unrealized choices to bring its keeper toward a long-delayed call to her sister.

## Grounded reading
The voice is tender and wry, finding emotional weight in small, concrete details: a wrongly named husband, tea preferences, and a telephone waiting in a dusty room. Its pathos comes from regret that has become routine, then from the awkward courage of trying again without demanding a perfect reconciliation. The story invites the reader to treat ordinary contact as meaningful and to let some old absences remain undisturbed.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story centers on near-misses preserved as museum exhibits: swallowed words, almost-shared objects, and a call Mara postponed for years. It gives regret a literal collection, but refuses to make every almost a tragedy; the old woman’s recovered laugh loosens the museum’s rules and helps prompt Mara’s own choice. The final moral emphasis is modest: repair can begin with an imperfect, ordinary conversation, and not every trace of the past needs to be put back in order.

## Evidence line
> Some absences, she decided, did not need tidying.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story’s distinctive museum premise, recurring language of almosts, and understated reconciliation make its chosen emotional emphasis especially clear.
