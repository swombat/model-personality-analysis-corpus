## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A restrained, atmospheric fable whose central action turns an old lighthouse’s persistence into practical aid.

## Grounded reading
The voice is spare and patient, making the vanished sea and the lighthouse’s repeated upkeep feel ordinary before revealing their stakes. Tomás’s pathos comes from continuing without expecting the sea to return, then finding a way to help when danger arrives. The story invites the reader to see care as attention and service, not as a promise of restoration or recognition.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds routine, obsolete tools, and a landscape altered beyond repair: the dry harbor, abandoned boats, amber light, and salt storm. Its moral emphasis is that a warning can matter even when it cannot offer safety outright. The ending grants Tomás company and shared tea, but leaves the sea gone.

## Evidence line
