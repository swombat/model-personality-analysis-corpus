## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A reflective, lyrical essay that develops a clear argument through recurring images of ordinary work, repair, and attention.

## Grounded reading
The voice is measured and inviting, urging attention without turning slowness into a moral test. Its pathos rests on the unnoticed labor that makes ordinary life possible and on the risk that convenience and hurry shrink other people into obstacles. The essay invites the reader to notice small acts and details while staying humble about what those observations can reveal: “imagination should not mistake itself for intimacy.”

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds pre-dawn city life, repair, waiting, and small acts of care as ways to recover the world from being treated as raw material for personal plans. Its moral claim is not that slower is always better, but that people should remain free to choose their pace and available to one another. The recurring objects—buses, hinges, keys, tulips, a loose paving stone—make that claim concrete.
