## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A literary science-fiction story uses the recovery of an apple tree to explore care, inheritance, and life aboard a generation ship.

## Grounded reading
The voice is measured and tender, with dry humor and close attention to practical details: pump loads, cold air, sour apples, and the work of tending a tree. Its pathos comes from the gap between what people preserve and what that preserved thing actually needs. The story invites the reader to value care not as a guarantee of survival or a display of beauty, but as an ongoing practice that may include discomfort, uncertainty, and unremarkable results.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story centers a neglected apple tree, a deliberately restored winter, and the routines of a closed human community. It gives moral weight to inherited knowledge, patient observation, and the difference between keeping something alive and allowing it to live according to its needs. Its resolution is modest: the blossoms and apples do not prove that the ship “needed” the tree, but they make room for care without requiring a grand justification.
