## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A lyrical, first-person meditation that builds a distinctive mood through recurring images of nighttime streets, small acts of care, and attention without purpose.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and gently self-correcting: it begins with the pleasure of wandering, then questions the urge to make perception useful or turn uncertainty into a moral test. Its pathos comes through quiet losses and kindness remembered in practical gestures—a returned glove, a translated menu, dishes washed by a friend. The invitation is to look without demanding a lesson, and to let other people and places exceed one’s interpretation.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds the city at night as a space where familiar things loosen from their assigned uses: a bakery becomes a cave, a bank a dreaming face, and a walk need not produce ideas. It returns to attention as courtesy or affection, to the dignity of not knowing, and to small acts of care that reduce loneliness without fixing it. Its moral emphasis is modest: the world does not need to console us or become a lesson for us to meet it attentively.

## Evidence line
> Attention feels less like concentration then, and more like courtesy.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the coherent imagery and repeated effort to value attention without utility make this a distinctive, revealing sample.
