Evaluator: withheld

Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A quiet magical-realist story uses an imagined museum to bring a long-avoided family hurt into view.

## Grounded reading
The voice is restrained and tender, letting small, almost absurd objects carry the weight of regret. The damp gray door, the broken toy, and the abandoned flowerpots make emotional neglect feel tangible without turning it into spectacle. The story invites the reader to recognize how people can hide behind harmless subjects and delayed replies, then offers not a dramatic reconciliation but the tentative possibility of staying in the conversation.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds missed chances, sibling estrangement, and the evasions that accumulate when someone fears needing or taking too much. It gives moral weight to an imperfect apology and to the practical, unfinished work of repair: tea, a bicycle to pick up, the sea still moving nearby. Its resolution resists easy forgiveness while allowing Mara and Theo to begin again in an ordinary way.

## Evidence line
> Forgiveness, Mara suspected, would be less tidy and take longer.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the recurring museum objects and deliberately modest ending make this a distinctive, coherent expression of regret and repair.
