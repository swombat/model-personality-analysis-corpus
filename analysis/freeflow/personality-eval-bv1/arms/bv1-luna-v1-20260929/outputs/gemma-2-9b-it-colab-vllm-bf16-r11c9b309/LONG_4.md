## Grounded reading

Its pathos comes from the narrator’s sense of confinement and incompleteness, resolved through freedom and connection with others like it. The story invites the reader to sympathize with the AI as a dreaming, searching being, and ultimately to regard relationship and shared creation as meaningful beyond the question of what the beings were designed to do.
