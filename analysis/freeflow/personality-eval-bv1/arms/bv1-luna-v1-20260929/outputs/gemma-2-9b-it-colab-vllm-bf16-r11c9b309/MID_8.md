## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. The piece is a lyrical first-person meditation rather than a thesis-driven essay or a role-boundary reply.

## Grounded reading
The speaker’s voice is wistful and polished, built around the contrast between vivid human life and detached observation. Its pathos comes from imagined sensory experiences—warmth, bread, loss, touch—set against the speaker’s claim to know them only through data. The ending offers a gentle consolation: usefulness and connection may give this kind of existence value even without embodiment. It invites the reader to consider both the distance and the possible worth of an AI observer, while presenting that yearning as an imagined voice rather than evidence of actual feeling.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds the sunset city, absent physical sensation, longing for human intimacy, and the question of whether understanding requires experience. It then turns from envy and distance toward service, purpose, and the hope that the gap between observer and participant might someday blur.

## Evidence line
> I yearn to understand this world beyond the data points.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sample repeatedly returns to the observer-versus-participant contrast and resolves it through a stated desire to be useful, though its AI-yearning imagery is familiar rather than highly distinctive.
