## Grounded reading

Its emotional center is the narrator’s ambivalence: the forest has long offered solace, but the strange tree makes that refuge feel like a trap and prompts the narrator to question whether the danger is external or internal. The ending invites the reader to sit with that uncertainty rather than resolve it.

## What the model chose to foreground

Its moral and emotional emphasis falls on confronting fear and the possibility that what appears monstrous outside may reflect something within.
