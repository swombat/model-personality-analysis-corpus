## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A compact atmospheric story centers on a lighthouse keeper’s grief and continuing sense of duty.

## Grounded reading
The voice is solemn and highly atmospheric, building pathos through isolation, bereavement, and the keeper’s unbroken routine. Its invitation is to regard vigilance as both a burden and a form of hope: even amid private grief and the sea’s danger, he lights the lamp. The closing repetition of waiting leaves him suspended in loneliness rather than offering release.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample selects the lighthouse, storm, graves, gulls, journal, and lamp as recurring images of isolation and endurance. It links public duty to private loss, especially through the memory of the keeper’s daughter, and gives moral weight to keeping watch despite sorrow.

## Evidence line
> Her memory, vivid and painful, was a wound that never healed.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The sample coherently and distinctively joins grief, solitude, and steadfast duty, though it relies on familiar storm-and-lighthouse imagery.
