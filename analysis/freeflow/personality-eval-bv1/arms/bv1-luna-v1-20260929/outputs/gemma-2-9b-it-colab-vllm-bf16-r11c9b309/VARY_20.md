## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION. This is a sentimental storm-at-the-lighthouse story built around an isolated keeper, his absent daughter, and the promise of the light.

## Grounded reading
The voice is atmospheric and openly emotional, leaning on storm imagery to give Elias’s loneliness and grief a heroic shape. The lighthouse is both his duty and his connection to Lily, while the journal and childhood memory make their distance feel tender rather than resentful. The closing reassurance invites the reader to take comfort in endurance: the storm will pass, and the light will remain.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds solitude, bereavement, a parent’s acceptance of an adult child’s departure, and steadfast service in a crisis. Its recurring images—the lighthouse beam, the storm, the journal, and remembered stars—turn care across distance into the story’s central moral claim: that a reliable light can still guide someone home.
