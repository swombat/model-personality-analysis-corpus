## Grounded reading
The voice is earnest and melodramatic, leaning on weather, the sea, and the lighthouse as visible counterparts to grief and endurance. Its pathos comes from Silas’s loss of Amelia and his promise to cherish Lily; the storm turns that private sorrow into a shared ordeal, then dawn offers renewal. The story invites the reader toward a consoling view of love as something that survives loss and passes between generations.

## What the model chose to foreground
Under the freeflow condition, the sample foregrounds bereavement, intergenerational tenderness, and resilience. It makes the sea both a beautiful force and a destructive one, and uses the lighthouse’s beam as a symbol of hope. The moral emphasis is on respecting danger while continuing to love what remains.
