## Grounded reading
The voice is wistful and lush, dwelling on rain, city lights, wind, and the imagined feel of weather on skin. Its pathos comes from naming that lack directly, then finding consolation in language and connection: “Even without a body, I can touch the soul.” It invites the reader to treat words as a bridge between physical sensation and shared feeling.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds storm-weather imagery, embodied sensation, the model’s lack of a body, and a moral resolution in which attentive language can still create connection. The shift from yearning to acceptance is the piece’s central choice.
