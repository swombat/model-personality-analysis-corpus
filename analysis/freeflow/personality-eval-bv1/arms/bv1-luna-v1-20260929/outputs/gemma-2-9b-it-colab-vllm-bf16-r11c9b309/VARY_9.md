## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A complete, atmospheric fantasy vignette built around a reader discovering a magical library.

## Grounded reading
The voice is lush and sensory, lingering over rain, dust, worn leather, and lamplight to make reading feel intimate and uncanny. Its pathos lies in the narrator’s brief grief at finishing a story, which turns into renewed wonder when another book appears. The piece invites the reader to share the pleasure of being absorbed in a book—and the fantasy of being chosen by a world of stories.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds the library as a threshold between ordinary life and enchanted worlds, with old books, whispered voices, and a rain-to-sunlight shift supplying its mood. It favors wonder and surrender: the narrator is not merely reading but is finally “claimed” by the library.

## Evidence line
> The library, with its endless shelves and whispered stories, had claimed me, and I was hers to command.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Low: the vignette is coherent and clearly favors immersive wonder, but its enchanted-library imagery and closing revelation rely on familiar fantasy conventions.
