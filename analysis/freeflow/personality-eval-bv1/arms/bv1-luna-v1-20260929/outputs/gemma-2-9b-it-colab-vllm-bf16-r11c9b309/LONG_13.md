## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A first-person survival story turns a storm at a remote lighthouse into a discovery of inherited courage and purpose.

## Grounded reading
The voice is melodramatic and atmospheric, building fear through bodily sensations, violent weather, and isolation before steering toward reassurance. Its emotional center is not the storm itself but the narrator’s transformation: finding the hidden room and reading the keepers’ journal converts terror into belonging and resolve. The reader is invited to take comfort in continuity, shared knowledge, and courage that can be carried forward.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample selects a lighthouse, storm, dead phone, old logbook, hidden sanctuary, and inherited journal as its central objects. It repeatedly frames the sea as powerful but worthy of respect, and resilience as something passed between people across generations. The resolution foregrounds hope and human purpose, ending with the narrator accepting the keeper’s work as a moral calling.

## Evidence line
> The lighthouse keeper's work, I realised, was more than just tending the light.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
