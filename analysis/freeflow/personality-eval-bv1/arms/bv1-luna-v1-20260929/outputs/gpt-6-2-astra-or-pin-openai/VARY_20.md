## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A restrained literary story turns a small, odd act of planting into a family portrait and an account of shared life.

## Grounded reading
The voice is dryly observant and affectionate without becoming sentimental. It lets care show through practical actions—the father bringing the tree, the neighbors helping stake it, the narrator cooking bruised pears—while their dialogue keeps emotion understated. The story invites the reader to notice how a makeshift garden gathers a community, and how the father’s advice and habits remain present even as his health changes.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds a pear tree, bus journeys, improvised neighborhood cooperation, and the father’s particular way of advising and withholding sentiment. The tree’s slow growth gives shape to a story about patience, inheritance, and care that does not guarantee a perfect outcome: the harvest is damaged, but still becomes something shared. The final willingness to plant again leaves the story in a mood of quiet persistence rather than tidy consolation.

## Evidence line
> The first time my father came to visit me in the city, he brought a pear tree on the bus.
