## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds benches, maps, bus routes, and other humble features of civic life, treating them as measures of whether a place accommodates its people. It favors attention over grand gestures: the narrator’s developing understanding begins with taking a bench apart, grows through mapping where people actually sit, and returns in the final moment of making room. The mood is warm but unsentimental, with humor that punctures self-importance without dismissing the people involved.
