# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: LONG

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION. This is a polished literary short story, built around a family recording the sounds of a house they are leaving.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant, gently comic, and emotionally restrained. It lets grief surface through practical details—the measured chairs, the missing Welsh cassette, the laugh the mother allows them to keep—rather than through declarations. The narrator’s work with sound gives the story a precise way to ask what memory preserves and what should remain private. Its invitation is to listen with care, including to the ordinary, imperfect sounds that make a voice feel situated in a life.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds domestic sounds and objects—the spoon drawer, stairs, tap, rain, cassette, trumpet—as carriers of family memory. It gives particular weight to the ethics of keeping a recording: the mother’s laugh may stay, the running water may go. Alongside bereavement and moving house, it chooses humor, practical kindness, and the possibility that a new home can acquire its own sounds.

## Evidence line
> A human voice ought to sound as though it comes from somewhere, even if the somewhere is an appliance in need of service.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story’s coherent voice, recurring attention to ordinary sounds, and unusually careful treatment of memory and consent make its choices distinctive.
