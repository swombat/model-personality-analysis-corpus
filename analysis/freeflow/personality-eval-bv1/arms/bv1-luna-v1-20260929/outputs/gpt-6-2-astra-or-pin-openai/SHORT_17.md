# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: SHORT

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A compact literary vignette turns a librarian’s record of unfulfilled searches into a meditation on the unofficial ways people know a place.

## Grounded reading
The voice is quiet, observant, and gently wistful, with humor and tenderness in the child’s map and its blunt label, “HOLE.” The piece treats small, easily overlooked details as traces of people’s lives: a vanished kitchen’s paint, a swan’s takeoff, shortcuts through town. Its pathos comes from the narrator’s wish for proof that private experience coexists with the town on official signs. The ending invites the reader to see belonging as something discovered through other people’s routes, not just one’s own.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds lost or hard-to-shelve knowledge, informal maps, childhood marks, and the gap between official geography and lived experience. Its emotional center is not recovering the narrator’s own map, but accepting another person’s route as another way home.

## Evidence line
> I suppose I wanted proof that the town I knew existed alongside the one on the signs.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the recurring map and homeward-path imagery gives the vignette a distinctive, coherent attentiveness to ordinary details as evidence of belonging.
