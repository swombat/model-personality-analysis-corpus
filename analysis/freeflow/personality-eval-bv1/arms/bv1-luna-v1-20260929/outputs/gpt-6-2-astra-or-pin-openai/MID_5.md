## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A carefully shaped literary narrative uses an apartment table and its accumulating marks to connect small mishaps with friendship, family change, and memory.

## Grounded reading
The voice is wry, observant, and gently affectionate. Its pathos comes from restraint: the sister leaves her job, Martin moves away, and the narrator neither makes these events grand nor pretends that objects preserve their meaning intact. The reader is invited to notice how care accumulates through ordinary acts—tightening a leg, doing a crossword, putting the kettle on—and how comedy can sit beside loss without canceling it.

## What the model chose to foreground
The table, tools, crumbs, paint, and fish drawing become the story’s recurring objects: practical things that gather traces of shared life without fully explaining them. The narrative foregrounds friendship across an age difference, the sister’s decision to leave her job, and the awkward persistence of memory in everyday clutter. Its moral emphasis is modest: meanings are real to the people who lived them, even when they leave little legible evidence behind.

## Evidence line
> Objects are unreliable custodians of the meanings we assign them.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The recurring objects, dry humor, and understated emotional turns form a distinctive, coherent pattern within the narrative.
