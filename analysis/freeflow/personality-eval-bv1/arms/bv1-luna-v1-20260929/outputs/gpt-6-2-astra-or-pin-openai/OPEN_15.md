## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A compact domestic parable turns a small act of letting go into a shift from guarding a useless object to imagining someone who could use it.

## Grounded reading
The voice is wry and self-aware, with its pathos kept light: the speaker admits to preserving an unknown key while discarding a colander, then lets the niece’s imaginative hotel change the key’s meaning. The ending offers the reader a gentle invitation to replace anxious possession with attention to another person’s needs.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds an everyday object, the irrational persistence of “what if I needed it?”, and a child’s play as a way to reimagine its purpose. Its moral emphasis is not simply on decluttering, but on noticing who might need what we have.

## Evidence line
