## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and dryly amused, from the “frankly optimistic” drawer for single gloves to the librarian’s practical distinction between books and fruit. The tenderness comes through in small gestures rather than explanation: the father helping Cal return what he took, the librarian allowing him to keep the lemon, and her finding a pot for the seeds. The story invites the reader to see care as a way of making room for growth, not merely enforcing rules.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample centers on a lemon, a child’s earnest understanding of borrowing, and an adult’s gentle flexibility. It gives moral weight to returning what was taken while also suggesting that living things do not fit neatly into the rules for books and lost property.
