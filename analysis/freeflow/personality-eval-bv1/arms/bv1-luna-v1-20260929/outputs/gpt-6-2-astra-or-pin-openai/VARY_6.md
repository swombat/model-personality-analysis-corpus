## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A carefully shaped literary story uses a family anecdote and an object’s uncertain history to build toward a quiet, open-ended resolution.

## Grounded reading
The voice is wry, observant, and tender without making its tenderness explicit. The lighter gathers meaning through small domestic details—the loose carpet tack, brittle rubber bands, the cutlery drawer—while the narrator’s changing account of Gordon reflects how family stories can be embellished, corrected, and passed on. The ending invites the reader to enjoy the modest possibility that the lighter might still find its owner, and to see repair as a way of keeping both an object and a story alive.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds an ordinary misplaced lighter, the mother’s practical attachment to it, and the narrator’s invented ballroom-dancer story. Across generations, it emphasizes the pull of a good joke alongside the value of telling a more truthful story; its mood is comic, affectionate, and gently wistful. The unresolved question of Gordon gives this domestic history a small imaginative afterlife.
