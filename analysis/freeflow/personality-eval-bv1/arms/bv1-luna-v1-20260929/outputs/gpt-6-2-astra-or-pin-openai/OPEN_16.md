## Grounded reading
The voice is restrained and attentive to how grief settles into ordinary work: a drawer being emptied, a wall being painted, a stop for petrol. Its pathos comes through small acts of preservation and release, especially the woman photographing the imagined man with soup before telling the painter to continue. The ending invites the reader to linger with the painter’s playful shadow, where wonder briefly survives without needing to be explained.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds memory attached to domestic marks, a mother’s absent son, and the unstable life of images made by light and words. Its mood shifts gently from loss to permission and then to a modest, almost childlike wonder; the painter’s shadow holding a horse gives that wonder the final word.
