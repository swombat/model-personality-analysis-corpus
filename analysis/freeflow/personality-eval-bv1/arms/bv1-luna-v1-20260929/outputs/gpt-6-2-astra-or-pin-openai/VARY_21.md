## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A quietly comic literary vignette whose emotional meaning gathers through ordinary details rather than an explicit thesis.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant, dryly funny, and gently self-implicating. It notices care arriving indirectly: a wife making it to a game, a daughter’s career reduced to a photograph and a building, a spare key offered and then taken back. The narrator’s regret is understated, especially in the recognition that they had stopped watching their mother’s bad knee. The reader is invited to linger with these small acts and missed moments, and with the choice to stay.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds memory carried by imperfect recordings, family care expressed through practical gestures, and attention that can lapse without anyone noticing. Keys, crossword clues, rabbits, tea, and the old baseball game keep the mood domestic and wry; the final decision to stay gives the story its quiet resolution.

## Evidence line
> For months I had watched that bad knee of hers every time she crossed a room, and then one day, apparently, I had quit watching.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The controlled, distinctive narration and recurring emphasis on everyday acts of attention make this a strong sample of an understated, relational voice.
