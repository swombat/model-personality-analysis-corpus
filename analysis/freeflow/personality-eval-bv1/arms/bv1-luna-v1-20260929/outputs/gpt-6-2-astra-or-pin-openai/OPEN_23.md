## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A quiet, carefully shaped vignette turns an ordinary street-side repair into a reflection on patience and what counts as finished.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and gently amused, attentive to small changes rather than dramatic events. Its pathos lies in the chair’s visible history—the worn patches where hands pull it out—and in the narrator’s mistaken expectation that repair must culminate in a pristine object. The ending invites the reader to recognize completion in use: a man sitting down to eat a pear.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds a wooden chair, the stages of its repair, and the time glue needs to set. It gives special attention to the contrast between fresh sanding and old varnish, then closes on ordinary use rather than a reveal of perfect restoration. Patience and the value of noticing are the moral emphasis, kept understated.
