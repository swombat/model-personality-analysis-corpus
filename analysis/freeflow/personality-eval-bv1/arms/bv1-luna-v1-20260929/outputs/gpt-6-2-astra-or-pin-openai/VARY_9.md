## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION. A tightly shaped family story uses the fork tree as a small magical premise for a tender, dryly comic account of inheritance and loss.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and wry, attentive to how affection often arrives disguised as fuss, mischief, or an ordinary household ritual. The narrator’s irritation at her father’s staged discovery gives way to recognition of his trembling hand and the care behind the trick. The ending invites the reader to feel grief as a familiar presence in daily life—not grand or resolved, but close enough to make the drawer sound like someone coming home.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds forks, counting, and the kitchen drawer as objects that carry family memory; a child’s willingness to believe; and the awkward, loving effort to make a small piece of magic. Its moral emphasis is gentle: tenderness may require accepting another person’s imperfect performance, and family habits can outlast the people who began them.

## Evidence line
