## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A quiet domestic vignette turns an ordinary keepsake into a reflection on usefulness, imagination, and the appeal of the unknown.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and lightly amused, attentive to the small objects that make up a household. The niece’s invented future house gives the useless key a playful imaginative life, while the ending makes the narrator’s discovery feel less like a solution than a loss. The piece invites the reader to value what cannot be justified by practical use.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds a brass key kept despite having no known lock, alongside household objects that earn their place through usefulness. It gives special weight to the niece’s imaginative answer and to the mystery the narrator would rather preserve than resolve.

## Evidence line
> For the first time in thirty years, I knew exactly what the thing had done, and already I missed the mystery.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
