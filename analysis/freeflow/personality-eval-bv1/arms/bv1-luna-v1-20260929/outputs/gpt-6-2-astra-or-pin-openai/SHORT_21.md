## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A compact personal-style vignette uses a comic repair mishap to connect craft, writing, and practical care.

## Grounded reading
The voice is wry and unshowy, beginning with the narrator making a chair worse through overcorrection. The chair becomes a way to notice a habit of cutting away what seems wrong, then to discover that a person may need something added instead—an extra detail in a paragraph, or soup for a quiet friend. The closing attention to Ruth’s sore fingers and muddy lace gives the piece its tenderness. It invites the reader to look past the visible flaw and ask what would make life easier for someone.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds repair, modest practical kindness, and attention to small physical details: a scrap of cork, a wet cuff, soup, a kettle, cold fingers, and a muddy lace. Its moral emphasis is on adding support rather than reflexively removing what seems wrong.

## Evidence line
> The chair now lives by the front door.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the chair metaphor is carried coherently through several parts of the vignette, and the closing detail gives its practical ethic a distinctive, human scale.
