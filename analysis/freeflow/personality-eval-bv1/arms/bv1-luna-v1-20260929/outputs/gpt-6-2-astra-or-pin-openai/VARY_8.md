## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A literary short story built around a family keepsake and a small shift in what the narrator remembers.

## Grounded reading
The voice is restrained and observant, letting grief surface through practical tasks, sibling banter, and objects rather than direct declarations. The “meantime drawer” gives the story its gentle pathos: a place for things without an immediate use becomes a way of keeping the father’s habit, and then a family memory, in circulation. The narrator’s tenderness is mixed with uncertainty about whose memories are accurate; the closing invitation is to look again, with patience, before correcting someone else.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds bereavement, ordinary household objects, and the uneven inheritance of family memory. Its moral emphasis is quiet: keeping something can matter even when its purpose is unclear, and attention can reveal what haste or certainty missed. The strawberry knob’s movement from the childhood home to the sister’s bathroom connects generations without making the connection grand or sentimental.
