## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A quiet, carefully structured story turns forgotten objects and small acts of care into a reflection on how people learn what matters to one another.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and gently self-deprecating, attentive to the emotional lives that gather around ordinary things: a horse photograph, a half-read book, a lunch receipt. Its pathos comes through missed understanding rather than dramatic loss—the narrator once chose gifts based on their own enthusiasms, then gradually learns to notice what others need. The ending invites the reader to see the mother’s request not as a punchline but as an intimate continuation of their shared habit of making room for a story.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds forgotten objects as carriers of private meaning, the awkwardness of trying to know another person, and care expressed through practical attention. Its recurring images—the bookmark, the receipt, the bathroom, the packed household—make reading and domestic routine feel like ways of keeping someone’s place.

## Evidence line

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
