# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: MID

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A literary short story shaped around a small community’s attachment to an inaccurate clock.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant, gently comic, and alert to the emotional weight of ordinary objects: the fried-egg clock, the index cards, the book about buttons, the umbrella. Its pathos comes quietly, through the narrator’s lingering in the library and the mother’s unceremonious care for people and their ideas. The story favors humane accommodation over abstract correctness, without turning that choice into a grand lesson. It invites the reader to notice how small acts of attention can make a place feel shared—and how those acts endure even when their records are discarded.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds timekeeping, public-library life, eccentric objects, and the small negotiations between accuracy and kindness. It gives special weight to overlooked suggestions and temporary moments of companionship, ending with the narrator offering shelter to a stranger rather than telling the whole story.

## Evidence line
> She simply felt that an idea ought to have somewhere to go after its owner went home.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The recurring clock, discarded cards, and shared umbrella create a coherent, distinctive emphasis on everyday kindness and the lives of small gestures.
