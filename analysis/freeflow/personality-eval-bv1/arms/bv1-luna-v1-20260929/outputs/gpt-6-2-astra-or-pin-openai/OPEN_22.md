## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A compact domestic scene uses a repaired drawer to carry understated feeling about habit, siblinghood, and a shared past.

## Grounded reading
The voice is plainspoken and gently amused, letting emotion gather around practical details rather than naming it directly. The narrator’s continued kneeing of the now-smooth cabinet, and the sister’s question about the old key, turn a small household repair into a scene of shared memory. The reader is invited to notice how an inconvenience can become a familiar ritual—and how an object left in a drawer can keep a past close without explaining it away.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds a sticky drawer, the learned bodily choreography of opening it, and the sister who fixes it. It gives particular weight to the old apartment key and the fleck of green paint, making ordinary objects carry the sisters’ shared history. Its mood is affectionate and lightly comic, with a quiet note of attachment to what the repair has made unnecessary.

## Evidence line
