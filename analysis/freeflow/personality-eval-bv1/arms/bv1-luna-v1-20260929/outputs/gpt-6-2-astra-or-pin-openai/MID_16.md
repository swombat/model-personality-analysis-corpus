## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A carefully shaped family story whose quiet emotional weight grows through recurring details of repair, work, and passing time.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and gently wry, attentive to the small physical particulars that make people recognizable: a rooster clock, a heel striking pavement, orange peel on a radiator, a pink repair thread. Its grief is restrained rather than solemn. The narrator regrets having mistaken nearness to his father’s work for learning, but the story does not resolve that regret with a grand inheritance; instead, the tools pass through the narrator’s hands, a shelf slopes, and a marble becomes a game for a child. The reader is invited to notice how care and family memory can survive as habits, jokes, and shared time, even when the practical knowledge itself is lost.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds repair as both a trade and a way of attending to people: the clock that gives a couple more time over coffee, the father’s recognition of customers by their footsteps, and the narrator’s imperfect attempt to carry his work forward. It also selects intergenerational handoffs, ordinary domestic objects, and the gap between being present and truly learning. Its emotional center is not the tools alone, but the small moments of companionship they make possible.

## Evidence line
> I had been present for so much of the work that I mistook proximity for instruction.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The recurring repair objects, restrained grief, and warm comic turns give the sample a distinctive, coherent emotional and stylistic pattern.
