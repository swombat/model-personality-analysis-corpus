## Sample kind
GENERIC_ESSAY — A polished, thesis-driven essay that coherently praises creativity across art, science, technology, and everyday life without developing a highly distinctive personal voice.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warmly exhortative, repeatedly turning creativity into a life-affirming force: it “breathes life,” makes “personal pockets of sanity,” and opens “uncharted” possibilities. Its pathos comes from elevating ordinary acts—a mother folding a book page or a father building a skate-ramp—alongside famous art and scientific achievement. The reader is invited to recognize their own capacity for creativity and let it unfold, though the grand metaphors and long catalogues keep the appeal broad rather than intimate.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds creativity as both a universal human quality and a driver of progress, moving through art, scientific discovery, technology, family life, nature, and cosmic imagery. It favors expansive, affirmative language and ends with an invitation to embrace individual creative potential.

## Evidence line

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
