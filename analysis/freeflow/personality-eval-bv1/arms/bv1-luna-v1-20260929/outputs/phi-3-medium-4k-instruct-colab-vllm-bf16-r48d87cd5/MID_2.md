## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A first-person allegory uses a rediscovered garden to stage a journey from anxiety toward acceptance and gratitude.

## Grounded reading
The voice is lush and earnest, arranging sensory nature imagery—fountains, flowers, butterflies, and a labyrinth—around a familiar inward passage from feeling burdened to feeling renewed. Its pathos comes from naming anxieties, insecurities, anger, and despair, then resolving them through contemplation rather than conflict. The reader is invited to treat the garden as both refuge and mirror, and to carry its lessons back into ordinary life.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds nature as a guide to self-knowledge, the value of learning from past experience, and harmony between individuals and their surroundings. Its moral emphasis is on slowing down, accepting life’s joys and difficulties, and turning reflection into gratitude and coexistence. The garden’s elaborate beauty and the narrator’s explicit transformation make personal growth the central attraction.

## Evidence line
> The garden became a mirror for my soul, with each rose and thorn intimately reflecting a fragment of my being.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
