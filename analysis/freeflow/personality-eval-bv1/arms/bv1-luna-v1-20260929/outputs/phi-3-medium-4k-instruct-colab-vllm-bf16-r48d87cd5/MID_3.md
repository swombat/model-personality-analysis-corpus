## Grounded reading

Its pathos comes from curiosity turning into loss—the young woman’s eagerness is gradually replaced by a cosmic transformation that strips away her humanity. The reader is invited to share the thrill of discovery, then accept the story’s warning that knowledge can carry a price.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds the bookshop as a threshold between ordinary life and the uncanny, and knowledge as both alluring and dangerous. Its recurring objects—the old book, the grandfather clock, the dusty shelves—give the tale a mood of suspended time and gathering dread. The moral emphasis is explicit: curiosity and learning are precious, but pursuing power without humility may estrange a person from their human self.
