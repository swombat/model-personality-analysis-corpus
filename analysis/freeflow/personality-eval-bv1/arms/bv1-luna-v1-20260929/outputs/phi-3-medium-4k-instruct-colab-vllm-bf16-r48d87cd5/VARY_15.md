## Grounded reading

Its emotional center is Lily’s discovery of “true home” among welcoming creatures, though the late suggestion that she cannot return home sits uneasily beside the ending’s claim that she becomes a storyteller bridging both worlds. The story invites the reader to share the pleasure of a hidden world and to see curiosity as a path toward belonging.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds curiosity, escape from monotonous routines, magical discovery, and acceptance in a new community. Its moral emphasis is that an adventurous spirit can lead to a more fitting home and a life of sharing stories; this is delivered through familiar fantasy motifs rather than unusually distinctive details.
