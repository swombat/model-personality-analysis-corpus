## Grounded reading
The voice is quietly intimate and contemplative, moving between explanation and personal response without making the science feel like a pretext for a grand claim. Its pathos is restrained: the Moon’s retreat brings “wonder mixed with” a qualified grief, while its familiar monthly return offers a counterpoint to long-term change. The essay invites the reader to see stability as a matter of timescale and to hold satisfying origin stories loosely rather than treating them as established fact.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model chose the Moon’s gradual recession, tidal exchange, fossil growth records, changing eclipses, and the contrast between the Moon’s long-term departure and its monthly return. It foregrounds slowness, impermanence, and scale, with a moral emphasis on noticing that the present arrangement is neither permanent nor uniquely natural.
