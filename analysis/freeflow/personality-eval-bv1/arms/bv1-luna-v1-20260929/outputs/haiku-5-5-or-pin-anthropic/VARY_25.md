EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A reflective miniature essay uses a family memory and recurring jar metaphor to turn its meditation on withheld words into direct advice.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and gently insistent, moving from concrete details—the cold coffee, the grandmother’s mayonnaise jar, its rusted nails—to the emotional cost of saving words for a better moment. Its pathos comes from regret made ordinary: saying “fine” or “nothing” when someone nearby needs a more honest answer. The closing imperative invites the reader not merely to admire the reflection but to speak plainly to someone now.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds communication delayed by caution, the difference between saving objects and spending words, and the irreversibility of particular moments. It favors domestic, tactile images and relationships across generations, with a moral emphasis on saying ordinary truths before the chance passes.

## Evidence line
