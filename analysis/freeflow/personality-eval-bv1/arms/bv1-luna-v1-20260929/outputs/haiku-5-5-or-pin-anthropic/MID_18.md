Evaluator: withheld

Condition: MID

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. It is a reflective personal essay whose recurring rubber-band image gives its polished observations a distinctive emotional center.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gently self-questioning and attentive to the emotional weight of ordinary objects. It begins with a useless rubber band and gradually connects keeping things to memory, time, and reluctance to dismiss past selves as worthless. Its melancholy is tempered by tenderness and a practical invitation: keep things consciously, and notice whether they are useful, meaningful, or hard to release.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds junk drawers, worn and forgotten objects, and the small acts of keeping and discarding. It treats these objects as imperfect archives and as wagers on a future self, while presenting both attachment and letting go as ways of negotiating with time.

## Evidence line
> Every object we keep is a refusal to fully settle the account with our previous selves.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the rubber band recurs as a coherent, unusually specific anchor for the essay’s reflections, though the polished, familiar reflective-essay form makes the voice less singular overall.
