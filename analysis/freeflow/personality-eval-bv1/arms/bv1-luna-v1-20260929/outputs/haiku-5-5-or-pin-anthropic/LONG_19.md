The voice is curious and gently persuasive, turning cartographic errors into a meditation on how inherited beliefs acquire authority. Its pathos is quiet: the vanished island matters partly because people remember believing in it, and the blank map matters because it makes ignorance visible rather than shameful. The essay invites readers to hold their beliefs provisionally—to use the chart, but keep watching the water.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds maps, phantom places, blank spaces, and the difference between what has been observed and what has merely been repeated. Its moral emphasis is intellectual humility: mark guesses as guesses, leave unknowns visible, and treat correction as part of learning rather than humiliation. The movement from geographic unknowns to the human body, brain, and dark matter gives that theme an expansive, hopeful mood.
