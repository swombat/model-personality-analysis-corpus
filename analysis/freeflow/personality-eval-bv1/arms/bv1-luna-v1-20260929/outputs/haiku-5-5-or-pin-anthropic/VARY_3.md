## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical first-person meditation turns a quiet night and ordinary household sounds into reflections on memory, loss, and what people keep.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and attentive, finding emotional weight in small sensory details: a refrigerator’s hum, a coffee can of carefully untangled string, the faint sounds of a house at night. Its pathos is restrained rather than dramatic; grief appears through remembered gestures and habits, while loneliness becomes a reason to listen. The piece invites the reader to notice how people and feelings return indirectly, through words, sounds, and movements.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds collecting and keeping—string, words, and traces of lost people—then gently revises the idea of an “inventory” from possessions into recurring memories. Its mood favors nocturnal quiet, patient attention, and tenderness toward both practical habits and unresolved absence.
