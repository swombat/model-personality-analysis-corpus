EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. The sample turns a compact account of lunar physics into a reflective meditation on change, time, and scale.

## Grounded reading
The voice is curious and gently intimate, moving from delight in a scientific mechanism to melancholy about its far-future outcome, then to comfort in the slow pace of change. The dancer image makes the Earth–Moon exchange feel like a relationship rather than a diagram. The reader is invited to feel how familiar measures—a day, a year, the Moon’s place in the sky—are temporary arrangements, and to find wonder in change that happens almost imperceptibly.

## What the model chose to foreground
It foregrounds tidal friction, the changing length of the day, ancient tides, and the distant prospect of tidal locking. Its moral and emotional emphasis is that dramatic transformation need not arrive dramatically: small increments can remake the heavens, and that gradualness can be comforting.
