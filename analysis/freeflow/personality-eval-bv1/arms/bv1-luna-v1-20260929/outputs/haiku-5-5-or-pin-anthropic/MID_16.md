## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. The sample is a personal, image-rich reflection that uses an unfinished book to explore attention, guilt, and change.

## Grounded reading
The voice is measured and gently intimate, beginning with a specific paperback and its old coffee-shop receipt before widening into a meditation on unfinished commitments. Its pathos comes from uncertainty rather than regret: the narrator cannot tell whether a book was dull or too close to personal experience, and leaves room for both possibilities. The piece invites the reader to treat abandoned interests with less self-reproach and to see them as traces of changing attention, not automatic failures.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds half-read books, bookmarks, doors left open, and other unfinished projects as records of earlier selves. It weighs the virtue of persistence against the relief and honesty of stopping, then favors a forgiving view of shifting interests and finite attention.

## Evidence line
> An unfinished life is not necessarily a wasted one.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sample sustains a coherent, recurring metaphor and makes a distinctive choice to frame unfinished things as evidence of curiosity rather than failure.
