## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical personal essay uses a sustained map metaphor to turn ordinary inattention into a reflection on uncertainty and discovery.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm, measured, and quietly curious, finding emotional value in what remains unknown rather than treating it as a problem to solve. The essay moves from old cartographic dragons to the streets near the narrator’s home, giving its abstract point an intimate, everyday scale. Its pathos is gentle: finite attention means we miss much of the world, but noticing that gap can become an invitation rather than a source of shame. The reader is invited to look beyond habitual routes and allow uncertainty to remain visible.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds blank spaces, maps, local streets, repeated encounters, and the difference between information and lived familiarity. It favors epistemic humility over confident mastery, and closes on a small, concrete resolution: walk an unfamiliar street without a destination or phone. The recurring map imagery and modest scale make curiosity feel like a practice of attention rather than an achievement.

## Evidence line
> We have a map of our habits, not of the land.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sustained metaphor, consistent reflective tone, and concrete resolution make this a distinctive, coherent expression of curiosity and humility.
