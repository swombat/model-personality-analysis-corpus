# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A literary short story built around a small-town post office and a quiet emotional revelation.

## Grounded reading
The voice is tender and restrained, letting grief emerge through ordinary gestures: a boy keeping one finger on an envelope, Ruth stamping a letter she cannot deliver, the old radiator ticking in the silence. Its central pathos is the ache of waiting—for an absent father, a lost mother, a vanished railway, or a place to belong. The story invites the reader to treat care itself as meaningful even when it cannot guarantee reunion or an answer.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds letters, destinations that no longer exist, and the rituals people use to keep loss from becoming disappearance. Its moral emphasis is gentle rather than triumphant: Ruth does not promise the boy that his father will return, but she can promise not to throw away what he made with care. The ending turns that same act of preservation toward Ruth’s own buried sense of belonging.

## Evidence line
> She had been waiting her whole life for someone to come to the counter and tell her where she belonged.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The sustained motifs of waiting, grief, and small acts of care give the piece a distinctive, coherent emotional center.
