EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical, personally voiced reflection turns the small physical traces in used books into a meditation on attention and connection across time.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm, intimate, and gently elegiac, finding tenderness in ordinary objects: a grocery receipt, a pencil dent, a handwritten “yes.” Its pathos comes from the gap between not knowing who left a mark and feeling close to them anyway. The piece invites the reader to treat marginalia not as damage but as evidence of someone’s attention—and to consider what their own marks might offer a stranger.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds secondhand books and their handwritten traces as signs of embodied reading, vulnerability, and shared feeling. It contrasts the physical residue of a well-used book with cloud-stored digital highlights, and returns to the idea that readers pass meaning along by leaving marks for whoever comes next.
