## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and gently wistful, finding emotional weight in ordinary traces: an underline, a grocery list, a sharply penciled “NO.” It invites the reader to imagine the unknown people behind those marks, then shifts toward the writer’s own wish to leave a sentence that might matter to a stranger. The pathos is modest rather than grand: connection across time appears as a small, accidental exchange.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds used books, marginal notes, and disagreement as evidence of reading made visible. It treats attention as something that can be passed between strangers, and frames correction and revision as part of how knowledge moves. The closing emphasis is on writing not for fame but for the possibility of being met, years later, by an attentive reader.
