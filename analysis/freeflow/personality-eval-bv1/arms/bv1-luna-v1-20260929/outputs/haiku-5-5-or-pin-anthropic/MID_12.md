## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and contemplative, inviting the reader to treat incompletion less as failure than as a way of being engaged with life. A half-finished crossword anchors the essay’s abstract claims in ordinary experience; the cathedral, Schubert, and the grandmother’s garden widen that thought without losing its intimate scale. Its pathos comes from recognizing mortality, but the resolution is warm rather than mournful: care and curiosity can matter even when nothing is brought to completion.

It contrasts completion metrics and perfectionism with tending, openness, and the limited time people have. The closing choice to leave the crossword in the drawer makes that acceptance concrete.
