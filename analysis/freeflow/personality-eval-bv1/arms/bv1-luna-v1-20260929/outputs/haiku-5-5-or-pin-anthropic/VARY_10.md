## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and observant, finding emotional meaning in workmanship rather than declaring it outright. The narrator’s grief for his father quietly echoes the unknown writer’s message, while precise details—the scored screws, tired mainspring, and tiny drop of oil—make care feel both practical and intimate. The ending invites the reader to see the repaired watch as a link between people who may never meet, carried forward through small acts of attention.

Its central objects—the watch, the folded note, and the fresh slip recording the repair—make continuity tangible. The moral emphasis is on care without applause: keeping something running, and leaving a record for whoever comes next.
