## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A polished first-person family mystery turns an inherited collection of keys into a meditation on memory and unanswered questions.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and measured, letting ordinary domestic details—the pastry, the stuck drawer, the box of letters—carry the grief. Its pathos comes from the narrator’s gradual discovery that the grandmother had a life and attachments beyond the story the family knew. Rather than solve the mystery, the ending invites the reader to value keeping and wondering, leaving the key as a quiet emblem of continuing connection.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds inherited objects as carriers of hidden histories: keys, letters, and a drawer preserved across generations. It gives particular weight to private, possibly forbidden love and to the idea that an unanswered mystery can still matter. The mood moves from childhood wonder through bereavement and investigation toward tender acceptance.

## Evidence line
> I hung the blue key on a hook by the front door, next to my own.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sample’s recurring attention to memory, domestic objects, and unresolved tenderness is coherent and distinctive, though the plot resolves in a familiar sentimental register.
