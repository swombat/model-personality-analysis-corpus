## Grounded reading
The voice is calm, curious, and gently hopeful. It finds pathos in the mapmaker’s imperfect act of looking and recording, then invites the reader to treat the limits of knowledge as openings for discovery and collaboration.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds blank spaces, sea monsters, unmapped places, and the imagined mapmaker in a rocking boat. Its moral emphasis is that honest uncertainty can be courageous and that knowledge should leave room for correction by others.
