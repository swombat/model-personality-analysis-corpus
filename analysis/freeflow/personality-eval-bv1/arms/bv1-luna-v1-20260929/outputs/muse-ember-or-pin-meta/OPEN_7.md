## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and quietly intimate, moving from a backyard after a gathering to a broader account of emotional continuity. Its pathos comes less from loss than from the wish to check whether warmth is still there: the speaker pokes the coals without trying to revive the fire, and carries the scene indoors rather than explaining it. The piece invites the reader to recognize enduring attachments in ordinary traces—a song, a grandmother’s voice, a friend’s late text—without making them grand or dramatic.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds embers, smoke, late-night quiet, and small sources of light as images for memory and lasting connection. It contrasts spectacular, temporary flames with patient warmth, and treats tending to what remains as a form of care rather than an attempt to restore the past.

## Evidence line
