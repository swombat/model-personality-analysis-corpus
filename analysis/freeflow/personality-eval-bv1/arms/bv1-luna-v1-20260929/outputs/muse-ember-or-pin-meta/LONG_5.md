## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. It is a polished essay, but its recurring images and intimate, wandering voice make it more distinctive than a generic public-intellectual argument.

## Grounded reading
The voice is hospitable and contemplative, inviting the reader to slow down and follow a thought without demanding a practical payoff. Its pathos is gentle rather than anguished: the remembered field, the crooked ditch, and the wish to “waste a little” attention beautifully make wandering feel like a way to recover a private, unmeasured self. The essay acknowledges that maps, efficiency, and phones are useful, but resists letting them define what counts as worthwhile. The reader is invited not just to agree, but to try a small act of purposeful detouring.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds wandering as a way of thinking and living: curiosity, slowness, attention, and comfort with uncertainty. Fields, rivers, old maps, libraries, music, conversation, and the phone recur as ways to contrast open-ended discovery with optimized routes and algorithmic herding. Its moral claim is that attention and interest have value even when they produce no measurable return.

## Evidence line
