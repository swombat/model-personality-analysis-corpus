## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical, personal essay that builds its argument through recurring domestic details and a sustained first-person voice.

## Grounded reading
The voice is reflective and gently self-implicating: it admits to ignored emails, a neglected plant, and a drifting attention rather than presenting the narrator as having mastered the life lesson. Its pathos comes from ordinary acts of care—repairing a toaster, sitting beside an absence, noticing a neighbor’s flowers—and from the fear that a life can pass unwitnessed while attention is elsewhere. The recurring language of returning and staying makes the essay feel like an invitation to look more closely, not a demand to become more disciplined or more content.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds staying, attention, repair, and the everyday labor of caring for people and places. Its chosen objects—the dripping faucet, pothos, grandfather’s workbench, Delia’s clocks, and Mrs. Alvarez’s marigolds—make a moral claim that patience and witness matter even when they produce no spectacle or guaranteed fix. It also holds space for the counterclaim that leaving can be necessary, framing discernment rather than endurance at any cost as the task.
