## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. It is a lyrical first-person essay that uses the night train as both a travel experience and a case for slower, less efficient ways of living.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warmly nostalgic without treating the past as perfect: its affection comes through in tactile details—the crisp sheet, the tea, the fogged mirror—and in the pleasure of sharing a temporary village with strangers. Beneath the romance is a quiet resistance to speed and optimization. The reader is invited not just to admire sleeper trains, but to value idleness, anticipation, and the in-between time that practical travel tries to eliminate.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds night travel as intimacy, ritual, and unhurried passage: blue-hour stations, rain-streaked windows, shared soup, passing lights, and the gradual arrival into a city. It also chooses a modest moral claim—that inconvenience and waiting can preserve wonder—connecting renewed interest in trains to climate anxiety and fatigue with airports.

## Evidence line
> In a world that measures everything by how quickly it can be done, there is something profoundly hopeful about a journey that takes all night on purpose.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — the essay is coherent and distinctive in its recurring sensory details and its consistent preference for slowness over efficiency, making those choices unusually visible in the sample.
