Evaluator: withheld

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A reflective first-person meditation built around vivid night-walking scenes and a personal memory of the narrator’s grandmother.

## Grounded reading
The voice is calm, intimate, and gently persuasive, finding emotional relief in unhurried attention rather than achievement. Its pathos gathers in small, quiet scenes—the grandmother taking a detour, a lone passenger on a bus, a heron held still in winter—and in the narrator’s wish to loosen the grip of planning and productivity. The reader is invited to treat wandering not as escape or conquest, but as a way to become more present and receptive to a familiar place.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds nighttime streets, wet pavement, amber streetlights, the river, and the sensory details that daylight and routine can obscure. It repeatedly contrasts maps, schedules, and purposeful travel with detours, uncertainty, and attention. Its moral emphasis is that getting lost and doing something “unproductive” can restore presence; the closing image turns wrong turns into a chosen practice.

## Evidence line
> Wandering, at its best, is a practice in stillness disguised as movement.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the essay sustains a coherent, distinctive preference for attention and uncertainty over efficiency, reinforced by recurring scenes of walking, detours, and quiet observation.
