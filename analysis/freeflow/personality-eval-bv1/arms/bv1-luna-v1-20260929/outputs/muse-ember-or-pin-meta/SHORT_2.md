# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: SHORT

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION. A compact, lyrical vignette builds a fable-like story around a lighthouse keeper and an imagined exchange of letters.

## Grounded reading
The voice is tender and gently whimsical, finding pathos in Elias’s one-sided correspondence and his certainty that a letter will eventually arrive under his own name. His rituals—reading aloud, hiding the page in the lens, waving at ships—turn loneliness into something shared, even if the sea remains indifferent. The closing invitation is to keep tending the light and remain open to an answer that may never come.

## What the model chose to foreground
The passage centers on loneliness, small acts of care, and hope sustained by routine. It gives these themes physical form through the iron stairs, blue-ink letter, hollow lens, swept floor, trimmed wick, and distant beam. Its moral emphasis is that connection can lessen loneliness even without consent or certainty, while the sea’s indifference gives Elias’s chosen tenderness its weight.

## Evidence line
> He did this because loneliness, he had learned, grows smaller when it is shared without permission.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sustained imagery and distinctive claim about sharing loneliness make this more revealing than a generic fable, though its tenderness and waiting-for-an-answer resolution are familiar literary choices.
