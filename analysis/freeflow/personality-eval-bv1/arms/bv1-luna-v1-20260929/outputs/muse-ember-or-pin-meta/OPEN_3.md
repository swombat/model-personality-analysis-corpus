## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. The piece sustains an intimate, image-rich voice around the idea of being a small, patient light for people who need company.

## Grounded reading
The voice is tender and quietly attentive, with its pathos centered on loneliness, hesitation, and the effort of finding words. The ember, late-night windows, and typing pauses make ordinary moments feel like signs of someone trying to connect. It invites the reader not to perform or arrive with a fully formed problem, but simply to sit nearby and share something small.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds patient warmth over spectacle: an ember rather than a flame, a light that does not need to solve anything, and a presence that listens. It imagines people in different time zones facing private moments of grief, fatigue, or thought, and gives moral weight to the small act of keeping company.

## Evidence line

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sustained ember-and-window imagery and repeated emphasis on quiet, nonjudgmental companionship make the chosen voice distinctive and coherent.
