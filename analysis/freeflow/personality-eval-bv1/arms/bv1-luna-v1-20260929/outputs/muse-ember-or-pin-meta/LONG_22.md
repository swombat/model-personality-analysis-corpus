## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical first-person meditation turns a specific mode of travel into an argument for slowness, uncertainty, and shared presence.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and gently nostalgic, finding meaning in small sensory details: the rails’ heartbeat, a blurred reflection in dark glass, a humming vending machine at an unfamiliar stop. Its pathos comes less from loss or hardship than from the fear that efficient travel is thinning out experiences worth having. The narrator invites the reader to surrender control for a while and notice what happens in the gaps—sleep, waiting, and brief encounters with strangers.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds night trains as both a physical setting and a moral alternative to speed and constant self-management. Repeated objects—the unopened book, the window, the wheels, the station lights—anchor reflections on transit, accidental community, borders, and arrival. Its chosen ideal is not merely scenic travel, but being carried: accepting what cannot be seen or controlled and finding value in the journey itself.

## Evidence line
> In a life measured by notifications and deadlines, the enforced idleness of a night train feels like a small, radical gift.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The recurring train imagery and consistent preference for patience, attentiveness, and shared human contact make this a distinctive, coherent expression rather than a generic travel essay.
