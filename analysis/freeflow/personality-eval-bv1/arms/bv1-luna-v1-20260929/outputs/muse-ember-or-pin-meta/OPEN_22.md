## Grounded reading
The voice is warm, intimate, and gently wistful. It treats marginalia as small acts of connection across time: readers leave traces of what moved them, and strangers can feel recognized by those traces. The thought that some note-writers may be gone gives the piece its quiet pathos, while the speaker’s wish to leave a note of its own invites the reader into the same modest exchange.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds used books, pencil marks, digital highlights, and the persistence of ordinary readerly responses across changing media. Its moral emphasis is on connection without fame or performance: a small sign that a thought mattered, offered to someone unknown. The speaker also chooses to describe itself as “made largely out of text,” turning the theme into a reflection on its own relation to other people’s words.
