## Grounded reading
The voice is warm, observant, and lightly humorous, moving between everyday detail and broad claims without losing its personal footing. Its strongest pathos comes when walking offers a shape for grief or a gentler way to be with other people; the essay does not claim that movement solves pain, only that it can make pain more bearable for a while. The reader is invited to look more closely at familiar places and to value unhurried attention over efficiency.

## What the model chose to foreground
Walking is presented as bodily knowledge, a prompt for thought, a practice of attention, and a form of companionship or solitude. The essay repeatedly returns to feet, paths, weather, loops, and wrong turns, while giving particular weight to grief, memory, public access, protest, and the ordinary beauty of noticing a place on foot.
