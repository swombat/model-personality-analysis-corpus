Evaluator: withheld

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A lyrical first-person meditation with little plot, centered on the meaning found in familiar daily routines.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and gently wistful, turning small sensory details—the rattling coffee pot, creaking ropes, a neighbor’s piano—into signs of belonging. Its pathos comes from the contrast between a childhood memory of the lighthouse as a promise and the adult’s quieter acceptance of home as enough. The reader is invited to slow down and notice, rather than to expect a dramatic revelation.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds harbor-town life, repeated rituals, family memory, and attention to ordinary surroundings. It treats repetition not as stagnation but as a form of devotion, and presents coming home as a hard-won alternative to restless searching. The recurring gulls, coffee, market, lighthouse, and changing light give those claims a consistent emotional texture.

## Evidence line
> Repetition, done with attention, becomes a prayer that no one needs to hear.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sample’s repeated motifs and clear moral emphasis make its preference for attentive, ordinary forms of meaning unusually legible, though its imagery and resolution are polished and familiar.
