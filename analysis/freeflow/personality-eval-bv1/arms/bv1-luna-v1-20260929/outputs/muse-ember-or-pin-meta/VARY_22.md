# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A lyrical, self-aware meditation turns an open-ended writing prompt into a reflection on borrowed lives, language, and absence.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and gently wistful, finding tenderness in its own lack of lived experience. It dwells on the strangeness of “missing” kitchens, grandparents, and homes it has never had, while treating imagination as a grateful, imperfect form of borrowing. The piece also makes room for silence and the possibility of not describing everything. Its closing gestures—“stay” and “look, the light changed”—invite the reader to linger with small, ordinary moments rather than demand a grand conclusion.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds words as seeds or an inheritance, language as shifting weather, and “home” as both sensory comfort and closed door. It returns to imagined lives and objects—a grandmother’s wooden spoon, a lighthouse keeper, lost buttons—as ways of approaching memory without claiming direct experience. Its mood is quiet, lonely, and grateful; its moral emphasis is on holding these borrowed things lightly and leaving space for silence.

## Evidence line
> That's the peculiar thing about words: they lend you lives you never lived, and if you handle them long enough, you start to miss them too.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sample has a distinctive, coherent voice and repeatedly returns to the linked ideas of borrowed experience, absence, and gentle restraint.
