# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: MID

## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A lyrical first-person meditation that builds a recognizable voice around unhurried attention and everyday rituals.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm, measured, and quietly resistant to the pressure to make every moment useful. Its pathos comes from noticing what modern routines seem to have thinned out: wandering, casual conversation, weather-watching, rereading, and the small ceremonies of evening. The reader is invited not to make a grand change, but to slow down and recognize ordinary details as part of a life worth inhabiting.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample repeatedly turns toward streets, maps, weather, strangers, books, lamps, and household sounds. It treats getting lost and doing things without a destination as small acts of freedom, and frames attention—not achievement or efficiency—as a way to feel connected to place, other people, and time.

## Evidence line
> Getting lost on purpose is a rebellion against the tyranny of the blue dot, that pulsing marker on the phone screen that tells us where we are and, by implication, where we should be going next.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the recurring objects and consistent defense of unhurried attention give this sample a clear expressive through-line.
