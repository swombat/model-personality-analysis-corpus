## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A compact, lyrical harbor story uses a lighthouse keeper’s grief and daily duty to give shape to a quiet moral fable.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and earnest, turning bereavement into a story of continued care rather than dramatic recovery. Its pathos rests on small acts: the keeper’s nightly climb, the villagers’ soup left without asking for thanks, and the dead wife’s remembered laughter. The reader is invited to see steadfast service as a form of love, and to find comfort in the idea that helping others find their way home can make grief bearable.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds the lighthouse, the storm, the keeper’s deceased wife, and the village’s understated kindness. Its moral emphasis is that keeping watch matters because it guides others, even when the watcher is grieving or cannot be sure of safety. The warm, consoling resolution makes persistence feel like companionship with the lost.

## Evidence line
> He does not fear the storm because he understands that keeping watch is less about remaining safe than ensuring others can find their way home.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: The sample is coherent and consistently shaped by a distinctive preference for quiet grief, unasked-for kindness, and duty as a source of comfort.
