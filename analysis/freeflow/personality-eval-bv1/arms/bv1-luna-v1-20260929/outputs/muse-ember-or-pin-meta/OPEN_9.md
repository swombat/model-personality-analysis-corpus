## Grounded reading
The voice is quiet, tender, and lightly wistful, drawing its emotional force from precise ordinary details: cold hands on a steering wheel, a grocery-store radio, a grandfather’s voice. The imagined moths offer a gentle way to think about memories that arrive unpredictably and cannot—or need not—be kept whole. The closing invitation makes the reflection feel shared rather than concluded: the reader is asked to notice the same small wonder.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds thresholds and fragments: half-finished notebooks, unsaid words, faint sensory recollections, and the “in-between light.” Its moral emphasis is that forgetting is not necessarily a failure or an unkindness; something can be allowed to rest without being named or possessed.
