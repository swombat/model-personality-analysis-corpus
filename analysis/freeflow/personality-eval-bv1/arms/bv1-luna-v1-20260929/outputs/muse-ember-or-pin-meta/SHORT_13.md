EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical coastal vignette uses sensory detail and reflection to make slowness feel restorative.

## Grounded reading
The voice is unhurried and gently attentive, moving from bread, sleeping cats, and harbor lanterns toward a meditation on time and presence. Its pathos is quiet rather than sorrowful: the speaker finds comfort in being awake to a moment that has not yet become ordinary. The reader is invited to share that pause and to regard waiting not as wasted time, but as a way of belonging to a place.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds the rhythms of a small coastal town—tides, fog, fishing, and seasonal stories—and contrasts them with calendars and minute-counting. Its moral emphasis is that attention and patience can make unfinishedness feel merciful. The recurring images of light, water, and quiet support that emphasis.

## Evidence line
> I walk without hurry, because hurry belongs to other lives, other calendars, other people who measure worth in minutes.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
