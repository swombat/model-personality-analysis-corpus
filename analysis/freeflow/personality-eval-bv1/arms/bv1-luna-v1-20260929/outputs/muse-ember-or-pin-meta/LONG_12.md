## Sample kind
GENERIC_ESSAY — A polished, coherent meditation that develops a clear thesis through familiar reflective essay conventions rather than a highly distinctive personal voice.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm, contemplative, and gently instructive. Its pathos comes from ordinary objects standing in for loss: a coat, a letter, a drawer of keepsakes. It treats keeping as both tenderness and burden, and resists the easy answer that we should preserve only what makes us happy. The invitation is to regard memory as a practice of attention and release, not simply accumulation.

## What the model chose to foreground
The essay repeatedly returns to drawers, keys, photographs, letters, stones, and digital files as witnesses to lived experience. It foregrounds the tension between preserving memory and becoming weighed down by it, with grief, fear of mortality, family inheritance, and the value of deliberate attention as its main moral concerns.

## Evidence line
> We are, all of us, accidental archivists.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the essay sustains its object-centered meditation and balanced emphasis on keeping and letting go, though its polished, familiar essay form makes the choices less stylistically distinctive.
