## Sample kind
GENERIC_ESSAY — A polished, thesis-driven essay that develops a familiar case for attention through a first-person morning vignette and a sequence of everyday examples.

## Grounded reading
The voice is calm, inviting, and gently self-correcting: it begins with a coffee mug, a tree, and a few minutes of quiet, then turns those details into an argument about how distraction narrows experience. Its pathos comes from the tension between wanting to be present and having trained oneself to fill every gap. The essay does not promise that attention makes life easy; it insists that looking and listening more closely also reveal suffering. The reader is invited to practice small acts of staying—putting down the phone, walking without headphones, listening without preparing a reply—rather than to master a grand system.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample repeatedly centers ordinary particulars: ants on a tree, a kettle, a beetle, a painting, a friend’s pauses. It frames attention as generosity, love, memory, and truthful contact with the world, while casting phones and hurried habits as forces that pull perception away from those particulars. The mood is contemplative and earnest, with a moral emphasis on presence that stops short of treating distraction as a personal failure.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
