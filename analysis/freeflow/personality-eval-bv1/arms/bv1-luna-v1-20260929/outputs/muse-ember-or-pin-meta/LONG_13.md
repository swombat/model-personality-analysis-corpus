## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical, wandering essay turns ordinary observations into recurring images and reflections rather than building a tightly argued thesis.

## Grounded reading
The voice is attentive, self-conscious, and gently inviting: it admits the vertigo of an open prompt, then makes wandering itself the subject. Its pathos comes from noticing how much meaning gathers around small, perishable things—cold tea, marked-up books, unclaimed keys, a mother’s laugh—and from the wish to keep them without pretending they can be preserved intact. The reader is invited to slow down alongside the narrator, to treat attention as a form of care and to let a thought’s detours count as part of its meaning.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample repeatedly foregrounds repair, marginal notes, boredom, old maps, hinges, weather, and remembered moments. These objects and habits support a moral emphasis on keeping, noticing, and returning rather than replacing or rushing on. The closing image returns to changing light, reinforcing the essay’s investment in ordinary attention rather than a definitive conclusion.

## Evidence line
> Everything in them has once been broken and wanted back.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The sample is coherent and stylistically distinctive, with recurring objects and themes that make its preference for patient attention unusually clear.
