## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A lyrical magical-realist story built around a lighthouse that restores lost memories.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and attentive to ordinary sensory details: yeast and cinnamon, a peach, a train seat vibrating under someone’s legs. The story’s tenderness comes from treating small, embodied memories as precious, while its turn toward Silas’s own loss gives the helper a quiet vulnerability. It invites the reader to notice what they might miss in the texture of everyday life, without insisting on a lesson.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds memory as intimate and sensory rather than grand or historical, and imagines care as a reciprocal act: Silas helps others recover what they have lost, then receives something of his own. Its mood is wistful but warm, with humor in the umbrella episode and a consoling ending in which imperfect recollection is enough.
