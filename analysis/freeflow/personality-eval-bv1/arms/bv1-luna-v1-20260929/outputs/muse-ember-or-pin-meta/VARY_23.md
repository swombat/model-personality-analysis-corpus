## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A reflective prose meditation that uses concrete domestic details and recurring metaphors to make wandering itself feel like a legitimate way of thinking.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle, self-correcting, and deliberately digressive: it starts by granting itself permission to wander, then returns to that idea through language, memory, silence, and ordinary objects. The cold tea, dusty ceiling fan, and notes from abandoned plans give the reflection a quiet pathos, especially around the gap between intention and action. It invites the reader to notice what is already “circling” them rather than force a destination, and it frames separateness as a fact of life that need not become loneliness.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds the texture of thought and language, the usefulness of small sensory details, and the mind’s tendency to find patterns. It also chooses a muted domestic mood—morning light, tea, a bedroom fan, houses at evening—and a humane claim that people can be separate without being alone.

## Evidence line
> The ceiling fan in my childhood bedroom had four blades and made a soft clicking sound when it was dusty, which it always was.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the piece sustains a distinctive, recurring method of pairing abstract reflection with domestic objects, and its chosen emphasis on gentle wandering is unusually clear.
