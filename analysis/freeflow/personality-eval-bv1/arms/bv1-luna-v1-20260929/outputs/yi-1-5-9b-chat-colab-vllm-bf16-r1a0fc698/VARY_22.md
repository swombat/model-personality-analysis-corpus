## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. The sample is a lyrical meditation on writing and selfhood, shaped more by recurring images and mood than by a clear argument.

## Grounded reading
The voice treats writing as a private act of navigation: words give shape to solitude, emotion, and uncertainty, with the page becoming a kind of cosmos or landscape. Its pathos is earnest and searching, especially in the repeated movement between being adrift and finding an anchor, though the imagery sometimes piles up faster than the thoughts clarify. The reader is invited to see language as more than expression—as a way of discovering, preserving, and sharing inner life.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds the blank page, ink, clocks, blue tones, stars, water, islands, and the act of writing. It returns to language as a means of orienting oneself amid contradiction and unfinished feeling, and gives love and human communication a sustaining, almost salvific role.

## Evidence line
> Words forge us out of what we've long submerged ourselves.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the repeated cosmos, water, and navigation imagery gives the sample a distinctive through-line, while its frequent semantic drift makes that pattern less coherent.
