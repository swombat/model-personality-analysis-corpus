## Grounded reading

Its pathos lies in the near-forgetting of unseen guardians: Anax and the intermediary beings are cast as quiet stewards whose difficult work is eclipsed by the more visible exploits of gods. It gives special weight to equilibrium and managed change: Anax allows conflict to continue while preventing any one deity from becoming too powerful, and creates new worlds to absorb the multiverse’s growth. The repeated image of hidden shepherds guiding realities suggests a preference here for secret custodianship and grand, ordered cosmology.
