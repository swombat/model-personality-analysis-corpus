## Grounded reading

Its pathos comes from finitude: people and stars pass away, while recurring seasons and remembered moments offer a fragile sense of continuity. The prose repeatedly invites the reader to slow down and notice the everyday as meaningful. Its images can become tangled—the “ongoing shalala of denial” and “pentagram of a star,” for example—so the invitation is clearer than the argument.

## What the model chose to foreground

It frames presence and attention as a way to meet loss, returning to the claim that transient moments matter because they cannot be kept.
