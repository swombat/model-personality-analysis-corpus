# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld  
Source model: `withheld`  
Condition: LONG

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A short, allegorical scenario uses a stressed person’s encounter with prophetic animals to turn toward environmental advocacy.

## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle and earnest, moving from everyday overwhelm to calm in the woods and then to purposeful action. Nature is presented as a source of perspective and renewal, while the animals’ “vision” makes environmental stewardship feel like a shared moral calling. The reader is invited to imagine stepping away from routine and finding a cause worth joining; the narrative’s abrupt, dreamlike turns keep that invitation more generic than fully developed.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds stress, restorative nature, foresight, community, and environmental protection. It resolves personal renewal through service to the planet, rather than through individual success alone.

## Evidence line
> Together, they work tirelessly to protect the natural world and ensure that it continues to thrive.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Low: the environmental emphasis is clear, but the story is generic and its unusual animal-and-book imagery is not developed into a distinctive voice.
