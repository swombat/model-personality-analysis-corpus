## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and carefully atmospheric, inviting the reader to linger over the small traces left in books rather than focus on collecting rare editions. Its pathos is gentle: a grocery list, a leaf, or a personal inscription makes absent lives briefly present. The closing image turns this tenderness toward the reader, framing us as temporary keepers of stories.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds tactile and sensory details—dust, old paper, cedar, creased spines—and the human residue tucked among them. It values used books as vessels of affection and memory, set against a world “obsessed with the frictionless and the new.” The mood is warm and contemplative, with a quiet awareness of time and loss.

## Evidence line
