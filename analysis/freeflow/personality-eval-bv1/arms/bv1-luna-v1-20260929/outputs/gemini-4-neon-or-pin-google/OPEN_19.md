## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and gently wistful, finding intimacy not in grand revelations but in a stranger’s underlined sentence, an unsent postcard, or a grocery list. Its pathos comes from the contrast between ordinary, vanished lives and the durable marks they leave behind. The reader is invited to treat attention as a form of connection—and perhaps to leave a small trace for someone who comes later.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds used bookstores, marginalia, improvised stories about anonymous owners, and the physical persistence of marks on paper. Its moral emphasis is that a life need not leave a grand legacy to be visible: attention to a sentence, recorded in pencil, can become a modest gesture across time. The contrast with frictionless digital media gives that gesture a quiet defiance.
