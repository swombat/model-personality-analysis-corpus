## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. It is a polished reflective essay, but its recurring cartographic metaphor and close attention to sensory detail give it a distinctively lyrical voice.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and warmly attentive, inviting the reader to treat ordinary sensations and worn objects as meaningful landmarks rather than background. Its pathos is quiet: home and belonging emerge through familiar sounds, the marks of use, and memories of seemingly minor moments. The closing invitation is to keep noticing, not to make grander plans or recover some lost past.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds sensory memory as a way of mapping a life: apartment noises, desire paths and polished surfaces, the stillness of early morning, and small remembered gestures. It favors contact and familiarity over pristine order, and everyday footnotes over public milestones. The chosen moral emphasis is that attention makes ordinary experience into a form of belonging.
