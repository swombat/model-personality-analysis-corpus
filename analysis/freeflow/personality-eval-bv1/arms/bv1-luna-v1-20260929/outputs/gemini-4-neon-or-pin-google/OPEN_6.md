## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A lyrical reflection turns an ordinary feature of city parks into a metaphor for collective agency and human habit.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm, observant, and quietly admiring. It finds pathos not in dramatic rebellion but in the accumulation of small, ordinary choices: strangers repeatedly stepping across grass until a shared route becomes visible. The reader is invited to notice these traces and to see them as evidence that people’s lived needs can outlast a planner’s tidy design.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds desire paths, footsteps, grids, fences, and the contrast between satellite views and life on the ground. It chooses a gentle moral emphasis: design should attend to how people actually move, and human agency often emerges through repeated, uncoordinated acts rather than explicit protest.

## Evidence line
> It is a democracy of footsteps.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the recurring contrast between imposed order and lived behavior gives the piece a distinctive, coherent voice, and its chosen metaphor carries through to the closing reflection.
