## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical essay uses marginal notes, neglected places, and unfilled time to make a case for attention to overlooked forms of life.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and gently elegiac, inviting the reader to slow down and notice what ordinary efficiency and ambition tend to erase. Its pathos comes from small traces of unknown lives—a pressed leaf, an underlined sentence, a kindness whose recipient’s name was never learned—and from the possibility that these traces offer companionship across time. The essay’s invitation is not simply to be more productive or creative, but to leave room for unscheduled thought and for the quiet, imperfect marks people make on one another.

## What the model chose to foreground
The essay repeatedly turns to margins: notes in used books, wild growth in neglected land, idle intervals, and people temporarily outside their usual roles. It opposes managed efficiency and constant stimulation to pauses where creativity and unexpected connection can emerge. Its closing image returns to the secondhand paperback, presenting small acts of attention and kindness as faint marks that may comfort someone later.

## Evidence line
> Marginalia is one of the most accidental forms of intimacy human beings have ever invented.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The essay’s recurring imagery and moral emphasis make its attentiveness to overlooked traces unusually clear and coherent.
