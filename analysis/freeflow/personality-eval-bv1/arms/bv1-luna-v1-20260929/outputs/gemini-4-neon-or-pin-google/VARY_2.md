## Grounded reading
The voice is restrained and tactile, using rain, fluorescent hum, warm cloth, and small domestic objects to carry grief without explaining it away. Leo’s separation and Salvo’s restaurant closing echo one another; their shared work offers comfort without pity or forced intimacy. The story invites the reader to trust practical kindness and ritual: folding, washing, and carrying something warm can make an ending more bearable, even before it feels resolved.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds departures and the labor of leaving well: a quilt tied to a former home, tablecloths from a closing restaurant, and a mother’s rule about cleaning before departure. Its mood is lonely but gently restorative, with warmth, food, and shared work standing in for grand consolation. The closing image turns unpacking mugs into a modest sign of beginning again.
