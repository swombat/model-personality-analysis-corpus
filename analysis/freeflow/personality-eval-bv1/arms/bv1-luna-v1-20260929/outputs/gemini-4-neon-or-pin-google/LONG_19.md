## Grounded reading

Its central pathos is quiet loss—not simply of paper maps, but of the attentive, embodied experience of finding one’s way. ## What the model chose to foreground

It contrasts the blue dot and turn-by-turn route with Polynesian wayfinding, private memory, and urban drifting. Its moral emphasis is that location is not the same as presence, and that some uncertainty and inefficiency make room for attention, surprise, and personal meaning.
