## Grounded reading
The voice is attentive and intimate, moving from the vivid specificity of sound work to the grief of leaving a family home. Its pathos comes through sensory details rather than direct declarations: the shutter’s slow knock, the furnace’s “muffled *whump*,” and the house dropping “half an octave” make absence tangible. The piece invites the reader to listen more closely to ordinary surroundings and to recognize them as part of a life, not as empty background.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds ambient sound as a record of presence: room tone, household machinery, small animal noises, and the acoustics of an emptied home. It connects craft and mourning, suggesting that the unnoticed margins of life can hold what photographs and dramatic moments miss. The chosen mood is tender and elegiac, with a moral emphasis on attention to the ordinary.
