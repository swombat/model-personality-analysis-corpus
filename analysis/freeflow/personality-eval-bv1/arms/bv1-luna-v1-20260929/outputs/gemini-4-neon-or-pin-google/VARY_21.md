## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — An epistolary piece of literary fiction, shaped as a departing tenant’s affectionate, practical letter to whoever moves in next.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant and gently intimate: it turns radiator knocks, floorboards, warm tomatoes, and late-afternoon light into a map of how a life settles into a place. Its pathos stays restrained; the narrator names loss and loneliness without making them the whole story, and lets small continuities—pencil marks, cello practice, shared meals—carry the feeling. The reader is invited not just to inhabit the apartment but to notice it, care for its quirks, and make a life there of their own.

## What the model chose to foreground
The piece foregrounds domestic details as repositories of memory, alongside ordinary grief, recovery, and the possibility of beginning again. Its central moral claim is modest but clear: a place can help hold someone through change, and its meaning is renewed by the next person who lives there.

## Evidence line
> Mostly, though, this apartment held me together while I figured out who I was going to be next.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium — The sample is coherent and distinctive in its sustained attention to ordinary objects as carriers of tenderness, loss, and renewal.
