## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A lyrical first-person meditation built around a vividly observed late-night ferry crossing.

## Grounded reading
The voice is attentive and unhurried, finding tenderness in the temporary anonymity of strangers sharing a crossing. Its pathos is quiet: a tired worker, an older reader, and two teenagers appear only in passing, yet their small gestures make the ferry feel like a refuge from private demands. The piece invites the reader to value the in-between—not as wasted time, but as a brief state of completeness before ordinary responsibilities return.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds transit after dark: fluorescent light, engine vibration, salt air, black water, scattered windows, and a buoy’s patient warning. These details gather around a moral claim that suspension and anonymity can be meaningful in themselves, even as arrival restores people to “our names, our keys” and the business of home.

## Evidence line
> There is a particular kindness to late-night transit.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the coherent imagery and recurring emphasis on quiet attention give this sample a distinct voice and a clear preoccupation with liminal moments.
