## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A quiet, atmospheric short story in which a brief power outage brings three strangers into a moment of shared attention.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and sensory, making room for small sounds and textures—the B-flat hum, rain on glass, wax and tea—to carry emotional weight. Its pathos is restrained: the older man’s grief over his brother’s coat is met not with advice or a cure, but with silence and acknowledgment. The story invites the reader to notice how ordinary, temporary acts of care can make loneliness feel less vast.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model chose a late-night laundromat, storm-darkened city, stalled machines, candles, hot tea, and a coat kept after a death. These objects turn a power failure into a pause in which exhaustion and grief can be shared without being solved. The moral emphasis is modest and communal: attention, hospitality, and sitting quietly with another person matter, even when nothing grand changes.

## Evidence line
> People came in the dark because their lives didn’t fit daylight hours, or because insomnia felt less lonely beside a warm dryer.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the story coherently returns to sound, warmth, and ordinary objects as ways of expressing loneliness and care, though its resolution follows a familiar gentle-literary arc.
