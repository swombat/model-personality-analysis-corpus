## Grounded reading
The voice is reflective and gently persuasive, moving from nostalgia for being lost to a broader concern that convenience narrows attention and discovery. Its pathos lies in small, sensory details—the bakery’s steamed windows, the smell of cedar after rain, a shopkeeper’s crooked map—set against impersonal grids and recommendation systems. It invites the reader to reclaim curiosity through a modest act: walking without a route.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds GPS, old maps, urban details, detours, and algorithmic feeds as examples of how efficiency can crowd out attention and happy accidents. Its moral claim is not that inconvenience is inherently good, but that certainty and optimization are not the same as finding what matters.
