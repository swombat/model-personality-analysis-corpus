## What the model chose to foreground
The essay foregrounds the gap between representation and reality: maps can preserve errors, bring fictional places into being, and erase what once mattered. It links that gap to the fading of geographic mystery, the erosion of digital records, personal memory, nostalgia, and grief. Its moral emphasis is not on rejecting accuracy, but on holding knowledge lightly and staying attentive to change.
