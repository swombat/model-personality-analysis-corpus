## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A lyrical first-person story that turns a night-shift pumping station into a meditation on unseen civic labor.

## Grounded reading
The narrator’s voice is observant, patient, and quietly proud. Technical details—the pressure gauges, turbines, and demand curve—make the work concrete, while the imagined lives behind each tremor in the flow give it human warmth. The story’s pathos lies in service that succeeds by going unnoticed: the city sleeps through its rescue, and the worker finds that more satisfying than applause. It invites the reader to notice the buried systems and people that make ordinary life feel effortless.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds hidden labor, public infrastructure, and pride in work that receives little recognition. Its recurring images of water moving through darkness connect the narrator’s father’s bricklaying, the rescue during the outage, and the small leak of the narrator’s own kitchen faucet. The moral emphasis is that care can be most meaningful when those who benefit never have to think about it.

## Evidence line
> Good infrastructure is supposed to feel like magic, which is just another word for work done out of sight.
