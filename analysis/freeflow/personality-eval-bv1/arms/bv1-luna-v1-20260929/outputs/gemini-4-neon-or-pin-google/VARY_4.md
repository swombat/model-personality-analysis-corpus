## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical meditation on fleeting intervals and how ordinary sensory details become unexpectedly durable memories.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate, patient, and attentive to small physical particulars: amber streetlights, warm lint, a raincoat’s ochre, a chipped mug. Its pathos comes from the gap between what we expect to matter and what memory actually keeps. The piece invites the reader to slow down and regard unremarkable pauses not as wasted time but as places where life quietly gathers meaning.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds transitional times and spaces: the hinge of dawn, a midnight laundromat, a delayed train, the moments before and after more defined events. It gives special weight to transience, sensory observation, and memory’s unpredictable selection of details. Its moral emphasis is gentle rather than prescriptive: attention to passing moments can itself be enough.
