## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical, scene-rich meditation on insomnia turns a late-night walk through the city into a source of quiet consolation.

## Grounded reading
The voice is intimate and observant, inviting the reader to stop treating wakefulness as a private failure and notice the shared life of the city. Its pathos comes from the contrast between anxious thoughts in a dark bedroom and the steady, largely unseen work continuing outside: baking, street cleaning, keeping watch over a child. The comfort it offers is gentle rather than triumphant; the narrator returns to bed with worries not solved, but put into a larger perspective.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds the hour between days, ordinary urban details, and people awake for different reasons. Streetlights, bakery vents, a laundromat, a glowing kitchen window, and a sparrow become signs of continuity and companionship. Its moral emphasis is that strangers’ quiet labor sustains the world, and that recognizing this can make private distress feel less isolating.

## Evidence line
> We imagine ourselves stranded alone in the dark, yet the night is stitched together by these small, luminous squares—a quiet archipelago of the awake.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sustained imagery, recurring attention to overlooked workers, and consistent movement from unease toward modest comfort make this a distinctive sample.
