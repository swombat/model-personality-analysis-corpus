## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. A reflective personal vignette turns secondhand-book traces into a meditation on intimacy across time.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm, observant, and gently sentimental, finding emotional presence in ordinary marks: a crease, an underline, a tea ring. Eleanor’s marginal notes give the reflection its human center; the narrator imagines companionship without claiming to know her. The piece invites the reader to see used books not as damaged objects but as places where strangers have briefly met. Its closing gesture—adding an underline for a future reader—makes that invitation reciprocal.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds marginalia, forgotten objects, and the physical wear of books as evidence of lives lived alongside reading. It contrasts polished public self-presentation with private, unguarded reactions, and gives moral weight to the idea of leaving something for an unknown person in the future.

Medium: the piece is coherent and distinctive in its recurring attention to intimate traces, though its reflective, lyrical voice is concentrated in one carefully shaped vignette.
