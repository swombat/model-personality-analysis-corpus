## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle, observant, and quietly wistful. It finds emotional weight in small, concrete traces—a grocery list, a faded boarding pass, an abandoned bookmark—and imagines the unknown readers behind them without claiming to know their stories. The piece invites the reader to treat books not as pristine objects but as places where lives briefly meet, and closes by making the speaker’s own marginalia an act of kindness toward a future stranger.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds secondhand books, marginal notes, and ordinary paper keepsakes as links between strangers across time. Its mood is tender rather than mournful: lost context matters, but so does the possibility that a marked sentence can make someone feel less alone. It also favors physical wear and personal imperfection over digital cleanliness, ending on the moral image of leaving a lantern lit for someone who comes later.

## Evidence line
