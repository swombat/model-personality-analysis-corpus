## Grounded reading
The voice is gentle, observant, and quietly sentimental. It finds pathos not in grand loss but in small traces of attention: an inscription, a boarding pass, a forcefully underlined sentence. The piece invites the reader to imagine the previous life of a book and to see reading as less solitary than it seems.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds secondhand books as vessels of lived history, especially the intimate marks left by their owners. Its central moral claim is that stories gain meaning through being handled and passed along, set against the weightlessness of digital text.
