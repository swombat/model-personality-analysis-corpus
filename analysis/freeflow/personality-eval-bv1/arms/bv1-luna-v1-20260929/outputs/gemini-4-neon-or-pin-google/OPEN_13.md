## Grounded reading
The voice is tender, observant, and gently wistful. It lingers over pencil marks, a grocery list, a pressed leaf, and the physical wear of a book, treating them as traces of lives that remain partly unknowable. The piece invites the reader to see reading as less solitary: another person’s attention can make a stranger feel briefly present, and leaving one’s own marks behind may comfort someone in the future.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds accidental traces of use—marginalia, bookmarks, coffee rings, and worn pages—and the possibility that these small objects connect strangers across time. Its moral emphasis is that visible evidence of having loved and used something can be more humanly meaningful than digital cleanliness or convenience.
