# BV1_SAMPLE — `withheld`

Evaluator: withheld
Source model: `withheld`
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION. This is a quiet, atmospheric short story built around a stranger’s farewell and the small object he cannot leave behind.

## Grounded reading
The voice is patient and sensory, using the fluorescent hum, black water, fog, and bitter coffee to make the terminal feel suspended between departure and morning. Its pathos is restrained: Arthur offers coffee rather than pity, and the older man’s grief emerges through practical details—selling the house, giving things away, carrying a lemon tree onto the ferry. The story invites the reader to notice what people preserve when the rest of a life is being packed away.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds night-shift solitude, ordinary acts of care, and the way grief attaches itself to a living thing. The lemon tree, grown from a grocery-store seed and kept beside the wife’s bed, becomes a portable memory; Arthur’s quiet hospitality gives that memory a witness. The ending favors continued waiting over a dramatic resolution: the man departs, while Arthur returns to his booth.

## Evidence line
> Everyone sitting on the bolted wooden benches at this hour is carrying something heavy, even when their hands are empty.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the carefully recurring imagery of fog, light, and carried weight makes the story distinctive within itself, while its restrained grief and ferry-terminal setting remain familiar literary choices.
