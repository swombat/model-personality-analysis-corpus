## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A polished, intimate reflection turns secondhand-book traces into a meditation on ordinary lives and connection across time.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warmly observant and gently wistful, attentive to small physical evidence: fading pencil, a pressed clover, a grocery list tucked into a book. Its pathos comes from the gap between a person’s brief, ordinary moment and the long life of the object left behind. The narrator invites the reader to imagine previous owners without insisting on knowing their stories, then makes that imagined continuity personal by reaching for a pencil too.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds marginalia and accidental bookmarks as quiet records of other people’s lives. It favors ordinary detail over grand revelation—the list of “milk, batteries, stamps, call Mom”—and frames reading as solitary but not entirely alone. Its closing moral gesture is modest: leave a small sign of having been present, and let the book continue onward.

## Evidence line

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
