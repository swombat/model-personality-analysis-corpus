It turns small marks—a labyrinth, a penciled *Lost.*, a date beside a sentence about grief—into traces of lives that briefly meet through a shared book. The sample foregrounds secondhand books, handwritten marginalia, grief, time, and the contrast between physical books and digital screens. Its central moral claim is that ideas gain human meaning through being marked, disputed, and passed between people.
