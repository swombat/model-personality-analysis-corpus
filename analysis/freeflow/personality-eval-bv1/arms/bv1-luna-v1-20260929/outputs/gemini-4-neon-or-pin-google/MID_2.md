EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical first-person meditation that turns used-book annotations into a way of feeling connected to unknown readers across time.

## Grounded reading
The voice is observant, affectionate, and gently melancholy. It lingers on tactile traces—the fading ink, pressed leaf, worn staircase—as evidence that people have touched and cared about things. The essay’s emotional center is not literary discovery but the intimacy of recognizing a stranger’s response in a book. Its closing gesture invites the reader to join that chain of attention by leaving a note for someone in the future.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds secondhand bookstores, marginalia, and ordinary objects left between pages, then links them to a broader contrast between human wear and digital or pristine surfaces. It treats use not as damage but as proof of presence, attention, and continuity between strangers.
