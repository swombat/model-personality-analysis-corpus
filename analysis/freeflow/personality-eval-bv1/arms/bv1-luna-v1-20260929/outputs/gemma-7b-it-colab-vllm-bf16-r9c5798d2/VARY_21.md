Its pathos comes through people who arrive hungry, weary, or seeking comfort, and through their reactions to her bread. ## What the model chose to foreground

It foregrounds Maria’s energetic, generous labor and a community joined by her bakery; the girl and soldier function as brief witnesses to the comfort she provides. The chosen moral emphasis is that care offered through simple work can reach beyond the immediate moment.
