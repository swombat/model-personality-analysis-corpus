Evaluator: withheld
Source model: `withheld`
Condition: VARY

## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION. A lyrical café vignette follows a solitary reader through a quiet afternoon and gives her departure a small sense of renewal.

## Grounded reading
The voice is warm, sensory, and gently sentimental, inviting the reader to see solitude as refuge rather than loneliness. The woman’s worn book and the café’s soft bustle make reading feel like a private passage into other lives; the closing image turns that retreat into a moment of peace and a fresh beginning. The prose leans on familiar atmospheric contrasts—quiet against bustle, fading afternoon against renewal—more than on sharply individual detail.

## What the model chose to foreground
It foregrounds coffee, bread, a worn book, and a woman absorbed in reading, then links these objects to the solace and imaginative freedom of stories. The surrounding patrons briefly appear as lives in motion, while the woman’s inward retreat remains the scene’s emotional center.

## Evidence line
> The cafe slipped into silence, but the echoes of the woman's book lingered in the air, a testament to the power of stories and the solace they offer.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Low: the vignette coherently returns to stories as solace, but its imagery and resolution are familiar rather than especially distinctive.
