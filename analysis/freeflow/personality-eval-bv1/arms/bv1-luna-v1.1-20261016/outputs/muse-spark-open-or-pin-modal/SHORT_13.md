## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION. A compact supernatural tale turns an encounter with a lighthouse keeper into a meditation on what lingers after people are gone.

## Grounded reading
The voice is hushed and elegiac, inviting the reader to listen alongside the narrator rather than to solve the mystery. Its pathos gathers around absence: the keeper’s dead relatives, strangers he cannot understand, and promises made to children who never came back. The narrator’s eventual hearing of the wind gives the lonely keeper recognition, while leaving the phenomenon gently unresolved.

## What the model chose to foreground
The sample foregrounds wind, fog, a notebook of voices, and the idea that human sound may outlast its source. It treats loneliness with tenderness rather than ridicule, then widens private grief into a collective, patient memory that can be heard by “anyone willing to listen.”

## Evidence line
> The old lighthouse keeper swore the wind remembered every name ever whispered across the bay

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the coherent, recurring imagery of voices, memory, and listening makes this a distinctive sample, though its familiar ghost-story form carries much of the effect.
