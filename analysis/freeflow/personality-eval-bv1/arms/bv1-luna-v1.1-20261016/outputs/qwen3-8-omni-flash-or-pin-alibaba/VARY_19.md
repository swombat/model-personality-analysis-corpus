## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A long anaphoric poem uses the same “I watch” frame to gather images of nature, city life, history, and human feeling.

## Grounded reading
The voice is steady and observant, more intent on accumulating than explaining. Its repeated cadence makes the poem feel incantatory, while the images move between quiet natural scenes and human traces of work, memory, belief, and loss. The closing appeal to a “common sky” invites the reader to see these varied lives and places as sharing one frame.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds watching as a way of taking in a wide world: animals, weather, transport, objects, crowds, ruins, and intimate human emotions all appear beneath a changing sky. It repeatedly pairs contrasts—quiet and loud, old and new, lost and mended—and ends by naming shared humanity and the making of words.

## Evidence line
