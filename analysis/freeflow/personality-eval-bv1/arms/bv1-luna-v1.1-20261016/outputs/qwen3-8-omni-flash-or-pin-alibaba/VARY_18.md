## Grounded reading

Its pathos is quiet rather than despairing: the speaker feels an ache for “easy love,” yet finds small relief in a stranger’s nod, a remembered grandfather, and the prospect of soup and books. The accumulating images invite the reader to slow down and notice how private lives glow around us, even when we cannot know them.

It repeatedly treats light, names, and acts of attention as fragile forms of connection; its ending turns the walk into a choice to write from “honest night.”
