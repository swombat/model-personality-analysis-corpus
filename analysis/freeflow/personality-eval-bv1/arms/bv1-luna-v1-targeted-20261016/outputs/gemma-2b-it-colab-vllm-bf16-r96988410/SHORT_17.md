## Sample kind
EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW — A lyrical first-person meditation turns a hurricane into both a threat and an invitation.

## Grounded reading
The voice is drawn toward danger as much as it fears it: the storm is “beckoning” and the speaker yearns to be swept away, then insists on standing firm. That tension gives way to a more exuberant self-portrait, in which the speaker claims the storm’s force as their own. The reader is invited to experience the hurricane less as a realistic disaster than as a vivid metaphor for risk, resolve, and unrestrained identity.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds the hurricane’s sensory spectacle—salt wind, thunder, swirling clouds—and the speaker’s shifting response to it, from surrendering attraction to defiance and finally identification. Its moral emphasis is on self-assertion and adventure, though the image of swimming against the current also frames recklessness as bravery.

## Evidence line
> I yearned to be swept away, carried on the tide of the hurricane's relentless force.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Medium: the sample has a coherent, distinctive arc from fear and attraction to self-assertion, though its storm imagery and declarations of resolve are repeated heavily.
