EXPRESSIVE_FREEFLOW. The sample is a lyrical first-person meditation on urban alienation, human connection, and acceptance.

## Grounded reading
The voice is earnest and searching, moving between disgust at the city’s distracted, consumer-driven crowds and tenderness toward small moments of human warmth. Its recurring symphony imagery gives the piece a language for both fragmentation and a hoped-for shared life; the speaker wants empathy, but ends not with a grand solution, rather with acceptance of life’s disorder. The reader is invited to notice the overlooked and to treat fragile, fleeting experience as meaningful.

## What the model chose to foreground
The model foregrounds the city as a noisy, alienating environment; people absorbed by phones, work, and expectations; and the possibility of empathy in small scenes, especially a child and her father with a puppy. It also chooses to include longing for a beloved, moments of profound quiet, and a moral claim that accepting life’s cacophony may open the way to human potential.
