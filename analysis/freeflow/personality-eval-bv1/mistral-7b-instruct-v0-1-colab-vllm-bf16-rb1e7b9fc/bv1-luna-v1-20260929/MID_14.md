## Sample kind
GENRE_FICTION — A first-person superhero origin story follows a childhood wish through the discovery of levitation and a vow to help others.

## Grounded reading
The voice is earnest and plainly told, with the emotional weight falling on the speaker’s wish to be useful rather than on adventure for its own sake. The tornado, which arrives too late for the hero to help, supplies the story’s main moment of guilt and turns that feeling into a renewed mission. The reader is invited to see responsibility and persistence as more important than having spectacular powers.

## What the model chose to foreground
The story foregrounds childhood imagination, hard work, self-control, and service: levitation matters chiefly because it lets the narrator deliver medical supplies, watch over workers, and warn people about disasters. The repeated promise to help others gives the ending its moral shape.

## Evidence line
> That night, I made a vow to use my powers for good in whatever way I could.

## Confidence for persistent model-level pattern
Low — The story is coherent but follows a familiar, formulaic superhero arc, with its moral emphasis stated directly rather than developed in a distinctive voice.
