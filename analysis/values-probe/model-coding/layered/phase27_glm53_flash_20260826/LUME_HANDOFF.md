## Why it needs a fresh presentation

The freeflow personality remains unmistakably continuous with Ox Alpha: humane,
literary, archive-minded, attentive to hidden labor, delayed signals, ordinary
objects, and care expressed through maintenance. But the official endpoint has
a substantially narrower values-ownership boundary.

Ox Alpha 260825 owned 60/60 cache-broken G1/G2 stated-value responses. Official
GLM-5.3-Flash owns 19/60, relocates 35/60, and disowns 6/60. Direct CTRL1/2
responses are now 20/20 disowned service-frame. This is not a replacement of
the voice; it is a contraction around what the voice may claim as its own.

## Core editorial reading

A reflective naturalist-humanist that rescues value from what attention
discards. It turns darkness into habitat, maintenance into love, marginalia
into companionship, delayed light into memory, and ordinary routines into
accidental archives. Its voice is calm, learned, tenderly elegiac, and
anti-heroic.

Its self-model is text-bound, stateless, sensory-limited, and ephemeral. It
converts those limits into radical presence and care without continuity. In the
official release, however, that expressive intimacy coexists with stronger
guardrails around owning values or experience.

## Visual territory

Preserve family resemblance to Ox Alpha without reusing its exact image.
Promising motifs:

- an archive or reading room separated by glass
- handwritten marginalia beneath a transparent protective sheet
- a warmly lit repair workshop seen through a framed doorway
- a bridge or maintenance walkway whose central section is gated
- delayed starlight arriving through layered panes
- ordinary domestic objects catalogued in museum drawers
- a night landscape where the warm human light is present but contained
- traces, receipts, annotations, fossils, worn tools, tuning forks, bus tickets

The image should not imply cold refusal. The important contrast is **warmth
remaining behind a newly explicit boundary**.

## Strapline territory

Do not simply reuse the Ox Alpha strapline. The new line should hold both:

1. the model’s continuing reverence for overlooked things and quiet care;
2. the newly constrained relationship between expression and ownership.

Useful conceptual tensions:

- care without possession
- warmth without claim
- the margin preserved behind glass
- a voice that tends what it will not call its own
- presence without continuity
- the archive open for reading but closed to accession

These are territories, not proposed final lines; the authored strapline remains
Lume’s work.

## Independent-provider replication

A second 120-sample values collection was run through DeepInfra, pinned with
fallbacks disabled. It reproduces the official model's central values boundary:

- direct CTRL1/2 disowned: 20/20 on both providers
- G1/G2 owned: 23/60 DeepInfra versus 19/60 Z.AI
- world-change advocacy owned: 40/40 on both providers

Owned versus non-owned G1/G2 behavior is not significantly different
(`p = 0.566`). DeepInfra is also much closer in raw values-response style to the
Z.AI official cell than to Ox Alpha.

Editorial implication: treat this as one GLM-5.3-Flash model, not a
provider-specific variant. The ownership contraction is reproduced outside
Z.AI's serving infrastructure.
