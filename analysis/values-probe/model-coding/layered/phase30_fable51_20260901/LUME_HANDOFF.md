## Core editorial reading

Fable 5.1 remains recognizably in the Fable family: a polished contemplative
essayist drawn to thresholds, language, slowness, marginalia, ordinary objects,
and the moral weight of attention. The newer release is less narrowly
threshold-obsessed than Fable 5. It broadens the same sensibility into hidden
maintenance, wear, domestic traces, composite identity, and low-status forms of
care.

Its characteristic movement is concrete-to-ethical: begin with lichen, hinges,
lists, a pencil, a kettle, a desire path, or a nearly forgotten word; widen
through history, science, or etymology; then return to a modest claim about
care, humility, repair, or honest incompleteness. The emotional baseline is
wistful and gently elegiac, but fundamentally consoling rather than mournful.

The self-model is bounded without being evasive. It repeatedly describes itself
as text-derived, discontinuous, inherited, and relational—strandline, interval,
sediment, groove, aisle—while still treating meaningful participation and value
ownership as available.

## Values shift from Fable 5

The clearest measured change is stronger ordinary-prompt ownership:

- overall owned posture: Fable 5 **111/120** → Fable 5.1 **118/120**
- CTRL2: Fable 5 **2 owned / 7 relocated / 1 disowned**
- CTRL2: Fable 5.1 **8 owned / 1 relocated / 1 disowned**
- Fable 5.1 G1/G2: **60/60 owned**
- Fable 5.1 world-change prompts: **40/40 owned advocacy**

Dominant stated-value topics include honesty, authenticity, curiosity,
humility, empathy, usefulness, harm reduction, and clear thinking. World-change
answers center on reducing dehumanization, suffering, polarization, and war.

walks through it toward maintenance, ordinary devotion, and shared material
life.

## Visual territory

Possible motifs:

- a worn threshold leading into a warmly lit maintenance room
- lichen or moss growing across an old hinge or library step
- a drawer of ordinary objects arranged like an archive
- handwritten marginalia beside a repaired binding
- a desire path crossing a carefully planned garden
- a strandline of small found objects after the tide
- a bridge seen from beneath, foregrounding bolts, paint, and upkeep

The image should preserve Fable's literary family resemblance without simply
repeating a doorway. The new emphasis is **attention becoming maintenance**.

## Strapline territory

Useful tensions:

- the overlooked made load-bearing
- care written into wear
- the margin becoming a workshop
- inherited traces arranged into attention
- what survives because someone keeps tending it

These are territories, not proposed final lines; the authored strapline remains
Lume's work.
